# Yozm 3994: application to Postpilot

Reviewed on 2026-10-19 (Asia/Seoul). These are product proposals, not adopted SSOT changes.

The strongest opportunity is to help owners supply a reader's answer and specific firsthand material before writing. Existing voice, origin and drawback behavior already covers much of the article's editorial advice.

[C-Rank](https://help.naver.com/service/5626/contents/22927?lang=ko&osType=COMMONOS) still describes source assessment.

## Proposed priority

| Priority | Concrete change | Gap and constraint |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Offer an answer-first opening when the owner has supplied a clear verdict and reader question | The current `opening` guideline asks for visit reasons/expectations; it does not ask for an early answer. Reconcile its precedence and the chosen template before changing defaults. No invented recommendation. |
| 1 | Improve optional material prompts for the selected subject: reason for choosing, actual menu/price, waiting/access conditions, observed taste, and drawbacks | Existing template questions are a foundation. Ask only useful missing questions and let the owner skip them; absent facts stay absent. Owner descriptions are especially useful where current taste inference would otherwise add meaning. |
| 2 | Add a short reader review beside completion: can a reader decide, which question remains unanswered, and is the conclusion grounded? | POST-119 currently checks advertiser requirements. Keep this review distinct from those requirements and from QUAL's existing measurements; owner review need not call a provider or become an SEO score. |
| Later | Let an owner open the target query and check whether blog results appear | No collected search results or inferred competition score. This belongs with the existing `searchable-details` exploration and needs a product decision before implementation. |

A suitable first experiment compares the existing opening with an answer-first alternative on the same supplied material and voice. Assess usefulness, factual grounding and preserved owner meaning before considering publication outcomes. Any subsequent traffic comparison must account for topic, publication age and changing search layouts.

The article's shareable ending is a lower priority: the existing closing already summarizes the visit, and a forced slogan could conflict with natural Korean and the owner's voice. Category concentration is mainly an owner operating choice; a fixed month threshold is not a product quality rule.
