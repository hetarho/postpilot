Postpilot bundled ink resources

Wanted Sans Variable 1.0.3 is the declared source face. The unmodified source
WantedSansVariable.ttf and its copyright/OFL notice in LICENSE remain bundled.

PostpilotWantedInk-400.ttf, -600.ttf, -700.ttf and -800.ttf are fixed wght
instances of that same source, generated with fontTools 4.60.2. Their internal
families are Postpilot Wanted Ink W400/W600/W700/W800. The instances preserve
the corresponding source outlines, shaping, metrics and character coverage;
the names identify internal raster resources and are not owner font choices.
Only the wght instancing and internal name records are modified. No font file
is supplied by a project or user. The derivatives remain under OFL-1.1.

Reproducible generator: scripts/build-clip-ink-fonts.py in the Postpilot source.
Resource/source checksums and outline coverage are recorded in
frontend/src/entities/clip-design/config/clip-ink-fonts.json. Frozen ink
identity is frontend/src/entities/clip-design/config/clip-ink-identity.json.

Paperlogy, Jua and NanumMyeongjo resources remain unmodified, with their original
OFL notices in LICENSE-Paperlogy, LICENSE-Jua and LICENSE-NanumMyeongjo.

The compatible raster adapter is @resvg/resvg-wasm 2.6.2. Its binary dependency
source graph is a separate release qualification gate; these font proofs do
not approve distribution or activate a browser renderer.
