# IDEATION post-quality-and-related-links
> st:converted@260923 | Let the owner mark a post 발행됨 by pasting its 네이버 블로그 URL, measure what code can count across those published posts and offer one rule per finding, author the title in the template that already owns the body, and put a 분야's top-ranking phrases in front of the owner as substitutions in the prompt where the source already says the same thing and as hover suggestions on ② where it does not

## vision
- [o] Problem A: the product owns how a post sounds (VOICE), what shape it has (TMPL) and what it must avoid (GUIDE), but nothing measures what the account's posts look like next to each other, which is where an outside scorer and Naver's own spam rules both look.
- [o] Problem B: publication happens entirely outside the product now — automatic publication and its verified-URL retention were retired outright (PUB-38, PUB-47) — so `finalized` is the last thing the product knows about a post, nothing distinguishes one that reached readers from one that stayed in the editor, and the account's own published back catalogue cannot appear at the foot of a later post.
- [o] Problem C: the title and the tags are the two fields of a generated post that no rule reaches — `NaturalnessBaseline` excludes 제목 in its own first line (GEN-17) and never mentions tags, no voice section describes either, and a template decides body shape only; `tag_count` (POST-63) fixes how many tags there are and nothing about what they are — so every account inherits the model's defaults for both.
- [o] Problem D: readers increasingly arrive through a generated answer rather than a ranked link (네이버 AI 브리핑 live 2025-03-27), and nothing in the product is shaped for being cited rather than ranked.
- [o] Target: the owner publishing to Naver repeatedly, whose blog is judged on behaviour across posts rather than on one post's prose.
- [o] Core value: the product measures what it can count and hands the owner one switch per finding, so blog-level quality stops being luck without a rule ever being applied behind the owner's back.
