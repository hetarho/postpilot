# IDEATION prompt-engineering
> st:converted@261007 | Make prompt behavior reviewable and improve writing with visible semantic origins before owner-run traffic observations

## vision
- [o] Improve context efficiency, material/instruction boundaries and maintainability while preserving useful long guidance.
- [o] Help the owner write faster through expression and arrangement, retaining awareness and control of AI additions.
- [o] Improve writing and grounded tags first; the owner manually publishes and observes views before revisiting traffic hypotheses.
- [o] Product positioning: AI는 속도를, 글의 주도권은 나에게.

## explored
- [o] Current prompt map identifies real composition/code paths and the 995bcee9 tag default; [source map](../../docs/design/prompt-map-2026-10-07.md).
- [o] [Thirty-one-candidate audit](../../docs/research/prompt-audit-2026-10-07.md), [offline measurements](../../docs/research/prompt-audit-measurements-2026-10-07.json) and [synthetic reproductions](../../docs/research/prompt-audit-reproductions-2026-10-07.json) distinguish observed contracts from untested model-quality claims.
- [o] Three origins classify meaning: directly supplied owner meaning, visual interpretation and AI-added meaning. AI paraphrasing does not itself change origin; visual interpretation is not certified truth.
- [o] Review by meaningful phrase, including mixed origins within a sentence, across all human-readable post text. Named categories, legend and selectable source details accompany accessible colors.
- [o] Preserve useful terminology, transitions and richer sensory proposals, visibly identifying new meaning; prohibit unsupported objective experience under grounding and any photo-order-derived chronology.
- [o] Photo upload/storage/file/capture order supplies no event time, movement or action sequence. Owner-supplied event information and actual video source-time evidence are separate.
- [o] Planning, partial edits, template/style examples and finalization cannot silently promote AI meaning into owner facts. Approved memories can ground facts; new impressions inferred from preferences remain AI-added.
- [o] Keep usable canonical content when origin information fails; show unconfirmed review state without guessing a category, an extra paid repair call or a new finalization/export gate.
- [o] Tag count is a 1–10 upper bound, default 4; fewer or no supported tags are valid. Specific tag quality stays in the switchable tags 기본 지침, without broad-label padding.
- [o] Preserve literal/instruction/fact roles after template rendering; align LIST/VIDEO and other output fields across instructions, schemas, parsing and consumers.
- [o] Apply declared stock rules to stages consuming their meaning while preserving all scope-resolved verbatim owner lines. Storyline-only calls omit stock final title/tag/prose instructions; settings authoring scopes kind/mode; revision preserves unrelated content/tags and their origins.
- [o] Both developer/operator inventory and owner effective-request inspection use the real composition, with roles, activation, source files, precedence, output contracts and versions.
- [o] Distinguish current configuration, prepared preview and captured issued product request. No SDK payloads, credentials, signed links, other-owner access, ordinary prompt logging or blind contestant identity leaks.
- [o] Korean instructions remain the Korean-writing baseline. English common instruction comparisons preserve Korean facts, voice, examples, output and model conditions; no new user test factor or translation pipeline.
- [o] Structure/source tests, human prose review, actual usage and response validity measure different outcomes. Economical model JSON/origin success requires real evaluation and is not established by a price grade.
- [o] Sparse-material composition rules, naturalness density and revision noun freshness retain current policies until separately evaluated; no blanket shortening or unsolicited quality-score/SEO expansion.
- [o] [Accepted owner-control concept](../../docs/design/owner-controlled-writing-2026-10-07.md) and [interactive synthetic mockup](../../docs/design/writing-origin-review-2026-10-07.html) illustrate review without claiming shipped behavior or actual classification accuracy.
