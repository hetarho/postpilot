<!-- Plan 03 criteria owned by this job (plan AC numbers in parentheses). -->

- [ ] A1 (plan AC1) With an empty profile (no styleguide, no samples) the writing screen shows the warning; after one successful analysis it disappears.

- [ ] A5 (plan AC8, UI half) Progress is visible after 학습; reloading the page mid-analysis resumes polling from `active_job_id` to completion, and the new styleguide appears without a manual refresh.
- [ ] A6 (derived) 학습 requires an analyze-stage model; with none selected the button is disabled with "모델을 선택하세요".
