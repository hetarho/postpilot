      `Analyze llm.ReasoningEffort`, `:90` sets it to `llm.ReasoningUnset`, and `cmd/api/main.go:239` forwards only
      `Observe` and `Write`. No analyze path reads it — every `llm.Request` in `internal/voice`
      (`analyze_handler.go:49`, `learning.go:258`, `diff.go:192` and `:317`,
      `validation.go:207`/`:454`/`:489`/`:506`, `experiment_surface.go:52`) leaves `Reasoning` at its zero value.
      **Deleting is recommended**: the policy says analyze sends nothing, and "nothing" is already expressed by the
      absence of a stage value. Wiring it through would add a seam for a policy that does not exist yet. Record the
      decision in the job before writing code.
- [ ] T002 (if deleting) Remove `Analyze` from `LLMReasoningPolicy` (`config.go:83`) and from
      `defaultLLMReasoningPolicy()` (`:90`), and drop the assertion at `config_test.go:105`. Note in the struct's
      comment that analyze deliberately sends no effort and that this is expressed by *not* having a field —
      otherwise the next reader adds it back.

      decision"). Only one of T002/T003 is performed.
