# 16. Writing Guidelines

> Save reusable *writing guidelines* (작문 지침) — account-owned rules about what every post must avoid or watch out
> for — applied to all generation by default, optionally scoped to specific templates, capturable from the AI-revision
> flow, plus a fixed built-in grounding constraint so the writer stops inventing facts | Scope: FS | Status: built (job 35)

## Template

The product injects two kinds of authored guidance today, and both have a defined, deliberately narrow job: the
**voice** decides how sentences sound (endings, vocabulary, rhythm — plan 03/10, [I4]), and the **template** decides
what kind of text a post is (genre, structure, required content — plan 11). Neither covers a third category of
failure the owner keeps seeing in real output: writing that is fluent and on-genre but *unnatural* or *ungrounded*.
Two real examples from a review of a 24-hour unmanned rice-cake shop:

- The draft praised the CCTV ("안전하네요~") — a cliché observation, since every unmanned store has CCTV; the owner
  never wants this kind of filler sentiment.
- The draft said the owner handed the goods over ("주인분에게 건네받았다") — an invented fact that directly
  contradicts the store being unmanned; nothing in the memo or photo observations supports it.

Today there is nowhere to put the rule that prevents these. A voice rule is the wrong aggregate: these rules are not
register, they would need re-registering for every voice, and [I4]/plan 10 deliberately keep voices about *how it
sounds*. A template brief is also wrong: a template is optional per post (default 없음), so a rule placed there never
reaches unassigned posts, and a universal rule ("don't invent facts") would need pasting into every template. The memo
is per-post and retyped.

A **writing guideline** (작문 지침, user-facing noun **지침**) is a saved, account-owned rule: one short instruction
about what to avoid or watch out for when writing. Guidelines apply to **every** generation and revision of the
account by default; a guideline may instead be scoped to specific templates ("CCTV 언급 금지" on the 무인가게 리뷰
template) so it fires only when a post has one of them. The applicable guidelines are frozen at enqueue and injected
into the write/revise prompts after the voice profile and the template brief. Alongside the user-authored system, the
fixed write/revise prompt text (code) gains a universal **grounding constraint** — never state facts absent from the
memo and photo observations — because the invented-fact failure is one every user hits and should not require
per-account authoring. (The complementary universal *stylistic* floor — corpus-confirmed Korean AI-tell caps — is
[change 10](../changes/archive/10.korean-naturalness-baseline-beneath-the-.md) against plan 06, now built by job 36; this
plan's built-in layer is grounding only, so the two fixed-text additions never overlap.)

This serves PRD goal 1 (a publish-ready draft in ~3 minutes — recurring manual deletions of the same bad sentence
disappear) and S1's "읽어보고 한 번 고치기" loop (the same correction stops being needed twice), without touching
goal 2's voice ownership (the voice profile is neither read nor written differently). It extends F-4 (글 생성) and
F-6 (AI로 수정) prompt inputs the same way plan 11 did.

## Scope / Non-goals

### In scope

- A `Guideline` aggregate per account: `text` (one authored rule) plus a `scope` — **global** (applies to every
  post) or **templates** (applies only to posts whose selected template is in the guideline's template set) — with
  create, update, delete lifecycles and an authenticated list.
- Scope resolution at enqueue: a post with template P receives the global guidelines plus the guidelines linked to P;
  a post with no template receives the global ones only.
- Prompt injection: the frozen guideline texts enter the write prompt and the revise prompt as one `[작문 지침]`
  section at one fixed position — after the template section when the post has one, otherwise immediately after the
  voice profile's `[종결어미 제약]` — and always before the per-post material, with a fixed precedence sentence
  (guidelines win over the template on content conflicts; the voice profile keeps owning register).
- Freezing: ordinary generation, both A/B write candidates, and every revision freeze the applicable guideline
  texts at enqueue in the job payload / experiment snapshot, exactly as the template brief is frozen; later edits
  never change in-flight or resumable work.
- Capture from revision: the AI-revision result surface offers `지침으로 저장` beside the existing `규칙으로 저장`,
  seeding a dialog with the revision instruction (editable before save) and a scope choice (전역, or the post's
  current template when it has one). It calls the same create RPC; it is an explicit user save, not learning.
- A built-in grounding constraint in the fixed write and revise prompt text (code, not config, not a row): the
  writer must not state facts absent from the memo and photo observations, and must omit or hedge what it cannot
  confirm. Golden prompt files update once for this. This layer is grounding only — the corpus-derived stylistic
  naturalness baseline belongs to [change 10](../changes/archive/10.korean-naturalness-baseline-beneath-the-.md) (plan 06)
  and is not duplicated here.
- A management screen `/guidelines` (nav 지침, after 템플릿) and the migration adding `guidelines` and
  `guideline_templates`; existing accounts start with zero guidelines.
- **Candidate accrual from revisions** (change 26, job 54). A completed revision records its instruction verbatim
  as a `pending` **candidate** — occurrence-counted, deduplicated exact-after-trim across guidelines and all three
  candidate states, bounded at `GUIDELINE_CANDIDATE_MAX_PENDING` — and `/guidelines` gains a **후보** section where
  each is approved (with the scope chosen at that moment) or dismissed. A candidate carries no scope, reaches no
  prompt, and is never read or written by a model.

### Non-goals

- **Learning, inferring, or auto-generating guidelines.** No model writes, suggests, ranks, or retires a guideline
  or a candidate; there is no evidence pipeline, no similarity or semantic dedupe, no auto-approval and no threshold
  at which a candidate becomes a rule by itself. [I4]'s learning boundary stays entirely with voice. The revision
  capture and every approval are explicit user actions on user-authored text.

  Change 26 amended this non-goal's *reach*, not its substance: it added the **candidate lifecycle** this bullet
  originally excluded (`pending`/`approved`/`dismissed`), because **recording what the user wrote is not learning
  about it**. A candidate is a receipt — copied verbatim, deduplicated by exact string equality, counted, and inert
  until the user approves it with a scope. Nothing infers a rule from it and nothing applies it.
- **Voice scoping.** A rule tied to how one voice sounds already belongs to that voice's user rules; a
  voice-scoped *content* rule is deferred until a real case appears (a later CHANGE can add a third scope).
- **Per-post guideline selection or opt-out.** Applicability derives from scope alone; the editor gets no guideline
  selector, toggle, or preview in v1. The one editor surface this plan touches is the revision capture.
- **Enable/disable toggles, manual ordering, version history, import/export.** Delete is the off switch; injection
  order is deterministic by scope group and creation time.
- **Seeded or shipped guidelines.** No built-in library rows; the empty state shows one worked example as copy, not
  as a row (same principle as templates). The universal grounding rule ships as fixed prompt text, not as a seeded row.
- **Output validation against guidelines.** Guidelines are prompt material; no post-generation validator checks
  compliance (the block validator of plan 06 is unchanged).
- **Rendering frozen guidelines in the experiment detail or job views.** They participate in the snapshot and input
  hash; they are not displayed (unlike `template_name`, nothing names them after the fact in v1).
- **Changing the memo's or the template's role.** Per-post facts (이 가게는 무인이다) stay in the memo; genre and
  required content stay in the template. A guideline is a reusable prohibition/caution, not a brief.
- **Counting affected guidelines in the DeleteTemplate confirmation.** The confirmation keeps naming detached posts
  only; a guideline orphaned by a template deletion surfaces on `/guidelines` instead.
- Anything in PRD §8; canonical blocks, browser image work, copy-only export, and the explicit publishing boundary
  are unchanged ([I1], [I2], [I6]). No guideline surface starts, implies, or retries a publication.

## Design

### Domain rules and terminology

The user-facing noun is **지침** (full form 작문 지침; `Guideline` in contracts and code). A guideline is an
account-owned aggregate with one authored text and a scope. Nothing references a guideline: posts, jobs, and
experiments carry frozen *texts*, never guideline ids, so deleting a guideline affects nothing already enqueued and
detaches nothing.

Invariants:

1. every guideline belongs to exactly one account; every RPC derives the account from the session and none accepts a
   user id (as posts, voices, and templates);
2. `scope` is `global` or `templates`; a `templates` scope names ≥ 1 owned templates **at creation and on every scope
   update** — only a template deletion may leave the set empty, and such a guideline applies nowhere until rescoped;
3. a prompt uses the guideline texts frozen at enqueue, never the live rows;
4. the voice profile's bytes/order/injection and the template section's bytes are identical whether or not guidelines
   exist — the `[작문 지침]` section is appended after them;
5. no guideline read, write, or deletion enqueues work or makes a provider call ([I5]);
6. no model writes or learns a guideline; the revision capture stores exactly the user-approved dialog text.

**Field rules** (validated server-side, mirrored client-side; limits are config, see *Policy / Config Impact*):

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| `text` | trimmed, non-empty, ≤ `GuidelineTextMaxChars` Unicode chars, unique among the account's guidelines (exact match after trim → `AlreadyExists` with a Korean message) |
| `scope` | `global` (must carry no template ids) or `templates` (must carry ≥ 1 distinct owned template ids; an unknown or foreign id is `NotFound` and nothing is applied) |
| account cap | creating beyond `GuidelineMaxPerAccount` is refused (`FailedPrecondition`, Korean message naming the cap) |

`UpdateGuideline` uses field presence like `UpdateTemplate`: an update carrying only `text` leaves the scope intact;
an update carrying a scope patch replaces the **whole** scope (kind + template set) atomically in one transaction.

### Prompt injection — the one contract this plan adds

`BuildWritePrompt` and `BuildRevisePrompt` gain an optional ordered `guidelines []string`. When non-empty, the
**system** prompt (the stable part) is:

```
WritePrompt / RevisePrompt fixed text (now includes the grounding constraint, see below)
[스타일가이드] … [활성 대조 규칙] … [글 예시 발췌] … [사용자 규칙] … [종결어미 제약] …   ← unchanged voice prefix (plan 03/06 order)
[글 템플릿: {name}] …                                                                  ← unchanged template section (plan 11), when present
[작문 지침]
- {text₁}
- {text₂}
…
지침은 이 글에서 지켜야 할 주의 사항과 피해야 할 내용·표현을 정합니다. 지침이 템플릿의 요구와 충돌하면 지침을
우선하고, 문체·종결어미·어휘는 위의 말투 프로필을 따르세요.
```

followed by the unchanged per-post material (`[이번 글]` …). Rationale:

- **Order.** The voice prefix must stay byte-stable across posts (PRD §5's caching note) — guidelines come after it.
  The template section already varies per post, and template-scoped guidelines vary *with the template*, so the
  guideline section sits after the template section; prohibitions also benefit from sitting closest to the task
  material. One section, one position: after the template when present, otherwise directly after `[종결어미 제약]`;
  always before `[이번 글]`.
- **Within the section**: global guidelines first, then template-scoped ones, each group ordered by `created_at, id`
  ascending — deterministic, so prompts and snapshots are reproducible.
- **Precedence sentence.** A guideline is typically a prohibition ("CCTV 언급 금지") that must beat a template's
  general content instruction ("시설을 소개") — the user added it precisely because the default output was wrong.
  Register stays with the voice profile, consistent with plan 11's precedence sentence. The sentence is fixed prompt
  text (code, not config).
- **No applicable guidelines → no section.** The prompt is then byte-identical to the post-grounding-baseline prompt
  (snapshot tests guard this; see next paragraph for the one deliberate baseline change).

**Built-in grounding constraint.** The fixed write and revise prompt text gains one universal constraint, next to
the existing "do not copy example facts/phrases" rule, requiring that the writer state no concrete fact absent from
the memo and the photo observations (interactions with people, facilities, service, conversations, prices, …) and
omit or confine to the observed range whatever it cannot confirm. Exact Korean wording is finalized in the job; it
is code, not config. This is the layer that catches "주인분에게 건네받았다" for every account with zero setup. The
observe prompt is untouched ([I3]). It is disjoint from change 10's stylistic baseline section: when both land, the
grounding line sits with the static task rules (exact adjacency finalized by the later-landing job) and neither
restates the other. Golden prompt files are regenerated once for this addition, and every other acceptance
criterion about byte-identity is stated **relative to that new baseline**.

### Freezing

Applicable guidelines are frozen the same way the template brief is:

- `StartGeneration` and `StartRevision` resolve the post's current `template_id` server-side (already done for the
  template brief), ask the guideline context for the applicable ordered texts (`global` + linked-to-that-template),
  and write them into the job payload (`generation_jobs.payload`, next to `target_length` and `template`). One read,
  one consistent view: the same template id used for the brief drives the guideline resolution.
- `StartWriteExperiment` puts the same ordered texts into the shared prepared snapshot, so both candidates receive
  byte-identical system prompts except for model ref. The snapshot input hash therefore changes with the applicable
  guidelines — a different rule set is a different experiment.
- Resumed/retried jobs use the payload; they never reload guideline rows. Old payloads without the member decode as
  "no guidelines".
- A guideline edited, rescoped, or deleted after enqueue changes nothing in flight; a template deleted between the
  post save and the enqueue simply yields the global set (consistent with the brief's absence).

### Proto / Connect contract

New `proto/postpilot/v1/guideline.proto`:

```proto
service GuidelineService {
  rpc ListGuidelines(ListGuidelinesRequest) returns (ListGuidelinesResponse);
  rpc CreateGuideline(CreateGuidelineRequest) returns (CreateGuidelineResponse);
  rpc UpdateGuideline(UpdateGuidelineRequest) returns (UpdateGuidelineResponse);
  rpc DeleteGuideline(DeleteGuidelineRequest) returns (DeleteGuidelineResponse);
}

enum GuidelineScope {
  GUIDELINE_SCOPE_UNSPECIFIED = 0;
  GUIDELINE_SCOPE_GLOBAL      = 1;
  GUIDELINE_SCOPE_TEMPLATES    = 2;
}

message GuidelineTemplateRef { string id = 1; string name = 2; }

message Guideline {
  string id = 1;
  string text = 2;
  GuidelineScope scope = 3;
  repeated GuidelineTemplateRef templates = 4;  // empty for GLOBAL; may be empty for PURPOSES after a template deletion
  string created_at = 5;
  string updated_at = 6;
}

message CreateGuidelineRequest { string text = 1; GuidelineScope scope = 2; repeated string template_ids = 3; }
message GuidelineScopePatch   { GuidelineScope scope = 1; repeated string template_ids = 2; }
message UpdateGuidelineRequest {
  string id = 1;
  optional string text = 2;
  GuidelineScopePatch scope = 3;   // message presence = replace the whole scope
}
message DeleteGuidelineRequest  { string id = 1; }
message DeleteGuidelineResponse {}
```

`ListGuidelines` returns the guidelines in injection order (global group, then scoped group, each by creation time)
so the management screen shows exactly what the writer will see. Template names in `templates` are projected through
the guideline context's consumer-owned `TemplateDirectory` port, never a SQL join. Generated code is regenerated with
`pnpm gen:proto`; generated files are never hand-edited.

### SQLite model and migration

```sql
guidelines (
  id         TEXT PRIMARY KEY,
  user_id    TEXT NOT NULL REFERENCES users(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  text       TEXT NOT NULL,
  scope      TEXT NOT NULL CHECK (scope IN ('global','templates')),
  created_at TEXT NOT NULL,
  updated_at TEXT NOT NULL,
  UNIQUE (id, user_id),
  UNIQUE (user_id, text)
)

guideline_templates (
  guideline_id TEXT NOT NULL,
  template_id   TEXT NOT NULL,
  user_id      TEXT NOT NULL,
  PRIMARY KEY (guideline_id, template_id),
  FOREIGN KEY (guideline_id, user_id) REFERENCES guidelines(id, user_id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  FOREIGN KEY (template_id,   user_id) REFERENCES templates(id, user_id)   ON DELETE CASCADE
)
```

- The composite FKs keep a guideline from linking another account's template even if a service check is bypassed
  (same pattern as `posts.template_id`).
- Deleting a **template** cascades its link rows (whole-row `ON DELETE CASCADE` is safe on a composite key — the
  `SET NULL` trap that forced plan 11's trigger does not apply to a delete). The guideline row survives; one left
  with zero links applies nowhere and `/guidelines` shows it as such. Deleting a **guideline** cascades only its own
  links. `DeleteTemplate`'s response and post-detach behavior are unchanged.
- Embedded goose migration `0014_writing_guidelines.sql` creates both tables. No backfill, no seed rows; existing
  accounts have zero guidelines. Timestamps follow the fixed-width UTC RFC3339 rule. SQL regenerated with
  `pnpm gen:sql`, boot-fatal on failure ([I7]).

### Job and state transitions

No new job kind, stage, status, or queue behavior, and **no new column on posts**: nothing references a guideline.
The only new state is the two tables above plus the frozen `guidelines []string` payload/snapshot member.

```
guideline: created ──(text/scope edits)──▶ updated ──delete──▶ gone (name-free, nothing detaches)
template P deleted:  P's link rows vanish; a 'templates' guideline with no links left applies nowhere until rescoped
job payload:        frozen ordered texts at enqueue; never re-read
```

### Frontend behavior and placement

- **`/guidelines`** (top-level destination 지침, after 템플릿 in the nav; lazy route split following the current
  router conventions from job 31): the account's guidelines in injection order — text, a scope badge (`전역`, or the
  template-name chips; `적용 대상 없음` for an orphaned scoped guideline), delete with a plain confirmation stating
  that already-enqueued work keeps its frozen text and nothing else is affected. Create form: textarea with a live
  remaining-character count from `shared/config`, scope control (radio 전역 / 특정 템플릿 with a template multi-select
  fed by the template directory). Text edits in place via the shared `Editable` primitive, read-first; the scope is
  edited through the same control the create form uses, saved as one whole-scope patch. Empty state shows one worked
  example as copy (e.g. "무인 매장 글에서 직원·주인과의 상호작용이나 CCTV를 언급하지 않기") and the create form; no
  row is created for the user.
- **Revision capture**: the `edit-with-ai` result surface renders `지침으로 저장` beside the existing
  `규칙으로 저장`. It opens a small dialog seeded with the revision instruction, editable before saving (a raw
  instruction like "무인 매장이니까 주인 얘기 빼줘" is usually generalized by the user before it becomes a rule),
  with a scope choice: 전역 (default), or 이 글의 템플릿 「{name}」에만 when the post currently has a template (read
  from the already-loaded post; no new query). Saving calls the standard create mutation; `AlreadyExists` renders as
  "이미 같은 지침이 있어요" (informational, not a failure state); other refusals show the server message verbatim.
- **Editor**: otherwise untouched — no guideline selector or preview (non-goal).
- **Caches**: query key `(account_id, 'guidelines')`, re-read on mount (`staleTime: 0`, `refetchOnMount: 'always'`)
  like the template directory, because template names are a projection. Create/update/delete invalidate the list.
  `features/edit-template`'s rename invalidation additionally marks the guideline list stale (it projects the name);
  template deletion does the same (scope chips change). Account switches partition as today.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. An authenticated user can create, list, update, and delete guidelines. `text` is trimmed, non-empty, bounded, and
   unique within the account (an exact duplicate after trim is `AlreadyExists` with a Korean message). No RPC accepts
   a user id; a foreign guideline id is indistinguishable from an unknown one (`NotFound`). Creating beyond
   `GuidelineMaxPerAccount` is refused with a Korean message naming the cap.
2. Scope is validated: `global` with template ids, or `templates` with zero ids, is `InvalidArgument`; an unknown or
   foreign template id is `NotFound` and nothing is applied; duplicate ids in one request collapse to one link. The
   list projects each scoped guideline's templates as `{id, name}` through the template directory, never a SQL join,
   and returns guidelines in injection order.
3. `UpdateGuideline` changes only what the request carries: a text-only update leaves the scope intact (store test
   against a concurrent scope edit), and a scope patch replaces the whole scope atomically in one transaction.
4. Generating a post whose account has applicable guidelines appends exactly one `[작문 지침]` section — after the
   `[글 템플릿]` section when the post has a template, otherwise immediately after `[종결어미 제약]`, and always
   before `[이번 글]` — containing the texts verbatim as list lines, global group first then scoped group, each
   ordered by creation time, followed by the fixed precedence sentence (prompt snapshot).
5. Scope resolution is exact: a post with template P receives the global guidelines plus P-linked ones; a post with
   no template receives the global ones only; guidelines linked only to other templates never appear; a
   `templates`-scoped guideline with zero remaining links appears in no prompt.
6. With no applicable guidelines the prompt contains no `[작문 지침]` section and is byte-identical to the
   post-grounding baseline (golden files). The voice profile prefix and the template section are byte-identical with
   and without guidelines (prefix-comparison tests), so plan 11's acceptance criteria 4–5 keep holding.
7. Every write and revise prompt — with or without user guidelines, with or without a template — contains the fixed
   built-in grounding constraint forbidding facts absent from the memo and photo observations. It lives in the fixed
   prompt text (code, not config, not a database row); the observe prompt is unchanged; golden files are regenerated
   exactly once for this addition.

  문장은 사실 확인 없이 그대로 두세요.). The split is load-bearing: the revise prompt receives neither the memo nor
  the observations, so the unscoped "omit what you cannot confirm" the plan describes would have licensed stripping
  real facts out of blocks the request never mentioned — directly against the byte-for-byte preservation rule on the
  next line. It sits on the second line of the static task rules, before the format rules and well before change 10's

## Note — job 54 / change 26

The accrual path this plan's non-goal originally excluded now exists, with the model still entirely out of it.

- **Recording.** A `revise` job that completes writes its instruction verbatim as a `pending` candidate, from
  inside its own completion path — after the revised content is persisted and before the last progress tick. It
  creates no job and calls no provider ([I5]), and **a recording failure never fails the revision**: the result
  the user is looking at is authoritative. A failed, cancelled or still-running revision records nothing, which
  follows from the call position alone.
- **Deduplication.** Exact after trim, the same rule `guidelines(user_id, text)` already uses, and enforced by
  `guideline_candidates(user_id, text)` rather than by a service check. A repeat of a `pending` candidate
  increments its occurrence count and advances its last-seen; a text that is already a saved guideline, or an
  `approved`/`dismissed` candidate, records nothing and revives nothing. All three states are kept, because the
  row is what suppresses re-recording.
- **The bound.** Recording stops at `GUIDELINE_CANDIDATE_MAX_PENDING` (default 50, backend-only) rather than
  evicting the oldest. The dedupe read, the guideline check and the pending count all happen inside one
  transaction, so two concurrent recordings can neither both insert nor both pass a full queue.
- **The bound split.** A candidate is stored at the **revision** instruction bound (500), not the guideline bound
  (300): refusing a long instruction at recording time would lose exactly the most specific corrections. The
  guideline bound is enforced at approval, with the live counter.
- **Approval is the create.** There is no Approve procedure. `CreateGuideline` gained an optional
  `from_candidate_id` and marks the candidate approved in the **same transaction as the insert** — by that id, and
  by the saved text either way. A guideline text **edit** runs the same approval, so no way of saving a text can
  leave that text sitting as a pending candidate. A refused create (text bound, account cap, duplicate) approves
  nothing and the candidate stays pending. Only a `pending` row may be moved, so a stale tab cannot approve one
  candidate twice or dismiss one already approved.
- **`지침으로 저장` is unchanged.** Saving on the spot also approves the candidate the same revision recorded,
  matched by text, so it never afterwards appears in the 후보 section. An **edited** save is a different rule from
  the one recorded, and the original correction correctly stays a pending candidate.
- **Lifecycle.** Deleting the source post drops the candidate's `post_slug` (a plain nullable column, no foreign
  key) after the post row is actually gone, and keeps the text — the row is listed without a link. Deleting a saved
  guideline neither revives nor resets its approved candidate.
- **The boundary.** No candidate in any state reaches a write or revise prompt; only a saved guideline is injected.
  An account with candidates but no new guidelines produces byte-identical prompts to the same account before this
  change, which is asserted directly rather than argued.
- Migration `0023_guideline_candidates` is embedded and boot-run ([I7]). It seeds nothing and backfills nothing:
  there is no revision history to reconstruct, so an existing account starts with an empty 후보 section.
