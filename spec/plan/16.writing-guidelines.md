# 16. Writing Guidelines

> Save reusable *writing guidelines* (작문 지침) — account-owned rules about what every post must avoid or watch out
> for — applied to all generation by default, optionally scoped to specific purposes, capturable from the AI-revision
> flow, plus a fixed built-in grounding constraint so the writer stops inventing facts | Scope: FS | Status: planning

## Purpose

The product injects two kinds of authored guidance today, and both have a defined, deliberately narrow job: the
**voice** decides how sentences sound (endings, vocabulary, rhythm — plan 03/10, [I4]), and the **purpose** decides
what kind of text a post is (genre, structure, required content — plan 11). Neither covers a third category of
failure the owner keeps seeing in real output: writing that is fluent and on-genre but *unnatural* or *ungrounded*.
Two real examples from a review of a 24-hour unmanned rice-cake shop:

- The draft praised the CCTV ("안전하네요~") — a cliché observation, since every unmanned store has CCTV; the owner
  never wants this kind of filler sentiment.
- The draft said the owner handed the goods over ("주인분에게 건네받았다") — an invented fact that directly
  contradicts the store being unmanned; nothing in the memo or photo observations supports it.

Today there is nowhere to put the rule that prevents these. A voice rule is the wrong aggregate: these rules are not
register, they would need re-registering for every voice, and [I4]/plan 10 deliberately keep voices about *how it
sounds*. A purpose brief is also wrong: a purpose is optional per post (default 없음), so a rule placed there never
reaches unassigned posts, and a universal rule ("don't invent facts") would need pasting into every purpose. The memo
is per-post and retyped.

A **writing guideline** (작문 지침, user-facing noun **지침**) is a saved, account-owned rule: one short instruction
about what to avoid or watch out for when writing. Guidelines apply to **every** generation and revision of the
account by default; a guideline may instead be scoped to specific purposes ("CCTV 언급 금지" on the 무인가게 리뷰
purpose) so it fires only when a post has one of them. The applicable guidelines are frozen at enqueue and injected
into the write/revise prompts after the voice profile and the purpose brief. Alongside the user-authored system, the
fixed write/revise prompt text (code) gains a universal **grounding constraint** — never state facts absent from the
memo and photo observations — because the invented-fact failure is one every user hits and should not require
per-account authoring. (The complementary universal *stylistic* floor — corpus-confirmed Korean AI-tell caps — is
[change 10](../changes/archive/10.korean-naturalness-baseline-beneath-the-.md) against plan 06, now built by job 36; this
plan's built-in layer is grounding only, so the two fixed-text additions never overlap.)

This serves PRD goal 1 (a publish-ready draft in ~3 minutes — recurring manual deletions of the same bad sentence
disappear) and S1's "읽어보고 한 번 고치기" loop (the same correction stops being needed twice), without touching
goal 2's voice ownership (the voice profile is neither read nor written differently). It extends F-4 (글 생성) and
F-6 (AI로 수정) prompt inputs the same way plan 11 did.

## Scope / Non-goals

### In scope

- A `Guideline` aggregate per account: `text` (one authored rule) plus a `scope` — **global** (applies to every
  post) or **purposes** (applies only to posts whose selected purpose is in the guideline's purpose set) — with
  create, update, delete lifecycles and an authenticated list.
- Scope resolution at enqueue: a post with purpose P receives the global guidelines plus the guidelines linked to P;
  a post with no purpose receives the global ones only.
- Prompt injection: the frozen guideline texts enter the write prompt and the revise prompt as one `[작문 지침]`
  section at one fixed position — after the purpose section when the post has one, otherwise immediately after the
  voice profile's `[종결어미 제약]` — and always before the per-post material, with a fixed precedence sentence
  (guidelines win over the purpose on content conflicts; the voice profile keeps owning register).
- Freezing: ordinary generation, both A/B write candidates, and every revision freeze the applicable guideline
  texts at enqueue in the job payload / experiment snapshot, exactly as the purpose brief is frozen; later edits
  never change in-flight or resumable work.
- Capture from revision: the AI-revision result surface offers `지침으로 저장` beside the existing `규칙으로 저장`,
  seeding a dialog with the revision instruction (editable before save) and a scope choice (전역, or the post's
  current purpose when it has one). It calls the same create RPC; it is an explicit user save, not learning.
- A built-in grounding constraint in the fixed write and revise prompt text (code, not config, not a row): the
  writer must not state facts absent from the memo and photo observations, and must omit or hedge what it cannot
  confirm. Golden prompt files update once for this. This layer is grounding only — the corpus-derived stylistic
  naturalness baseline belongs to [change 10](../changes/archive/10.korean-naturalness-baseline-beneath-the-.md) (plan 06)
  and is not duplicated here.
- A management screen `/guidelines` (nav 지침, after 용도) and the migration adding `guidelines` and
  `guideline_purposes`; existing accounts start with zero guidelines.

### Non-goals

- **Learning, inferring, or auto-generating guidelines.** No model writes, suggests, ranks, or retires a guideline;
  there is no evidence pipeline, no candidate/active lifecycle. [I4]'s learning boundary stays entirely with voice.
  The revision capture is an explicit user action on user-authored text.
- **Voice scoping.** A rule tied to how one voice sounds already belongs to that voice's user rules; a
  voice-scoped *content* rule is deferred until a real case appears (a later CHANGE can add a third scope).
- **Per-post guideline selection or opt-out.** Applicability derives from scope alone; the editor gets no guideline
  selector, toggle, or preview in v1. The one editor surface this plan touches is the revision capture.
- **Enable/disable toggles, manual ordering, version history, import/export.** Delete is the off switch; injection
  order is deterministic by scope group and creation time.
- **Seeded or shipped guidelines.** No built-in library rows; the empty state shows one worked example as copy, not
  as a row (same principle as purposes). The universal grounding rule ships as fixed prompt text, not as a seeded row.
- **Output validation against guidelines.** Guidelines are prompt material; no post-generation validator checks
  compliance (the block validator of plan 06 is unchanged).
- **Rendering frozen guidelines in the experiment detail or job views.** They participate in the snapshot and input
  hash; they are not displayed (unlike `purpose_name`, nothing names them after the fact in v1).
- **Changing the memo's or the purpose's role.** Per-post facts (이 가게는 무인이다) stay in the memo; genre and
  required content stay in the purpose. A guideline is a reusable prohibition/caution, not a brief.
- **Counting affected guidelines in the DeletePurpose confirmation.** The confirmation keeps naming detached posts
  only; a guideline orphaned by a purpose deletion surfaces on `/guidelines` instead.
- Anything in PRD §8; canonical blocks, browser image work, copy-only export, and the explicit publishing boundary
  are unchanged ([I1], [I2], [I6]). No guideline surface starts, implies, or retries a publication.

## Design

### Domain rules and terminology

The user-facing noun is **지침** (full form 작문 지침; `Guideline` in contracts and code). A guideline is an
account-owned aggregate with one authored text and a scope. Nothing references a guideline: posts, jobs, and
experiments carry frozen *texts*, never guideline ids, so deleting a guideline affects nothing already enqueued and
detaches nothing.

Invariants:

1. every guideline belongs to exactly one account; every RPC derives the account from the session and none accepts a
   user id (as posts, voices, and purposes);
2. `scope` is `global` or `purposes`; a `purposes` scope names ≥ 1 owned purposes **at creation and on every scope
   update** — only a purpose deletion may leave the set empty, and such a guideline applies nowhere until rescoped;
3. a prompt uses the guideline texts frozen at enqueue, never the live rows;
4. the voice profile's bytes/order/injection and the purpose section's bytes are identical whether or not guidelines
   exist — the `[작문 지침]` section is appended after them;
5. no guideline read, write, or deletion enqueues work or makes a provider call ([I5]);
6. no model writes or learns a guideline; the revision capture stores exactly the user-approved dialog text.

**Field rules** (validated server-side, mirrored client-side; limits are config, see *Policy / Config Impact*):

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| `text` | trimmed, non-empty, ≤ `GuidelineTextMaxChars` Unicode chars, unique among the account's guidelines (exact match after trim → `AlreadyExists` with a Korean message) |
| `scope` | `global` (must carry no purpose ids) or `purposes` (must carry ≥ 1 distinct owned purpose ids; an unknown or foreign id is `NotFound` and nothing is applied) |
| account cap | creating beyond `GuidelineMaxPerAccount` is refused (`FailedPrecondition`, Korean message naming the cap) |

`UpdateGuideline` uses field presence like `UpdatePurpose`: an update carrying only `text` leaves the scope intact;
an update carrying a scope patch replaces the **whole** scope (kind + purpose set) atomically in one transaction.

### Prompt injection — the one contract this plan adds

`BuildWritePrompt` and `BuildRevisePrompt` gain an optional ordered `guidelines []string`. When non-empty, the
**system** prompt (the stable part) is:

```
WritePrompt / RevisePrompt fixed text (now includes the grounding constraint, see below)
[스타일가이드] … [활성 대조 규칙] … [글 예시 발췌] … [사용자 규칙] … [종결어미 제약] …   ← unchanged voice prefix (plan 03/06 order)
[글의 용도: {name}] …                                                                  ← unchanged purpose section (plan 11), when present
[작문 지침]
- {text₁}
- {text₂}
…
지침은 이 글에서 지켜야 할 주의 사항과 피해야 할 내용·표현을 정합니다. 지침이 용도의 요구와 충돌하면 지침을
우선하고, 문체·종결어미·어휘는 위의 말투 프로필을 따르세요.
```

followed by the unchanged per-post material (`[이번 글]` …). Rationale:

- **Order.** The voice prefix must stay byte-stable across posts (PRD §5's caching note) — guidelines come after it.
  The purpose section already varies per post, and purpose-scoped guidelines vary *with the purpose*, so the
  guideline section sits after the purpose section; prohibitions also benefit from sitting closest to the task
  material. One section, one position: after the purpose when present, otherwise directly after `[종결어미 제약]`;
  always before `[이번 글]`.
- **Within the section**: global guidelines first, then purpose-scoped ones, each group ordered by `created_at, id`
  ascending — deterministic, so prompts and snapshots are reproducible.
- **Precedence sentence.** A guideline is typically a prohibition ("CCTV 언급 금지") that must beat a purpose's
  general content instruction ("시설을 소개") — the user added it precisely because the default output was wrong.
  Register stays with the voice profile, consistent with plan 11's precedence sentence. The sentence is fixed prompt
  text (code, not config).
- **No applicable guidelines → no section.** The prompt is then byte-identical to the post-grounding-baseline prompt
  (snapshot tests guard this; see next paragraph for the one deliberate baseline change).

**Built-in grounding constraint.** The fixed write and revise prompt text gains one universal constraint, next to
the existing "do not copy example facts/phrases" rule, requiring that the writer state no concrete fact absent from
the memo and the photo observations (interactions with people, facilities, service, conversations, prices, …) and
omit or confine to the observed range whatever it cannot confirm. Exact Korean wording is finalized in the job; it
is code, not config. This is the layer that catches "주인분에게 건네받았다" for every account with zero setup. The
observe prompt is untouched ([I3]). It is disjoint from change 10's stylistic baseline section: when both land, the
grounding line sits with the static task rules (exact adjacency finalized by the later-landing job) and neither
restates the other. Golden prompt files are regenerated once for this addition, and every other acceptance
criterion about byte-identity is stated **relative to that new baseline**.

### Freezing

Applicable guidelines are frozen the same way the purpose brief is:

- `StartGeneration` and `StartRevision` resolve the post's current `purpose_id` server-side (already done for the
  purpose brief), ask the guideline context for the applicable ordered texts (`global` + linked-to-that-purpose),
  and write them into the job payload (`generation_jobs.payload`, next to `target_length` and `purpose`). One read,
  one consistent view: the same purpose id used for the brief drives the guideline resolution.
- `StartWriteExperiment` puts the same ordered texts into the shared prepared snapshot, so both candidates receive
  byte-identical system prompts except for model ref. The snapshot input hash therefore changes with the applicable
  guidelines — a different rule set is a different experiment.
- Resumed/retried jobs use the payload; they never reload guideline rows. Old payloads without the member decode as
  "no guidelines".
- A guideline edited, rescoped, or deleted after enqueue changes nothing in flight; a purpose deleted between the
  post save and the enqueue simply yields the global set (consistent with the brief's absence).

### Proto / Connect contract

New `proto/postpilot/v1/guideline.proto`:

```proto
service GuidelineService {
  rpc ListGuidelines(ListGuidelinesRequest) returns (ListGuidelinesResponse);
  rpc CreateGuideline(CreateGuidelineRequest) returns (CreateGuidelineResponse);
  rpc UpdateGuideline(UpdateGuidelineRequest) returns (UpdateGuidelineResponse);
  rpc DeleteGuideline(DeleteGuidelineRequest) returns (DeleteGuidelineResponse);
}

enum GuidelineScope {
  GUIDELINE_SCOPE_UNSPECIFIED = 0;
  GUIDELINE_SCOPE_GLOBAL      = 1;
  GUIDELINE_SCOPE_PURPOSES    = 2;
}

message GuidelinePurposeRef { string id = 1; string name = 2; }

message Guideline {
  string id = 1;
  string text = 2;
  GuidelineScope scope = 3;
  repeated GuidelinePurposeRef purposes = 4;  // empty for GLOBAL; may be empty for PURPOSES after a purpose deletion
  string created_at = 5;
  string updated_at = 6;
}

message CreateGuidelineRequest { string text = 1; GuidelineScope scope = 2; repeated string purpose_ids = 3; }
message GuidelineScopePatch   { GuidelineScope scope = 1; repeated string purpose_ids = 2; }
message UpdateGuidelineRequest {
  string id = 1;
  optional string text = 2;
  GuidelineScopePatch scope = 3;   // message presence = replace the whole scope
}
message DeleteGuidelineRequest  { string id = 1; }
message DeleteGuidelineResponse {}
```

`ListGuidelines` returns the guidelines in injection order (global group, then scoped group, each by creation time)
so the management screen shows exactly what the writer will see. Purpose names in `purposes` are projected through
the guideline context's consumer-owned `PurposeDirectory` port, never a SQL join. Generated code is regenerated with
`pnpm gen:proto`; generated files are never hand-edited.

### SQLite model and migration

```sql
guidelines (
  id         TEXT PRIMARY KEY,
  user_id    TEXT NOT NULL REFERENCES users(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  text       TEXT NOT NULL,
  scope      TEXT NOT NULL CHECK (scope IN ('global','purposes')),
  created_at TEXT NOT NULL,
  updated_at TEXT NOT NULL,
  UNIQUE (id, user_id),
  UNIQUE (user_id, text)
)

guideline_purposes (
  guideline_id TEXT NOT NULL,
  purpose_id   TEXT NOT NULL,
  user_id      TEXT NOT NULL,
  PRIMARY KEY (guideline_id, purpose_id),
  FOREIGN KEY (guideline_id, user_id) REFERENCES guidelines(id, user_id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  FOREIGN KEY (purpose_id,   user_id) REFERENCES purposes(id, user_id)   ON DELETE CASCADE
)
```

- The composite FKs keep a guideline from linking another account's purpose even if a service check is bypassed
  (same pattern as `posts.purpose_id`).
- Deleting a **purpose** cascades its link rows (whole-row `ON DELETE CASCADE` is safe on a composite key — the
  `SET NULL` trap that forced plan 11's trigger does not apply to a delete). The guideline row survives; one left
  with zero links applies nowhere and `/guidelines` shows it as such. Deleting a **guideline** cascades only its own
  links. `DeletePurpose`'s response and post-detach behavior are unchanged.
- The **next available** embedded goose migration (0012 unless an in-flight job claims it first — jobs 32–34 are
  pending; the implementing job reserves the number) creates both tables. No backfill, no seed rows; existing
  accounts have zero guidelines. Timestamps follow the fixed-width UTC RFC3339 rule. SQL regenerated with
  `pnpm gen:sql`, boot-fatal on failure ([I7]).

### Job and state transitions

No new job kind, stage, status, or queue behavior, and **no new column on posts**: nothing references a guideline.
The only new state is the two tables above plus the frozen `guidelines []string` payload/snapshot member.

```
guideline: created ──(text/scope edits)──▶ updated ──delete──▶ gone (name-free, nothing detaches)
purpose P deleted:  P's link rows vanish; a 'purposes' guideline with no links left applies nowhere until rescoped
job payload:        frozen ordered texts at enqueue; never re-read
```

### Frontend behavior and placement

- **`/guidelines`** (top-level destination 지침, after 용도 in the nav; lazy route split following the current
  router conventions from job 31): the account's guidelines in injection order — text, a scope badge (`전역`, or the
  purpose-name chips; `적용 대상 없음` for an orphaned scoped guideline), delete with a plain confirmation stating
  that already-enqueued work keeps its frozen text and nothing else is affected. Create form: textarea with a live
  remaining-character count from `shared/config`, scope control (radio 전역 / 특정 용도 with a purpose multi-select
  fed by the purpose directory). Text edits in place via the shared `Editable` primitive, read-first; the scope is
  edited through the same control the create form uses, saved as one whole-scope patch. Empty state shows one worked
  example as copy (e.g. "무인 매장 글에서 직원·주인과의 상호작용이나 CCTV를 언급하지 않기") and the create form; no
  row is created for the user.
- **Revision capture**: the `edit-with-ai` result surface renders `지침으로 저장` beside the existing
  `규칙으로 저장`. It opens a small dialog seeded with the revision instruction, editable before saving (a raw
  instruction like "무인 매장이니까 주인 얘기 빼줘" is usually generalized by the user before it becomes a rule),
  with a scope choice: 전역 (default), or 이 글의 용도 「{name}」에만 when the post currently has a purpose (read
  from the already-loaded post; no new query). Saving calls the standard create mutation; `AlreadyExists` renders as
  "이미 같은 지침이 있어요" (informational, not a failure state); other refusals show the server message verbatim.
- **Editor**: otherwise untouched — no guideline selector or preview (non-goal).
- **Caches**: query key `(account_id, 'guidelines')`, re-read on mount (`staleTime: 0`, `refetchOnMount: 'always'`)
  like the purpose directory, because purpose names are a projection. Create/update/delete invalidate the list.
  `features/edit-purpose`'s rename invalidation additionally marks the guideline list stale (it projects the name);
  purpose deletion does the same (scope chips change). Account switches partition as today.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. An authenticated user can create, list, update, and delete guidelines. `text` is trimmed, non-empty, bounded, and
   unique within the account (an exact duplicate after trim is `AlreadyExists` with a Korean message). No RPC accepts
   a user id; a foreign guideline id is indistinguishable from an unknown one (`NotFound`). Creating beyond
   `GuidelineMaxPerAccount` is refused with a Korean message naming the cap.
2. Scope is validated: `global` with purpose ids, or `purposes` with zero ids, is `InvalidArgument`; an unknown or
   foreign purpose id is `NotFound` and nothing is applied; duplicate ids in one request collapse to one link. The
   list projects each scoped guideline's purposes as `{id, name}` through the purpose directory, never a SQL join,
   and returns guidelines in injection order.
3. `UpdateGuideline` changes only what the request carries: a text-only update leaves the scope intact (store test
   against a concurrent scope edit), and a scope patch replaces the whole scope atomically in one transaction.
4. Generating a post whose account has applicable guidelines appends exactly one `[작문 지침]` section — after the
   `[글의 용도]` section when the post has a purpose, otherwise immediately after `[종결어미 제약]`, and always
   before `[이번 글]` — containing the texts verbatim as list lines, global group first then scoped group, each
   ordered by creation time, followed by the fixed precedence sentence (prompt snapshot).
5. Scope resolution is exact: a post with purpose P receives the global guidelines plus P-linked ones; a post with
   no purpose receives the global ones only; guidelines linked only to other purposes never appear; a
   `purposes`-scoped guideline with zero remaining links appears in no prompt.
6. With no applicable guidelines the prompt contains no `[작문 지침]` section and is byte-identical to the
   post-grounding baseline (golden files). The voice profile prefix and the purpose section are byte-identical with
   and without guidelines (prefix-comparison tests), so plan 11's acceptance criteria 4–5 keep holding.
7. Every write and revise prompt — with or without user guidelines, with or without a purpose — contains the fixed
   built-in grounding constraint forbidding facts absent from the memo and photo observations. It lives in the fixed
   prompt text (code, not config, not a database row); the observe prompt is unchanged; golden files are regenerated
   exactly once for this addition.
8. Freezing: the applicable ordered texts are resolved once at enqueue and written into the job payload beside
   `target_length` and the purpose brief; editing, rescoping, or deleting guidelines after `StartGeneration` /
   `StartRevision` returns does not change the prompt the worker builds, including after a restart-resume or an
   explicit retry (test edits rows between enqueue and drain). Payloads without the member decode as no guidelines.
9. An A/B write experiment freezes the same texts into the shared snapshot: both candidates receive byte-identical
   system prompts including them, and changing the applicable guidelines changes the input hash.
10. Revision injects the same section at the same relative position in the revise prompt; a revision of a post
    without applicable guidelines is unchanged from the baseline (snapshots).
11. After a completed revision, `지침으로 저장` opens a dialog seeded with the revision instruction, editable before
    save, offering 전역 (default) and the post's current purpose as scope when one is assigned. Saving creates the

## Policy / Config Impact

**Rules that become true when the job lands** (to be written into policy then, not before):

- New `spec/policy/guidelines.md`: account ownership and no request user id; field rules, dedup, and limits; scope

  position, ordering, and precedence; freezing; the revision capture as an explicit user save; no learning, no

- `spec/policy/generation.md`: the write prompt appends the frozen `[작문 지침]` section after the purpose section
  (or after the voice profile when no purpose) and before per-post material with the fixed precedence sentence;
  absence yields a baseline-identical prompt; ordinary jobs, both A/B candidates, and retries use the frozen texts;
  the A/B snapshot includes them in the input hash; the fixed prompt text carries the universal grounding
  constraint.
- `spec/policy/revision.md`: revision re-injects the frozen guideline texts at the same position; `지침으로 저장`
  saves the user-edited instruction as a guideline through the standard RPC; nothing about a guideline is learned.
- `spec/policy/purposes.md`: one added rule — deleting a purpose also cascades guideline scope links; guidelines
  themselves survive and may become 적용 대상 없음.
- `spec/policy/voice.md`: one clarifying sentence — writing guidelines are not voice data, never enter
  `ProfileForPrompt`, and leave the profile's bytes unchanged (as the purpose sentence already states).
- `spec/tech/`: no new document. The injection position, ordering, and precedence are recorded in this plan and,
  once shipped, in policy/generation and policy/guidelines; a separate tech doc would only restate them.
