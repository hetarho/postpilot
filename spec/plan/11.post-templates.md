# 11. Post Templates

> Save reusable *templates* — the shape a kind of post takes, as a small authored grammar — and let a post pick one
> so the writer produces that skeleton in the chosen voice |
> Scope: FS | Status: done (job 22 as 용도 · [change 25](../changes/archive/25.replace-purposes-with-drag-and-drop-post.md) → job 53 as 템플릿)

## Purpose

Everything the product learns and injects is **style**: endings, vocabulary, sentence rhythm, banned phrases, user
rules. Nothing tells the writer what shape the post takes. The failure the owner sees in practice is a post that
should be a plain informational review — a restaurant visit written in exchange for a meal, where each photo needs a
factual description — coming out as a first-person diary, because the memo is a diary-shaped list and the voice
profile only governs how sentences sound.

This plan shipped twice. Job 22 shipped it as a **용도**: a name, a description, and one prose `instructions` field
injected as a brief. That was prose *about* shape, and three things followed from it. There was no way to require a
skeleton — the frame had to be described in Korean and the model re-derived it every run, so the same 용도 produced a
different outline each time. There was no way to require a literal, so a disclosure line came back paraphrased. And
there was no way to bind output to the attachments: "one paragraph per photo" was something the model counted for
itself, so a fourteen-photo post silently dropped photos.

Change 25 replaced it with a **템플릿**: the shape itself, written in a small grammar
([tech/post-template-grammar](../tech/post-template-grammar.md)). Literal text is literal, `<write>` marks where
prose goes, `<slot>` reserves a position the app cannot invent, `<repeat each="photo">` is expanded **by the server**
against the post's attachments, and `<note>` says something to the model that never reaches the page. Three authored
axes now stand beside each other, each owning one question: 말투 decides how it sounds, 템플릿 decides what shape it
has, 지침 decides what to avoid.

This serves PRD goal 1 (a publish-ready draft in ~3 minutes: no per-post re-explaining) and goal 2 (in *my* voice:
the voice profile is untouched) and addresses S1 — the "it reads like a diary" and "it lost a photo" corrections both
disappear.

## Scope / Non-goals

### In scope

- A `Template` aggregate per account: `name`, `description`, `body`, with create/update/delete lifecycles and an
  authenticated list. `body` is the single source of truth for the shape.
- The grammar, its server-authoritative parser, its client mirror, its parse-error contract, and its bounds.
- Server-side `repeat` expansion against the post's attached photos, bounded and refused when out of bounds.
- Slot resolution: `photo` to an `IMAGE` block, `place`/`link` to an **unfilled slot** that holds its position
  through generation, revision, finalization and export.
- An optional `template_id` on every post. A post may have none; the server never picks one.
- A 템플릿 picker on the editor's 글 생성 dock row (default 없음) and a management screen `/templates` with a
  drag-and-drop structure editor and a source view over the same string.
- Prompt injection: the frozen, expanded template enters the write and revise prompts at one fixed position — after
  the complete voice profile and before the per-post material — with a fixed legend and precedence sentence, and
  **without the word 지침** in any of its framing.
- Freezing: ordinary generation, both A/B write candidates, and every revision freeze the **rendered** template at
  enqueue, exactly as target length is frozen.
- Deleting a template detaches it from posts (`NULL`) after a confirmation naming the count, and cascades its
  guideline scope links; frozen payloads keep their text.
- A migration retiring `purposes` for `templates`, rescoping guidelines, and renaming the experiment's frozen name.

### Non-goals

- **Learning a template.** Finalization learning, rules, evidence, feedback, comparisons and validations remain voice
  learning ([I4]); nothing about a template is learned, inferred, suggested, ranked or written by a model. A template
  is authored text only.
- **Guessing.** No selection from title, memo, tags, photos, or content. No default template; 없음 is the default.
- **Seeded or shipped templates.** No built-in library; the empty state shows one worked example as copy, not a row.
- **Filling a slot in the app.** No map/place lookup, no URL validation, no provider or platform call to resolve one.
  A slot is a reserved position; a person fills it after pasting.
- **Strict compliance validation.** Literal fidelity, section order and heading levels are not verified, and no
  template deviation fails a run, retries it, or discards a usable generation.
- **A richer grammar.** No iterator beyond `each="photo"`, no conditionals, variables, expressions, nested repeats,
  inheritance or includes.
- **Per-template model selection, target length, tags, reasoning effort or quota**; no template version history,
  soft-delete/restore, import/export, or sharing.
- **Per-voice templates or a template↔voice link.** Templates are account-scoped and orthogonal to voices; a post
  combines exactly one voice and at most one template.
- **Changing the memo's role.** The memo stays per-post experience notes; the template is the reusable shape.
- Anything in PRD §8; canonical blocks, browser image work, the copy-only export, and plan 12's explicit publishing
  boundary are unchanged ([I1], [I2], [I6]). Selecting or editing a template never starts a publication.
- **The model-catalog "purposes" are a different concept.** The five stage registration targets of plans 04/18 are
  untouched by this plan and share only the English word.

## Design

### Domain rules and terminology

The user-facing noun is **템플릿** (`Template` in contracts and code). A template is an account-owned aggregate with
three authored fields. `Post` references at most one template by id; the reference is a plain nullable pointer, not
ownership — deleting the template detaches posts, it never deletes them.

Invariants:

1. every template belongs to exactly one account; every RPC derives the account from the session and none accepts a
   user id (as posts and voices);
2. a post has zero or one template, and it must belong to the post's account;
3. a prompt uses the template **rendered and frozen at enqueue**, never the live row and never a re-expansion;
4. the voice profile's content, order and injection are byte-identical whether or not a template is present — the
   template is appended after it;
5. no template read, write, selection, parse, or deletion enqueues work or makes a provider call ([I5]);
6. a body that does not parse cannot be stored.

**Field rules** and the bounds are the as-built contract in
[policy/templates](../policy/templates.md) *Field rules*; the grammar and its parse errors are
[tech/post-template-grammar](../tech/post-template-grammar.md) §2–§4.

### Prompt injection

`BuildWritePrompt` and `BuildRevisePrompt` take an optional `*TemplateBrief{Name, Body, Slots}` — the body already
expanded for the post's photos and rendered with its copy tokens. When present, the **system** prompt (the stable
part) is:

```
WritePrompt / RevisePrompt fixed text
[스타일가이드] … [활성 대조 규칙] … [글 예시 발췌] … [사용자 규칙] … [종결어미 제약] …   ← unchanged voice prefix (plan 03/06 order)
[글 템플릿: {name}]
아래 템플릿의 구성을 그대로 따르세요. {fixed grammar legend}
---
{rendered body}
---
템플릿은 글의 구성·순서·포함할 내용을 정하고, 문체·종결어미·어휘는 위의 말투 프로필을 따릅니다.
[작문 지침] …                                                                        ← plan 16's section, after this one
```

The framing is deliberately free of the word 지침. The retired 용도 section rendered its own field as `작성 지침:`
and then said *"지침이 문체와 충돌하면…"* one section above `[작문 지침]`'s *"지침이 용도의 요구와 충돌하면 지침을
우선하고…"*, so the word named two different things in one prompt and the guideline-beats-brief rule could be read as
pointing at the brief itself. Retiring the overlap was half the point of change 25.

Change 25 regenerated the prompt goldens **exactly once**: the fixed output-language line named 용도 / "purpose", a
concept that no longer exists. Every byte-identity rule is stated relative to that new baseline, as plan 16's
grounding constraint did before it.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. An authenticated user can create, list, update, and delete templates. `name` is trimmed, non-empty, bounded, and
   unique within the account (a duplicate is `AlreadyExists`); `body` is trimmed, non-empty, bounded, **and must
   parse**; `description` may be empty. Creating past the per-account cap is refused. No RPC accepts a user id; a
   foreign template id is indistinguishable from an unknown one.
2. `UpdateTemplate` changes only the fields present in the request; an update carrying only `body` leaves a
   concurrently saved `name` intact (store test).
3. A post can be saved with a template, with it cleared, or with the field omitted; omitted preserves the current
   assignment under autosave. An unknown or foreign `template_id` is rejected and nothing else in the request is
   applied. Assignment is allowed in every status and changes no content, revision, baseline, or finalization field.
4. Generating **without** a template produces a write prompt byte-identical to the no-template golden. Generating
   **with** one appends exactly one `[글 템플릿: …]` section after `[종결어미 제약]` and before `[작문 지침]` /
   `[이번 글]`, carrying the legend, the rendered body between fences, and the precedence sentence.
5. The voice profile portion of the prompt is byte-identical with and without a template for the same voice and
   post, and the word 지침 appears in the prompt only inside the guideline section and its precedence sentence.
6. Revision of a post with a template injects the same section at the same relative position; revision of a post
   without one is unchanged (goldens).
7. The template is rendered and frozen at enqueue: editing or deleting it after `StartGeneration` /
   `StartRevision` returns does not change the prompt the worker builds, including after a restart-resume or an
   explicit retry, and **attaching more photos after the start does not change the expansion**.
8. A `<repeat each="photo">` produces exactly one iteration per attached photo, in attachment order, each bound to
   its own filename; zero photos drops the whole block including its literals; an expansion past
   `TEMPLATE_MAX_REPEAT_EXPANSION` is refused at start and enqueues nothing.
9. A `place` or `link` slot survives generation, an AI revision that does not mention it, finalization and export:
   it holds its position, is counted in the editor and the export panel, blocks neither finalization nor export, and
   appears in the four derived outputs as a bracketed placeholder in marker order without disturbing the
   `[사진 …]` markers or the photo copy controls.
10. A model result that omits a declared slot has it re-inserted **at its template position** — before a later slot
    that did resolve, not appended after it — and one that buries several tokens in a paragraph resolves every slot
    it named and leaves no raw token in the prose. A result that drifts on literal text or section order is still
    accepted, and no run fails or retries because of template deviation. The pass is idempotent.
11. A body that fails to parse cannot be saved; the failure names the offending line and one reason, and no partial
    template is stored. The **Go and TypeScript parsers reach the same verdict, line and reason** on every shared
    fixture and on the generated corpus, and every accepted body round-trips byte for byte.
12. Builder → stored body → builder round-trips byte-identically for anything the builder produced, including a slot
    label containing a double quote. A pasted body that parses opens in the structure view; one that does not opens
    in the source view with its error and cannot be saved.
13. An A/B write experiment on a post with a template gives both candidates byte-identical system prompts; the
    comparison detail shows the template name and keeps reading after a rename or delete; changing the template
    changes the input hash.
14. Deleting a template referenced by N posts returns `detached_posts = N`, clears those posts' `template_id` in the
    same transaction, cascades its `guideline_templates` links, and deletes no post, content, photo, guideline or
    history. A guideline left with no links lists as 적용 대상 없음 and reaches no prompt until it is rescoped.
15. After migration `0022`, `/purposes` is gone, no `purposes` or `guideline_purposes` table remains,
    `model_experiments.template_name` carries the frozen names, an account with no templates sees the empty state,
    and every existing post reads and generates with no template. The rollback restores the retired shape (not its
    data) and loses no post.
16. `catalog_model_purposes` and `purpose_reasoning_effort` are unchanged: every 모델 관리 purpose tab, its
    per-purpose reasoning effort, and every stage dropdown behave exactly as before.
17. Template list/detail, selection, page mount, parsing, the builder, polling, copy, and export make no provider
    call and enqueue no job.
18. Domain/store/RPC/prompt/payload tests cover validation limits, uniqueness, presence semantics, foreign-id
    rejection, the cap, expansion and its bound, freeze-after-edit, detach counting, slot resolution and insertion,
    and snapshot equality; proto/sql generation checks, backend vet/build/tests, frontend tests/lint/FSD/style/build,
    and spec lint pass without weakening existing assertions.
