# 26. Change: Collect Guideline Candidates From Revisions

> A **change proposal** (a WHAT delta) that modifies the already-shipped behavior of [plan/16.writing-guidelines](../plan/16.writing-guidelines.md).
> Filled by /create-change through a user interview — a reviewable doc, not a one-line prompt.
> The STEPS live in the job that /create-change-job creates. Write it in English.

## As-is

Plan 16 (job 35) gives an account reusable **guidelines** (지침): one `text` of at most 300 characters, a scope of
`global` or specific purposes, a per-account cap of 100, and injection into the write and revise prompts as one
`[작문 지침]` section. Every guideline is authored by hand; nothing is learned, inferred, or auto-generated, and no
model writes, suggests, ranks or retires one.

There is exactly one shortcut from a correction to a rule: after a **completed** revision, `지침으로 저장` opens a
dialog seeded with the revision instruction, editable before saving, and calls the standard create RPC. It is a good
button in a bad position. Three things about it mean corrections mostly do not become guidelines:

1. **It is available only in the moment.** There is no revision history ([policy/revision](../policy/revision.md):
   *"There is no revision history or partial-range update"*), so the instruction exists only in the client that ran
   it. Close the tab, keep drafting, or come back tomorrow and the correction is gone with nothing recorded.
2. **It asks for a decision at the worst time.** The user just got a revision back and wants to read it. Choosing a
   scope — 전역 or this post's purpose — is a durable decision about every future post, asked in the middle of
   reviewing one.
3. **It fires once per correction, in isolation.** A user who has typed "여기 너무 광고 같아" into five posts has no
   way to notice that they typed it five times, which is exactly the signal that it should be a standing rule.

The result is that the same instruction gets retyped per post — the failure plan 16 was written to remove — while the
guideline list stays near-empty because filling it requires the user to have predicted the rule in advance.

## To-be

A completed revision **records its instruction as a guideline candidate**, and the guideline screen becomes the place
where candidates are reviewed in a batch, on the user's schedule, with the scope decided at approval time.

### Recording

- When a revision job **completes**, its instruction is written verbatim as a `pending` candidate, together with the
  post it came from and the timestamp. A failed, cancelled, or still-running revision records nothing.
- The text is stored **verbatim, exactly as the user typed it**. Nothing rewrites, summarizes, normalizes,
  generalizes, translates, clusters or ranks it, and no provider is called. This is the load-bearing constraint of
  the whole change: a candidate is a receipt for something the user wrote, not a model's opinion about it.
- Recording is a side effect of a run the user already started, inside the revision job's own completion path. It
  creates no new job, no new provider call, and no new queue ([I5]).
- **A failure to record never fails the revision.** The revision's result, status and content are authoritative and
  unaffected.

### Deduplication and the queue bound

- A candidate is not recorded if its trimmed text already exists for the account as a guideline, or as a candidate in
  any state — `pending`, `approved` or `dismissed`. Repeating an instruction the user already dismissed does not
  bring it back, and repeating one already saved does not offer to save it twice.
- Instead of duplicating, a repeat **increments the candidate's occurrence count and updates its last-seen
  timestamp**. That count is the signal item 3 of As-is is missing: the list shows "5번 요청함", which is what tells
  the user this is a standing rule rather than a one-off.
- Recording stops at `GUIDELINE_CANDIDATE_MAX_PENDING` pending candidates. It stops rather than evicting: evicting
  the oldest would silently discard something the user might have approved. The guideline screen states that the
  queue is full, and clearing it resumes recording.

### Review

- `/guidelines` gains a **후보** section above the saved list, ordered by occurrence count descending then last-seen
  descending, so the most-repeated correction is first.
- Each candidate offers **승인**, **무시**, and inline text editing before approval, and names the post it came from
  as a link.
- **Scope is chosen at approval, not at recording.** A candidate carries no scope at all; approving one opens the
  same scope control the create form uses (전역 default, or 특정 템플릿) and calls the standard create RPC. This is
  the reason recording can be automatic: nothing is ever applied to a post without the user picking, at approval
  time, what it applies to.
- Approving marks the candidate `approved`; 무시 marks it `dismissed`. Neither deletes the row, because both are what
  the deduplication above reads.
- **A candidate never reaches a prompt.** Only a saved guideline is injected. A pending candidate is inert.

### The instruction bound

A revision instruction may be up to 500 characters; a guideline's text may be at most 300. A candidate is therefore
stored at the revision bound and **approval enforces the guideline bound**: a candidate over 300 characters opens for
editing with the live counter and the standard refusal, rather than being silently truncated or refused at recording
time. Recording a long instruction and letting the user shorten it keeps the receipt; refusing it at recording time
would lose exactly the corrections that were most specific.

### The existing dialog stays

`지침으로 저장` after a completed revision is unchanged and remains the immediate path: a user who already knows the
rule saves it on the spot. Doing so records the candidate as `approved` in the same transaction, so it never appears
in the 후보 section afterwards. `규칙으로 저장` is untouched and stays a pre-flight checkbox, because the voice
learns from the run itself.

### Lifecycle

- Deleting the source post leaves the candidate's text intact and drops the post link, for the same reason plan 16
  gives for guidelines: nothing references a candidate's origin, so nothing breaks when it goes away. The list shows
  the candidate without a link.
- Deleting a saved guideline does **not** revive or reset its approved candidate. The approved row keeps suppressing
  re-recording; re-creating the rule is an explicit create.
- Migration `0023` creates the candidate table. It seeds nothing and backfills nothing — no past revision is
  reconstructable, since no revision history exists — so an existing account starts with an empty 후보 section and
  every prompt built before this change stays byte-identical to one built after it.

## Scope / Non-goals (regression boundary)

**In scope**

- A `GuidelineCandidate` record per account (`text`, source post, occurrence count, first/last seen, status), written
  from the revision job's completion path.
- Verbatim recording, deduplication across guidelines and all candidate states, occurrence counting, and the pending
  bound with its full-queue message.
- The 후보 section on `/guidelines`: ordering, occurrence count, source-post link, inline edit, 승인 with the standard
  scope control and create RPC, 무시.
- Approval enforcing the guideline text bound and the account guideline cap through the existing create path.
- Recording an on-the-spot `지침으로 저장` as an approved candidate in the same transaction.
- Post-deletion and guideline-deletion behavior for candidates.
- Migration `0023`, embedded and boot-run ([I7]).

**Non-goals**

- **Nothing is learned, inferred or generated.** No model reads, rewrites, summarizes, generalizes, translates,
  clusters, scores, ranks, prioritizes or retires a candidate; there is no similarity matching, no fuzzy or semantic
  dedupe (deduplication is exact after trim, the same rule the `guidelines(user_id, text)` unique index already uses),
  and no evidence pipeline. [I4]'s learning boundary stays entirely with voice, and plan 16's *"Nothing is learned,
  inferred or auto-generated"* stands unamended in substance: a candidate is user-authored text, recorded.
- **No candidate is ever applied.** A pending candidate reaches no prompt, no post and no job; only approval, with
  an explicit scope, creates a guideline. There is no auto-approval, no threshold at which a candidate becomes a
  rule by itself, and no default scope applied without the user choosing it.
- **No provider call and no new job** ([I5]). Recording is a side effect of an already-running revision job; the
  candidate list, edit, approve and dismiss are plain CRUD.
- No change to the guideline entity: `text`, the 300-character bound, the scope kinds, the 100-per-account cap, the
  injection position and order, the `[작문 지침]` section, the precedence sentence, and freezing at enqueue are all
  unchanged.
- No change to the revision contract: the instruction bound, the language rules, the revise prompt, the
  smallest-change and verbatim-preservation rules, retry behavior, `규칙으로 저장`, and the absence of a revision
  history are untouched. This change adds no revision history — a candidate is one recorded instruction, not a log
  of what a revision did.
- No candidates from any source other than a completed AI revision: not from manual block edits, not from finalized
  drafts, not from voice rules, not from failed generations, not from the memo.
- No editing of a candidate's occurrence count, no manual candidate creation, no bulk approve, no import/export, no
  candidate history or undo of a dismissal beyond re-creating the guideline by hand.
- No change to plan 11/[change 25](archive/25.replace-purposes-with-drag-and-drop-post.md)'s template work. This change is
  independent of it: because scope is chosen at approval, the approval dialog simply uses whichever scope control the
  guideline entity has at the time.
- No change to publishing ([I1]), the canonical block array ([I2]), the observe/write split ([I3]), browser-side
  image handling ([I6]), quota or plan gating.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Running a revision to completion records one pending candidate holding the instruction **verbatim**, with the
   source post and the timestamp. A failed or cancelled revision records none.
2. Running a revision with the identical instruction again creates no second row: the existing candidate's occurrence
   count becomes 2 and its last-seen advances. The 후보 section shows the count.
3. An instruction that already exists as a saved guideline, or as an `approved` or `dismissed` candidate, records
   nothing and revives nothing.
4. No candidate — pending, approved or dismissed — appears in any write or revise prompt, and a pending candidate
   changes no generated output. An account with candidates but no new guidelines produces prompts byte-identical to
   the same account before this change.
5. Approving a candidate opens the scope control with 전역 preselected, and saving creates a guideline through the
   standard RPC with exactly the chosen scope. The candidate becomes `approved` and leaves the 후보 section.
6. 무시 marks the candidate `dismissed` and removes it from the section; the same instruction from a later revision
   does not reappear.
7. A candidate longer than the guideline text bound opens for editing with the live remaining count, and approving it
   unedited is refused by the server's existing bound contract — never silently truncated. Recording it was not
   refused.
8. Approving past the account guideline cap is refused with the existing `FailedPrecondition` naming the cap; the
   candidate stays pending.
9. With `GUIDELINE_CANDIDATE_MAX_PENDING` pending candidates, a completed revision still completes normally, records
   nothing, and the section states that the queue is full. Approving or dismissing one resumes recording.
10. `지침으로 저장` after a completed revision behaves exactly as before, and the saved instruction never afterwards
    appears as a pending candidate.
11. Deleting the source post leaves the candidate listed with its text and no link; deleting a saved guideline
    neither revives nor resets its approved candidate.
12. No revision, candidate list, approval or dismissal makes a provider call or enqueues a job, and no candidate text
    is read or written by a model anywhere in the flow.
13. After migration `0023`, an existing account's 후보 section is empty, no past revision is reconstructed, and every
    existing guideline, scope and prompt is unchanged.
