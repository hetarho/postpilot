# 25. Change: Replace Purposes With Drag And Drop Post Templates

> A **change proposal** (a WHAT delta) that modifies the already-shipped behavior of [plan/11.post-templates](../../plan/11.post-templates.md).
> Filled by /create-change through a user interview — a reviewable doc, not a one-line prompt.
> The STEPS live in the job that /create-change-job creates. Write it in English.

## As-is

Plan 11 (job 22) gives an account reusable **purposes** (용도): `name`, `description`, and a single prose
`instructions` field of up to 2000 characters. A post optionally selects one (`posts.purpose_id`), the brief is frozen
at enqueue into the job payload and the experiment snapshot, and it enters the write and revise prompts as one
`[글의 용도]` section between the complete voice profile and the per-post material. `/purposes` manages them, deletion
detaches posts through a trigger and cascades `guideline_purposes` scope links, and `model_experiments.purpose_name`
records the brief by name.

A purpose is therefore **prose about shape, never shape itself**. Plan 11 states this deliberately: *"Templates of
blocks / fixed outlines"* is one of its non-goals. Three consequences follow from that boundary, and all three are
what this change removes.

1. **There is no way to require a skeleton.** The author of a sponsored restaurant review needs the same frame every
   time — an intro, a rating line, a map, a photo-by-photo section, a closing verdict — and today the only way to ask
   is to describe the frame in Korean prose and hope. The model re-derives the structure from that description on
   every run, so the same purpose produces a different outline each time and the per-post correction the purpose was
   supposed to eliminate comes back as a structural one.
2. **There is no way to require a literal.** A disclosure line or a fixed sign-off has to be *described* in
   `instructions`; plan 11's non-goals name verbatim required text as a follow-up. The model paraphrases it.
3. **There is no way to bind output to the attachments.** "One paragraph per photo" is prose the model counts by
   itself, so a fourteen-photo post silently drops photos or collapses several into one paragraph.

The naming is also genuinely overloaded, and not only in the UI. In a post that has both a purpose and applicable
guidelines the write prompt contains the purpose's own field rendered as `작성 지침:`, then the sentence *"지침이
문체와 충돌하면 말투 프로필을 우선하세요"* (about the purpose's instructions), then a `[작문 지침]` section, then
*"지침이 용도의 요구와 충돌하면 지침을 우선하고…"* (about the guideline entity). The word 지침 names two different
things in one prompt, and the second precedence sentence can be read as pointing at the first section — which makes
the guideline-beats-purpose rule self-referential exactly when both are present. Separately, the bracket form
`[사진 …]` is already taken: it is the Naver export's photo marker in copied body text ([policy/export](../../policy/export.md)).

Generated content is a flat `PostContent` block array of `TEXT`, `HEADING`, `IMAGE`, `QUOTE`, `LIST`
([I2]); one validation function runs immediately after parsing, drops malformed blocks, and checks every
`IMAGE.file` against the post's attached filenames. Nothing in that contract can express *"a place link belongs here
and nobody has supplied it yet."*

## To-be

The product presents three orthogonal authored axes, each owning exactly one question:

| Axis | Owns | Entity |
|---|---|---|
| **말투** (voice) | how sentences sound — register, endings, vocabulary | unchanged (plan 10) |
| **템플릿** (template) | what shape the post has — sections, literals, slots, repetition | **replaces 용도** |
| **지침** (guideline) | what the post must avoid or watch out for | unchanged (plan 16), rescoped to templates |

### The template entity replaces the purpose entity

A **template** is a reusable, account-owned document: `name`, `description`, and `body`. `body` is the template
markdown and is the **single source of truth** — it is what the database stores, what the prompt receives, and what
the editor round-trips. The drag-and-drop builder is a parser plus a renderer over that text, not a second
representation of it.

`posts.purpose_id` becomes `posts.template_id` with the same semantics plan 11 established and that this change does
not revisit: optional, never guessed, presence-aware on save, validated before anything else in the request is
applied, assignable in every status including `finalized` and while a job runs, and costing the post nothing because
nothing is ever learned from it. A guideline's `purposes` scope becomes a `templates` scope, and
`model_experiments.purpose_name` becomes `template_name`, stored rather than derived, for the same reason it was.

### The template grammar

The grammar is **XML-tag delimited**. That shape is chosen against the alternatives for four concrete reasons: an
LLM reads tag-delimited structure most reliably; closing tags make nesting unambiguous to a parser, which brace and
comment forms are not; attributes carry the machine-readable parts (`kind`, `each`) without being confusable with
prose; and it collides with nothing already in flight — not Korean prose, not the protojson block output the model
must also produce, not markdown, and not the export's `[사진 …]` photo markers.

| Construct | Meaning | Reaches the prompt as | Appears in the post as |
|---|---|---|---|
| bare text | literal | verbatim | verbatim, unaltered |
| `<write>지시</write>` | write prose here, per the instruction | the instruction | generated prose |
| `<slot kind="photo"/>` | a content slot | a position marker | a resolved block, or an unfilled slot |
| `<repeat each="photo">…</repeat>` | expand once per attachment | N expanded copies | N repetitions |
| `<note>…</note>` | an instruction for the model only | verbatim | nothing |

The user's interview example rewritten in it:

```
<write>인트로를 작성합니다.</write>

=========================
별 <write>별점을 별 기호로</write>

<slot kind="place" label="네이버 지도"/>

=========================
<repeat each="photo">
<slot kind="photo"/>
<write>이 사진에 대한 설명</write>
</repeat>

<write>총평 및 재방문 의사</write>
```

Grammar rules:

- **Unknown tags, unclosed tags, a `repeat` without a known `each`, a `slot` without a known `kind`, and nesting a
  `repeat` inside a `repeat` are parse errors.** A template with a parse error cannot be saved, and the error names
  the line. There is no lenient fallback: a template that half-parses would silently drop the structure the user
  asked for.
- A literal `<` in body text is literal unless it opens one of the five known tags; the raw-mode editor offers
  `&lt;` for the escape and the parser accepts it.
- The builder palette offers **정해진 문구** as a first-class draggable, which serializes to bare literal text. It is
  a UI affordance over the literal construct, not a sixth construct and not a new entity — an account-level reusable
  snippet library is a separate future plan.

### `repeat` is expanded by the server, not counted by the model

At enqueue, `<repeat each="photo">` is expanded against the post's attached photo count **before** the template
reaches the prompt, so the model receives N literal iterations with the photo index already bound and never counts
anything. `each="photo"` is the only iterator in this change — attached photos are the only countable material a post
has. Zero photos drops the whole repeat block, including its literals. An expansion that would exceed
`TEMPLATE_MAX_REPEAT_EXPANSION` is refused at start with an actionable failure rather than sending an unbounded
prompt.

### Slots resolve as far as the app can, and stay honest about the rest

- `kind="photo"` resolves to an `IMAGE` block bound to the k-th attached filename (inside a `repeat`, the k-th
  iteration's photo). This is the existing automatic placement, now deterministic; the validator's existing
  `IMAGE.file`-against-attachments check is unchanged.
- `kind="place"` and `kind="link"` are content the app cannot invent, so they resolve to an **unfilled slot** that
  holds its position through generation, revision, finalization and export. The model is told, in the prompt, that a
  slot is a reserved position it must not fill with prose — which is what keeps it from inventing a map URL or a
  phone number, and is the reason this is a slot rather than a `<write>`.
- An unfilled slot is represented as a `TEXT` block carrying an optional typed `slot {kind, label}`, **not** a sixth
  `BlockType`. A new enum member would force every existing switch — reading view, block editor, export mappings,
  revision, validator — to change to stay correct; an optional field on a block type they already handle means only
  the surfaces that render slots specially need to know they exist. [I2] holds either way: the canonical post stays a
  block array and every per-platform output stays derived from it.

### Template compliance is checked as slot presence, and repaired rather than failed

After the existing block validation, one additional check runs: every slot the frozen template declared must be
present in the result. A missing one is **inserted as an unfilled slot at its template position**. Literal text
fidelity and section ordering are *not* enforced, and no template deviation fails a run or triggers a retry — a
generation that came back usable is never thrown away over a punctuation drift.

### Prompt shape

`[글 템플릿]` replaces `[글의 용도]` at the identical position — after the complete voice profile
(styleguide → active rules → excerpts → user rules → ending constraint) and before the per-post material — carrying,
in order: a fixed one-paragraph grammar legend, the repeat-expanded template body verbatim, and a fixed precedence
sentence. Guidelines' `[작문 지침]` keeps following it and keeps outranking it on content.

The template section's heading, legend and precedence sentence **never use the word 지침**, which is what retires the
collision described in As-is: after this change 지침 names exactly one thing in the prompt, and the
guideline-beats-template sentence has one possible referent. As with the purpose section today, the framing stays
Korean for every target language (plan 13's target-language rule still outranks a conflicting language instruction
inside a template's text), and a post with no template adds **no bytes at all**, so the no-template prompt stays
byte-identical to the current no-purpose golden and the cache-stable voice prefix of PRD §5 is preserved.

### Freezing

The **expanded** template body is frozen at enqueue into the job payload beside `target_length`, and into the shared
prepared snapshot for a write A/B experiment, exactly as the purpose brief is today. Handlers read only the payload
or the snapshot. Editing or deleting a template after `StartGeneration` / `StartRevision` returns — including across
a restart-resume and an explicit retry — cannot change the prompt, and a different template is a different input
hash. Because expansion happens before freezing, adding photos after a start also cannot change an in-flight run.

### Editing UI

`/templates` (nav 템플릿, in 용도's position after 말투) replaces `/purposes`. Each template edits in two modes over
the same stored body:

- **builder** (default) — a palette of 작문 · 사진 · 장소 · 링크 · 정해진 문구 · 반복 · 메모 dragged into a vertical
  canvas, reordered, edited and deleted in place, with the repeat block accepting children.
- **원문** — the body markdown in a plain editor, for pasting an existing template in and for reading what the model
  will actually receive. Parse errors are surfaced here, on the offending line.

The round trip must be **lossless for any body the builder itself produced**: builder → body → builder changes no
byte. A hand-written body that parses is editable in the builder; one that does not parse opens in 원문 mode with its
error and cannot be saved until it parses.

### Unfilled slots warn, they do not gate

Finalization and export are **not** blocked by unfilled slots. The editor and the export panel state how many remain
and where, and the exported body carries each one as a bracketed placeholder line in marker order, so the user fills
it in the platform's own editor after pasting. The existing finalization gates are unchanged.

### Migration

Migration `0022` drops `purposes` and `guideline_purposes`, creates `templates` and `guideline_templates`, and
replaces `posts.purpose_id` with `posts.template_id` (with the same composite account foreign key and the same
detach-on-delete trigger, for the same SQLite reason plan 11 documents). **Existing purpose rows are dropped with no
backfill and no conversion.**

This is destructive. The precondition was checked before the migration was written and it **did not hold**: the
deployed database held 2 purposes, 2 finalized posts assigned to them, and 10 experiment rows recording a purpose
name. The owner was shown that and chose to drop rather than convert (2026-09-04), so those two briefs and both
assignments are gone and unrecoverable. `model_experiments.purpose_name` is the one thing carried across — renamed to
`template_name`, because a frozen comparison record must keep saying what both candidates were given.

## Scope / Non-goals (regression boundary)

**In scope**

- A `Template` aggregate per account (`name`, `description`, `body`) with create / list / update / delete, replacing
  the `Purpose` aggregate, its context, its RPCs, its screen and its nav entry.
- The template grammar, its parser (server-authoritative, mirrored client-side for the builder), its parse-error
  contract, and its bounds.
- Server-side `repeat` expansion against attached photo count, bounded and refused when out of bounds.
- Slot resolution: `photo` to an `IMAGE` block, `place` / `link` to an unfilled slot; the optional typed `slot` field
  on a block and every surface that renders it (reading view, block editor, revision, export).
- The slot-presence check after block validation, and position-preserving insertion of a missing slot.
- The `[글 템플릿]` prompt section — legend, expanded body, precedence sentence — at the purpose section's exact
  position, and retiring the word 지침 from it.
- Freezing the expanded body in the job payload and the experiment snapshot; `model_experiments.template_name`.
- `posts.template_id`, the editor's 템플릿 picker in 글 생성's dock row, and the post list's template name.
- Rescoping guidelines from purposes to templates (`guideline_templates`, the 특정 템플릿 scope control, the
  적용 대상 없음 state, and the purpose-delete cascade becoming a template-delete cascade).
- `/templates` with the builder + 원문 toggle, lossless round trip, and unfilled-slot counts in the editor and the
  export panel.
- Migration `0022`, embedded and boot-run ([I7]).

**Non-goals**

- **No change to 말투.** Profiles, samples, rules, evidence, comparisons, validations and prompt excerpts are
  untouched, and **nothing about a template is learned, inferred, suggested, ranked or written by a model** — [I4]'s
  learning boundary stays entirely with voice. There is no template evidence pipeline and no candidate lifecycle.
- **No guessing.** No template is selected from a title, memo, tags, photos or content; 없음 stays the default; there
  is no default template and no seeded or shipped template library. The empty state shows one worked example as
  **copy**, never as a row.
- **No provider call and no job from any template surface** ([I5]): listing, creating, editing, deleting, parsing,
  the builder, and the raw-mode preview are plain CRUD and pure client work.
- **`catalog_model_purposes`, `purpose_reasoning_effort`, and every "purpose" in the model catalog and provider
  registry are a different concept** — the five model-stage purposes (`photo-analysis` · `style-analysis` ·
  `writing` · `image-generation` · `video-generation`) of plans 04/18 and changes 20/24. They are **not** renamed,
  moved, or touched by this change, and the implementing job must not treat the shared English word as a signal.
- **[I2] is not weakened.** No sixth `BlockType`, no HTML anywhere in the canonical post, and every per-platform
  output stays derived from the block array.
- **[I3] is not weakened.** The observe/write split, the one-active-writer rule and the explicit-A/B-only fan-out are
  unchanged, and the observe prompt never sees a template.
- No strict compliance validation: literal text fidelity, section order and heading levels are not verified, and no
  template deviation fails a run, retries it, or discards a usable generation.
- No template re-application at revision time: a revision keeps the template frozen at its own start, and the
  existing byte-for-byte preservation rule for untouched blocks is unchanged.
- No in-app UI for *filling* a `place` or `link` slot, no map/place lookup, no URL validation, no provider or
  platform API call to resolve one. A slot is a reserved position and nothing more.
- No new iterator beyond `each="photo"` (no per-tag, per-observation, per-section or numeric repeat), no conditionals,
  no variables, no expressions, no nested repeats, no template inheritance or includes.
- No template version history, soft delete/restore, import/export, sharing, or cross-account copying.
- No template↔voice link, no per-template model selection, target length, tags, reasoning effort or quota, and no
  change to plans 04, 05, 09, 17 or 18.
- No change to the plan 06 block validator's existing behavior for the five canonical types, to
  finalization gates, to autosave, to publishing ([I1]), to browser-side image handling ([I6]), or to the durable-job
  boundary ([I5]).
- The guideline entity's own fields, cap, precedence, injection order and capture dialog are unchanged here — only
  its scope target moves from purpose to template. Automatic candidate collection is [change 26](../26.collect-guideline-candidates-from-revisi.md).

## Acceptance Criteria

1. An account creates a template whose body is the interview example, assigns it to a post with three attached
   photos, and generates. The result contains the intro prose, both `=========================` literal lines
   byte-exactly, a rating line, one unfilled 장소 slot, then **exactly three** photo-plus-description pairs in
   attachment order, then the closing verdict — with no leftover grammar text (`<write>`, `<repeat>`, `<note>`, a
   note's instruction, or a slot's `label`) anywhere in the post.
2. The same post with **zero** photos generates with the entire repeat block absent, including its literals, and
   still produces the intro, the rating line, the 장소 slot and the closing verdict.
3. A `<note>` never appears in the post, and its text is present in the frozen prompt.
4. A post with **no** template produces a write prompt byte-identical to the fixed no-template golden under
   `internal/generation/testdata`, and the voice-profile prefix is byte-identical across posts with different
   templates and with/without guidelines.
5. `[글 템플릿]` occupies the position `[글의 용도]` held — after the complete voice profile, before the per-post
   material — with `[작문 지침]` after it; a guideline still overrides the template on content and the voice still
   overrides both on register. The word 지침 appears in the prompt only in the guideline section and its precedence
   sentence.
6. Editing or deleting a template after `StartGeneration` / `StartRevision` returns changes neither the running run
   nor a restart-resume nor an explicit retry; attaching more photos after the start does not change the expansion.
   Both write A/B candidates receive byte-identical prompts, a different template yields a different input hash, and
   the comparison detail still reads correctly after the template is renamed or deleted.
7. A template body that fails to parse cannot be saved; the failure names the offending line and the reason
   (unknown tag, unclosed tag, unknown `kind`, unknown `each`, nested `repeat`), and no partial template is stored.
   A body over `TEMPLATE_BODY_MAX_CHARS` is refused with the same allowlisted `field`/`max`/`actual` detail contract
   the purpose bounds used.
8. Builder → stored body → builder round-trips byte-identically for every template the builder produced. A pasted
   body that parses opens in the builder; one that does not opens in 원문 mode with its error and cannot be saved.
9. A `place` or `link` slot survives generation, an AI revision that does not mention it, finalization, and export:
   it holds its position, is counted in the editor and the export panel, blocks neither finalization nor export, and
   appears in the Naver output as a bracketed placeholder line in marker order without disturbing the `[사진 …]`
   markers or the photo copy controls.
10. A model result that omits a declared slot has it re-inserted at its template position; a result that drifts on
    literal text or section order is still accepted, and no run fails or retries because of template deviation.
11. A photo count whose expansion would exceed `TEMPLATE_MAX_REPEAT_EXPANSION` refuses at start with an actionable
    failure and enqueues nothing.
12. Deleting a template detaches it from its posts (returning the count), cascades its `guideline_templates` links,
    and deletes no post, content, photo, guideline or history. A guideline left with no links lists as 적용 대상 없음
    and reaches no prompt until it is rescoped. Frozen payloads and experiment snapshots keep their text and stay
    readable.
13. A foreign or unknown template id is `NotFound` and is indistinguishable from the other; naming one in a draft
    save leaves the post exactly as it was, title and memo included, and mints no post on a create. A foreign
    template id in a guideline scope applies nothing.
14. `catalog_model_purposes` and `purpose_reasoning_effort` are unchanged: every 모델 관리 purpose tab, its
    per-purpose reasoning effort, and every stage dropdown behave exactly as before.
15. After migration `0022`, `/purposes` is gone, no `purposes` or `guideline_purposes` table remains, an account with
    no templates sees the empty state, and every existing post reads and generates with no template.
