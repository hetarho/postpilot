# 10. Change: Korean naturalness baseline beneath the voice profile

> A **change proposal** (a WHAT delta) that modifies the already-shipped behavior of [plan/06.two-stage-generation-and-contact-sheet](../../plan/06.two-stage-generation-and-contact-sheet.md).
> Filled by /create-change through a user interview — a reviewable doc, not a one-line prompt.
> The STEPS live in the job that /create-change-job creates. Write it in English.

## Why (source review)

The user asked to review the Korean prose-refinement ("윤문") formulas claimed by
[epoko77-ai/im-not-ai](https://github.com/epoko77-ai/im-not-ai) and to fold what survives scrutiny into our
generation prompts. That repo is a *post-hoc* rewriting pipeline for AI-written Korean; its durable asset is not the
pipeline but its **AI-tell taxonomy (10 categories × ~70 patterns) with per-pattern corpus measurements**
(human 60 essays vs 3 models × 20 prompts, log-likelihood G² tests, plus a 24-pair topic-matched baseline and a
task-matched control). Postpilot's job is the opposite direction — *generate* text that never carries the tells —
so the taxonomy's detect-and-fix prescriptions are inverted here into generation-time constraints.

Two findings from that review shape this change and must not be lost:

1. **Only the empirically confirmed signals are worth prompt space.** Confirmed across all three models and
   robust to task matching: negative-parallelism rhetoric "A가 아니라 B" (9–18×, their single strongest signal),
   comma directly after a connective ending -고/-지만/-면서 (4.84× separation), paragraphs repeatedly *ending* on
   obligation "~해야 한다" (7.2×), cleft closers "필요한/중요한/핵심은 ~이다" (~10×), formulaic closers
   "결국 ~로 이어진다"/"~하는 이유다"/vague-future "향후·앞으로" closings/empty concession "과제도 남아 있다"
   (each ≈0 in human prose), long-sentence *absence* (humans produce 100+-char sentences ~11× more), convergence
   on generic policy verbs 확대·강화·개선·구축 (3.4×), piled-on generative metaphors 잠식·청사진·신호탄 (4.3×),
   abstract "~적 N" chains (1.6×), and uniform sentence rhythm (identical endings in a row; monotone
   simple-sentence runs).
2. **The folk rules their measurements *rejected* must stay out of the prompt.** Humans use "~에 대해" 3× more
   than AI, "~를 통해" 2× more, "~것이다" endings 2× more; sentence-initial conjunctions are a single-model
   (haiku) habit, not an AI signal. A prompt that bans these makes output *less* human. Their reverse indicators
   (first-person self-qualification "솔직히", time anchors "당시", emotional spikes) are exactly what a personal
   voice profile carries — stage 2 below.

## As-is

- `WritePrompt` ([backend/internal/generation/prompts.go](../../../backend/internal/generation/prompts.go)) defines
  "natural" with a single clause — "자연스러운 한국어 블로그 글을 작성하세요" — followed only by format/JSON
  rules. `RevisePrompt` ([backend/internal/generation/revise.go](../../../backend/internal/generation/revise.go)) has
  no naturalness guidance at all.
- Everything style-shaped comes from the voice projection (plan 03/10 contract): `[스타일가이드]` →
  `[활성 대조 규칙]` → `[글 예시 발췌]` → `[사용자 규칙]` → `[종결어미 제약]` → optional purpose section with the
  `purposePrecedence` line. When a voice is new or thin (`Empty` presence — a real state the FE warns about but
  does not block), the model writes from its own defaults, which is precisely the register the taxonomy documents.
  Even a rich profile only *adds* the user's habits; nothing *subtracts* the model's stock rhetoric (cleft
  closers, negative parallelism, obligation closings) unless the user happened to generate a contrast rule
  against it.
- Both prompt builders keep a byte-stable system prefix per voice for prompt caching (PRD §5); plan 06 AC 6–7
  snapshot-test the assembly order.

## To-be

**Stage 1 of the user's two-stage direction** (stage 2 — learning each author's own voice — is already shipped as
plans 03/10 and is *not* re-built here; this change slots a floor underneath it).

- A new **Korean naturalness baseline** section is added to the *static* half of both the write and the revise
  system prompts: after the task/format rules (`WritePrompt` / `RevisePrompt` constants + the tags line), before
  `[스타일가이드]`. It is a Go constant in `internal/generation` next to the prompts it joins (ARCHITECTURE §4:
  prompt text is code, not config), shared by both builders so a revision enforces the same floor as the pass
  that wrote the draft.
- The section carries **only generation-time constraints derived from the confirmed signals** (Why §1), phrased
  as caps and bans on the model's own defaults, roughly:
  - **Rhetoric caps**: at most one "A가 아니라 B / ~것이 아니라" antithesis per post; do not close paragraphs
    with cleft formulas ("필요한/중요한/핵심은 ~이다"), "결국 ~로 이어진다", "~하는 이유다", vague-future
    framing ("향후/앞으로" + no concrete time), or content-free concession slots ("과제도 남아 있다"); at most
    one paragraph may end on obligation ("~해야 한다").
  - **Punctuation**: no comma immediately after connective endings (-고/-며/-지만/-면서/-아서); most sentences
    should carry no comma at all.
  - **Rhythm**: vary sentence length inside a paragraph — mix short sentences with at least an occasional long,
    multi-clause one; mix simple and complex sentences (this complements, never overrides, the measured
    `[종결어미 제약]`).
  - **Lexicon**: prefer concrete action verbs over generic policy verbs (확대/강화/개선/확보/구축 남발 금지);
    do not stack invented conceptual metaphors (잠식·청사진·신호탄 류) — plain statements first; avoid chains of
    "~적 N" abstractions; no hype-adjective stacking.
  - **Subtraction-only spirit**: do not decorate — no aphoristic closers, no rhetorical flourishes the memo does
    not warrant.
- The section **must not** ban the rejected patterns (Why §2): no rule against "~에 대해", "~를 통해",
  "~것이다" endings, or sentence-initial conjunctions. This is a review gate on the constant's text, not just
  doc guidance.
- **Precedence**: a closing line states the layering — the baseline is the floor; the voice profile, contrast
  rules, and user rules above it win on any conflict (a user whose measured voice *loves* antithesis or "결국"
  keeps it; the six-axis profile and `[활성 대조 규칙]` already express such habits). This mirrors
  `purposePrecedence` in shape: purpose owns content/genre, voice owns register, baseline owns the default floor
  beneath both.
- **Blocks and formatting stay out of scope**: the taxonomy's structure/decoration categories (bullets, headings,
  emoji, bold) are postpilot product features (`LIST`/`HEADING` blocks, purpose briefs may demand lists) or
  measured voice habits (`emoji_use`), so the baseline says nothing about them. It governs prose inside TEXT
  blocks only.
- The baseline is **Korean-specific**: plan 13's per-post target-language generation must omit the section for
  non-Korean output (today all output is Korean, so this lands as a seam note for job 32, not a branch built
  here).

## Scope / Non-goals (regression boundary)

**In scope**

- The new baseline constant in `backend/internal/generation` and its injection into `BuildWritePrompt` and
  `BuildRevisePrompt` at the stated position (both A/B experiment write candidates inherit it automatically,
  since they share `BuildWritePrompt`).
- The precedence line and the placement note (why it sits in the stable prefix — byte-stability per voice is
  preserved; the prefix changes once, globally, which is an accepted one-time cache invalidation).
- Updating plan 06's prompt-assembly snapshot expectations (AC 6–7) and policy
  ([policy/generation.md](../../policy/generation.md)) to record the new section and its precedence.

**Non-goals (must NOT change)**

- No change to the voice learning system (plans 03/10), the analysis prompts, the contrast-rule pipeline, or the
  profile projection — stage 2 exists and is not touched.
- No change to injection order of the existing sections (styleguide → active rules → excerpts → user rules →
  ending constraint → purpose), the purpose precedence, the `[종결어미 제약]` mechanics, or the per-post user
  half of the prompt.
- No post-hoc rewrite/verification pass (no gates, metrics, or second LLM call — the repo's pipeline is
  explicitly not imported); no new RPC, schema, proto, or DB change; no new config value.
- Revision stays minimal-change: the baseline shapes sentences the revision *touches*; `RevisePrompt`'s
  "untouched blocks stay verbatim" rule keeps its authority.
- `ObservePrompt` untouched (observation writes no prose).
- No new tunables: caps like "at most one antithesis" are prompt text, not config (ARCHITECTURE §4).

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The write system prompt contains the Korean naturalness baseline section between the static task/format rules
   and `[스타일가이드]`, ending with the precedence line that the voice profile and user rules override the
   baseline on conflict (prompt snapshot test).
2. The revise system prompt contains the identical baseline section at the same relative position (snapshot
   test), and its existing minimal-change rules are unchanged.
3. The baseline text includes each confirmed constraint family (antithesis cap, formulaic-closer bans,
   obligation-ending cap, connective-ending comma ban, sentence-length/structure variation, generic-verb and
   metaphor-stacking avoidance) and contains **no** rule against "~에 대해", "~를 통해", "~것이다", or
   sentence-initial conjunctions (unit assertion on the constant, so a later edit cannot silently re-introduce a
   rejected folk rule).
4. With an `Empty` voice profile the baseline is still present — a brand-new voice gets the naturalness floor
   with zero personalization.
5. Prompt assembly remains byte-stable per voice: two posts with the same voice, purpose state, and length option
   produce identical system prefixes; a post without a purpose differs from the pre-change prompt only by the
   inserted baseline section (assembly test).
6. Existing plan 06 acceptance criteria 6–7 (profile order, format rules, filename rules) still pass with
   updated snapshots; ordinary generation and A/B write candidates carry the same baseline.

## Open questions (for review before /create-change-job)

- Exact Korean wording of the baseline constant is the job's main craft: it must stay short enough not to crowd
  the profile (the repo's own finding: rules without examples don't move the model — one or two inline
  before→after examples for the highest-value bans, e.g. the cleft closer, may earn their tokens).
- Whether the antithesis/obligation caps should mention numbers ("한 번만") or stay qualitative — proposal:
  numeric caps only where the evidence is positional (obligation *endings*), qualitative elsewhere.
