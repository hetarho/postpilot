# 12. Change: Consolidate the writing brief and dock the revision step

> A **change proposal** (a WHAT delta) that modifies the already-shipped behavior of [plan/02.post-drafting-and-list](../../plan/02.post-drafting-and-list.md).
> Filled by /create-change through a user interview — a reviewable doc, not a one-line prompt.
> The STEPS live in the job that /create-change-job creates. Write it in English.
>
> Owner request (2026-09-01), eight items in one writing-UX revision: (1) every model choice moves
> into the generation options surface, (2) no screen may keep a native `<select>`, (3) the options
> surface must stop opening over the draft, (4) the observation area becomes a swipeable carousel on
> a phone, (5) the 가제 belongs to 글 생성 alone and the AI title becomes the post's title at
> confirmation, (6) 글 다듬기 gets a permanent floating confirm action, (7) the revision input is
> always available, in a two-row dock, and (8) the dock gets tighter phone padding plus a
> glassmorphism variant to look at before deciding. The surface being reshaped is the post editor,
> which belongs to plan 02, so the change targets plan 02 and lists the other touched plans under
> Grounding.
>
> Interview decisions the owner made explicitly: the whole AI brief (not only the models) moves into
> options; every `Select` mount is migrated and the primitive is deleted; `posts.title` is **kept**
> and repurposed rather than dropped, because it mints the slug and feeds the write prompt;
> `규칙으로 저장` **stays** exactly as it is after its mechanics were explained; the options surface is a
> bottom sheet on a phone and a right-aligned popover from `sm:`; the carousel shows a sliver of the
> next card; both confirm actions share the dock's second row; the revision's secondary controls
> collapse; and the glassmorphism background ships as a comparison to be reviewed, not as a decision
> already made.

## As-is

- **The brief is scattered across the screen and the steps.** `DraftEditor` renders the 제목
  `Textarea`, `PostVoiceSelect` (말투), `PostPurposeSelect` (용도), and `PostLanguageSelect`
  (목표 언어) **outside** the step panels, so all four are on screen on 글 생성, 글 다듬기 and 글 완성
  alike. The 글 생성 panel then holds its own `sm:grid-cols-3` block of `StageModelSelect
  stage="observe"`, `StageModelSelect stage="write"`, and a `/ai-models` link labelled
  `writeCandidates`. The dock's `GenerationOptions` popover holds only the target-length checkbox
  and number field. Four different places therefore describe one thing: what the next AI run is
  given.
- **Native `<select>` is still the app's picker.** `shared/ui/select/Select` is a native `<select>`
  wearing the field well. [design-language §7](../../tech/design-language.md) already records the owner
  decision (2026-08-31) that an OS-drawn option list breaks the design system, forbids the primitive
  in new surfaces, and slates the existing mounts for migration to an app-drawn listbox that has
  never been built. It is currently mounted in twelve slices: `select-model`, `select-post-voice`,
  `select-post-purpose`, `select-post-language`, `edit-post-content` (block type, image file,
  heading level), `create-voice`, `configure-model-pair`, `configure-publishing-agent` (two),
  `publish-post` (two), `give-voice-feedback`, `manage-users`, plus `pages/ai-models` (two).
  `shared/ui/menu/Menu` (landed in archived change 11 / job 38) is the app-drawn *menu-button*,
  built for an icon trigger and a short preference list, not for a labelled form field.
- **The options popover opens over the draft.** `shared/ui/popover/Popover`'s panel is
  `absolute right-0 w-72` with `bottom-full mb-2` for the `above` placement. The 옵션 trigger is the
  first control in the dock's left cluster, so a 288px right-aligned panel opens upward and to the
  **left** of the trigger, covering the bottom-left of the draft; there is no width or viewport
  clamping, and no phone-specific shape.
- **The contact sheet does not swipe on a phone.** `ContactSheet` renders a plain vertical list of
  full-width cards at the base breakpoint and only becomes a `snap-x` horizontal strip of `w-60`
  cards from `sm:` up. The code comment records why the strip was withdrawn from the phone: a 240px
  card inside 328px of content left a 27% dead gutter and squeezed the observation prose to 216px.
  Eight photos are therefore eight full-height cards the user scrolls past vertically.
- **Two titles, and the wrong one is authoritative.** `posts.title` is the user's 가제;
  `content.title` is the generated title inside `PostContent`. The 가제 `Textarea` sits outside the
  step panels and stays editable on every step, beside a `BlockEditor` header that edits
  `content.title` — two title fields on one screen from 글 다듬기 on. `posts.title` is load-bearing in
  three places: `MintSlug` builds `YYYYMMDD-<sanitized title>` from it at create
  ([slug.go](../../../backend/internal/post/slug.go)), `BuildWritePromptForLanguage` receives it as the
  post's topic, and `profileForTopic` uses `post.Title + " " + post.Memo` as the excerpt-retrieval
  key for write, revise and A/B alike. The list row shows `posts.title`, falling back to
  `content.title` only when the 가제 is empty. Nothing ever writes the confirmed AI title back, so a
  finalized post is still listed under whatever placeholder was typed first.
- **Revision and confirmation are in flow, and the dock on ② holds no action.** `ReviseForm` is a
  `section` at the bottom of 글 다듬기 carrying the instruction `Textarea`, the character counter, the
  `규칙으로 저장` checkbox, the language-mismatch note, the post-revision `지침으로 저장` button, the
  blocker line, failures, and a `w-full sm:w-auto` submit. `FinalizeActions` follows it with 확정 and
  확정하고 말투 학습 behind a confirm `Dialog`. Both sit below a draft that is routinely thousands of
  pixels tall, so reaching either means scrolling to the end.
  [policy/posts.md](../../policy/posts.md) states the current rule explicitly: *"The dock carries at
  most one committing action — 생성 on ①, and none on ② or ③"*, and *"On a quiet 글 다듬기 / 글 완성 it
  is absent rather than an empty card."*
- **`규칙으로 저장` mechanics (unchanged by this change, recorded because the owner reviewed them).**
  When the checkbox is set, `StartRevision` appends the trimmed instruction to the post's voice
  profile `rules` through `voice.AppendRule` **at request time, before enqueue**, so the rule stands
  even if the revision itself fails; an exactly-duplicate line is not appended twice; the rules text
  is injected last into every later generation and revision prompt for that voice; and the save is
  refused (`ErrVoiceContentLanguageMismatch`, checkbox disabled in the FE) when the post's content
  language differs from the voice's source language.
- **The dock has one padding and one background.** `ActionBar` is `p-4` at every width on an opaque
  `surface-highest` card with `shadow-md`, floating clear of the phone tab bar (`bottom-dock-nav`)
  and of the viewport edge from `sm:` up.

## To-be

### 1. One options surface holds the whole AI brief

The 글 생성 step's options control becomes the single place the post's brief is set. Its panel holds,
in this order: **관찰 모델 · 작성 모델 · 말투 · 용도 · 목표 언어 · 목표 분량 · A/B 후보 설정 링크**. The
글 생성 panel then holds only the 제목, the 메모, the photos, the voice warning, and the contact sheet;
글 다듬기 and 글 완성 show none of these controls at all.

Every control keeps the exact semantics it has today, only relocated: the voice reassignment still
confirms through its dialog and is still blocked by `reassignmentBlocker`; the purpose select stays
enabled while a job runs and still says the running job keeps its enqueued brief; the language
select still shows the frozen-language note; the target length still validates against
`POST_TARGET_LENGTH_MIN`/`MAX` and saves without advancing `content_revision`; and every assignment
still travels through the draft autosave queue so a title save in flight cannot revert it.

Because a wrong voice silently ruins a draft, the **post's current voice must remain readable without
opening the panel** — the trigger names it (e.g. `옵션 · 일상 말투`) rather than reading `옵션` alone.

### 2. No native `<select>` remains in the app

A new app-drawn **listbox** primitive lands in `shared/ui` — a labelled field trigger showing the
current option, an app-drawn option list on the app's own surfaces, WAI-APG listbox semantics
(`aria-expanded`, `role="listbox"`/`option`, `aria-activedescendant` or roving focus, Home/End,
Escape, type-ahead optional), 44px rows, the `field-*` well at rest, and the `FieldMessage`/
`aria-invalid` validation contract the current `Select` already honours.

**Every** one of the twelve `Select` mounts migrates to it, or to `SegmentedControl` where the choice
is a bounded 2–5 (§7's existing rule decides per surface). `shared/ui/select/` and its export are
then **deleted**, so no new surface can reach for it. §7's Menu/Select paragraph is rewritten to
describe the shipped primitive instead of promising it.

### 3. The options surface fits the phone and stops covering the draft

- **On a phone it is a bottom sheet** — full-bleed to the bottom edge, `rounded-t-xl`, safe-area
  padded, its own body the one thing that scrolls, dismissed by the scrim, by `Escape`, and by a
  visible control, with the body scroll locked while open (§7 Overlays). The existing `Dialog` is
  confirm-shaped (one title, one confirm button), so this needs a sheet that takes arbitrary
  content — a generalization of `Dialog` or a sibling primitive, the job's call.
- **From `sm:` up it stays a popover**, opening **upward and aligned to the right edge** of the dock
  rather than to the left of its trigger, and never extending past the viewport on either side.
- `Popover`'s placement API grows whatever it needs to express this; the existing `AccountMenu`
  and `GenerationOptions` callers keep their current behavior.

### 4. The observation area swipes on a phone

At the base breakpoint the contact sheet becomes a horizontal snap carousel: cards at roughly 85% of
the content width so **the next card's edge is visible**, `snap-x snap-mandatory`,
`overscroll-x-contain`, and a position indicator (`3 / 8`) under the strip. This is §4.4's deliberate
horizontal exception, so it does not compete with the page's vertical scroll, and no card gains an
inner vertical scroller — a verbose observation makes its card taller, as today. The `sm:` and wider
shape keeps its current `w-60` strip. The width fraction is a registered token, not an arbitrary
class (§1.2).

### 5. The 가제 belongs to 글 생성; the AI title becomes the post's title at 확정

- `posts.title` is **kept**. Its three jobs — minting the slug, carrying the topic into the write
  prompt, and keying excerpt retrieval — are unchanged.
- The 가제 `Textarea` **moves into the 글 생성 panel** and is editable only there. It is not rendered
  on 글 다듬기 or 글 완성.
- From 글 다듬기 on, the only title on screen is `content.title`, edited through the `BlockEditor`
  header's existing `Editable`.
- **`FinalizePost` copies the finalized content's title into `posts.title`** in the same transaction
  as the finalization write. A `content.title` that is empty after trimming leaves `posts.title`
  untouched. The copy advances no `content_revision`, changes no `machine_baseline_*`, starts no job,
  and makes no provider call.
- **The slug never changes.** A post confirmed under a different title keeps the URL it was minted
  with.
- Consequently the list shows the 가제 while a post is `draft` or `review`, and the confirmed title
  once it is `finalized`; the existing empty-title fallback to `content.title` stays. A later content
  save returns the post to `review` and leaves the copied title in place until the next finalize
  overwrites it.

### 6 & 7. 글 다듬기 docks: revision input and both confirm actions, always

The 글 다듬기 dock gains two rows and the corresponding in-flow sections are removed from the panel,
so nothing is duplicated and §4.3's one-dock-per-scroller still holds:

| Row | Contents |
|---|---|
| 1 | the revision instruction field + an **icon-only send button** |
| 2 | **확정** and **확정하고 말투 학습**, side by side |

- The instruction field's secondary controls — the character counter, the `규칙으로 저장` checkbox with
  its language-mismatch note, and the post-revision `지침으로 저장` button — live in a row that
  **expands** while the field is focused or holds text and **collapses** when it is empty and
  unfocused. A running or failed revision keeps it expanded so its state is readable.
- Blockers, validation and failures render **inside the dock, above the controls**, so the software
  keyboard covers at most the controls and never the reason one is disabled (§8.3).
- Both confirm actions keep their current confirmation `Dialog`, their `canFinalize`/`canLearn`
  gating, their blocked-reason lines, and their carry to 글 완성. The `finalized` state's success
  notice and `글 완성으로` button move into the dock with them.
- The dock is mounted outside the step panel, so both actions must still flush the mounted block
  editor before naming a revision — the existing `contentEditorRef.flush()` → `flushContentQueue`
  fallback chain must keep working from the dock.
- `policy/posts.md`'s "no committing action on ② or ③" and "absent on a quiet 글 다듬기" lines are
  amended: ② now always docks. ③ is unchanged.

### 8. A tighter dock, and a glassmorphism variant to look at

- `ActionBar`'s padding drops one step on a phone (`p-3`, `sm:p-4`); nothing else about its float,
  its safe-area handling, or its `sm:` geometry changes.
- The job **ships both backgrounds and shows them side by side** — today's opaque `surface-highest`
  card and a translucent variant (a backdrop blur over a translucent `surface-highest`) — rendered in
  light and dark for the owner to compare **before** either is adopted. If the owner does not pick
  the translucent one, its code and tokens are removed in the same job. Any blur radius or alpha is a
  registered token in `frontend/src/app/styles/index.css`, never an arbitrary class (§1.2), and the
  translucent variant must still meet the §9 contrast baseline for every control resting on it.

## Scope / Non-goals (regression boundary)

**In scope**

- `pages/editor` (panel composition, step ownership of the 가제, the ② dock), `features/generate-post`
  (options surface contents), `features/edit-with-ai` and `features/finalize-post` (moved into the
  dock), `widgets/contact-sheet` (phone carousel), `shared/ui` (new listbox, new sheet/popover
  placement, `ActionBar` padding + background variant, `Select` deletion), and every slice that
  mounts `Select`.
- One backend edit: the title copy inside the existing `FinalizePost` transaction, plus its policy
  reflection.
- Both locales for every new or moved string
  ([ui-localization-and-error-contract](../../tech/ui-localization-and-error-contract.md)).

**Must NOT change (the regression boundary)**

- **No proto change and no migration.** No new RPC, no new field, no schema edit. `FinalizePost`
  keeps its request/response shape and its exact-revision precondition.
- **`규칙으로 저장` is untouched.** The checkbox, the append-at-request-time behavior, the duplicate
  guard, the language gate, the prompt injection order, and its visibility on `/voices` all stay
  exactly as they are — only where the checkbox sits on the screen moves.
- **[I2] · [I3] · [I5] hold.** The block array stays canonical; observe and write stay separate with
  every model choice explicit and never auto-applied; generation and revision stay durable jobs
  polled by the client. Relocating a model select changes what the screen looks like, never what is
  sent.
- **[I4] holds.** Nothing about voice isolation, rules, or profile ownership changes.
- The slug never changes after minting; `content_revision`, `machine_baseline_*`,
  `finalized_revision`/`finalized_at` and the `review ⇄ finalized` demotion rules are unchanged; the
  title copy is not a content save.
- Autosave is unchanged: the 가제 still rides the draft queue, and the voice/purpose/language
  assignments keep their presence-aware paths and their existing confirmation and frozen-job rules.
- A/B comparison behavior is unchanged; only the location of its link moves.
- The block reading view and its read-first `Editable` editing model are unchanged — this change adds
  no hand-editing capability and removes none.
- 글 완성 (step ③) is untouched: the learning panel, export and publishing keep their current shape
  and their in-flow placement.
- The wide-screen contact sheet keeps its current `w-60` strip and its filename/observation fields.
- The observation-to-photo pairing, the empty/waiting states, and the batch-by-batch refresh are
  unchanged.
- **Archived job 38 (change 11) already edited several of these files** for the `Typography`
  migration. Its landed work is the prerequisite baseline and must not be reverted.

**Non-goals**

- No new generation or revision capability, no prompt change, no model behavior change.
- No version history, undo, or diff view for revisions.
- No re-slugging of existing posts and no redirect layer.
- No change to `/ai-models`, `/voices`, `/purposes`, `/guidelines` or `/admin` beyond swapping their
  `Select` mounts for the new primitive.
- No decision on the glassmorphism background inside this document — the job produces the comparison
  and the owner decides.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. On 글 생성, one options surface holds 관찰 모델, 작성 모델, 말투, 용도, 목표 언어, 목표 분량 and the
   A/B 후보 설정 link; none of them is rendered anywhere else in the editor, and the 글 생성 panel
   itself shows only 제목, 메모, 사진, the voice warning and the contact sheet.
2. The options trigger names the post's current voice without the panel being opened.
3. Every relocated control keeps its behavior: a voice reassignment still confirms and is still
   blocked by `reassignmentBlocker`; the purpose select stays enabled during a job and says the
   running job keeps its brief; the language select still shows the frozen note; an out-of-range
   target length still shows its range message and does not save.
4. `grep -r '<select'` over `frontend/src` (excluding generated code) returns nothing, and
   `shared/ui/select/` no longer exists; every previous mount renders an app-drawn control that is
   operable by keyboard (open, arrow, Home/End, Escape, select) and exposes the same accessible name
   and validation state as before.
5. At 360px width, opening the options surface produces a bottom sheet that is full-bleed to the
   bottom edge, scrolls only its own body, locks the page scroll, and closes by scrim, Escape and a
   visible control; from `sm:` up it is a popover that opens upward, aligns to the dock's right edge,
   and does not extend past the viewport.
6. At 360px width the contact sheet is one horizontal snap carousel showing a sliver of the next
   card, with a `현재 / 전체` position indicator, `overscroll-x-contain`, and no inner vertical
   scroller; the page still scrolls vertically from anywhere on the strip. From `sm:` up the current
   `w-60` strip is unchanged.
7. The 가제 field appears only on 글 생성; 글 다듬기 and 글 완성 render exactly one title, which is
   `content.title`.
8. Confirming a post writes the finalized `content.title` into `posts.title`: the post list shows the
   confirmed title afterwards, the slug is unchanged, `content_revision` is unchanged, no job is
   created, and no provider is called. An empty `content.title` leaves `posts.title` as it was.
9. A post still `draft` or `review` is listed under its 가제; the existing fallback to `content.title`
   for an empty 가제 still applies.
10. On 글 다듬기 the dock is always present and shows row 1 (instruction field + icon send button) and
    row 2 (확정, 확정하고 말투 학습); neither the revision section nor the finalize section is rendered
    in the panel any more, and the page holds exactly one docked bar.
11. The counter, `규칙으로 저장` and `지침으로 저장` are hidden while the instruction field is empty and
    unfocused, and are shown while it is focused, holds text, or a revision is running or failed.
12. `규칙으로 저장` behaves exactly as before: checked, it appends the instruction to the voice's rules
    before enqueue and the rule survives a failed revision; a duplicate line is not appended twice;
    the checkbox is disabled with its reason shown on a content/voice language mismatch.
13. Every blocker, validation message and failure on 글 다듬기 renders above the dock's controls, and
    starting either confirm action still flushes a pending block edit before naming the revision (a
    finalize can never omit a pending edit).
14. `ActionBar` uses the reduced padding at the base breakpoint and its `sm:` geometry, float, and
    safe-area clearance are unchanged.
15. The opaque and translucent dock backgrounds are shown side by side in light and dark for the
    owner's decision, every value they use is a registered token, and the unchosen variant is removed
    before the job closes.
16. No proto file, generated client, or migration changed; `pnpm build`, lint, `go vet` and the full
    test suite pass; every new or moved string exists in both `ko` and `en`.
