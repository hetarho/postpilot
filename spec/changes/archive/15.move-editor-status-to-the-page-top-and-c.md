# 15. Change: Move Editor Status To The Page Top And Cut Its Information Load

> A **change proposal** (a WHAT delta) that modifies the already-shipped behavior of [plan/02.post-drafting-and-list](../../plan/02.post-drafting-and-list.md).
> Filled by /create-change through a user interview — a reviewable doc, not a one-line prompt.
> The STEPS live in the job that /create-change-job creates. Write it in English.

## As-is

The editor presents the post as three step panels over one mounted editor, and puts almost everything it has to
**say** into the docked action bar at the bottom of the scroller. That bar currently carries, stacked in one column:
the autosave state, the step's 말투/용도/brief header, the running job's progress, a failed job's retry notice, and
the step's own controls. The page top carries only the way back to the list, the post's status badge, the delete
control, and the three-step bar.

Four consequences, all of them visible on one 360px screen:

**The job speaks in a boxed notice.** A running job renders `ProgressLine` — an `info`-toned `Notice` block holding
`progressLabel(job)` plus a language badge. For a job whose stage is absent or unrecognized that label is the literal
sentence 작업 준비 중, so the first thing the bar shows after 생성 is pressed is a filled box announcing that nothing
has happened yet. The same box later reads 사진 3/5 관찰됨, 작성 후보 1/2 완료, 관찰 후보 …, 문체 분석 후보 …: the
job's numeric progress is spelled out as prose in a container whose weight was designed for warnings.

**Save state is a standing claim.** `SaveState` resolves to `saved` for the whole life of a queue that has ever
saved, so 저장됨 never comes down; the line is `sr-only` at `idle` purely so its flex gap does not deform the bar.
Its `error` state widens into a full danger notice inside the same column.

**The dock says the same refusal twice, in two grammars.** `GenerationActions` renders one line per action —
생성: {reason} and A/B 비교: {reason} — beneath the buttons. When a comparison is awaiting review, both lines read
먼저 대기 중인 A/B 결과를 확인해 주세요, directly above an A/B 결과 확인 link that is the actual way out; the same
duplication happens for every shared, purely temporal reason (`jobChecking`, `selectionSaving`, `selectionChecking`).

**A standing state is reported as an event.** On 글 완성, `VoiceLearningPanel` renders a `success` notice reading
이 revision을 확정했어요 whenever the finalized revision is the current one and nothing has been learned from it yet.
Nothing takes it down, because being finalized is a state the status badge at the top of the page already carries.

Two primitives underneath make overlays worse than the layout intends:

- The global focus ring is `outline: 2px solid` at `outline-offset: 2px` ([index.css](../../../frontend/src/app/styles/index.css)).
  `Sheet`'s body is the sheet's one scroller (`overflow-y-auto`), and CSS resolves the cross axis of a scroll
  container away from `visible` — so a `w-full` field inside that body has the left and right edges of its ring
  clipped away the moment it takes focus. Every sheet in the app is affected; 새 말투's first field is where it is
  seen first.
- `Listbox` opens its panel as an `absolute`, `z-30` sibling of its trigger, on `bg-surface-highest` — the same token
  `Sheet`'s own panel wears. Inside a sheet the panel is therefore clipped by the sheet's scroller (worst when it
  flips upward, which is exactly what a field near the bottom does) and, where it is visible, it is the same colour
  as the surface behind it with only `shadow-lg` to separate them.

## To-be

**One status region, at the top of the page, and it is the only place the editor reports state.**

- A 2px determinate progress bar is pinned along the top edge of the editor page while a job is running. Its value is
  the job's own `progressDone/progressTotal` where the stage reports one; a stage that reports no ratio renders the
  same 2px track in an indeterminate state rather than a different control. It occupies no layout height and it is
  absent when no job is running.
- One line of `meta`-weight text sits on the row that already holds 목록 and 삭제. It carries at most one thing, in
  this precedence: the running job's short label, then the save state, then the post's status. The status badge that
  stands there today is replaced by this line, so the row does not gain a second state indicator.
- The job's label loses its prose count: the numbers live in the bar's value, and the words say only which stage is
  running (사진 관찰 · 작성 · 문체 분석, and their A/B forms). 작업 준비 중 is not a stage and is not shown; a job
  with no reportable stage yet is exactly the indeterminate bar with the generic 생성 중 label.
- The save state settles. 저장 대기 중 / 저장 중 are shown while true, 저장됨 appears on a completed save and then
  goes quiet after a configured interval, and a retrying save says so. `saved` stops being a state that persists for
  the life of the editor.

**The dock holds controls and refusals, and nothing else.** No save line, no progress notice, no status badge. A
job's `FailureNotice` stays in the dock, because it carries a retry: a failure the user can act on is a control, not
a status. A bar left with neither a control nor a refusal is not rendered.

**A refusal is said once, by the surface that can resolve it.**

- 먼저 대기 중인 A/B 결과를 확인해 주세요 is removed. A pending comparison disables 생성 and A/B 비교 and leaves the
  A/B 결과 확인 link as the single statement of what to do.
- The shared, purely temporal reasons (`jobChecking`, `selectionSaving`, `selectionChecking`) are no longer written
  under the buttons; the top status line already says a job or a save is in flight.
- The per-action reason lines survive only for a **setup** refusal that names something the user must choose (no
  active write model, no vision-capable observe model, an A/B pair that is unset or identical, a deleted voice), and
  collapse to one line when both actions are refused for the same reason.

**A standing state is not announced as an event.** The 이 revision을 확정했어요 notice is removed from 글 완성; the
top status line reports 확정 as the post's status. 이 글에서 말투를 배웠어요 stays, because a completed learning run
is the outcome of an action the user took on that screen, not a re-statement of the post's status.

**글 다듬기's revision row commits with the space it has.** The instruction label 수정 요청을 입력하세요 takes a
smaller and heavier type role than the step title it currently borrows, and 확정하기 fills the entire remaining width
of that heading row rather than shrinking to its two words.

**Focus rings and dropdown panels are never clipped.**

- A `:focus-visible` ring is fully visible on every control inside a scrolling overlay body. The overlay reserves the
  ring's offset and width on both axes; the ring's own geometry does not change.
- `Listbox` renders its open panel through a portal at the document body, positioned against its measured trigger
  rect, above every overlay it may be opened from. It keeps the measured up/down flip and the height ceiling it has
  today, and it repositions or closes rather than detaching when its trigger's container scrolls.
- The panel is visually separable from the surface it opens over: it does not reuse the surface token of the sheet or
  popover that contains it, and it carries elevation of its own.

## Scope / Non-goals (regression boundary)

**In scope**

- `pages/editor` — where state is reported, and the composition of the top row, the step bar, and the dock.
- `entities/generation-job` — the progress presentation and the label vocabulary (`progressLabel`, `ProgressLine`).
- `features/save-draft` — the save state's presentation and the settling of `saved`.
- `features/generate-post` — which refusals are written under the actions.
- `features/finalize-post` — removal of the finalize success notice; `features/edit-with-ai` — the instruction
  label's type role and the heading row's widths.
- `shared/ui` — `Sheet`/`Popover` scroller gutters for the focus ring, and `Listbox`'s portalled, elevated panel.
- `spec/tech/design-language.md` — the status/progress placement rule and the overlay-panel rule this establishes.

**Non-goals — these must NOT change**

- The three-step model, its labels, the status→step mapping, and the fact that steps are panels over one mounted
  editor rather than routes ([tech/draft-autosave](../../tech/draft-autosave.md)).
- The autosave queue itself: its debounce, its retry schedule, its slug keying, its mint handoff, and its survival
  across a step change. Only the presentation of `SaveState` moves.
- What a job is and how it is observed: `generation_jobs`, the worker, the boot sweep, and 2s polling ([I5]).
- The block array as the canonical post ([I2]), and observe/write separation with explicit model choice ([I3]).
- Every generation **precondition** and every server refusal. Only the wording and placement of how a refusal is
  presented changes; no action becomes newly available or newly blocked.
- `FailureNotice` and retry behavior for a failed job, including which step offers the retry.
- Voice conditions that carry a way out — the empty-profile warning, the deleted-voice warning, the content/source
  language mismatch notice — stay as notices where they are. They are actions, not status.
- The export panel's content, the publish panel, and the 문장 의견 dialog's own body (changes 16 and 17).
- `Listbox`'s keyboard contract: trigger as the only tab stop, `role="listbox"` panel, programmatic option focus,
  Escape closing the innermost overlay only, and Tab traversing out.

**Sequencing** — change 16's 문장 의견 dialog depends on the `Listbox` portal and elevation delivered here.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. While a generation job with a reportable ratio runs, the editor page renders a determinate 2px progress bar at
   its top edge whose value tracks the job's `progressDone/progressTotal`, and the docked bar renders no progress
   notice.
2. A running job whose stage reports no ratio renders the same top bar in an indeterminate state; the string
   작업 준비 중 appears nowhere in the editor.
3. Candidate and photo counts (작성 후보 1/2 완료, 사진 3/5 관찰됨 and their A/B forms) no longer appear as prose in
   the editor; the running stage is named in the top status line and its numbers are carried by the bar.
4. A completed save shows 저장됨 in the top status line and the line goes quiet after the configured settle
   interval; a post that has saved and then sat untouched shows no save text.
5. With a comparison awaiting review, 생성 and A/B 비교 are both disabled, the A/B 결과 확인 way out is present, and
   the string 먼저 대기 중인 A/B 결과를 확인해 주세요 is not rendered.
6. With a job in flight or a save in flight, no reason line is rendered under the generation actions; with no active
   write model chosen, exactly one setup reason line is rendered.
7. On 글 완성 with a finalized, not-yet-learned revision, no 이 revision을 확정했어요 notice is rendered, and the top
   status line reports the post's 확정 status.
8. On 글 완성 after a successful learning run, 이 글에서 말투를 배웠어요 is still rendered.
9. On 글 다듬기, the 수정 요청을 입력하세요 label renders at a smaller, heavier type role than the step title, and
   확정하기 occupies the full remaining width of its heading row at the base breakpoint.
10. Focusing the first field of 새 말투 leaves the focus ring fully visible on all four sides; the same holds for a
    full-width field inside any scrolling overlay body.
11. A `Listbox` opened near the bottom of a sheet renders its full option panel outside the sheet's scroller, with
    every option reachable, and the panel's background is distinguishable from the sheet's own surface.
12. `Listbox`'s keyboard contract is unchanged: the trigger is the only tab stop, arrows move option focus, Escape
    closes only the listbox when it is open inside a sheet, and Tab leaves the panel.
13. No regression in plan 02's shipped behavior: title/memo autosave across a step change and across the
    `/posts/new` mint, photo upload and delete, the post list badges, and post deletion from the editor.
