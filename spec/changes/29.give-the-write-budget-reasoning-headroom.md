# 29. Change: Give the write budget reasoning headroom and disable reasoning without none

> A **change proposal** (a WHAT delta) that modifies the already-shipped behavior of [plan/04.model-provider-registry](../plan/04.model-provider-registry.md).
> Filled by /create-change through a user interview — a reviewable doc, not a one-line prompt.
> The STEPS live in the job that /create-change-job creates. Write it in English.

## As-is

Changes 07 and 24 built the reasoning/budget seam: a per-stage effort policy, a per-(model, purpose) operator
override, a per-stage completion budget, and a `TruncatedError` that separates "the body filled the budget" from
"the model spent the budget thinking". Writing still fails in production, and the seam cannot currently prevent it.

### The failure, as recorded

`deepseek/deepseek-v4-pro-0813`, write stage, from `usage_events` joined to `generation_jobs` (read 2026-09-05):

| Time | completion | of which reasoning | outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 09-04 16:16 | 2,054 | 827 | done |
| 09-04 09:18 | 2,328 | 347 | done |
| **09-04 08:56** | **8,192** | **8,116** | **failed — `MODEL_OUTPUT_TRUNCATED`** |
| 09-04 07:42 | 4,046 | 1,588 | done |
| 09-04 07:41 | 8,192 | 6,422 | done — 1,770 tokens of body |
| 09-04 07:33 | 2,672 | 2 | done |

`technical_detail` on the failed row is the string `TruncatedError` already produces: `completion budget exhausted:
8116 of 8192 completion tokens went to reasoning, 76 to visible output`. Four such failures are recorded against
this model.

### Why the effort override cannot bound it

OpenRouter's effort-to-budget mapping (`high ≈ 80% of max_tokens`, `low ≈ 20%`) applies only to models whose effort
**it converts**: "For models that only support `reasoning.max_tokens`, the effort level will be set based on the
percentages above." `deepseek/deepseek-v4-pro-0813` carries `reasoning_effort` in `supported_parameters`, so the
string is passed through and the provider decides how long to think. The 2 → 8,116 spread above is that decision,
not ours. **For a native-effort model the override is a hint, not a cap.**

The same distinction is why raising the budget is not uniformly safe, and change 07 measured that: on a model whose
effort OpenRouter converts, a larger `max_tokens` buys a proportionally larger think. Which behavior a model has is
recorded by [change 27](27.persist-catalog-reasoning-capabilities-a.md) as `reasoning_native_effort`; without it
this change cannot tell the two apart.

### The budget is the constraint, and it is the wrong size

`WriteFloor` is `LLM_MAX_TOKENS_DEFAULT` = 8,192, and `LLMCompletionBudget.Write` floors at it. That figure was
chosen by job 23 as the room a *body* needs. For a model that reasons into the same budget it is the room for the
body **and** the thinking, and OpenRouter states the requirement plainly: "`max_tokens` must be strictly higher than
the reasoning budget to ensure there are tokens available for the final response after thinking." The model's own
ceiling is far away — `top_provider.max_completion_tokens` is 384,000 for this model.

### There is no way to turn reasoning off on this model

[`buildRequest`](../../backend/internal/llm/openaicompat/client.go#L206) can only ever emit
`reasoning: {effort: …}`. `deepseek/deepseek-v4-pro-0813` is `mandatory: false` — reasoning *can* be disabled — but
its `supported_efforts` is `max·high·low` with no `none`, and the documented mechanism for that case is
`reasoning: {enabled: false}`, which the adapter cannot say. `anthropic/claude-sonnet-5` has the same shape.

### The diagnosis exists and nothing reads it

`TruncatedError`'s own comment names the remedy for each cause — "a body that filled the budget wants a larger
budget, while a body the model never wrote because it spent the budget reasoning wants a lower effort for this
purpose, or another model" — and then the split lives only in `technical_detail`. The operator's 모델 관리 surface
shows per-model reasoning spend (change 24) but not how often that spend ended in a truncation.

## To-be

### The write and revision budgets carry reasoning headroom

`LLMCompletionBudget` gains a **code-owned** headroom multiplier, applied to the writing and revision budgets when
the resolved model reasons natively on effort, and not otherwise. It is code-owned for the same reason the rest of
the budget policy is: how much room a stage needs is a property of the work, not of the deployment.

- Applied to **write and revise only**. Observation keeps its batch-derived budget untouched: it has no long output
  and no recorded truncation.
- Applied only when the model's `reasoning_native_effort` is true. On a model whose effort OpenRouter converts to a
  percentage, the effort already guarantees the body's share, and extra room would only buy a longer think.
- Bounded by the existing `Ceiling`, so a mistyped target still cannot ask for an unbounded completion.

The deployment constant `LLM_MAX_TOKENS_DEFAULT` is unchanged; the headroom is a multiplier over the budget derived
from it. [Change 28](28.price-credit-holds-from-the-stage-budge.md) has already made the credit hold price each
call at the budget it is actually sent, so a larger write budget raises that job's hold rather than being absorbed
at settlement.

### The adapter can disable reasoning

When the resolved effort is `none` and the model's recorded `reasoning_efforts` does not contain `none`, the
request body carries `reasoning: {enabled: false}` instead of `reasoning: {effort: "none"}`. Everything else about
the body is unchanged — in particular `reasoning.exclude` has never been sent and stays unsent, for the reason
change 24's regression test states.

### A truncation stays a failure, and says why to the operator

No retry and no model fallback. The user-facing outcome is unchanged: the job fails with
`MODEL_OUTPUT_TRUNCATED` and the same message. What changes is that the operator can see it: the per-model
reasoning-spend figures the 모델 관리 surface already shows gain the count of calls that ended in a
reasoning-caused truncation, so "this model burns its budget thinking" is visible next to the effort control that
answers it.

## Scope / Non-goals (regression boundary)

**In scope**

- `LLMCompletionBudget`'s write and revision derivation, and the headroom constant beside `writeBudgetPerChar` and
  `writeBudgetCeilingFactor` in `backend/internal/platform/config`.
- Whatever the registry needs to pass the resolved model's native-effort signal into the budget decision without
  leaking a catalog type across the `internal/llm` boundary.
- `openaicompat.buildRequest`'s disable path.
- The reasoning-caused-truncation count on the curation surface, sourced from the ledger the way change 24's
  reasoning spend already is.

**Non-goals — these must NOT change**

- **Automatic retry, and in-app fallback to another model.** Explicitly rejected in the interview, and plan 04's own
  non-goals already exclude in-app routing between models.
- Any user-facing copy, error reason or FE generation surface. `MODEL_OUTPUT_TRUNCATED` keeps its meaning and its
  message.
- The observation budget, and the observation stage generally.
- `LLM_MAX_TOKENS_DEFAULT`, and the meaning of `WriteFloor` as the floor a post with no requested target gets. Job
  23 raised that to 8,192 to stop write-stage truncation and change 24 recorded that it must never be quietly
  walked back; the headroom multiplies it, never lowers it.
- Per-user effort selection, rejected by change 24: the right effort is a per-model, per-task measurement.
- `reasoning.max_tokens` as a hard cap. Only 10 of 300 reasoning models advertise `supports_max_tokens` and none of
  the models registered in production does.
- The stage reasoning policy (`observe: low`, `write: low`, analysis sends nothing) and the per-(model, purpose)
  override resolution order.
- The `internal/llm` boundary: no adapter or catalog type crosses it, and nothing above it learns which vendor
  answered.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. A write call to a model recorded as native-effort is sent a completion budget larger than the same call is sent
   today, by the code-owned multiplier, and still bounded by `Ceiling`.
2. A write call to a model **not** recorded as native-effort is sent exactly the budget it is sent today.
3. An observation call is sent exactly the budget it is sent today, for every model.
4. A revision is sent the headroom-adjusted budget under the same native-effort condition, still derived from the
   larger of the existing content and the requested target.
5. A post with no requested target is never sent less than `WriteFloor`.
6. Resolving effort `none` on a model whose recorded list lacks `none` produces a body carrying
   `reasoning: {enabled: false}` and no `effort` key; on a model whose list contains `none` the body still carries
   `effort: "none"`.
7. `reasoning.exclude` is absent from every request body, and a request with an unresolved effort still carries no
   `reasoning` key at all.
8. A reasoning-caused truncation still fails the job with `MODEL_OUTPUT_TRUNCATED` and the unchanged user-facing
   message, and `technical_detail` still carries the reasoning/visible split.
9. The 모델 관리 surface shows, per model and purpose, how many calls ended in a reasoning-caused truncation
   alongside the reasoning spend it already shows.
10. Replaying the recorded 09-04 08:56 failure shape — 8,116 reasoning tokens against a 8,192 budget — against the
    new write budget leaves visible-output room rather than truncating.
