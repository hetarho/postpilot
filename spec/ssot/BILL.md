# BILL subscriptions, charges, credit purchase
> r4 | The money side QUOTA points at: an account subscribes to a tier itself, is charged in KRW against USD-defined prices on its own anchor day, buys credits at par whenever it wants, and manages all of it on one billing screen.

## decisions
- BILL-1 [o] a subscription belongs to the account that pays for it: the signed-in account registers its own payment method, picks its own tier and term, and cancels on its own; `free` needs no payment method; the operator path (`api setplan`) survives for support and never touches a payment method (→QUOTA-3)
- BILL-2 [o] prices are defined in USD beside the grants they size (→QUOTA-7) and charged in KRW at the previous business day's published base rate, VAT included; a screen shows the USD price together with the won figure that will actually be charged and states that the rate moves ← a Korean card must be charged in won, while a price defined in won would drift from the credit's fixed $0.01 list value
- BILL-3 [o] the cycle anchors on the day the subscription starts, not the calendar month: the charge and the monthly credit grant land on the same anchor day every month and an anchor past a shorter month's end clamps to its last day; the grant window follows the same anchor (→QUOTA-37)
- BILL-4 [o] two terms, monthly and annual, both carrying the same monthly grant: an annual term charges ten months' price for twelve months and still grants credits month by month on the anchor day, an unused month lapsing exactly as it does now ← a year of credits released at once concentrates provider cost in whichever month the user chooses and makes every refund a negotiation
- BILL-5 [o] an upgrade is immediate: the difference between the two prices is charged at once, the current cycle's credits rise by the credit difference (→QUOTA-35), and the anchor never moves; on an annual term only the current month's credits rise and the charge is BILL-18's
- BILL-6 [o] a downgrade and a switch from annual to monthly are scheduled, never refunded: the term already bought runs out at the tier it was bought at and the new tier takes effect on the next anchor; the schedule can be cancelled until that anchor and a second change replaces it rather than stacking
- BILL-7 [o] cancellation is a scheduled stop: auto-renewal is switched off, the tier and every credit already granted stand until the current term ends, the account becomes `free` on the next anchor and is charged nothing; resuming before that anchor is the same switch turned back on
- BILL-8 [o] a failed renewal charge drops the account to `free` at once — no retry, no grace period, no dunning ← payment-recovery machinery costs more to run than this product's charge volume is worth; the lapsed term's credit lot expires on its own schedule, purchased credits are untouched, and repairing the payment method starts a fresh cycle anchored on that day
- BILL-9 [o] a credit purchase is one charge at the par rate (→QUOTA-34) and is independent of any subscription: any signed-in account may buy, and a purchase changes no tier, no anchor and no renewal
- BILL-10 [o] registering a payment method grants one non-expiring 100-credit bonus per account, and it replaces the bonus handed out at provisioning (→QUOTA-9) ← self-signup makes an account-creation bonus farmable, and a card on file is the cheapest evidence of a real person this product can ask for
- BILL-11 [o] refunds: a subscription charge is never refunded and cancellation is the only exit (BILL-7); an unused credit purchase is refunded in full within 7 days of its charge, refused once any of it is spent, and the refund voids that purchased lot ← the statutory withdrawal right covers unused digital goods and the product offers exactly that, no more
- BILL-12 [o] the product mails what a card statement cannot explain — a renewal charged, a renewal failed with the drop to `free`, a scheduled cancellation taking effect, a purchase, a refund — to the account's verified address (→AUTH) and never leaves any of them visible only behind a login
- BILL-13 [o] receipts and tax documents are the payment provider's: the product reproduces its own charge history in the app and issues no cash receipt, tax invoice or business-registration collection of its own
- BILL-14 [o] one authenticated billing screen holds the whole money surface: the current subscription (tier, term, anchor, next charge with its won figure, any scheduled change or cancellation), the payment method, the charge and grant history, credit purchase, and cancel or resume; `/plans` keeps comparison and entry only (→QUOTA-28)
- BILL-15 [o] every charge, refund, grant and tier change is an append-only row carrying the USD amount, the rate applied and the KRW charged, and the screen renders that history ← a money question must be answerable without signing into a third party, and a disputed amount must show which rate produced it
- BILL-16 [o] the payment provider must offer KRW recurring card billing, hosted card entry, a refund call and server-to-server notifications; card numbers never reach the product or its logs (a provider token stands for the card) and the provider's notification, never the browser's return from checkout, is what makes a charge real ← a user closing the tab mid-redirect must not lose a subscription they paid for; which provider satisfies this is engineering's choice
- BILL-18 [o] an annual upgrade is charged for the months the term still has to run with the month already running counted as whole, at the difference between the two tiers' monthly equivalents of the annual price (a twelfth of it, →BILL-4) ← the term's two-month discount belongs to the term rather than to the tier, and counting only the completed months at the monthly list price both makes an upgrade inside the last month free and undercharges a fresh term by one of the twelve windows it grants
- BILL-17 [x] coupons and promotion codes, free trials, automatic top-up when the balance runs low, postpaid usage billing, team or organization billing, invoice or bank-transfer payment, self-serve tax documents, dunning and recovery flows, day-level proration (only whole-month differences are ever charged), and any currency display beyond the USD price and its KRW charge — out of scope

## flow
- subscribe: a rung on `/plans` → hosted card entry → the provider's token is stored → first KRW charge at the previous business day's rate → tier and anchor set → the monthly lot opens
- renew: anchor day → charge(ok → the next term plus the monthly grant | failed → `free` at once, mail sent)
- change tier: upgrade(the difference charged now → the current lot rises) | downgrade or annual→monthly(scheduled → applied on the next anchor)
- cancel: auto-renewal off → the term runs out → `free` on the next anchor (resume until then)
- purchase credits: amount → charge → a `purchased` lot; refund within 7 days while untouched → the lot is voided

## constraints
- self-signup, the account email and its verification belong to AUTH and are decided there (→AUTH-1, →AUTH-33, →AUTH-34, →AUTH-41); an operator-made account with no email registers one before it can hold a payment method (→AUTH-40)
- credits stay QUOTA's: the ladder and grants (QUOTA-7), the par purchase rate (QUOTA-34), lot kinds and consumption order (QUOTA-12), the grant effect of a tier change (QUOTA-35) and of starting one (→QUOTA-42); the anchor window (→QUOTA-37), the term's neutrality toward the grant (→QUOTA-38) and the payment-method bonus (→QUOTA-9) are decided there
- the KRW/USD rate source must be a published daily base rate whose value for a given day is reproducible after the fact, and the rate used is stored on the charge row
- the product is architecturally a paid public one, not a two-user tool (→ARCH-1, →ARCH-23)
- placement: a new `backend/internal/billing` context and `proto/postpilot/v1/billing.proto`, `frontend/src/entities/subscription` and the billing route; the provider adapter sits behind a consumer-declared port like every other outside service (→ARCH-6, →ARCH-7)
