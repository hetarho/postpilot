# CDS clip output design system
> r10 | Reusable visual quality and timing rules for template-authored clip content, with scene-grounded text, explicit placement and exposure, preview verification and deterministic final output.

## decisions
- CDS-1 [o] on-screen information is authored by the selected template, with no compulsory business, location, price, menu or minimum fact count; visual legibility and scene-to-fact correctness remain delivery requirements (→CLIP-60 →CLIP-63 →CLIP-68)
- CDS-2 [o] every text element stays inside its ratio's safe area, except an explicitly header-placed badge which uses the separate symmetric header bounds (→CDS-57)
- CDS-3 [o] every on-screen text reads muted and small: body ≥ 48 px, caption and badge ≥ 40 px, contrast ≥ 4.5:1 against its effective background, exposure scaled to its length (→CDS-41)
- CDS-4 [o] sentence captions use a 180 ms fade-in settling 12 px upward and a 120 ms fade-out; rapid phrases replace one another immediately with no fade or movement; per-glyph pops, typewriter, count-up, rotation, bounce, wipe, slide, zoom and blur are refused ← a restrained tone reads as brand-safe and edited rather than templated
- CDS-5 [o] disclosure appears only as a template-declared element for its resolved visibility interval; neither a campaign type nor a product default inserts a badge or requires whole-clip exposure (→CLIP-46 →CLIP-65)
- CDS-6 [o] original audio stays; stickers, emoji, decorative graphics, product logos and any platform watermark are refused (→CLIP-18 →CLIP-28)
- CDS-7 [o] every placement and style choice follows the rule tables below with no randomness; equal inputs render equal outputs
- CDS-8 [o] the base canvas is 9:16 at 1080×1920 and every dimension below is stated on it; 16:9 and 1:1 derive by CDS-46 through CDS-48
- CDS-9 [o] the 9:16 design safe area is x 64, y 40, w 856, h 1380 (x ≤ 920, y ≤ 1420); the top inset is 40 px and TOP elements add a further 40 px gap; platform UI estimates remain separate references, not a verified cross-platform intersection
- CDS-10 [o] the estimated Naver overlay geometry behind SA-N — top bar y 0–230, right action column x ≥ 930 from y 1000, bottom information block y ≥ 1440, left edge 64 — lives in runtime constants `SAFE_TOP` `SAFE_BOTTOM` `SAFE_RIGHT` `SAFE_LEFT`, never in literals ← Naver publishes no Clip UI guide and the bottom block varies most (±60 px)
- CDS-11 [?] the real-device measurement of Naver Clip's overlay geometry, how 16:9 and 1:1 uploads display inside the vertical feed, and the height the information-tag chips occupy; platform estimates remain unverified and do not override the design inset in CDS-9
- CDS-12 [o] 9:16 anchors inside the design safe area: `TOP` plate top y 80 · `UPPER_MID` plate centre y 700 · `LOWER_MID` plate centre y 1100 · `BOTTOM` plate bottom y 1380 (default) · horizontal `CENTER` x 492 · `LEFT` x 96 · `RIGHT` x 888; a nine-cell grid is not used ← subtle offsets between cells break the consistency CDS-7 promises
- CDS-13 [o] the 16:9 safe area is (96, 72, 1728, 936) on 1920×1080 and the 1:1 safe area is (64, 72, 952, 936) on 1080×1080 ← neither ratio is expected to meet feed UI; the margins are broadcast title-safe practice plus a bottom player control bar
- CDS-14 [o] colour tokens: `ink.900` #111111 α0.72 dark plate · `ink.900s` #111111 α0.88 card · `paper.50` #FFFCF5 α0.94 light plate · `text.white` #FFFFFF · `text.ink` #1A1A1A · `text.muted` #FFFFFF α0.72 · `stroke.dark` #0B0B0B α0.85 · `shadow.text` #000000 α0.55 blur 12 offset (0, 4) · `scrim.bottom` #000000 α0 → 0.55 over y 1040–1420 · `scrim.top` #000000 α0.45 → 0 over y 40–350 · `badge.ad` #111111 α0.80 under white text
- CDS-15 [o] the accent palette stays the seven approved colours coral #FF6B57 · amber #FFB020 · lime #9BD53A · teal #2BB8A6 · blue #3D7BFF · violet #8A63FF · pink #FF6FB5, one per project (→CLIP-14); as a text colour an accent is allowed only on a dark plate or scrim, never on `paper.50` and never on the disclosure badge ← lime and amber fall under 3:1 on light ground, and a decorated badge stops reading as a disclosure
- CDS-16 [o] a plated element (깔끔하게 · 메모 · hook card · ending card · information chip) needs no brightness sampling ← α ≥ 0.72 ink under white text stays ≥ 5.9:1 even over a white frame
- CDS-17 [o] typography: Pretendard Variable is the primary face (copy, information; weights 500–800), Paperlogy 7 Bold / 8 ExtraBold the secondary face for hook titles and 크게 강조, Noto Sans KR 700 the fallback; all three are product-bundled at a fixed version (→CLIP-13) and any face whose licence forbids redistribution is excluded ← all three are SIL OFL and may ship on the server
- CDS-18 [o] the face is fixed per style; no per-project or per-user font choice (→CLIP-28)
- CDS-19 [o] type scale on 9:16: `t.hook` 84 Paperlogy 8, -0.02em, 1.15 (min 72) · `t.title` 72 Pretendard 800, -0.02em, 1.2 (min 64) · `t.mark` 60 Pretendard 800, -0.01em, 1.3 (min 52) · `t.body` 56 Pretendard 700, -0.01em, 1.3 (min 48) · `t.caption` 44 Pretendard 600, 0, 1.3 (min 40) · `t.label` 36 Pretendard 600, +0.02em, 1.2 (min 34) · `t.badge` 40 Pretendard 700, +0.02em, 1.0 (fixed) ← 48 px on a 1080-wide phone is ≈ 17 pt, the iOS body size, and still ≈ 10 pt in a 0.6× home-feed tile
- CDS-20 [o] characters per line inside 856 px: `t.hook` 9 · `t.title` 11 · `t.body` 14 · `t.caption` 18 (Korean); wrapping is by word (어절) and never beyond 2 lines
- CDS-21 [o] spacing tokens: `pad.box` 22 vertical / 32 horizontal · `pad.chip` 12 / 20 · `gap.stack` 16 vertical and 12 between chips · `radius.box` 16 · `radius.chip` 999 · `radius.card` 24 · `bar.accent` 8 px at the plate's left inner edge, full plate height, clipped to the plate radius · `stroke.text` 6 px round-joined stroke painted under the fill · `shadow.card` 0 8 32 α0.35 · `underline.mark` height 0.42em, raised 0.28em above the baseline, extended 6 px each side
- CDS-22 [o] the copy styles are five: 깔끔하게 (default narrative) · 메모 (fact) · 크게 강조 (emotion and hook) · 형광펜 (one number or keyword) · 가벼운 텍스트 (unplated narrative); 기록처럼 is renamed 메모 and 강조형 is replaced by 크게 강조
- CDS-23 [o] 깔끔하게: `ink.900` plate, `radius.box`, `bar.accent` in the project accent, `pad.box` (left 40 including the bar), `t.body` 56/700 white, ≤ 2 lines × 14 characters (26 total), accent on the bar only, default anchor BOTTOM/CENTER
- CDS-24 [o] 메모: `paper.50` plate, `radius.box`, no bar, one 14 px accent dot at the top left, padding 18 / 28 (left 54 with the dot), `t.caption` 44/600 `text.ink`, exactly 1 line ≤ 18 characters, default anchor TOP/LEFT (x 96) with BOTTOM/LEFT allowed; automatic selection over 18 characters may fall back to an approved 깔끔하게; an explicitly authored style is preserved for correction under CDS-64
- CDS-25 [o] 크게 강조: no plate; `text.white` with `stroke.dark` 6 px and `shadow.text`, `t.title` 72 in Paperlogy 8 (Pretendard 800 until Paperlogy is bundled), ≤ 2 lines × 11 characters, one word may take the accent colour on a dark ground, default anchor UPPER_MID/CENTER with LOWER_MID as the alternative, a scrim when CDS-44 finds a bright ground; at most 2 automatic selections per clip; explicit template styles are preserved under CDS-40 ← the former 8 px outline read as meme typography
- CDS-26 [o] 형광펜: no plate; `text.white` with a 4 px `stroke.dark` and `shadow.text`, `t.mark` 60 Pretendard 800, exactly 1 line ≤ 16 characters, the single keyword carries an accent α0.9 `underline.mark` highlight drawn behind the text, default anchor BOTTOM/CENTER, `scrim.bottom` under CDS-44; the highlight's x-offset comes from measured glyph advances ← the text stays white so contrast never depends on the accent
- CDS-27 [o] automatically timed cut-relative copy uses cut start +120 ms through cut end −120 ms with CDS-4 motion; explicitly authored intervals follow CLIP-66 and CLIP-67, and output-relative fixed text may span a cut transition
- CDS-28 [o] an optional template-declared hook card uses an ink.900s plate, radius.card, padding 40 and shadow.card over real footage, with default x 96–888 centred y 840; declared rows may use t.label 36 on an accent pill, t.hook 84 at most two lines of nine characters, and t.body 56 text.muted; every text part, supported position and visibility interval comes from the template, with the existing 200 ms fade-out only when the declared interval permits
- CDS-29 [o] an optional template-declared ending card uses an ink.900s plate over real footage with default x 96–888 centred y 1040; declared rows may use t.body 56/700, t.label 36 text.muted, t.caption 44 and accent t.label CTA; the existing 200 ms fade-in applies only when the authored interval permits, and no business, location, price, CTA phrase or end window is inserted by default
- CDS-30 [o] optional template-declared information chips use full-round ink.900 pills, t.label text.muted labels, t.caption white values and no icon, with default TOP/LEFT at (96, 80), gap.stack, at most two per header row and width ≤ 600 px; labels, item-bound values and visibility intervals come from the template rather than a reserved vocabulary
- CDS-31 [o] a disclosure-role element renders its template-authored literal or answer-bound phrase using t.badge 40 Pretendard 700, white on badge.ad, minimum padding 10 vertical / 18 horizontal and radius.chip, without motion or accent; default header placement is right edge x 984, top y 80, while the template may select another supported position and interval without a campaign-type mapping
- CDS-32 [o] scrims: `scrim.bottom` (0, 1040, 1080, 380) and `scrim.top` (0, 40, 1080, 310) appear only under an unplated style at that anchor when CDS-44 demands one, share the copy's in/out timing and never lie on a plate
- CDS-33 [x] a second progress bar, a product logo or watermark, automatic background music, stickers, emoji, decorative graphics, per-glyph animation, count-ups, and browser or Lottie export rendering — not adopted; interactive draft preview is allowed under CLIP-56 (→CLIP-28)
- CDS-34 [?] an optional creator-handle watermark (`t.label` 34 `text.muted` α0.6 at BOTTOM/LEFT 96, 1380, hidden when copy lands there) for re-posting to Reels and Shorts — third stage, owner's call
- CDS-35 [o] original audio is loudness-normalised to −16 LUFS with true peak −1.5 dBTP and range 11 LU, with 60 ms cross-fades at cut boundaries; per-cut volume applies before normalisation and the −6 dB hook dip applies only during an explicitly declared hook card interval
- CDS-36 [o] transitions: a hard cut is the default; a 200 ms fade only where the place or scene type changes and on at most 40 % of the boundaries, keeping the earliest; a 300 ms fade-through-black is optional and off by default around the hook and ending cards; every other transition is refused (→CLIP-16)
- CDS-37 [o] cut rhythm follows the template's authored guidance, using the current 1.2–6.0 s and food-close-up 4.0 s targets only when unspecified; observed footage, readable copy and the approved output duration outrank rhythm targets, and no category preset imposes a range
- CDS-38 [o] automatic anchor selection, first match wins among template-approved anchors: 메모 chooses between TOP/LEFT and BOTTOM/LEFT, every other style walks its default then alternative anchor; a candidate whose plate leaves the safe area is dropped; a candidate overlapping a placed chip, badge or card is dropped and copy always yields to the badge; with a principal-subject box (one box, detected in the order food → product → signboard → menu board → face) the highest-ranked candidate covering ≤ 15 % of it wins, else the least covering; without a box the default anchor; a cut whose footage carries readable text (signboard, menu board) allows only TOP or BOTTOM, whichever is farther from the subject ← typeset copy beside photographed text reads as compositing; consecutive cuts move at most one anchor step (BOTTOM ↔ LOWER_MID, never BOTTOM → TOP) except under the readable-text rule; an explicit template or owner position is preserved under CDS-64, and overlap remains advisory under CDS-56
- CDS-39 [o] sentence classes in priority order: `NUM` an Arabic number with a unit 원 분 인 % 개 시 배 · `HOOK` ends in ? or ! or contains 이유 비밀 진짜 솔직히 결론 이거 and is ≤ 11 characters · `FACT` noun-led place, time or menu ≤ 15 characters · `DESC` everything else; these classes style already-grounded text only and never require adding a number, hook phrase or fact
- CDS-40 [o] an explicitly authored style takes precedence; automatic selection follows this table using only template-approved styles: an approved 가벼운 텍스트 replaces the primary narrative choice without requiring decoration; otherwise scene × class → style: 음식 클로즈업, 매장 내부, 제품 디테일 → NUM 형광펜 · HOOK 크게 강조 · FACT 메모 · DESC 깔끔하게; 매장 외관·간판 → NUM 메모 · HOOK 크게 강조 · FACT 메모 · DESC 깔끔하게; 메뉴판·가격표 → NUM 메모 (TOP or BOTTOM only) · HOOK 깔끔하게 · FACT 메모 · DESC 깔끔하게; 사람·얼굴 → NUM 깔끔하게 · HOOK 크게 강조 · FACT 깔끔하게 · DESC 깔끔하게; 풍경·이동 → NUM 깔끔하게 · HOOK 크게 강조 · FACT 메모 · DESC 깔끔하게; guards for decorated sentence captions (rapid runs and 가벼운 텍스트 keep a consistent style): 크게 강조 ≤ 2 per clip and first only on cut 1 or 2, else 깔끔하게; 형광펜 only with exactly one number or keyword, else 메모; a fourth consecutive use of one style alternates between 깔끔하게 and 메모 only when the template approves both; a template without that alternative keeps its approved style without a frequency refusal; none of these choices rewrites text or inserts facts
- CDS-41 [o] automatically timed cut-relative sentence copy uses minimum 900 + 90 × Korean characters ms (excluding spaces and punctuation), maximum cut length −240 ms and 60–100 % cut occupancy; when it does not fit, use a meaning-preserving grounded shorter sentence, extend within the same observed scene, then omit generated copy with a recorded reason; fixed text, explicit styles and authored intervals remain unchanged for correction under CDS-64
- CDS-42 [o] voice, viewpoint and narrative progression follow the template; AI-written claims must be supported by the relevant observed scene and bound owner-authored facts, and first-person taste or experience cannot be inferred from appearance; exact fixed text is rendered as authored (→CLIP-63 →CLIP-64 →CLIP-65)
- CDS-43 [o] sentence-mode scene copy is optional, with up to two sequential generated sentences when the template permits and their reading windows fit; rapid phrases follow CDS-59 and explicitly timed output-level fixed text is independent of the per-cut copy count
- CDS-44 [o] brightness sampling exists only for 크게 강조 and 형광펜: the first, middle and last frame of the copy window are cropped to the plate region and averaged to Rec.709 relative luminance L and deviation σ; L ≥ 0.6 or σ ≥ 0.25 adds the scrim; an accent word turns white when L ≥ 0.6; a final pairing under 4.5:1 falls back to 깔끔하게
- CDS-45 [o] layering applies only to elements the template actually declares: disclosure role → card → information chip → scene copy → scrim; authored visibility is preserved, automatic placement avoids occlusion and overlaps remain visible for owner review under CDS-56
- CDS-46 [o] 16:9 and 1:1 keep every font size (the short side is 1080 in all three), except `t.hook` 84 → 72 on 16:9 and → 76 on 1:1, and restate only positions and widths
- CDS-47 [o] 16:9: copy width ≤ 960 centred (16 characters at 56 px), TOP plate top y 112, BOTTOM plate bottom y 968, UPPER_MID / LOWER_MID 420 / 660, hook card width 1120 centred y 540 with the chip and name on one row, ending card width 1120 centred y 540 in two columns (name · location | price · CTA), chips two across at TOP/LEFT, badge right edge x 1824 top y 112, scrims 260 bottom / 200 top ← a wider line slows reading and the frame has no vertical room for two-row cards
- CDS-48 [o] 1:1: copy width ≤ 952 (15 characters), TOP 112, BOTTOM 968, UPPER_MID / LOWER_MID 420 / 660, hook card width 880 centred y 540, ending card width 880 centred y 560, chips stacked, badge right edge x 1016 top y 112, scrims 260 / 200
- CDS-49 [o] 9:16 is the product default; choosing 16:9 at project creation says it is for PC upload or blog embedding and choosing 1:1 says it suits blog and Instagram feeds more than Clip (→CLIP-8) ← Naver Clip takes landscape and long-form only from the PC web and square support is unconfirmed
- CDS-50 [x] a video template carries one category preset (→CLIP-4) fixing hook tone, style mix, chip priority, default CTA, cut rhythm and default accent: 음식점 fact-led hook (menu · price · wait), 깔끔 50 / 형광펜 25 / 메모 20 / 크게 5, 위치 → 가격 → 메뉴, 위치는 프로필 정보 태그에서, cuts 2.5–4 s cut-led, coral · 카페 space-led hook, 깔끔 45 / 메모 30 / 크게 15 / 형광펜 10, 위치 → 메뉴 → 영업, 저장해두고 방문해보세요, cuts 4–6 s fade-led, minimal copy, teal · 숙소·여행 location-led hook, 깔끔 50 / 메모 35 / 크게 10 / 형광펜 5, 위치 → 가격 labelled 1박 기준 → 영업, 자세한 후기는 블로그에, cuts 4–6 s fade-led, blue · 뷰티 usage-led hook, 깔끔 40 / 형광펜 30 / 메모 20 / 크게 10, 가격 → 메뉴 → 평점, 자세한 후기는 블로그에, cuts 2–3 s cut-led, pink, efficacy claims refused · 생활용품·가전 problem-solution hook, 깔끔 40 / 형광펜 35 / 메모 20 / 크게 5, 가격 → 메뉴 → 평점, 자세한 후기는 블로그에, cuts 3–4 s cut-led, amber, spec numbers only from answers
- CDS-51 [o] templates reuse the product's visual components, readability rules and audio normalisation without inheriting content, campaign type, hook/end-card presence or exposure from a preset
- CDS-52 [o] the renderer emits a resolved-element manifest and checks V1 safe-area/header breach 0 px · V2 body ≥ 48 px, caption ≥ 40 px, badge = 40 px · V3 contrast ≥ 4.5:1 · V4 readable exposure within the declared interval · V5 supported line and character limits · V6 exact declared disclosure text and interval, or absence when undeclared · V7 advisory component overlap · V8 automatic subject-cover target ≤ 15 % · V9 sentence motion 180/120 ms and 12 px, rapid motion 0/0 ms and 0 px · V10 supported text/visual vocabulary and one original-audio track at most · V11 scene/item-bound facts and exact authored literals · V12 output 1080×1920 | 1920×1080 | 1080×1080, 30 fps, H.264 High, AAC 48 kHz when audio exists, −16 LUFS ±1 · V13 automatic anchor-step target ≤ 1 except the readable-text rule · V14 template-approved styles; automatic caption repair follows CDS-55 and authored-content failures follow CDS-64
- CDS-55 [o] automatic generated-copy repair tries another template-approved fitting style, then an allowed automatic anchor, then omits that generated copy with its reason recorded; explicit styles, positions, fixed text and fixed timing never enter this repair ladder (→CDS-64)
- CDS-53 [o] release QA retains real-device overlay measurement (→CDS-11) and muted full-clip review, and checks every visible caption against its scene, item facts and template role; several differently priced items, missing optional fields, uncertain identities and a template with no badge or cards must all remain coherent without invented or injected text (→CLIP-72)
- CDS-54 [x] delivery is staged: first the safe areas and anchors, the badge, the size and contrast floors, the four styles, the exposure rule, the chips, the selection rules (default anchors when no subject box exists) and the motions; second the hook and ending cards, Paperlogy, brightness sampling, audio normalisation, transitions and presets; third two copies per cut, the handle watermark and ratio-specific layout refinement ← the first stage alone meets the minimum an advertiser checks: nothing hidden by UI, a disclosure, legible, informative
- CDS-56 [o] overlap never prevents automatic generation or manual rerendering, including caption, chip, badge and card collisions and crossfade windows; preserve the selected text, placement and timing when overlap is the only issue, deliver the preview and download, and let the owner revise it in step ②; diagnostic overlap checks remain available, and all other delivery checks still run even when overlap is present ← a reviewable video is preferable to losing the whole result over text placement

- CDS-57 [o] when template-declared disclosure and information occupy the header during overlapping intervals, they share a top edge and row height with glyphs measured by weight/tracking and optically centred; equal outer margins are 9:16 x 96/984, 16:9 x 96/1824 and 1:1 x 64/1016, reserving the visible badge width plus one stack gap; only generated information values may shorten to fit, authored text follows CDS-64, and other selected positions inherit no hidden header reservation

- CDS-58 [o] 가벼운 텍스트 uses bundled Pretendard Variable 56/600 (minimum 48), +0.03em tracking, white fill with a 4 px dark outline and subtle text shadow, no plate/bar/dot/highlight, at most two lines of 14 characters, default BOTTOM/CENTER; outline contrast is checked against its worst-case white background without sampling every rapid phrase
- CDS-59 [o] rapid captions are independent of copy style, occupy explicit sequential cut-relative windows of 300–1000 ms and replace each other without a gap or motion; generation preserves a first short word as its own opening beat and groups following adjacent words into short phrases, prefers 300 ms for ≤3 characters, 500 ms for ≤9, 800 ms for longer phrases, and may compress within the 300 ms floor to fit the cut; at most 24 phrases per cut, one line of ≤14 characters per phrase, no sentence-class or 60% occupancy requirement; insufficient room uses the grounded short alternative or preserves sentence mode rather than losing the video
- CDS-60 [o] step ② exposes each phrase's text, start and end time, add/remove, and split-to-rapid or merge-to-sentence actions; manual windows persist exactly in milliseconds, adjacent windows may touch but may not overlap, and invalid limits are reported before rendering; older plans retain their sentence-mode behavior

- CDS-61 [o] the design system owns reusable typography, contrast, safe areas, approved visual styles and output quality, while the template owns what appears, its meaning, repetition, explicit position and timing (→CLIP-59 →CLIP-65)
- CDS-62 [o] all template intervals resolve onto the edited output timeline before layout and verification; a full-clip or end-relative fixed element keeps its declared scope across cuts, and cut-relative elements retain their selected cut association
- CDS-63 [o] a scene-bound name, price, quantity or unit must belong to the identified item and its source evidence; finding the same characters or digits elsewhere in project answers is insufficient, and ambiguous footage cannot borrow another item's fact
- CDS-64 [o] invalid authored text, fixed placement or timing identifies the affected element and preserves the owner's draft for correction; neither AI nor layout repair silently shortens, rewrites, moves or drops that authored element
- CDS-65 [o] editing-preview acceptance compares current-draft footage, crop, text, position and visibility intervals against final output for every supported ratio and caption pace; unsupported effect or audio parity is identified before treating the preview as final-output proof

## flow
- compose: template composition + grouped answers + observed cut evidence → validate scene/item grounding → resolve declared text, roles and output intervals → apply explicit styles/positions or approved automatic choices → sample contrast where needed → manifest → verify → render
- fallback: generated-copy problem → allowed automatic style/anchor or grounded shorter copy → omit generated copy with explanation; authored-content problem → identify element and preserve draft for correction; overlap only → preserve and preview

## constraints
- existing canvas, safe areas, bundled type scale, approved style assets and output encoding quality remain the visual baseline; category presets and their implicit content are retired, while existing compositions convert under CLIP-70
- constants, held in one configuration file the renderer and verifier both read: `canvas` 9:16 [1080, 1920] · 16:9 [1920, 1080] · 1:1 [1080, 1080]; `safe` per CDS-9 and CDS-13 (SA-N y 230 w 866 h 1210 recorded beside it); `anchor_9x16` TOP 80 UPPER_MID 700 LOWER_MID 1100 BOTTOM 1380 LEFT 96 CENTER 492 RIGHT 888; `type` hook 84 title 72 mark 60 body 56 caption 44 label 36 badge 40 body_min 48; `color` per CDS-14 and CDS-15; `motion` in_ms 180 in_dy 12 out_ms 120 ease [0.22, 1, 0.36, 1]; `timing` sub_min_base_ms 900 sub_min_per_char_ms 90 cut_min_s 1.2 cut_max_s 6.0 cut_max_food_s 4.0 authored hook/end/disclosure intervals under CLIP-66 (no mandatory duration constants); `transition` default cut fade_ms 200 black_ms 300 fade_ratio_max 0.4; `audio` loudnorm I=-16 TP=-1.5 LRA=11 crossfade_ms 60 hook_dip_db -6; `luma` scrim_threshold 0.6 sigma_threshold 0.25
- contrast is WCAG 2.1 relative-luminance contrast; a plate's effective colour is the plate composited at its α over the sampled frame mean
- character counts are Korean syllables excluding spaces and punctuation
- all final text is typeset from bundled fonts through the existing SVG rendering path (→CLIP-12 →CLIP-13); AI-drawn lettering is excluded
- the owner's correction step (→CLIP-17) edits visible elements against its draft preview and timeline; overlap alone is delivered unchanged, while other authored-content failures identify the element and never silently move it (→CDS-64)
- worked SVG plates for each style and component, the measurement method and the research sources behind the ← reasons: `docs/design/clip-output-design-spec-v1.md`
