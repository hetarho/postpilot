# CDS clip output design system
> r7 | The deterministic visual, typographic and timing rules every rendered clip obeys — safe areas, tokens, four copy styles, on-screen components, placement and style selection, ratio conversion, category presets and a render-time verifier — so a clip reads muted, uses explicit layout bounds, carries its disclosure and shows checkable information.

## decisions
- CDS-1 [o] every clip shows at least two of 상호 · 위치 · 가격 · 메뉴 as on-screen text ← advertisers and Naver's information tags reward reviews whose place, price and menu are verifiable and searchable
- CDS-2 [o] no copy, chip, card or badge leaves its ratio's safe area by any pixel
- CDS-3 [o] every on-screen text reads muted and small: body ≥ 48 px, caption and badge ≥ 40 px, contrast ≥ 4.5:1 against its effective background, exposure scaled to its length (→CDS-41)
- CDS-4 [o] the only motions are a 180 ms fade-in settling 12 px upward and a 120 ms fade-out; per-glyph pops, typewriter, count-up, rotation, bounce, wipe, slide, zoom and blur are refused ← a restrained tone reads as brand-safe and edited rather than templated
- CDS-5 [o] disclosure (광고·협찬 표기) is a mandatory badge, shown throughout the clip by default and at minimum during the first 3.0 s and the last 3.0 s
- CDS-6 [o] original audio stays; stickers, emoji, decorative graphics, product logos and any platform watermark are refused (→CLIP-18 →CLIP-28)
- CDS-7 [o] every placement and style choice follows the rule tables below with no randomness; equal inputs render equal outputs
- CDS-8 [o] the base canvas is 9:16 at 1080×1920 and every dimension below is stated on it; 16:9 and 1:1 derive by CDS-46 through CDS-48
- CDS-9 [o] the 9:16 design safe area is x 64, y 40, w 856, h 1380 (x ≤ 920, y ≤ 1420); the top inset is 40 px and TOP elements add a further 40 px gap; platform UI estimates remain separate references, not a verified cross-platform intersection
- CDS-10 [o] the estimated Naver overlay geometry behind SA-N — top bar y 0–230, right action column x ≥ 930 from y 1000, bottom information block y ≥ 1440, left edge 64 — lives in runtime constants `SAFE_TOP` `SAFE_BOTTOM` `SAFE_RIGHT` `SAFE_LEFT`, never in literals ← Naver publishes no Clip UI guide and the bottom block varies most (±60 px)
- CDS-11 [?] the real-device measurement of Naver Clip's overlay geometry, how 16:9 and 1:1 uploads display inside the vertical feed, and the height the information-tag chips occupy; platform estimates remain unverified and do not override the design inset in CDS-9
- CDS-12 [o] 9:16 anchors inside the design safe area: `TOP` plate top y 80 · `UPPER_MID` plate centre y 700 · `LOWER_MID` plate centre y 1100 · `BOTTOM` plate bottom y 1380 (default) · horizontal `CENTER` x 492 · `LEFT` x 96 · `RIGHT` x 888; a nine-cell grid is not used ← subtle offsets between cells break the consistency CDS-7 promises
- CDS-13 [o] the 16:9 safe area is (96, 72, 1728, 936) on 1920×1080 and the 1:1 safe area is (64, 72, 952, 936) on 1080×1080 ← neither ratio is expected to meet feed UI; the margins are broadcast title-safe practice plus a bottom player control bar
- CDS-14 [o] colour tokens: `ink.900` #111111 α0.72 dark plate · `ink.900s` #111111 α0.88 card · `paper.50` #FFFCF5 α0.94 light plate · `text.white` #FFFFFF · `text.ink` #1A1A1A · `text.muted` #FFFFFF α0.72 · `stroke.dark` #0B0B0B α0.85 · `shadow.text` #000000 α0.55 blur 12 offset (0, 4) · `scrim.bottom` #000000 α0 → 0.55 over y 1040–1420 · `scrim.top` #000000 α0.45 → 0 over y 40–350 · `badge.ad` #111111 α0.80 under white text
- CDS-15 [o] the accent palette stays the seven approved colours coral #FF6B57 · amber #FFB020 · lime #9BD53A · teal #2BB8A6 · blue #3D7BFF · violet #8A63FF · pink #FF6FB5, one per project (→CLIP-14); as a text colour an accent is allowed only on a dark plate or scrim, never on `paper.50` and never on the disclosure badge ← lime and amber fall under 3:1 on light ground, and a decorated badge stops reading as a disclosure
- CDS-16 [o] a plated element (깔끔하게 · 메모 · hook card · ending card · information chip) needs no brightness sampling ← α ≥ 0.72 ink under white text stays ≥ 5.9:1 even over a white frame
- CDS-17 [o] typography: Pretendard Variable is the primary face (copy, information; weights 500–800), Paperlogy 7 Bold / 8 ExtraBold the secondary face for hook titles and 크게 강조, Noto Sans KR 700 the fallback; all three are product-bundled at a fixed version (→CLIP-13) and any face whose licence forbids redistribution is excluded ← all three are SIL OFL and may ship on the server
- CDS-18 [o] the face is fixed per style; no per-project or per-user font choice (→CLIP-28)
- CDS-19 [o] type scale on 9:16: `t.hook` 84 Paperlogy 8, -0.02em, 1.15 (min 72) · `t.title` 72 Pretendard 800, -0.02em, 1.2 (min 64) · `t.mark` 60 Pretendard 800, -0.01em, 1.3 (min 52) · `t.body` 56 Pretendard 700, -0.01em, 1.3 (min 48) · `t.caption` 44 Pretendard 600, 0, 1.3 (min 40) · `t.label` 36 Pretendard 600, +0.02em, 1.2 (min 34) · `t.badge` 40 Pretendard 700, +0.02em, 1.0 (fixed) ← 48 px on a 1080-wide phone is ≈ 17 pt, the iOS body size, and still ≈ 10 pt in a 0.6× home-feed tile
- CDS-20 [o] characters per line inside 856 px: `t.hook` 9 · `t.title` 11 · `t.body` 14 · `t.caption` 18 (Korean); wrapping is by word (어절) and never beyond 2 lines
- CDS-21 [o] spacing tokens: `pad.box` 22 vertical / 32 horizontal · `pad.chip` 12 / 20 · `gap.stack` 16 vertical and 12 between chips · `radius.box` 16 · `radius.chip` 999 · `radius.card` 24 · `bar.accent` 8 px at the plate's left inner edge, full plate height, clipped to the plate radius · `stroke.text` 6 px round-joined stroke painted under the fill · `shadow.card` 0 8 32 α0.35 · `underline.mark` height 0.42em, raised 0.28em above the baseline, extended 6 px each side
- CDS-22 [o] the copy styles are four, one per role: 깔끔하게 (default narrative) · 메모 (fact) · 크게 강조 (emotion and hook) · 형광펜 (one number or keyword); 기록처럼 is renamed 메모 and 강조형 is replaced by 크게 강조 ← three styles lack an informational emphasis and five or more overlap in role
- CDS-23 [o] 깔끔하게: `ink.900` plate, `radius.box`, `bar.accent` in the project accent, `pad.box` (left 40 including the bar), `t.body` 56/700 white, ≤ 2 lines × 14 characters (26 total), accent on the bar only, default anchor BOTTOM/CENTER
- CDS-24 [o] 메모: `paper.50` plate, `radius.box`, no bar, one 14 px accent dot at the top left, padding 18 / 28 (left 54 with the dot), `t.caption` 44/600 `text.ink`, exactly 1 line ≤ 18 characters, default anchor TOP/LEFT (x 96) with BOTTOM/LEFT allowed; over 18 characters falls back to 깔끔하게
- CDS-25 [o] 크게 강조: no plate; `text.white` with `stroke.dark` 6 px and `shadow.text`, `t.title` 72 in Paperlogy 8 (Pretendard 800 until Paperlogy is bundled), ≤ 2 lines × 11 characters, one word may take the accent colour on a dark ground, default anchor UPPER_MID/CENTER with LOWER_MID as the alternative, a scrim when CDS-44 finds a bright ground; at most 2 uses per clip ← the former 8 px outline read as meme typography
- CDS-26 [o] 형광펜: no plate; `text.white` with a 4 px `stroke.dark` and `shadow.text`, `t.mark` 60 Pretendard 800, exactly 1 line ≤ 16 characters, the single keyword carries an accent α0.9 `underline.mark` highlight drawn behind the text, default anchor BOTTOM/CENTER, `scrim.bottom` under CDS-44; the highlight's x-offset comes from measured glyph advances ← the text stays white so contrast never depends on the accent
- CDS-27 [o] copy enters at cut start +120 ms with the CDS-4 fade-in eased `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` and leaves at cut end −120 ms with the fade-out and no movement ← text must not straddle a transition
- CDS-28 [o] hook title card: an `ink.900s` card (`radius.card`, padding 40, `shadow.card`) over the first cut's own footage for 0.0–1.5 s, present from frame 0 with a 200 ms fade-out, x 96–888 centred at y 840, stacking a category chip (`t.label` 36 on an accent α1.0 pill with #111 text), the hook sentence (`t.hook` 84, ≤ 2 lines × 9) and the business name (`t.body` 56 `text.muted`); the hook is AI-written under CDS-42 and the name and category are the owner's answers verbatim ← the first frame stays real footage for the thumbnail, and most viewers leave content that has not hooked them within 5 s
- CDS-29 [o] ending CTA card: an `ink.900s` card over the last cut's footage from 2.5 s before the end, 200 ms fade-in and no fade-out, x 96–888 centred at y 1040, stacking the business name (`t.body` 56/700), the location (`t.label` 36 `text.muted`), one price or signature line (`t.caption` 44, only when given) and one accent `t.label` CTA the owner picks from the fixed three 자세한 후기는 블로그에 · 위치는 프로필 정보 태그에서 · 저장해두고 방문해보세요 ← the clip-to-blog and clip-to-place link is where Naver's reward and the advertiser's conversion meet
- CDS-30 [o] information chips: full-round `ink.900` pills with a fixed-vocabulary label (`t.label` `text.muted`, one of 위치 가격 메뉴 영업 평점) and a value (`t.caption` white), no icon, stacked from TOP/LEFT (96, 80) with `gap.stack`, at most 2 at once and ≤ 600 px wide, shown for the whole cut where the fact occurs (≥ 2.0 s); 평점 appears only from the owner's own input
- CDS-31 [o] disclosure badge: the phrase is one of 광고 · 협찬 · 제품 제공 · 소정의 원고료 지급 · 내돈내산, fixed by the owner's chosen campaign type and not editable; `t.badge` 40 Pretendard 700 on `badge.ad`, minimum vertical padding 10 / horizontal padding 18 within the shared header row, `radius.chip`, right edge x 888, top y 80; no motion, no accent, no reduced opacity ← the Fair Trade Commission's endorsement guideline requires a size, face and colour clearly distinct from the background, shown at a video's start and end
- CDS-32 [o] scrims: `scrim.bottom` (0, 1040, 1080, 380) and `scrim.top` (0, 40, 1080, 310) appear only under an unplated style at that anchor when CDS-44 demands one, share the copy's in/out timing and never lie on a plate
- CDS-33 [x] a second progress bar, a product logo or watermark, automatic background music, stickers, emoji, decorative graphics, per-glyph animation, count-ups, and a browser or Lottie renderer — not adopted (→CLIP-28)
- CDS-34 [?] an optional creator-handle watermark (`t.label` 34 `text.muted` α0.6 at BOTTOM/LEFT 96, 1380, hidden when copy lands there) for re-posting to Reels and Shorts — third stage, owner's call
- CDS-35 [o] audio: the whole track is two-pass loudness-normalised to −16 LUFS (true peak −1.5 dBTP, range 11 LU), cut boundaries cross-fade 60 ms, and the original audio dips −6 dB under the hook card; per-cut volume under CLIP-18 applies before normalisation ← phone footage varies in level cut to cut and a volume jump reads as unedited
- CDS-36 [o] transitions: a hard cut is the default; a 200 ms fade only where the place or scene type changes and on at most 40 % of the boundaries, keeping the earliest; a 300 ms fade-through-black is optional and off by default around the hook and ending cards; every other transition is refused (→CLIP-16)
- CDS-37 [o] cut length TARGETS 1.2–6.0 s, a food close-up 4.0 s, and the template's preset's own range where it names one (→CDS-50): the compiler holds a cut inside its target wherever the approved timeline still allows it and never refuses a plan for missing one ← these are editing rhythm, not correctness, and a clip that runs a second long is worth more than a generation that fails; a copy's minimum exposure may lengthen a cut past any of them (→CDS-41)
- CDS-38 [o] anchor selection, first match wins: 메모 chooses between TOP/LEFT and BOTTOM/LEFT, every other style walks its default then alternative anchor; a candidate whose plate leaves the safe area is dropped; a candidate overlapping a placed chip, badge or card is dropped and copy always yields to the badge; with a principal-subject box (one box, detected in the order food → product → signboard → menu board → face) the highest-ranked candidate covering ≤ 15 % of it wins, else the least covering; without a box the default anchor; a cut whose footage carries readable text (signboard, menu board) allows only TOP or BOTTOM, whichever is farther from the subject ← typeset copy beside photographed text reads as compositing; consecutive cuts move at most one anchor step (BOTTOM ↔ LOWER_MID, never BOTTOM → TOP) except under the readable-text rule
- CDS-39 [o] sentence classes in priority order: `NUM` an Arabic number with a unit 원 분 인 % 개 시 배 · `HOOK` ends in ? or ! or contains 이유 비밀 진짜 솔직히 결론 이거 and is ≤ 11 characters · `FACT` noun-led place, time or menu ≤ 15 characters · `DESC` everything else
- CDS-40 [o] scene × class → style: 음식 클로즈업, 매장 내부, 제품 디테일 → NUM 형광펜 · HOOK 크게 강조 · FACT 메모 · DESC 깔끔하게; 매장 외관·간판 → NUM 메모 · HOOK 크게 강조 · FACT 메모 · DESC 깔끔하게; 메뉴판·가격표 → NUM 메모 (TOP or BOTTOM only) · HOOK 깔끔하게 · FACT 메모 · DESC 깔끔하게; 사람·얼굴 → NUM 깔끔하게 · HOOK 크게 강조 · FACT 깔끔하게 · DESC 깔끔하게; 풍경·이동 → NUM 깔끔하게 · HOOK 크게 강조 · FACT 메모 · DESC 깔끔하게; guards: 크게 강조 ≤ 2 per clip and first only on cut 1 or 2, else 깔끔하게; 형광펜 only with exactly one number or keyword, else 메모; a fourth consecutive use of one style alternates between 깔끔하게 and 메모 only when the template approves both; a template without that alternative keeps its approved style without a frequency refusal
- CDS-41 [o] exposure: minimum `900 + 90 × Korean characters` ms (spaces and punctuation excluded), maximum the cut length −240 ms, and one copy occupies 60–100 % of its cut; when the minimum does not fit: shorten the sentence → extend the cut to minimum +240 ms within the source → drop the copy, in that order; a sentence over its style's limit is regenerated at most twice, then cut to two 깔끔하게 lines
- CDS-42 [o] copy voice: first person and experiential; emoji, ㅋㅋ, ㄹㅇ and superlatives (최고 역대급) are refused; every number and proper noun (상호 · 메뉴 · 가격 · 인원 · 시간) must exist in the owner's answers and a sentence inventing one is regenerated ← a wrong price or name loses the advertiser at once, and superlatives are an advertising-review risk
- CDS-43 [o] a cut carries 1 copy; a cut ≥ 4.0 s whose sentence splits naturally into a `DESC` and a `NUM` may carry 2 in sequence 120 ms apart, never together; chips and the badge do not count
- CDS-44 [o] brightness sampling exists only for 크게 강조 and 형광펜: the first, middle and last frame of the copy window are cropped to the plate region and averaged to Rec.709 relative luminance L and deviation σ; L ≥ 0.6 or σ ≥ 0.25 adds the scrim; an accent word turns white when L ≥ 0.6; a final pairing under 4.5:1 falls back to 깔끔하게
- CDS-45 [o] layering priority: disclosure badge (fixed) → hook or ending card in its window → information chips → copy → scrim; no copy or chip shows under the hook card and no copy under the ending card
- CDS-46 [o] 16:9 and 1:1 keep every font size (the short side is 1080 in all three), except `t.hook` 84 → 72 on 16:9 and → 76 on 1:1, and restate only positions and widths
- CDS-47 [o] 16:9: copy width ≤ 960 centred (16 characters at 56 px), TOP plate top y 112, BOTTOM plate bottom y 968, UPPER_MID / LOWER_MID 420 / 660, hook card width 1120 centred y 540 with the chip and name on one row, ending card width 1120 centred y 540 in two columns (name · location | price · CTA), chips two across at TOP/LEFT, badge right edge x 1824 top y 112, scrims 260 bottom / 200 top ← a wider line slows reading and the frame has no vertical room for two-row cards
- CDS-48 [o] 1:1: copy width ≤ 952 (15 characters), TOP 112, BOTTOM 968, UPPER_MID / LOWER_MID 420 / 660, hook card width 880 centred y 540, ending card width 880 centred y 560, chips stacked, badge right edge x 1016 top y 112, scrims 260 / 200
- CDS-49 [o] 9:16 is the product default; choosing 16:9 at project creation says it is for PC upload or blog embedding and choosing 1:1 says it suits blog and Instagram feeds more than Clip (→CLIP-8) ← Naver Clip takes landscape and long-form only from the PC web and square support is unconfirmed
- CDS-50 [o] a video template carries one category preset (→CLIP-4) fixing hook tone, style mix, chip priority, default CTA, cut rhythm and default accent: 음식점 fact-led hook (menu · price · wait), 깔끔 50 / 형광펜 25 / 메모 20 / 크게 5, 위치 → 가격 → 메뉴, 위치는 프로필 정보 태그에서, cuts 2.5–4 s cut-led, coral · 카페 space-led hook, 깔끔 45 / 메모 30 / 크게 15 / 형광펜 10, 위치 → 메뉴 → 영업, 저장해두고 방문해보세요, cuts 4–6 s fade-led, minimal copy, teal · 숙소·여행 location-led hook, 깔끔 50 / 메모 35 / 크게 10 / 형광펜 5, 위치 → 가격 labelled 1박 기준 → 영업, 자세한 후기는 블로그에, cuts 4–6 s fade-led, blue · 뷰티 usage-led hook, 깔끔 40 / 형광펜 30 / 메모 20 / 크게 10, 가격 → 메뉴 → 평점, 자세한 후기는 블로그에, cuts 2–3 s cut-led, pink, efficacy claims refused · 생활용품·가전 problem-solution hook, 깔끔 40 / 형광펜 35 / 메모 20 / 크게 5, 가격 → 메뉴 → 평점, 자세한 후기는 블로그에, cuts 3–4 s cut-led, amber, spec numbers only from answers
- CDS-51 [o] every preset shares the always-on badge, the 1.5 s hook card, the 2.5 s ending card and audio normalisation; preset values live in configuration, not code
- CDS-52 [o] the renderer emits a manifest of every text, plate, chip, card and badge (bbox, timing, font size, colours) and a verifier gates the render: V1 safe-area breach 0 px · V2 body ≥ 48, caption ≥ 40, badge = 40 · V3 contrast ≥ 4.5:1 (three-frame sample for unplated styles) · V4 exposure ≥ minimum and ≥ 60 % of the cut · V5 line and character limits · V6 badge phrase present in 0–3.0 s and the last 3.0 s at 40 px on #111 α0.80 · V7 zero overlap between components as a placement target, never a delivery gate (→CDS-56) · V8 subject cover ≤ 15 % · V9 motion only 180 / 120 ms and 12 px · V10 no emoji, sticker, logo, progress bar or second audio track · V11 every number and proper noun in the owner's answers · V12 output 1080×1920 | 1920×1080 | 1080×1080, 30 fps, H.264 High, AAC 48 kHz, −16 LUFS ±1 · V13 anchor step ≤ 1 · V14 style frequency (CDS-40); overlap is advisory under CDS-56; every other failing check is repaired — a check a CAPTION caused walks the ladder of CDS-55 and the render fails only when the ladder is exhausted, while a check the design system's own furniture caused (the badge, a card, a chip) is a defect in the renderer and fails at once
- CDS-55 [o] caption repair ladder for blocking checks other than overlap (→CDS-56), applied in order until the manifest verifies: the style falls back to 깔끔하게 (whose plate answers V2, V3 and V5 by itself) → the anchor falls back to the style's own default → the copy is dropped and the cut shows its footage; each step is recorded on the cut's composition so the correction step can say what happened and the owner can put it back ← every one of these produces a clip, and a clip with one caption fewer is worth more than no clip
- CDS-53 [o] human QA before release: play on the real Naver app clip tab and measure the overlays (→CDS-11); watch muted end to end and recall two of 상호 · 위치 · 가격; know the subject within 1.5 s; copy reads as editor-placed text, not a composite; tone unchanged throughout; the disclosure is legible
- CDS-54 [o] delivery is staged: first the safe areas and anchors, the badge, the size and contrast floors, the four styles, the exposure rule, the chips, the selection rules (default anchors when no subject box exists) and the motions; second the hook and ending cards, Paperlogy, brightness sampling, audio normalisation, transitions and presets; third two copies per cut, the handle watermark and ratio-specific layout refinement ← the first stage alone meets the minimum an advertiser checks: nothing hidden by UI, a disclosure, legible, informative
- CDS-56 [o] overlap never prevents automatic generation or manual rerendering, including caption, chip, badge and card collisions and crossfade windows; preserve the selected text, placement and timing when overlap is the only issue, deliver the preview and download, and let the owner revise it in step ②; diagnostic overlap checks remain available, and all other delivery checks still run even when overlap is present ← a reviewable video is preferable to losing the whole result over text placement

- CDS-57 [o] disclosure and the first information row share a top edge and row height; label/value glyphs are measured with their own font weight/tracking and optically centred within the row; information reserves the disclosure width and one stack gap, truncating its value before it can collide with the badge; vertical cards and centred copy share the midpoint of the LEFT/RIGHT anchors

## flow
- compose: owner answers (상호 · 위치 · 가격 · 메뉴 · 카테고리 · 체험단 유형 · 강조색) + cut metadata (scene type, subject box, readable text) + preset → badge placed → hook card 0–1.5 s → per cut: classify sentence (CDS-39) → style (CDS-40) → anchor (CDS-38) → exposure (CDS-41) → brightness sample for unplated styles (CDS-44) → scrim → ending card last 2.5 s → manifest → verifier (CDS-52: pass | reapply fallbacks)
- fallback: 형광펜 with two keywords → 메모; 메모 over 18 characters → 깔끔하게; 크게 강조 third use → 깔끔하게; contrast under 4.5:1 → 깔끔하게; exposure does not fit → shorten → extend cut → drop copy

## constraints
- supersedes the current renderer: 9:16 safe region (86, 192, 778, 1308) → (64, 40, 856, 1380) · 16:9 (154, 108, 1574, 756) → (96, 72, 1728, 936) · 1:1 (86, 108, 778, 756) → (64, 72, 952, 936); the positions `top` `center` `bottom` → the CDS-12 anchors; the style ids `clean` `diary` `emphasis` → the four CDS-22 styles; new ids and migration of stored plans are create-task decisions
- constants, held in one configuration file the renderer and verifier both read: `canvas` 9:16 [1080, 1920] · 16:9 [1920, 1080] · 1:1 [1080, 1080]; `safe` per CDS-9 and CDS-13 (SA-N y 230 w 866 h 1210 recorded beside it); `anchor_9x16` TOP 80 UPPER_MID 700 LOWER_MID 1100 BOTTOM 1380 LEFT 96 CENTER 492 RIGHT 888; `type` hook 84 title 72 mark 60 body 56 caption 44 label 36 badge 40 body_min 48; `color` per CDS-14 and CDS-15; `motion` in_ms 180 in_dy 12 out_ms 120 ease [0.22, 1, 0.36, 1]; `timing` sub_min_base_ms 900 sub_min_per_char_ms 90 cut_min_s 1.2 cut_max_s 6.0 cut_max_food_s 4.0 hook_card_s 1.5 end_card_s 2.5 badge_min_head_s 3.0 badge_min_tail_s 3.0; `transition` default cut fade_ms 200 black_ms 300 fade_ratio_max 0.4; `audio` loudnorm I=-16 TP=-1.5 LRA=11 crossfade_ms 60 hook_dip_db -6; `luma` scrim_threshold 0.6 sigma_threshold 0.25
- contrast is WCAG 2.1 relative-luminance contrast; a plate's effective colour is the plate composited at its α over the sampled frame mean
- character counts are Korean syllables excluding spaces and punctuation
- all text is typeset from the bundled fonts through the SVG renderer (→CLIP-12 →CLIP-13); no rasterised lettering
- the owner's correction step (→CLIP-17) edits copy text, anchor and style within these rules; overlap alone is delivered unchanged under CDS-56, while a manual choice failing another check is refused with the check named, never silently moved
- worked SVG plates for each style and component, the measurement method and the research sources behind the ← reasons: `docs/design/clip-output-design-spec-v1.md`
