# CDS clip output design system
> r19 | Reusable visual, transformed-time and owner-authorized source-audio quality rules for template-authored clip content, with scene-grounded text, preview verification and deterministic final output.

## decisions
- CDS-1 [o] on-screen information is authored by the selected template, with no compulsory business, location, price, menu or minimum fact count; visual legibility and scene-to-fact correctness remain delivery requirements (→CLIP-60 →CLIP-63 →CLIP-68)
- CDS-2 [o] every text element stays inside its ratio's safe area, except an explicitly header-placed badge which uses the separate symmetric header bounds (→CDS-57)
- CDS-3 [o] every on-screen text reads muted and small: body ≥ 48 px, caption ≥ 40 px, label ≥ 34 px, badge fixed at 36 px, contrast ≥ 4.5:1 against its effective background, exposure scaled to its length (→CDS-41)
- CDS-4 [o] sentence captions use a 180 ms fade-in settling 12 px upward and a 120 ms fade-out; rapid phrases replace one another immediately with no fade or movement; per-glyph pops, typewriter, count-up, rotation, bounce, wipe, slide, zoom and blur are refused ← a restrained tone reads as brand-safe and edited rather than templated
- CDS-5 [o] disclosure appears only as a template-declared element for its resolved visibility interval; neither a campaign type nor a product default inserts a badge or requires whole-clip exposure (→CLIP-46 →CLIP-65)
- CDS-6 [o] source audio is absent by default and included only in cuts derived from a source whose owner-controlled retention setting is on; templates, models and rendering never enable that setting, while in-product background music, stickers, emoji, decorative graphics, product logos and any platform watermark are refused (→CLIP-18 →CLIP-28)
- CDS-7 [o] every placement and style choice follows the rule tables below with no randomness; equal inputs render equal outputs
- CDS-8 [o] the base canvas is 9:16 at 1080×1920 and every dimension below is stated on it; 16:9 and 1:1 derive by CDS-46 through CDS-48
- CDS-9 [o] the 9:16 design safe area is x 64, y 40, w 952, h 1380 (x ≤ 1016, y ≤ 1420) with equal left and right insets; the top inset is 40 px and TOP elements add a further 40 px gap; centred copy keeps a 856 px measure so line length stays at CDS-20 ← reading width is a typographic choice, not an avoidance of anything on screen
- CDS-10 [x] the SA-N platform overlay estimate and its runtime constants — retired with the platform-specific inset (→CDS-78)
- CDS-11 [x] real-device measurement of one platform's overlay geometry — retired with SA-N (→CDS-78)
- CDS-12 [o] 9:16 anchors inside the design safe area: `TOP` plate top y 80 · `UPPER_MID` plate centre y 700 · `LOWER_MID` plate centre y 1100 · `BOTTOM` plate bottom y 1380 (default) · horizontal `CENTER` x 540 · `LEFT` x 96 · `RIGHT` x 984; a nine-cell grid is not used ← subtle offsets between cells break the consistency CDS-7 promises
- CDS-13 [o] the 16:9 safe area is (96, 72, 1728, 936) on 1920×1080 and the 1:1 safe area is (64, 72, 952, 936) on 1080×1080 ← the margins are broadcast title-safe practice plus a bottom player control bar
- CDS-14 [o] colour tokens: `text.white` #FFFFFF · `text.muted` #FFFFFF α0.72 · `stroke.dark` #0B0B0B α0.85 · `shadow.text` #000000 α0.55 blur 12 offset (0, 4) · `scrim.bottom` #000000 α0 → 0.55 over y 1040–1420 · `scrim.top` #000000 α0.45 → 0 over y 40–350 · `badge.ad` #111111 α0.80 under white text
- CDS-15 [o] the accent palette stays the seven approved colours coral #FF6B57 · amber #FFB020 · lime #9BD53A · teal #2BB8A6 · blue #3D7BFF · violet #8A63FF · pink #FF6FB5, one per project (→CLIP-14); an accent appears only as the caption's accent word on a dark ground (→CDS-25) — never in the intro or outro, on the information pair or on the disclosure — and every rule and bar is white ← saturation belongs to the footage, so one coloured word is a clip's whole accent budget
- CDS-16 [x] plated-element sampling exemption — retired with the plates (→CDS-44)
- CDS-17 [o] typography: Pretendard Variable is the primary face (copy, information; weights 500–800), Paperlogy the secondary face for the caption and both region blocks, Noto Sans KR 700 the fallback; all three are product-bundled at a fixed version (→CLIP-13) and any face whose licence forbids redistribution is excluded ← all three are SIL OFL and may ship on the server
- CDS-18 [o] the face is fixed per region preset and type role; no per-project or per-user font choice (→CLIP-28)
- CDS-19 [o] type scale on 9:16: `t.display` 132 Paperlogy 800, +0.04em, 1.0 (fixed) · `t.headline` 96 Paperlogy 800, -0.02em, 1.1 (fixed) · `t.hook` 84 Paperlogy 800, -0.02em, 1.15 (min 72) · `t.title` 72 Paperlogy 800, -0.02em, 1.2 (min 64) · `t.body` 56 Pretendard 700, -0.01em, 1.3 (min 48) · `t.caption` 44 Pretendard 600, 0, 1.3 (min 40) · `t.label` 36 Pretendard 600, +0.02em, 1.2 (min 34) · `t.badge` 36 Pretendard 800, +0.02em, 1.0 (fixed) ← 48 px on a 1080-wide phone is ≈ 17 pt, the iOS body size, and still ≈ 10 pt in a 0.6× home-feed tile
- CDS-20 [o] characters per line inside 856 px: `t.display` 6 · `t.headline` 8 · `t.hook` 9 · `t.title` 11 · `t.body` 14 · `t.caption` 18 · `t.label` 22 (Korean); a caption wraps by word (어절) and never beyond 2 lines, a region slot is one line (→CDS-77)
- CDS-21 [o] spacing tokens: `pad.chip` 16 vertical / 28 horizontal · `gap.stack` 16 vertical · `radius.chip` 12 (disclosure only) · `stroke.text` 6 px round-joined stroke painted under a Paperlogy fill · `stroke.small` 4 px under a Pretendard fill · `rule.hair` 520 × 2 px `text.white` α0.55 · `rule.bar` 160 × 6 px `text.white`
- CDS-22 [o] on-screen text sits in three regions — intro, caption, outro; the intro and the outro each offer two presets and the caption one treatment, chosen once per template (→CLIP-111), and there is no per-element style choice ← unrestricted style combination produced more unusable frames than usable ones

- CDS-26 [x] 형광펜 keyword-highlight style — retired; its highlight also mismeasured a keyword containing a comma
- CDS-27 [o] automatically timed cut-relative copy uses the cut's transformed output start +120 ms through its transformed output end −120 ms with CDS-4 motion; explicitly authored intervals follow CLIP-66 and CLIP-67, and output-relative fixed text may span a cut transition
- CDS-28 [x] the hook card — retired with every plated card
- CDS-29 [x] the ending card — retired with every plated card
- CDS-30 [o] an information pair renders unplated as a small dimmed label above a crisp value — `t.label` 36 `text.muted` +0.08em over `t.caption` 44 white, both with `stroke.dark` 4 px and `shadow.text`, CENTER column; labels, item-bound values and visibility intervals come from the template rather than a reserved vocabulary
- CDS-31 [o] the disclosure is a rectangle with `radius.chip` corners, `pad.chip` 16 vertical / 28 horizontal, white on `badge.ad`, and text smaller and heavier than the other roles at `t.badge` 36/800; it carries no motion or accent and the template may select its position and interval ← a disclosure has to read as an imposed marker rather than as part of the design
- CDS-32 [o] scrims: `scrim.bottom` (0, 1040, 1080, 380) and `scrim.top` (0, 40, 1080, 310) appear only under an unplated style at that anchor when CDS-44 demands one, share the copy's in/out timing and never lie on a plate
- CDS-33 [x] a second progress bar, a product logo or watermark, any in-product background-music generation, selection or mixing, stickers, emoji, decorative graphics, per-glyph animation, count-ups, and browser or Lottie export rendering — not adopted; post-download soundtrack addition is outside product scope and interactive draft preview is allowed under CLIP-56 (→CLIP-28)
- CDS-34 [?] an optional creator-handle watermark (`t.label` 34 `text.muted` α0.6 at BOTTOM/LEFT 96, 1380, hidden when copy lands there) for re-posting to Reels and Shorts — third stage, owner's call
- CDS-35 [o] retained source audio follows its cut's fixed playback rate with pitch preserved, applies per-cut volume, uses 60 ms fades at audible cut boundaries and is then loudness-normalised to −16 LUFS with true peak −1.5 dBTP and range 11 LU; a source whose retention setting is off contributes no audio, a clip with no enabled source has no audio track, and the −6 dB hook dip applies only during the intro block's declared interval
- CDS-36 [o] transitions: a hard cut is the default; a 200 ms fade only where the place or scene type changes and on at most 40 % of the boundaries, keeping the earliest; a 300 ms fade-through-black is optional and off by default around the intro and outro blocks; every other transition is refused (→CLIP-16)
- CDS-37 [o] cut rhythm follows the template's authored guidance on the transformed output timeline, using the current 1.2–6.0 s and food-close-up 4.0 s output-duration targets only when unspecified; observed footage, readable copy and the approved total output duration outrank rhythm targets, and no category preset imposes a range
- CDS-38 [o] automatic anchor selection for the caption walks its default then alternative anchor; a candidate whose plate leaves the safe area is dropped; a candidate overlapping the disclosure or a region block is dropped and the caption always yields to them; a candidate lying outside every observed caption-safe region (→CLIP-105) ranks below one inside it; with a principal-subject box (one box, detected in the order food → product → signboard → menu board → face) the highest-ranked candidate covering ≤ 15 % of it wins, else the least covering; without a box the default anchor; a cut whose footage carries readable text (signboard, menu board) allows only TOP or BOTTOM, whichever is farther from the subject ← typeset copy beside photographed text reads as compositing; consecutive cuts move at most one anchor step (BOTTOM ↔ LOWER_MID, never BOTTOM → TOP) except under the readable-text rule; an explicit template or owner position is preserved under CDS-64, and overlap remains advisory under CDS-56
- CDS-39 [x] sentence classes NUM/HOOK/FACT/DESC — retired with the style table they fed
- CDS-40 [x] the scene × class → style selection table — retired with the multiple styles it chose between
- CDS-41 [o] automatically timed cut-relative sentence copy uses minimum 900 + 90 × Korean characters ms (excluding spaces and punctuation), maximum transformed output cut length −240 ms and 60–100 % transformed cut occupancy; when it does not fit, use a meaning-preserving grounded shorter sentence, extend within the same observed scene, then omit generated copy with a recorded reason; fixed text, explicit styles and authored intervals remain unchanged for correction under CDS-64
- CDS-42 [o] voice, viewpoint and narrative progression follow the template; AI-written claims must be supported by the relevant observed scene and bound owner-authored facts, and first-person taste or experience cannot be inferred from appearance; exact fixed text is rendered as authored (→CLIP-63 →CLIP-64 →CLIP-65)
- CDS-43 [o] scene copy is optional, with up to two generated sentences per cut when the template permits; two sentences sharing a cut are always sequenced into disjoint reading windows at every caption pace, and a second sentence whose window cannot fit is omitted with its recorded reason; rapid phrases follow CDS-59 and explicitly timed output-level fixed text is independent of the per-cut copy count
- CDS-44 [o] brightness sampling covers every unplated text — the caption, both region blocks and an information pair: the first, middle and last frame of the copy window are cropped to the text's bounds and averaged to Rec.709 relative luminance L and deviation σ; L ≥ 0.6 or σ ≥ 0.25 adds the scrim; an accent word turns white when L ≥ 0.6; stroked text measures contrast against `stroke.dark` composited over the sampled mean, and a pairing still under 4.5:1 after the scrim is delivered with a CLIP-108 notice on that element instead of refused ← the regions have no plate left to fall back to, and a reviewable clip beats a refusal over one bright frame
- CDS-45 [o] layering applies only to elements the template actually declares: disclosure → region block → information pair → caption → scrim; authored visibility is preserved, automatic placement avoids occlusion and overlaps remain visible for owner review under CDS-56
- CDS-46 [o] 16:9 and 1:1 keep every font size (the short side is 1080 in all three), except `t.hook` 84 → 72 on 16:9 and → 76 on 1:1, and restate only positions and widths
- CDS-47 [o] 16:9: copy width ≤ 960 centred (16 characters at 56 px), TOP plate top y 112, BOTTOM plate bottom y 968, UPPER_MID / LOWER_MID 420 / 660, the intro and outro blocks placed by CDS-79, badge right edge x 1824 top y 112, scrims 260 bottom / 200 top ← a wider line slows reading and the frame has no vertical room for two-row cards
- CDS-48 [o] 1:1: copy width ≤ 952 (15 characters), TOP 112, BOTTOM 968, UPPER_MID / LOWER_MID 420 / 660, the intro and outro blocks placed by CDS-79, badge right edge x 1016 top y 112, scrims 260 / 200
- CDS-49 [o] 9:16 is the product default because every short-form feed takes it; 16:9 suits horizontal upload and blog or site embedding, and 1:1 suits square feed placements (→CLIP-8)
- CDS-50 [x] a video template carries one category preset (→CLIP-4) fixing hook tone, style mix, chip priority, default CTA, cut rhythm and default accent: 음식점 fact-led hook (menu · price · wait), 깔끔 50 / 형광펜 25 / 메모 20 / 크게 5, 위치 → 가격 → 메뉴, 위치는 프로필 정보 태그에서, cuts 2.5–4 s cut-led, coral · 카페 space-led hook, 깔끔 45 / 메모 30 / 크게 15 / 형광펜 10, 위치 → 메뉴 → 영업, 저장해두고 방문해보세요, cuts 4–6 s fade-led, minimal copy, teal · 숙소·여행 location-led hook, 깔끔 50 / 메모 35 / 크게 10 / 형광펜 5, 위치 → 가격 labelled 1박 기준 → 영업, 자세한 후기는 블로그에, cuts 4–6 s fade-led, blue · 뷰티 usage-led hook, 깔끔 40 / 형광펜 30 / 메모 20 / 크게 10, 가격 → 메뉴 → 평점, 자세한 후기는 블로그에, cuts 2–3 s cut-led, pink, efficacy claims refused · 생활용품·가전 problem-solution hook, 깔끔 40 / 형광펜 35 / 메모 20 / 크게 5, 가격 → 메뉴 → 평점, 자세한 후기는 블로그에, cuts 3–4 s cut-led, amber, spec numbers only from answers
- CDS-51 [o] templates reuse the product's visual components, readability rules and audio normalisation without inheriting content, campaign type, hook/end-card presence or exposure from a preset
- CDS-52 [o] the renderer emits a resolved-element manifest and checks V1 safe-area/header breach 0 px · V2 body ≥ 48 px, caption ≥ 40 px, label ≥ 34 px, badge = 36 px · V3 contrast ≥ 4.5:1 measured under CDS-44, a scrimmed shortfall recorded as a notice rather than a failure · V4 readable exposure within the declared interval · V5 supported line and character limits · V6 exact declared disclosure text and interval, or absence when undeclared · V7 advisory component overlap · V8 automatic subject-cover target ≤ 15 % · V9 sentence motion 180/120 ms and 12 px, rapid motion 0/0 ms and 0 px · V10 supported text/visual vocabulary, source audio absent unless its source setting is on, one simultaneous source-audio track at most and no background music · V11 scene/item-bound facts and exact authored literals · V12 output 1080×1920 | 1920×1080 | 1080×1080, 30 fps, H.264 High, AAC 48 kHz when audio exists, −16 LUFS ±1 and a decoded video-track duration within one output frame of the edited output timeline (→CDS-62), read from the video track alone because a container or audio-track declaration carries codec padding and always reads longer than the clip plays · V13 automatic anchor-step target ≤ 1 except the readable-text rule · V15 every cut uses one CLIP-98 rate and its source span, rate and pre-transition output duration agree · V16 every caption and element interval lies on the transformed output timeline · V17 preview and export agree on source ranges, cut order, timing, rates and source-audio state · V19 every text renders in the face its type role names, a substituted family failing the check rather than rendering · V20 the intro and outro render the template's selected presets — slot count, order, geometry and white-only colour · V18 two captions sharing a cut hold disjoint windows at every caption pace; a failed V15–V17 check identifies the cut or element and prevents rendering without substituting 1x, changing audio state, dropping a cut or retiming authored content

- CDS-57 [o] when template-declared disclosure and information occupy the header during overlapping intervals, they share a top edge and row height with glyphs measured by weight/tracking and optically centred; equal outer margins are 9:16 x 96/984, 16:9 x 96/1824 and 1:1 x 64/1016, reserving the visible badge width plus one stack gap; only generated information values may shorten to fit, authored text follows CDS-64, and other selected positions inherit no hidden header reservation

- CDS-61 [o] the design system owns reusable typography, contrast, safe areas, approved visual styles and output quality, while the template owns what appears, its meaning, repetition, explicit position and timing (→CLIP-59 →CLIP-65)

## flow
- compose: template composition + grouped answers + observed source evidence + owner source-audio settings → select, split, trim and order source ranges with one fixed rate per cut → map source time and transitions onto the edited output timeline → validate scene/item grounding and assembly contract → resolve declared text, regions and caption/output intervals → apply each region's selected preset and the caption's automatic anchor → sample contrast where needed → manifest → verify → render
- fallback: invalid assembly, transformed timing or audio contract → identify the exact cut or element and preserve the draft without substituting a rate, changing source-audio state, dropping a cut or silently retiming; generated-copy problem → a grounded shorter sentence or an allowed automatic anchor → omit generated copy with explanation; authored-content problem → identify element and preserve draft for correction; overlap only → preserve and preview

## constraints
- existing canvas, safe areas, bundled type scale and output encoding quality remain the visual baseline; category presets and their implicit content are retired, while existing compositions convert under CLIP-70
- constants, held in one configuration file the renderer and verifier both read: `canvas` 9:16 [1080, 1920] · 16:9 [1920, 1080] · 1:1 [1080, 1080]; `safe` per CDS-9 and CDS-13; `copy_max_width` 9:16 856 · 16:9 960 · 1:1 952; `anchor_9x16` TOP 80 UPPER_MID 700 LOWER_MID 1100 BOTTOM 1380 LEFT 96 CENTER 540 RIGHT 984; `type` display 132 headline 96 hook 84 title 72 body 56 caption 44 label 36 badge 36 body_min 48; `pad_chip` 16/28; `face_family` pretendard "Pretendard Variable" paperlogy "Paperlogy" (the typographic family — a name whose component begins with a digit is not a parsable CSS font-family and silently falls back); `rule` hair_w 520 hair_h 2 hair_alpha 0.55 bar_w 160 bar_h 6; `region_y` intro_a [940, 1020] intro_b [846, 960, 1010, 1090] outro_b [900, 950, 1040] outro_e [836, 980, 1030, 1110]; `color` per CDS-14 and CDS-15; `motion` in_ms 180 in_dy 12 out_ms 120 ease [0.22, 1, 0.36, 1]; `timing` sub_min_base_ms 900 sub_min_per_char_ms 90 cut_min_s 1.2 cut_max_s 6.0 cut_max_food_s 4.0 measured on transformed output time, authored hook/end/disclosure intervals under CLIP-66 (no mandatory duration constants); `rate` fixed_per_cut [0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.25, 1.5, 2] · slow_frame_synthesis false; `transition` default cut fade_ms 200 black_ms 300 fade_ratio_max 0.4; `audio` source_default off, in_product_bgm none, pitch_preserve true, loudnorm I=-16 TP=-1.5 LRA=11 crossfade_ms 60 hook_dip_db -6; `luma` scrim_threshold 0.6 sigma_threshold 0.25
- contrast is WCAG 2.1 relative-luminance contrast; a plate's effective colour is the plate composited at its α over the sampled frame mean
- character counts are Korean syllables excluding spaces and punctuation
