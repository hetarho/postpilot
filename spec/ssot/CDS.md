# CDS clip output design system
> r26 | Reusable visual, transformed-time and owner-authorized source-audio quality rules for instruction-led clip content inside project-selected fixed regions, with fact-grounded narration, an approved caption style set the owner places, preview verification and deterministic final output.

## decisions
- CDS-1 [o] the fixed regions — intro, outro and disclosure — are authored by the selected template and the narration is directed by the owner instruction, with no compulsory business, location, price, menu or minimum fact count; visual legibility and fact correctness remain delivery requirements (→CLIP-60 →CLIP-63 →CLIP-68 →CLIP-121)
- CDS-2 [o] every text element stays inside its ratio's safe area, except an explicitly header-placed badge which uses the separate symmetric header bounds (→CDS-57)
- CDS-3 [o] every on-screen text reads muted and small: body ≥ 48 px, caption ≥ 40 px, label ≥ 34 px, badge fixed at 36 px, contrast ≥ 4.5:1 against its effective background, exposure scaled to its length (→CDS-41)
- CDS-4 [o] a caption's motion is the one its style declares and no motion exists outside an approved style (→CDS-80); the default style keeps a 180 ms fade-in settling 12 px upward and a 120 ms fade-out, and rapid phrases replace one another immediately with no fade or movement whatever style they carry; the pace governs the motion alone and never which drawing a style gets, so a rapid phrase keeps its style's own plate, outline, colour and face (→CDS-85)
- CDS-5 [o] disclosure appears only as a template-declared element for its resolved visibility interval; neither a campaign type nor a product default inserts a badge or requires whole-clip exposure (→CLIP-46 →CLIP-65)
- CDS-6 [o] source audio is absent by default and included only in cuts derived from a source whose owner-controlled retention setting is on; templates, models and rendering never enable that setting, while in-product background music, stickers, emoji, decorative graphics, product logos and any platform watermark are refused (→CLIP-18 →CLIP-28)
- CDS-7 [o] every placement and style choice follows the rule tables below with no randomness; equal inputs render equal outputs
- CDS-8 [o] the base canvas is 9:16 at 1080×1920 and every dimension below is stated on it; 16:9 and 1:1 derive by CDS-46 through CDS-48
- CDS-9 [o] the 9:16 design safe area is x 64, y 40, w 952, h 1380 (x ≤ 1016, y ≤ 1420) with equal left and right insets; the top inset is 40 px and TOP elements add a further 40 px gap; centred copy keeps a 856 px measure that bounds a caption's characters (CDS-20) and a region slot's width (CDS-86) ← reading width is a typographic choice, not an avoidance of anything on screen
- CDS-12 [o] 9:16 anchors inside the design safe area: `TOP` plate top y 80 · `UPPER_MID` plate centre y 700 · `LOWER_MID` plate centre y 1100 · `BOTTOM` plate bottom y 1380 (default) · horizontal `CENTER` x 540 · `LEFT` x 96 · `RIGHT` x 984; a nine-cell grid is not used ← subtle offsets between cells break the consistency CDS-7 promises
- CDS-13 [o] the 16:9 safe area is (96, 72, 1728, 936) on 1920×1080 and the 1:1 safe area is (64, 72, 952, 936) on 1080×1080 ← the margins are broadcast title-safe practice plus a bottom player control bar
- CDS-14 [o] colour tokens: `text.white` #FFFFFF · `text.muted` #FFFFFF α0.72 · `stroke.dark` #0B0B0B α0.85 · `shadow.text` #000000 α0.55 blur 12 offset (0, 4) · `scrim.bottom` #000000 α0 → 0.55 over y 1040–1420 · `scrim.top` #000000 α0.45 → 0 over y 40–350 · `scrim.radial` #000000 α0.5 at the centre → α0.3 at 55 % → 0 at the edge · `badge.ad` #111111 α0.80 under white text
- CDS-15 [o] the accent palette stays the seven approved colours coral #FF6B57 · amber #FFB020 · lime #9BD53A · teal #2BB8A6 · blue #3D7BFF · violet #8A63FF · pink #FF6FB5, one per project chosen in ① (→CLIP-14 →CLIP-139); an accent appears only as the caption's accent word on a dark ground (→CDS-25) — never in the intro or outro or on the disclosure — and every rule and bar is white ← saturation belongs to the footage, so one coloured word is a clip's whole accent budget
- CDS-17 [o] typography: Wanted Sans Variable (weights 400–1000) sets information and copy, Paperlogy sets region text unless a region preset names NanumMyeongjo 800 or Jua, Jua and NanumMyeongjo (400 and 800) are also caption faces, and Noto Sans KR 700 stays the fallback; all are product-bundled at a fixed version and a caption takes the face its style names (→CLIP-13 →CDS-80 →CDS-88)
- CDS-18 [o] the face is fixed per region preset and type role, and a caption's face arrives with the style the project allowed (→CDS-80 →CLIP-142); no font file is ever supplied by a project or a user (→CLIP-28)
- CDS-19 [o] type scale on 9:16: `t.display` 132 Paperlogy 800, +0.04em, 1.0 (region floor 80) · `t.headline` 96 Paperlogy 800, -0.02em, 1.1 (region floor 60) · `t.hook` 84 Paperlogy 800, -0.02em, 1.15 (caption min 72, region floor 56) · `t.title` 72 Paperlogy 800, -0.02em, 1.2 (caption min 64, region floor 52) · `t.body` 56 Wanted Sans 700, -0.01em, 1.3 (min 48) · `t.caption` 44 Wanted Sans 600, 0, 1.3 (min 40) · `t.label` 36 Wanted Sans 600, +0.02em, 1.2 (min 34) · `t.badge` 36 Wanted Sans 800, +0.02em, 1.0 (fixed); a region preset may set a slot at another size of its role with its own floor, never below CDS-3 (→CDS-86) ← 48 px on a 1080-wide phone is ≈ 17 pt, the iOS body size, and still ≈ 10 pt in a 0.6× home-feed tile
- CDS-20 [o] a caption's characters per line inside 856 px: `t.hook` 9 · `t.title` 11 (Korean), wrapping by word (어절) and never beyond 2 lines; a region slot is bounded by its width under CDS-86, never by a character count (→CDS-77)
- CDS-21 [o] spacing tokens: `pad.chip` 16 vertical / 28 horizontal · `gap.stack` 16 vertical · `radius.chip` 12 (disclosure only) · `stroke.text` 6 px round-joined stroke painted under a Paperlogy fill · `stroke.small` 4 px under a Wanted Sans fill · `rule.hair` 520 × 2 px `text.white` α0.55 · `rule.bar` 160 × 6 px `text.white`
- CDS-22 [o] on-screen text sits in three regions — intro, caption, outro; the intro offers eight presets and the outro seven, chosen in ①, and the caption draws from the style set the project allowed, while the intro and outro keep no per-element style choice (→CLIP-111 →CLIP-142 →CDS-70 →CDS-80)
- CDS-25 [o] the default caption style is 크게 강조: no plate, `text.white` with `stroke.dark` 6 px and `shadow.text`, `t.title` 72 in Paperlogy, ≤ 2 lines × 11 characters, one word may take the accent colour on a dark ground, a scrim when CDS-44 finds a bright ground; an empty style selection resolves to it alone (→CDS-80 →CLIP-142)
- CDS-27 [o] a caption's interval is the narration's own absolute interval on the transformed output timeline and is never derived from a cut; a caption may begin or end inside a transition, and output-relative fixed text may span a cut transition (→CLIP-134 →CLIP-66)
- CDS-31 [o] the disclosure is a rectangle with `radius.chip` corners, `pad.chip` 16 vertical / 28 horizontal, white on `badge.ad`, and text smaller and heavier than the other roles at `t.badge` 36/800; it carries no motion or accent and the template may select its position and interval ← a disclosure has to read as an imposed marker rather than as part of the design
- CDS-32 [o] scrims appear only where CDS-44 demands one, share their text's in/out timing and never lie on a plate: an unplated caption at TOP or UPPER_MID takes `scrim.top` (0, 40, 1080, 310) and at BOTTOM or LOWER_MID `scrim.bottom` (0, 1040, 1080, 380); a centred region block takes `scrim.radial` centred on the block, 1360 px wide and 560 px taller than the block; an edge-anchored region block takes its edge's linear scrim, reaching from the frame edge to 150 px past the block
- CDS-33 [x] a second progress bar, a product logo or watermark, any in-product background-music generation, selection or mixing, stickers, emoji, decorative graphics, per-glyph animation, count-ups, and browser or Lottie export rendering — not adopted; post-download soundtrack addition is outside product scope and interactive draft preview is allowed under CLIP-56 (→CLIP-28)
- CDS-34 [?] an optional creator-handle watermark (`t.label` 34 `text.muted` α0.6 at BOTTOM/LEFT 96, 1380, hidden when copy lands there) for re-posting to Reels and Shorts — owner's call
- CDS-35 [o] retained source audio follows its cut's fixed playback rate with pitch preserved, applies per-cut volume, uses 60 ms fades at audible cut boundaries and is then loudness-normalised to −16 LUFS with true peak −1.5 dBTP and range 11 LU; a source whose retention setting is off contributes no audio, a clip with no enabled source has no audio track, and the −6 dB hook dip applies only during the intro block's declared interval
- CDS-36 [o] transitions: a hard cut is the default; a 200 ms fade only where the place or scene type changes and on at most 40 % of the boundaries, keeping the earliest; a 300 ms fade-through-black is optional and off by default around the intro and outro blocks; every other transition is refused (→CLIP-16)
- CDS-37 [o] cut rhythm follows the owner instruction, then the template guide, on the transformed output timeline, using the 1.2–6.0 s and food-close-up 4.0 s output-duration targets only when neither specifies one; observed footage, readable copy and the approved total output duration outrank rhythm targets, and no category preset imposes a range (→CLIP-136)
- CDS-38 [o] automatic placement for the caption walks its default then alternative anchor as a starting point: a candidate leaving the safe area is dropped; a candidate overlapping the disclosure or a region block is dropped and the caption always yields to them; a candidate lying outside every observed caption-safe region is dropped; an owner placement replaces that result and is never re-run over (→CDS-82 →CLIP-143 →CLIP-105)
- CDS-41 [o] a sentence caption needs at least 900 + 90 × Korean characters ms (excluding spaces and punctuation) of its own interval; a narration interval below that floor takes the meaning-preserving grounded shorter sentence, then extends to the floor where the neighbouring captions leave room, then omits the caption with a recorded reason; no cut occupancy rule applies, and fixed text, explicit styles and authored intervals remain unchanged for correction under CDS-64 (→CLIP-134)
- CDS-42 [o] voice, viewpoint and narrative progression follow the owner instruction, then the template guide; every number in AI-written text matches a collected fact, every description cites the observation it describes, first-person taste or experience is stated only where an instruction asks for it and never inferred from appearance, and exact fixed text is rendered as authored (→CLIP-63 →CLIP-64 →CLIP-65 →CLIP-137)
- CDS-43 [o] the narration is optional at every moment: captions hold disjoint windows on the output timeline at every caption pace, whatever cut lies beneath them, and a caption whose window cannot fit beside its neighbours is omitted with its recorded reason; rapid phrases follow CDS-59 and explicitly timed output-level fixed text is independent of the caption count (→CLIP-134)
- CDS-44 [o] brightness sampling covers every unplated text — the caption and both region blocks: the first, middle and last frame of the copy window are cropped to the text's bounds and averaged to Rec.709 relative luminance L and deviation σ; L ≥ 0.6 or σ ≥ 0.25 adds the scrim CDS-32 assigns to that text; an accent word turns white when L ≥ 0.6; stroked text measures contrast against `stroke.dark` composited over the sampled mean, and a pairing still under 4.5:1 after the scrim is delivered with a CLIP-108 notice on that element instead of refused ← the regions have no plate to fall back to, and a reviewable clip beats a refusal over one bright frame
- CDS-45 [o] layering applies only to elements the template actually declares plus the narration: disclosure → region block → caption → scrim; authored visibility is preserved, automatic placement avoids occlusion and overlaps remain visible for owner review under CDS-56
- CDS-46 [o] 16:9 and 1:1 keep every font size (the short side is 1080 in all three), except `t.hook` 84 → 72 on 16:9 and → 76 on 1:1, and restate only positions and widths
- CDS-47 [o] 16:9: copy width ≤ 960 centred (16 characters at 56 px), TOP plate top y 112, BOTTOM plate bottom y 968, UPPER_MID / LOWER_MID 420 / 660, the intro and outro blocks placed by CDS-79, badge right edge x 1824 top y 112, scrims 260 bottom / 200 top ← a wider line slows reading
- CDS-48 [o] 1:1: copy width ≤ 952 (15 characters), TOP 112, BOTTOM 968, UPPER_MID / LOWER_MID 420 / 660, the intro and outro blocks placed by CDS-79, badge right edge x 1016 top y 112, scrims 260 / 200
- CDS-49 [o] 9:16 is the product default because every short-form feed takes it; 16:9 suits horizontal upload and blog or site embedding, and 1:1 suits square feed placements (→CLIP-8)
- CDS-51 [o] templates reuse the product's visual components, readability rules and audio normalisation without inheriting content, campaign type or exposure from a preset

- CDS-55 [o] automatic generated-copy repair tries a grounded shorter sentence, then an allowed automatic anchor, then omits that generated copy with its reason recorded; explicit styles, positions, fixed text and fixed timing never enter this repair ladder (→CDS-64)
- CDS-53 [o] release QA prioritizes deterministic owner review over decorative complexity and reviews every full clip both muted and with its configured source audio; same-source splits, every rate preset, transition overlaps, captions crossing cuts, narration over footage it does not describe, differently priced items, missing optional fields and a template with no badge, intro or outro must remain coherent and preview-equivalent without unintended audio, temporal gaps, stale captions, invented text or silent repair (→CLIP-72)
- CDS-56 [o] overlap never prevents automatic generation or manual rerendering, including caption, disclosure and region-block collisions and crossfade windows; preserve the selected text, placement and timing when overlap is the only issue, deliver the preview and download, and let the owner revise it in step ②; diagnostic overlap checks remain available, and all other delivery checks still run even when overlap is present ← a reviewable video is preferable to losing the whole result over text placement

- CDS-57 [o] a header-placed disclosure keeps equal outer margins — 9:16 x 96/984, 16:9 x 96/1824 and 1:1 x 64/1016 — with glyphs measured by weight/tracking and optically centred, reserving its visible width plus one stack gap only while shown; authored text follows CDS-64, and other selected positions inherit no hidden header reservation

- CDS-59 [o] rapid captions are independent of copy style, occupy explicit sequential absolute output windows of 300–1000 ms inside their caption's own interval and replace each other without a gap or motion; generation preserves a first short word as its own opening beat and groups following adjacent words into short phrases, prefers 300 ms for ≤3 characters, 500 ms for ≤9, 800 ms for longer phrases, and may compress within the 300 ms floor to fit that interval; at most 24 phrases per caption, one line of ≤14 characters per phrase, no sentence-class or 60% occupancy requirement; insufficient room uses the grounded short alternative or preserves sentence mode rather than losing the video (→CLIP-134)
- CDS-60 [o] step ② exposes each phrase's text, absolute transformed-output start and end time, add/remove, and split-to-rapid or merge-to-sentence actions; manual windows persist exactly in output milliseconds and are revalidated after any edit that changes the output length, adjacent windows may touch but may not overlap, and invalid limits are reported before rendering without proportional retiming; older plans retain their sentence-mode behavior

- CDS-61 [o] the design system owns reusable typography, contrast, safe areas, approved visual styles and output quality; the template owns the outline — each entry's text and the disclosure's presence and position — and the owner instruction owns the flow and the narration (→CLIP-59 →CLIP-65 →CLIP-121 →CLIP-134)
- CDS-62 [o] a cut using source interval [start, end] and fixed rate r has pre-transition output duration (end − start) / r; cut order and transition overlap form the edited output timeline on which every template and caption interval resolves before layout and verification, while source evidence keeps its original timestamps, full-output and end-relative elements keep their declared scope across cuts, and captions keep their absolute output intervals through cut edits (→CLIP-67)
- CDS-63 [o] a name, price, quantity or unit in the narration matches one collected global or item fact exactly; digits or characters assembled from several answers are not a match, and no footage identity is required for a fact to be stated (→CLIP-137)
- CDS-64 [o] invalid authored text, fixed placement or timing identifies the affected element and preserves the owner's draft for correction; neither AI nor layout repair silently shortens, rewrites, moves or drops that authored element
- CDS-65 [o] editing-preview acceptance compares the current draft's source ranges, cut order, transformed timing, fixed rates, configured source-audio state, transitions, crop, text, position and visibility intervals with final export for every supported ratio and caption pace; a preview lacking parity for any supported field cannot be presented as final-output proof, and unsupported effects are rejected before save or rendering

- CDS-66 [o] no intro or outro element names a style: both regions render in the preset the project chose; a caption's style is named per caption by the narration and changed per caption by the owner in ②, always from the project's allowed set, and a name outside that set is an authoring error checked before generation (→CDS-80 →CLIP-142 →CLIP-143)
- CDS-67 [o] grammar examples, copied format instructions, template controls and acceptance fixtures cover every role/style combination and every supported ratio; rejecting a deterministic combination only in final rendering is a release-blocking contract mismatch
- CDS-68 [o] every cut uses exactly one CLIP-98 fixed playback rate; a slow rate is valid only when verified source cadence can produce the 30 fps output without synthesized or interpolated frames, and an unsuitable rate is identified before rendering rather than changed to 1x

## flow
- compose: owner instruction + template guide, the project's design selection and the fixed regions + grouped answers + observed source evidence + owner source-audio settings → select, split, trim and order source ranges with one fixed rate per cut → map source time and transitions onto the edited output timeline → write the narration as captions with absolute intervals → validate fact grounding, non-overlap and the assembly contract → resolve fixed regions and caption intervals → apply each region's selected preset and the caption's automatic anchor → sample contrast where needed → manifest → verify → render
- fallback: invalid assembly, transformed timing or audio contract → identify the exact cut or element and preserve the draft without substituting a rate, changing source-audio state, dropping a cut or silently retiming; generated-copy problem → a grounded shorter sentence or an allowed automatic anchor → omit generated copy with explanation; authored-content problem → identify element and preserve draft for correction; overlap only → preserve and preview

## constraints
- the canvas, safe areas, bundled type scale and output encoding quality are the visual baseline; no category preset supplies implicit content, and stored compositions convert under CLIP-70, CLIP-140 and CLIP-144
