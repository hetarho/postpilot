# QUOTA plans, credits, metering
> r20 | Every account carries one plan that grants monthly credits on its own anchor day, extra credits are purchasable at par at any time or arrive as expiring voucher credits (→GIFT), and LLM work is held before its first provider call and settled against a persisted ledger; clips require an approved charge ceiling, waive unconsumed failed work and charge explicit cancellation against its unused reservation, master is the operator tier, and the money side belongs to BILL.

## decisions
- QUOTA-1 [o] every account carries exactly one plan `free | basic | pro | max | master`, a `users.plan` column with a CHECK so an off-ladder value cannot be stored; `free` is the provisioning default (`api adduser <id> [--plan=<tier>]`, flag accepted in either order)
- QUOTA-2 [o] a tier decides the monthly credit grant and, for master alone, access to administrative procedures; automatic publishing is unavailable to every tier, and model access remains independent of tier (→QUOTA-19)
- QUOTA-3 [o] two paths change a tier and both land on the same grant rules (→QUOTA-35): the operator directly (`AdminService.SetUserPlan`, `api setplan <id> <tier>`, master only) and a subscriber through BILL; an already-granted monthly lot is edited by nothing except an upgrade's top-up
- QUOTA-4 [o] the last master cannot be demoted on either path; the guard is a condition inside the UPDATE statement, not a count taken before it ← two concurrent demotions must not both commit
- QUOTA-5 [o] the acting plan is resolved once per request on the session path, from the row (→AUTH-15)
- QUOTA-6 [o] a credit is a product-fixed $0.01 of list value stored as an integer — a billing unit, not a cost measurement; the ledger keeps true provider cost in micro-USD underneath and the two never reconcile ← what a user is entitled to must not move with provider prices or exchange rates
- QUOTA-7 [o] grants: free 50 · basic 330 ($3/mo) · pro 1150 ($10) · max 2400 ($20) · master unlimited — the paid rungs carry +10 % / +15 % / +20 % over the par purchase rate (→QUOTA-34) ← subscribing must beat topping up, and more so the higher the rung; prices are published through GetMyPlan so a comparison screen can name them; charging a card, the subscription lifecycle and receipts are BILL's, not this domain's
- QUOTA-8 [o] every number in the ladder is a code-owned product rule in `backend/internal/plan`, published to the client; the frontend hardcodes none ← two deploys must never disagree
- QUOTA-9 [o] creating an account grants no bonus; a non-expiring 100-credit bonus follows the first registered payment method instead, once per account (→BILL-10) ← self-signup makes a grant handed out at account creation farmable; `api grantcredits <id> <amount> [--expires=<RFC3339>]`, a purchase (→QUOTA-34) and a voucher redemption (→QUOTA-58) are the only other paths to credits beyond the monthly grant
- QUOTA-10 [o] charge: `credits = ChargeBase(2) + ceil(actual_cost_usd × 100 × ChargeMultiplier(3))`, integer-only (one credit = 10 000 micro-USD), rounding up so a call too cheap to reach a credit still costs ChargeBase + 1; an unpriced model costs ChargeBase alone; clip failure and explicit cancellation follow QUOTA-46 and QUOTA-49 respectively ← the base recovers per-request infrastructure and the multiplier covers provider top-up, fees, VAT and margin
- QUOTA-11 [o] a balance is lots, not a counter: one `credit_lots` row per grant (granted, remaining, `expires_at` NULL = never), `CHECK (remaining >= 0 AND remaining <= granted)`, spend and refund statements guarded by the amount available ← the never-negative guarantee rests on SQL, not on Go alone
- QUOTA-12 [o] consumption walks every lot that carries an expiry first — `monthly`, `voucher` and an expiring `bonus` alike — by `expires_at` ascending, then never-expiring `bonus`, then `purchased`, ties broken by creation (→GIFT-11) ← a credit that can lapse must burn before one that cannot, and a paid purchase must still be the last to burn; expiry is a read-time predicate; renewal is computed on access inside the same write transaction over the anchor window (→QUOTA-37); a lapsed remainder does not carry over and the lapsed row is kept for history
- QUOTA-13 [o] every LLM-consuming job start (`generate` `revise` `extract_memory` `analyze_voice` `learn_voice` `compare_voice_rule` `validate_voice_profile` `model_experiment`) passes one gate at the shared enqueue seam (`job.Queue.Enqueue`, a consumer-declared Admitter port wired in `cmd/api`); one A/B comparison is one admission even though it fans out to two candidates; clip preparation is the explicitly bounded exception in QUOTA-43
- QUOTA-14 [o] hold: price every planned call at its worst case — an assumed 30 000-token prompt (`holdInputTokens`), except clips freeze each stage's enforceable input allowance under QUOTA-53, at the model's input price plus that call's own completion budget at its output price — run the total through the charge formula, and deduct it from the lots in consumption order, inside one `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` transaction with the admission row, before the job row exists except for QUOTA-43's clip preparation; the owning service states the call count ← only it knows that observation batches photos per call or that a validation repeats per sampled post
- QUOTA-15 [o] settle once at the terminal state against usage_events, refund unused reservation to its original credit_hold_lots and guard settled_at; a non-clip job whose actual cost exceeds its hold drains lots to zero with no carried debt, while clips obey QUOTA-44 ceilings, QUOTA-46 failure settlement and QUOTA-49 explicit cancellation settlement
- QUOTA-16 [o] master is held, recorded and settled like everyone else but spends no lot and is never refused ← unlimited spend is exactly the spend the operator most wants readable
- QUOTA-17 [o] a hold whose job row then failed to insert is released in full on a context that outlives the caller's cancellation; a boot sweep settles any hold left open behind an already-terminal job
- QUOTA-18 [o] a refusal is `resource_exhausted` with reason `INSUFFICIENT_CREDITS` carrying `required`, `balance`, `renews_at` (RFC3339); it writes no admission or debit and creates no job row except that QUOTA-43 preserves the existing clip preparation job as failed; the client renders copy from the reason and formats machine values (micro-USD, instants, tier names) with i18next, never from the message string
- QUOTA-19 [o] there is no plan floor: `min_plan` exists nowhere (registry, `config/providers.yaml`, boot validation, RPCs); every account may select, save and run every registered model; affordability is the only access rule and it is the hold itself ← a floor would answer a question the balance already answers, worse, across every enforcement site
- QUOTA-20 [o] `ListModels` reports per model, for the caller, the credits one call would require and whether the balance covers it, computed by the same estimator the gate applies — display only; an unaffordable model is temporary state and a saved selection is never invalidated, cleared, or reported missing because of a balance
- QUOTA-21 [o] ledger: every server-side LLM call writes one `usage_events` row — prompt/completion tokens, provider-reported reasoning tokens (0 = not reported; kept for diagnosis, never re-priced), `cost_microusd`, `cost_source` resolved reported → estimated → unavailable; a failed call is recorded when the provider reported usage; rows are append-only and kept indefinitely
- QUOTA-22 [o] recording happens at the llm boundary: `cmd/api` wraps the registry every context receives and the worker stamps `usage.Work{user, kind, job}` on the handler context ← every present and future call site is metered by construction
- QUOTA-23 [o] the ledger `stage` is the stage the call named for itself (`observe` · `write` · `analyze`), falling back to ref inference; A/B candidate calls appear in both the experiment tables and the ledger; credit math never joins experiment internals
- QUOTA-24 [o] a post holds at most 30 photos (`UPLOAD_MAX_PHOTOS_PER_POST`, in both config owners); the server refuses the upload that would cross it and the browser reports the excess as skipped before decoding ← it bounds the worst case a hold must price, not storage
- QUOTA-25 [o] master-only surfaces are the AdminService procedures, including account administration and estimator-combo assignment (→QUOTA-39), in the closed masterProcedures set (→AUTH-18); the frontend hides /admin and redirects non-master visitors away from it while the server remains authoritative; no tier exposes a publishing entry, panel or pairing screen
- QUOTA-26 [o] GetMyPlan returns the caller's tier, balance, the individual lots with expiries and kinds, the renewal instant, the tier's monthly grant, every rung on offer with its grant, its intended price and whether it is the recommended rung, and the estimator's combos with their unit rates (→QUOTA-40); reading a balance also renews it
- QUOTA-27 [o] the header carries the remaining credits as one 44 px control that navigates to `/plans` (the figure plus the tier name, unlimited for master), read by the shell from GetMyPlan rather than only while a popover is open; the account popover keeps the meter (`shared/ui/meter`), the renewal instant, the lots behind the total and its `/plans` link, shown for every tier ← behind the profile glyph the balance is two taps away and `/plans` has no other entry, so nothing would lead a user to the ladder
- QUOTA-28 [o] `/plans` is the comparison and only subscription entry: free/basic/pro/max stack on the phone and form two columns at md and four at lg; each shows its monthly price, grant, bonus, four model-level production estimates and one applicable action; phone cards put the name and price side by side, show estimates in a compact two-column grid and the bonus as inline copy, and move shared model/renewal benefits outside individual cards; pro alone is recommended from the server ladder, the current rung has no action, and subscription lifecycle details stay on BILL's screen
- QUOTA-29 [o] a zero balance blocks every LLM-consuming action and nothing else: writing, editing, exporting and reading posts keep working
- QUOTA-30 [x] mid-flight cancellation on exhaustion, per-model or per-stage rate limits, charging non-LLM resources (storage, uploads), retroactive usage reconstruction, team plans — out of scope; payments and the subscription lifecycle are BILL's
- QUOTA-31 [o] renewal and reset instants use calendar Asia/Seoul as a fixed UTC+9 constant, not a tzdata lookup ← the distroless image ships no zoneinfo and KST observes no DST
- QUOTA-32 [o] a planned call carries the completion budget it will actually be sent: `PlannedCall` carries that figure beside `Ref` and `Count`, `worstCaseMicrousd` prices each entry at its own figure, a ref used for two stages with different budgets is two entries (entries with equal budgets collapse into one), and `CreditsFor` (behind the catalog's `RequiredCredits` and `EstimatePostCredits`) prices with the same per-call budgets, naming the stage it quotes for; non-clip `holdInputTokens` is 30 000, clips follow QUOTA-53, and a call declaring no budget uses a completion fallback ← spend follows per-stage budgets (observe from batch size, write/revise from target length up to a ceiling), so a hold pricing every call at `LLM_MAX_TOKENS_DEFAULT` would over-hold observation and under-hold long writes
- QUOTA-33 [o] hold pricing (QUOTA-32) changes no provider request body and no budget any stage is sent
- QUOTA-34 [o] credits are purchasable at any time at the par rate $1 = 100 credits, no bonus and no minimum beyond one dollar ← par is what makes a subscription's grant bonus the reason to subscribe; a purchase becomes one `purchased` lot that never expires and is consumed last (→QUOTA-12); the checkout, payment method, receipt and any refund of that purchase are BILL's
- QUOTA-35 [o] a tier change keeps the billing anchor: a downgrade leaves the current cycle's grant and charge untouched and sizes only the next cycle's lot; an upgrade charges the difference between the two monthly prices at once and raises the current cycle's monthly lot by the credit difference on both `granted` and `remaining`, which is the one case where a plan change edits an already-granted lot ← someone who pays more this minute must be able to spend it this minute; a second upgrade in one cycle charges and grants only the difference from the tier then in force, and a scheduled downgrade is cancelled rather than stacked
- QUOTA-36 [o] a rung states estimated finished blog posts or clips per monthly grant, proportional to per-item conditions and each assigned model-level pair; actual admission and approved clip ceilings remain the only spending authority
- QUOTA-37 [o] the grant window runs anchor day to anchor day rather than calendar month: the anchor is the subscription's start day (→BILL-3), or the account's creation day while it holds no subscription, clamped to a shorter month's last day, resolved in Asia/Seoul (→QUOTA-31) ← a charge and the credits it pays for must land on the same day, and a calendar boundary would hand a mid-month subscriber a full grant for a fortnight
- QUOTA-38 [o] a term changes the charge, never the grant: monthly and annual both open the tier's monthly credits on the anchor day and an unused month lapses (→BILL-4)
- QUOTA-39 [o] four estimator combos are the model levels `value` · `balanced` · `premium` · `top` (가성비 · 밸런스 · 고급 · 최고, →MODEL-57); each card offers and accepts only a `photo-analysis` registration and a `writing` registration carrying that same per-purpose level, with the server enforcing both registration and level; the operator assigns the pair through the third AdminService procedure, and a combo left unassigned, or whose registration is deregistered or relevelled, is left out of the client response rather than falling back ← quoting real prices means the comparison moves with the operator's curation, and an invented price that never moves would be the alternative
- QUOTA-40 [o] the server publishes per-combo blog rates (per photo, per video, per 1000 finished characters and per-post base) and optional clip rates (per original source, per finished second and per-clip base), derived from real catalog model prices and code-owned token assumptions with 1.5x generation tokens for AI edits; the rates change neither fixed overhead nor actual metering, and the client only multiplies and floors
- QUOTA-41 [o] `/plans` starts with blog-post/clip basis selection and a read-only per-item condition summary; one bottom-right floating "Change conditions" button opens a shared sheet (bottom sheet on mobile, dialog on desktop) containing only per-item inputs, never a model selector or production counts; blog inputs are characters/photos/videos and clip inputs are original-video count and finished-clip duration; the two sets persist independently, switching basis restores its conditions, and every card updates immediately without a request per change
- QUOTA-42 [o] starting a subscription is not a tier change (→QUOTA-35): the account's current monthly window closes at that instant and its unspent remainder does not carry over, while a fresh window opens on the subscription day carrying the new tier's whole monthly grant (→QUOTA-37, →BILL-3) ← an upgrade keeps the cycle it has already paid for and owes only a difference, but a first charge buys a whole month and must hand over a whole month's credits

- QUOTA-43 [o] clip generation creates a durable job, validates retained recovery work and verifies only missing source-analysis work before reserving its remaining AI calls including the frozen response-correction allowance; reservation is atomic and within the approved ceiling, and refusal permits zero AI calls and preserves completed work
- QUOTA-44 [o] clip AI consumes only reserved model refs, call counts and input/completion budgets; only bounded response correction under QUOTA-54 may repeat a call, with no model or supplier fallback or budget increase, and final debit never exceeds reservation or approval while service-owned overage cannot create debt
- QUOTA-45 [o] each clip AI approval binds the source selection, settings, models, frozen budgets, correction allowance and selected reusable checkpoint; only remaining AI work is quoted, changed inputs invalidate approval, and compatible render-only continuation requires no AI reservation
- QUOTA-46 [o] a failed clip AI attempt without accepted owner cancellation costs zero including ChargeBase when no billable usage is confirmed, otherwise charges confirmed usage under QUOTA-21 and QUOTA-10 within QUOTA-44; missing usage stays unknown and unconfirmed cost is absorbed without later debit; accepted explicit cancellation follows QUOTA-49 instead
- QUOTA-47 [o] every clip provider request is restricted to compatible input/processing paths whose applicable text, visual, audio and output prices are covered by the frozen admission and enforceable request limits; a nonzero media price is eligible when covered, unknown or unenforceable applicable pricing is refused rather than treated as free, automatic supplier fallback and silent limit relaxation are forbidden, and the absence of an eligible path stops the attempt without issuing that call ← a provider unit-price limit cannot by itself guarantee a total user-credit ceiling
- QUOTA-48 [o] a clip quote accounts for the documented billing units of its bounded text, visual and audible inputs and generated output, including applicable reasoning, caching and conditional prices, without double-counting overlapping usage; optional billable features outside the quoted work remain disabled, and an estimated final cost uses only sufficiently specified reported usage rather than charging the reserved maximum as if it were measured use

- QUOTA-49 [o] an explicitly cancelled clip AI job settles `C + ceil((R - C) / 2)` credits, where R is that job's successfully reserved credits and C is its confirmed billable-use charge under QUOTA-10 capped at R (C = 0 when no billable usage is confirmed); the addition uses the unused job reservation, never the account's remaining balance, and total debit stays within R and the approved ceiling
- QUOTA-50 [o] the clip approval and cancellation surfaces disclose the 50% unused-reservation addition and upward integer rounding; settlement records and displays confirmed-use charge, cancellation addition, total debit and refunded reservation separately, without representing the addition as measured provider usage
- QUOTA-51 [o] cancellation before successful reservation costs zero; cancellation after reservation with no confirmed billable usage costs ceil(R / 2), while cancelling credit-free manual rerender remains zero and master records the same shadow settlement without lot debit
- QUOTA-52 [o] accepted cancellation is a durable settlement cause, including after restart; cancellation/completion races and repeated requests settle once, already-completed jobs keep their normal settlement, and unconfirmed in-flight provider usage never causes a later extra debit

- QUOTA-53 [o] clip approvals freeze 30 000 input tokens per observation request and 64 000 per composition request, including correction attempts; quote/reservation, endpoint qualification and request admission share that allowance, and a frozen policy carrying no input allowance is held to 30 000 and cannot be enlarged silently

- QUOTA-54 [o] clip quotes reserve at most four requests per missing observation chunk and at most four for a missing composition (one initial plus three response corrections); a frozen policy carrying no correction allowance allows one, every issued request is independently metered, reused work is never charged again, and unused correction allowance is refunded under the settlement and cancellation rules

- QUOTA-55 [o] plan comparisons state the manual top-up baseline ($1 = 100 credits) and each paid offer's monthly extra credits and percentage versus the same spend at par, derived from the displayed grant and price; free never claims a subscription bonus

- QUOTA-56 [o] every offered plan always lists value/balanced/premium/top in that order, each naming the model level and estimated monthly blog-post or clip count; missing, unpriced or incompatible pairs retain their row with an unavailable explanation instead of an invented number
- QUOTA-57 [o] clip comparison estimates source analysis plus flow and narration writing from the assigned photo-analysis/writing pair, requiring video input and structured output for the observer and structured output for the writer; originals assume 60 seconds each, stated beside the clip conditions, while count is 1..20 and finished duration is 15..90 seconds; the estimate is not a provider qualification or an approved reservation and never alters the production clip quote or settlement policies
- QUOTA-58 [o] a `voucher` lot kind: a voucher redemption opens one lot of the voucher's credits expiring the voucher's validity after the redemption instant (→GIFT-9), and a revocation voids its unspent remainder while spent credits stay spent (→GIFT-10)

## flow
- job start → Enqueue → Hold(renew the anchor-window lot → price planned calls → spend lots in order) → ok(admission + hold lots → job row) | refused(INSUFFICIENT_CREDITS, no row)
- clip start → show and approve server-issued ceiling → durable prepare(missing source-analysis work and validated recovery) → required reservation within approval → Hold(remaining calls including up to three response corrections each) → ok(price-bounded AI calls → render) | refused(failed job, no AI or debit) → worker-copy cleanup and CLIP-retained originals
- worker call → metered registry → llm → `usage_events` row (stage, tokens, cost)
- terminal state → Settle(Σ usage_events → charge → refund remainder to the same lots; once)
- clip AI terminal state → accepted cancellation(C + ceil((R - C) / 2)) | failed without confirmed usage(zero including base) | otherwise(ledger charge within reservation/approval) → refund remainder once → display confirmed use, cancellation addition if any, total charge and returned credits
- credit purchase → BILL checkout → one `purchased` lot (never expires, consumed last)
- voucher redemption → one `voucher` lot (expires the voucher's validity after redemption, burns with the other expiring lots)
- tier change → upgrade(BILL charges the price difference → the current monthly lot rises by the credit difference) | downgrade(scheduled → the next cycle's lot is sized to the new tier)

## constraints
- config: no env for the ladder, charge constants, or timezone (code-owned); `LLM_MAX_TOKENS_DEFAULT` = completion cap for a caller with no budget and the base of the write budget's floor/ceiling; `UPLOAD_MAX_PHOTOS_PER_POST` 30 in BE + FE config
- clip quote and provider limits are server authorities: the client displays the maximum, reservation, final charge and returned credits distinctly, and neither a forged client ceiling nor a changed catalog price authorizes an unreserved call
- multimodal price coverage changes neither the approved/reserved user ceilings, service-owned overage, zero-charge unused failures nor the frozen response-correction allowance; unsafe pricing prevents a call rather than a later additional debit
- clip completion caps remain 8192 tokens per observation and 32768 per composition request, including reasoning where applicable; a cap is not a prediction of actual usage, and compression savings never justify lowering the reservation or increasing calls without a separately validated budget change
- clip failure settlement preserves the reported → estimated-from-reported-usage → unavailable distinction in QUOTA-21; missing usage is never reconstructed from the reserved maximum, and a waiver does not rewrite the raw provider ledger as a reported zero
- master remains exempt from lot debit and insufficient-balance refusal under QUOTA-16, but not from the bounded clip execution plan, compatibility checks or provider unit-price limits
- clip-only ceilings, failure waivers and cancellation additions change no post, voice, experiment, purchased-credit, subscription or payment-refund policy; a settled job is never retroactively adjusted
- schema: `users.plan` · `credit_lots`, whose kind is `monthly` · `bonus` · `purchased` · `voucher` and orders consumption (QUOTA-12) · `credit_hold_lots` · `usage_admissions` · `usage_events` (with its reasoning-token column)
- placement: `backend/internal/plan` (ladder, grants, charge, windows, typed refusals, the recommended rung, the estimator's token assumptions and rate derivation) · `backend/internal/usage` (lots, hold/settle, ledger, metering seam) · `backend/internal/auth` (plan on user/session, master set) · `frontend/src/entities/plan` · `pages/plans` · `pages/admin` + `features/manage-users` · `widgets/account-menu` · the header credit control in the app shell
