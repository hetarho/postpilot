# CLIP generated video projects and templates
> r24 | Account-owned footage becomes a downloadable, reviewable clip through bounded full-timeline scene observation, a template-grounded assembly document, deterministic source transforms and preview-led correction, independently of blog posts.

## decisions
- CLIP-1 [o] a clip project is independent of a post and owns its title, chosen video template, template answers, target duration, aspect ratio, analysis, edit plan and latest successful result
- CLIP-2 [o] only the authenticated owner may list, view, change, generate, cancel, finalize, download or delete a clip project or video template; an unknown or foreign id is presented as not found
- CLIP-3 [o] navigation groups 글 · 말투 · 글 템플릿 · 지침 under 글 and 클립 · 영상 템플릿 under 영상; account, plan, billing and administration remain common destinations
- CLIP-4 [o] a video template is a reusable account-owned composition with a design selection — one intro preset, the caption treatment and one outro preset (→CLIP-111) — authored footage sections, generated and fixed text, information fields, repeated item groups, copy guidance and independent caption pace; category presets do not govern its content and it declares no visual style of its own
- CLIP-5 [o] every generation selects exactly one valid video template and collects only the information that template explicitly marks required before it can start
- CLIP-6 [o] a project accepts up to 20 source videos whose combined duration is at most 30 minutes
- CLIP-7 [o] the owner chooses a target result duration from 15 to 90 seconds before generation; composition measures every cut, transition and result on the playback-rate-transformed output timeline, may split or reconcile multiple non-overlapping ranges only within their observed source scenes, and may deliver a shorter result of at least 15 seconds when no valid assembly can reach the target; insufficient selected footage remains inspectable under CLIP-85 instead of being presented as a completed clip
- CLIP-8 [o] the owner chooses one project ratio at creation: 세로 9:16, 가로 16:9 or 정방형 1:1
- CLIP-9 [o] a project's ratio is fixed; producing another ratio starts another project ← reframing after cut and copy placement would make the approved composition unreliable
- CLIP-10 [o] generation records each prepared source as ordered source-time scenes covering its full timeline, with concise events, action and motion, visual subjects, quality, audible speech and explicit unknown, uncertain or unusable spans; observation describes the material and makes no narrative, cut, copy or effect decision
- CLIP-11 [o] the composition model uses the template narrative, exact owner facts and completed source observations to produce one validated edit plan containing ordered source-bound cuts, supported transforms and transitions, scene-grounded exact copy and its timing; it owns narrative and edit choices but never observes pixels or executes media work (→CLIP-61 →CLIP-63 →CLIP-64 →CLIP-97)
- CLIP-12 [o] generated copy is rendered as exact typeset text through approved visual templates ← AI-drawn Korean lettering can be misspelled or visually inconsistent
- CLIP-13 [o] the copy renderer uses only product-bundled, fixed-version fonts — Pretendard Variable as the primary face, Paperlogy for hook and emphasis text, Noto Sans KR as the fallback (→CDS-17) — and never a device or host system font ← the same project must render identically across environments
- CLIP-14 [o] a template declares no per-element style: it selects one intro preset and one outro preset from the CDS set and takes the caption treatment, whose size, placement and colour are fixed, and supplies each slot's text plus one project accent from the approved seven-colour palette that only the caption's accent word uses (→CDS-22 →CDS-70 →CDS-15 →CLIP-111)
- CLIP-15 [o] template-authored text uses the CDS safe areas and supported anchors, with header-role elements using the symmetric header bounds; automatic placement applies only where the template leaves placement automatic (→CDS-9 →CDS-12 →CDS-13 →CDS-57)
- CLIP-16 [o] cuts join with a hard cut by default and a short fade only where the scene type changes (→CDS-36)
- CLIP-17 [o] step ② 클립 다듬기 is a preview-led timeline editor for cut creation, splitting, order, range and fixed playback rate, generated or fixed text, approved placement, phrase timing, per-source original-sound retention and per-cut volume (→CLIP-53 →CLIP-54 →CLIP-55)
- CLIP-18 [o] source audio is excluded by default; each source has one owner-controlled original-sound retention setting, and only enabled sources contribute audio to their selected cuts before per-cut volume and whole-track loudness normalisation (→CDS-35)
- CLIP-19 [o] AI analysis and AI composition regeneration consume the account's existing credits within the maximum shown and approved before generation under QUOTA-45; preparation verifies sources and prepares bounded analysis copies before reserving the complete AI run under QUOTA-43, and explicit cancellation settles under QUOTA-49 through QUOTA-52; no AI call may precede a successful reservation
- CLIP-20 [o] manual corrections, rerendering the corrected plan, preview, download and finalization consume no additional credits; cancelling a manual rerender is also credit-free
- CLIP-21 [o] unfinalized projects retain private source originals for 24 hours under CLIP-73; analysis copies and local worker files remain attempt-scoped, while metadata, analysis, the edit plan and latest successful result remain until project deletion
- CLIP-22 [o] before source selection, the upload surface discloses external analysis of compressed footage and audible speech through OpenRouter, the 24-hour original-retention rule and original deletion on finalization; service cleanup makes no promise about external-provider retention
- CLIP-23 [o] while retained originals remain available, refresh, reopening, AI retry and manual rerender reuse matching originals without reselection; after expiry or missing media, only operations needing those pixels request matching originals again and never start automatically
- CLIP-24 [o] deleting a project removes its originals, result, analysis, edit plan and metadata, with object cleanup recoverable after interruption
- CLIP-25 [o] deleting a video template detaches it from projects while preserving their answers, analysis, resolved composition, edit plan and result; manual correction uses that retained composition, while another AI generation requires a selected valid template
- CLIP-26 [o] a failed or cancelled attempt preserves the previous successful result and offers explicit continuation from compatible completed work under CLIP-93; remaining AI work requires a newly approved ceiling, render-only continuation stays credit-free, and only unavailable originals require reselection
- CLIP-27 [o] the result is previewable and downloadable as a standard MP4 in the selected ratio, accepted by short-form video services without re-encoding; the product submits it to no platform (→CLIP-115)
- CLIP-28 [x] direct publishing to any video platform, generated source footage, AI-drawn copy images, per-project fonts, arbitrary decorations, any in-product background music, custom transitions, variable speed ramps, reverse playback, freeze frames, frame synthesis and advanced motion templates — out of scope; the disclosure badge, information pairs, the intro and outro blocks, loudness normalisation and CLIP-98's fixed playback rates are in scope under CDS and are not decorations
- CLIP-29 [o] analysis sends only bounded compressed copies directly through the existing OpenRouter connection as inline video data, not a signed object URL or the original file; each copy covers at most 60 seconds, has a long edge at most 720 pixels and occupies at most 8 MiB before encoding ← an input modality flag alone does not establish signed-video-URL compatibility
- CLIP-30 [o] clip-analysis model admission is vendor-neutral: every registered observe model whose current catalog entry advertises video input is evaluated, and generation enables it only when a current OpenRouter endpoint can accept the bounded inline MP4 with every required request parameter and a complete price ceiling; no provider slug or model family allowlist may decide eligibility, and incompatibility is refused before credit approval or a model call; the quote, admission and each completion recheck read the live endpoint contract rather than relying on a previously cached eligibility result
- CLIP-31 [o] the composition model receives only bounded structured source analysis, template guidance and answers, never video bytes or video URLs; final rendering uses the original footage and only owner-enabled original audio, never the compressed analysis copies
- CLIP-32 [o] preparation verifies every source and every analysis copy before the first AI call; bounded recompression may occur only during preparation, and a copy still exceeding a limit fails without AI rather than silently increasing the chunk count, omitting footage or changing its timing
- CLIP-33 [o] clip media work runs one job at a time on the shared service and processes originals sequentially through bounded temporary disk storage; source verification and analysis-copy preparation share the same source read, analysis copies are not uploaded back to object storage, and transmission never accumulates whole original files or all encoded copies in memory
- CLIP-34 [o] local source previews survive attempt completion while the working page still uses them; leaving or refreshing releases local references without stopping a server job, and reopening obtains authorized playback access to unexpired retained originals without persisting media in browser storage
- CLIP-35 [x] a separate force-reanalysis or composition-only regeneration action remains deferred; explicit failure/cancellation continuation reuses compatible analysis under CLIP-93
- CLIP-36 [o] `/clips/$clipId` has three steps 클립 생성 · 클립 다듬기 · 클립 완성 under THEME-39: no editing state opens ①, an unfinalized editing state opens ② even when its render matches, and explicit finalization opens ③; ① contains settings/sources/models, ② correction with preview and download, and ③ only the retained result preview/download; step selection never finalizes, and finalized projects cannot reopen editing; panels share one mounted page and `/clips/new` has no step bar

- CLIP-38 [o] outside the focused job view, one status region prioritizes failing autosave, upload phase, unsaved/unrendered correction, save state and project state, with one `top-chrome` progress bar for upload; while a job is active CLIP-78 supplies the sole progress/status region instead (→THEME-40)
- CLIP-39 [o] the settings autosave on a pause as a draft's title and memo do (→POST-4): no 저장 button, no dirty gate on 생성 and no leave-confirmation dialog, and the approval action flushes the queue before it starts; `/clips/new` keeps exactly one committing action, 클립 만들기, in its dock ← the ratio it carries can never be changed again (CLIP-9), so the project may not be minted by an incidental first keystroke
- CLIP-40 [o] each editable step has one ActionBar: ① 생성 with model refusals and the approved ceiling; ② primary 확정하기 with its refusal, alongside 다시 렌더 and download of the identified latest successful result; ③ offers result download only; the template and model selectors remain in ①, and the focused job view replaces these controls with cancellation (→CLIP-75 →CLIP-78)
- CLIP-41 [o] `/clips` uses whole-row links, title, one status badge, template/ratio metadata, relative updated time, URL-backed title search/status filtering and one docked 새 클립 under POST-43 and POST-65 through POST-69; badges prioritize active work 생성 중/취소 중, failure 실패, cancellation 취소됨, then 초안/다듬는 중/완성, with 완성 reserved for explicit finalization and automatic polling until the latest job is terminal
- CLIP-42 [o] the video-template list and editor follow the post-template surfaces, with one saved draft shared by a composition editor and 원문 and one docked save; the editor presents the design selection before the body (→CLIP-111) and composition-specific controls follow CLIP-58 through CLIP-67
- CLIP-43 [o] analysis sends the prepared bounded copy as one pass, selects `static` processing only for an endpoint that supports it and otherwise omits provider-specific video-processing controls; adaptive video exploration is excluded
- CLIP-44 [o] the clip analysis-model picker shows every registered observe model, enables those currently eligible under CLIP-30 and gives each disabled model one stable reason: video input absent · compatible inline endpoint absent · required request parameters absent · complete price ceiling unavailable

- CLIP-45 [o] caption pace is independent of typography and footage playback rate: templates choose 문장형 (default, including older templates) or 빠른 구절형; generated rapid copy is split into sequential short phrases without an extra AI call, and step ② lets the owner change pace and edit individual phrases and transformed-output times; this is editorial timing, not forced alignment to recorded speech

- CLIP-46 [o] campaign disclosure is a template-authored fixed or answer-bound text element with editable content, visibility, position and timing; project creation and generation require no separate product-owned campaign-type selection (→CLIP-65 →CLIP-68)

- CLIP-47 [o] source selection uses a horizontal snapping strip with thumbnail, filename, duration, per-source upload state and an owner-only 원본 소리 유지 toggle that defaults off; selecting a source opens one larger preview from a matching local file or an authorized retained original, and progress follows CLIP-38 or CLIP-78 (→THEME-25)
- CLIP-48 [o] editable, non-running steps ① and ② offer read-only observations from the latest successful generation and, separately, completed observations from the latest failed or cancelled attempt: horizontal source choice, a recorded summary and expandable ranges, events, subjects, audible speech and quality notes; inspection makes no AI call and consumes no credits
- CLIP-49 [o] each observed scene shows the intersecting source ranges, cut numbers and playback rates used by the saved edit plan; usage follows saved cut order, creation, splitting, deletion, trimming and rate changes, and is labelled as the saved edit plan whenever its revision differs from the rendered result or there is no result ← unsaved corrections and a previous render must not be presented as the delivered video.
- CLIP-50 [o] observations remain readable without source footage in editable steps; source playback uses matching local media or an unexpired authorized retained original, with distinct empty states for expired/missing media, absent observations and a source with no observed ranges
- CLIP-51 [o] regeneration, failure and cancellation preserve previous successful observations labelled as previous results; unsuccessful-attempt observations and selected ranges never replace or borrow the provenance of the saved edit plan, and no selection reasons or confidence scores are invented
- CLIP-52 [x] observation editing, source-range exclusion before generation, live partial streaming and mandatory observation approval remain deferred; bounded response correction and failed-attempt continuation follow CLIP-93 and CLIP-94

- CLIP-53 [o] step ② places one composed video preview above a horizontally navigable timeline and the selected item's controls; the preview remains visible while editing, shrinks when the phone keyboard opens and preserves one document scroller and the committing action (→THEME-9 →THEME-25)
- CLIP-54 [o] timeline cut thumbnails and text-duration bars share one playhead; selecting a cut or text seeks its visible interval and opens only that item's fields, and playback highlights the current cut without switching the owner's focused editing field
- CLIP-55 [o] cut creation from an observed scene, splitting and trimming use source-time selection, draggable range handles, scrubbing and current-frame actions with precise time entry as an alternative; fixed-rate changes, reordering, deleting, source-sound changes, text edits and timing edits support undo/redo, and every drag operation has touch and keyboard alternatives
- CLIP-56 [o] the editing preview reflects the current draft's selected footage, fixed playback rate, crop, text, position, exposure and presence or absence of enabled source audio without requiring a full export; the latest completed render is separately identified and cannot stand in for an updated draft preview
- CLIP-57 [o] an unfinalized project without matching source media still permits text/timing editing and playback/download of its revision-labelled last render; current draft footage preview requests missing originals and never represents old burned-in text as updated composition (→CLIP-23 →CLIP-50)
- CLIP-58 [o] video templates offer 원문, 원문 복사 and 형식 안내 복사 like post templates (→TMPL-41 →TMPL-42), so an outside AI can author a supported composition for paste import; authoring, parsing and previewing a template make no model call
- CLIP-59 [o] the video composition vocabulary covers footage sections, repetition over selected scenes or authored item groups, scene-bound AI copy, exact fixed text, answer-bound text and instructions invisible in the output; only explicitly declared visible elements appear
- CLIP-60 [o] information fields have template-authored labels, prompts and required or optional presence; blank optional fields omit their dependent optional element without a placeholder, and neither price nor any business category field is universally required
- CLIP-61 [o] a repeated item group declares the owner-visible name it is called by and the number of items it admits, and the owner's controls carry that name and open at its minimum; each item retains its own name, price and other template-defined facts together, any number, unit, currency and price basis belongs to that item rather than to a shared project-wide price, item repetition never requires footage that was not selected, and a group saved without the declaration keeps a generic name and admits any number ← an undeclared group cannot be named or counted on screen, and refusing to load one would strand every template saved before the declaration existed
- CLIP-62 [o] an item may be associated with one or more observed source ranges; automatic association requires supporting subject evidence, uncertain associations remain unassigned, and the owner can assign or correct them beside the preview without changing the recorded observations (→CLIP-104)
- CLIP-63 [o] each generated scene-bound statement retains its source range and any referenced item facts; a caption about a visible item may use only that item's facts, while project-wide context is allowed only in a template-declared context section and cannot be presented as that item's price or identity
- CLIP-64 [o] copy follows the template's narrative and the selected scenes as a coherent sequence, avoids repeated generic promotion, and never invents taste, experience, numbers or identities from appearance alone; an unsupported item-specific statement falls back to an observed description or no copy, with the reason recorded as a CLIP-108 notice
- CLIP-65 [o] each visible template element declares generated or exact text and fact bindings; a content-region element also declares a supported automatic or explicit position and visibility, while an intro or outro slot inherits size, position and decoration from its preset skeleton (→CLIP-112); fixed text remains exact after answer substitution and is never rewritten by AI
- CLIP-66 [o] text intervals may cover the whole output, a span measured from the output start or end, or a span relative to a selected cut; output-relative elements may span cuts, while cut-relative elements follow their cut through reordering and are removed with it
- CLIP-67 [o] the editor distinguishes original source time from playback-rate-transformed output time in seconds with precise entry; creating, splitting, trimming, reordering or changing a rate recomputes cut and resolved element intervals, identifies intervals outside the output or their cut and requires correction before rendering rather than silently moving, retiming or dropping authored text
- CLIP-68 [o] a template without a disclosure or information element, or whose intro or outro slots are all empty, produces none of them (→CDS-73); no hidden category preset, fixed label list, minimum fact count, compulsory CTA or campaign type may insert or require on-screen content
- CLIP-69 [o] a generation freezes the selected template composition, answers and item associations; subsequent template edits do not change that attempt or an existing result, and manual correction edits the project's retained composition rather than its reusable template
- CLIP-70 [o] existing templates and projects remain readable with their prior content and visibility represented as explicit editable composition; conversion preserves current results and project-specific disclosure choices without applying one project's choices to other users of the template
- CLIP-71 [o] the composition editor and source mode share a lossless draft and the same supported contract as preview and final rendering; invalid syntax, an intro or outro departing from the selected preset skeleton, bindings and known timing or text constraints identify the element and line and prevent saving or generation before media preparation or AI without silently changing authored content
- CLIP-72 [o] the copy and timeline quality gate covers several differently priced items, ambiguous subject matches, missing optional information, same-source split cuts, every supported playback rate, source audio on and off and reordered cuts; the owner must be able to connect each item-specific sentence and transform to its visible subject, source-time evidence and own supplied facts

- CLIP-76 [o] finalization is irreversible for that project: preserve the confirmed result for preview/download, delete its originals and disable further source upload, editing, generation and rerender; downloading in ② neither finalizes nor deletes originals
- CLIP-77 [o] existing projects with successful results remain unfinalized and open in ② after migration; migration preserves their results and never infers consent to finalization or restores originals already deleted

- CLIP-82 [o] finalization and project deletion immediately deny new original access and durably request physical cleanup; interrupted deletion retries without restoring editing or losing the retained confirmed result, and temporary playback links remain short-lived without a promise to revoke an already issued link before object deletion

## flow
- create: author or paste a video template → define required/optional information and repeated items → choose template, ratio and target duration → fill required facts → select source footage and explicitly enable any original sound to retain → inspect or correct item associations when available → approve credit ceiling and generate
- template: choose intro preset · caption treatment · outro preset → skeleton with slots → 형식 안내 복사 → outside AI authors slot text and the content region → paste 원문 → validate with element/line errors → inspect composition and representative timing preview → save → select for a project
- observe: prepared bounded source intervals → observation model records ordered scenes across every interval → retain source-time evidence and explicit uncertainty → validate bounded coverage without editorial choices
- compose: ordered scene observations + template narrative + grouped answers → associate subjects with items or leave uncertain → choose, split and order non-overlapping source ranges with one fixed rate per cut → write exact scene-bound copy and explicit context → resolve transformed output times → validate, then repair or remove what validation refuses and record each notice → freeze the assembly document
- execute: validated assembly document + originals + owner source-audio choices → apply declared cut/rate/crop/transition/audio operations → split and typeset exact caption text by fixed rules → verify transformed output → encode result
- steps: ① settings → generate → ② preview, edit, rerender and download → flush saves and explicitly confirm a matching result → ③ result preview/download only
- generate: approve ceiling and cancellation rule → focused durable job → verify sources and prepare bounded analysis copies → reserve within approval (refusal: no AI/debit, preserve prior result and reusable originals) → reuse compatible evidence or observe missing chunks as ordered scenes with bounded response correction → reuse compatible assembly document or compose with bounded response correction → validate, repair or remove, and save recovery plan → execute originals and enabled source audio → ② preview and settlement → clean worker copies, renew original retention
- correct: ② composed draft preview → select observed scene, cut or text → create/split/trim/reorder, set fixed rate and source sound, edit/place/time → preview transformed draft → validate → 다시 렌더 → ② matching result; download is available before confirmation
- failure: identify stage and known check → inspect completed observations and selected original ranges from this attempt → keep prior successful state and reusable originals → settle under QUOTA-46 → clean worker copies → explicit continuation(missing AI work with new ceiling | retained plan render without AI or credits)
- cancel: open confirmation → continue production | explicitly confirm cancellation → durable 취소 중 → stop further work and release active media → settle under QUOTA-49 → 취소됨 with previous result/originals preserved → explicit retry
- finalize: flush saves → verify matching render and no active work → record confirmation and deny original access → ③ retained result → delete originals with recoverable cleanup
- expire: last meaningful work + 24 hours → deny new access to expired originals → cleanup when no active attempt protects them → keep result/plan/analysis, request reselection only when pixels are needed
- inspect: editable non-running step → successful-result observations | separately labelled unsuccessful-attempt work → source/range choice → recorded details and known failure measurements → local or retained-original playback, with reselection after expiry
- delete: deny project access → recoverably remove originals · result · analysis · edit plan · metadata

## constraints
- source limits: 20 videos per project · 30 minutes combined
- result limits: 15–90 seconds on the playback-rate-transformed output timeline after transition overlap · exactly one of 9:16, 16:9 or 1:1 per project
- source originals are private, time-limited editing inputs; confirmation or project deletion ends retention before the 24-hour deadline
- analysis limits apply before provider transmission: at most 60 seconds · long edge at most 720 pixels · at most 8 MiB per compressed copy; the encoded request and temporary workspace have separate finite bounds, and compression never authorizes extra paid calls
- analysis copies preserve source timing, visible events and audible speech; codec, frame-rate and bitrate choices require media-quality and resource verification under ARCH-24 and provider-contract verification under ARCH-33, not a change to original-footage rendering
- scene observations cover every prepared source-time span with a concise scene or explicit unknown, uncertain or unusable state; completeness never promises semantic perfection, and an observation that cannot satisfy this bounded contract fails without silently omitting a span under CLIP-90
- original uploads remain browser-to-private-storage transfers; inline analysis is a worker-to-provider exception and does not enlarge public RPC bodies or make source objects public
- worker-local original copies, analysis copies and encoded request bodies are cleaned on success, failure, cancellation and crash recovery; private object originals follow CLIP-73 through CLIP-76 and CLIP-82, and media bytes/signed links never enter logs, durable job payloads or persistent browser storage
- credit authority is QUOTA-43 through QUOTA-54; a provider unit-price limit and a per-attempt user-credit ceiling are distinct protections
- delivery must satisfy the common short-form upload contract — container, codec, resolution, frame rate and loudness (→CDS-52) — for all three ratios; a single platform's extra requirement belongs to a delivery profile, never to this contract or to the design constants (→CDS-78)
- surface: directories and editor chrome follow THEME-39 and POST-43 through POST-49 and POST-65 through POST-69; step ② uses CLIP-53 through CLIP-57 while preserving the mounted page and one scroller, with THEME-40 governing the focused running view

- template semantics stay bounded by the existing output durations, supported ratios, bundled fonts and the CDS region presets; source grammar, preview implementation and migration mechanics are implementation-task decisions
- draft preview supports source selection, split cuts, fixed rates, source-audio presence and text verification; frame-identical real-time rendering of all transitions, contrast adaptation, pitch treatment and final loudness on every device is not an established capability
- displayed item identity and scene-to-fact meaning require representative video review; schema validity and a project-wide text match alone do not establish semantic correctness
