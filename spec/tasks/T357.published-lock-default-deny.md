## goal
The published-post lock (POST-74) gets one service guard (`writablePost`), and two default-deny tests make every new write statement and every new exported `*post.Service` method fail until it is classified; photo and video deletes stop scanning every post.

## acceptance
- [ ] `post.Service` has one unexported `writablePost(ctx, userID, slug) (Post, error)` (ownedPost, then the published refusal). The nine pre-checks and two re-reads listed in impl notes call it. `refusePublished(` is called in exactly two places in `service.go`: inside `writablePost` and in `SaveContent`. `lockedOrGone` is built on `writablePost`, and its 13 call sites are unchanged.
- [ ] SQL default-deny: `TestEveryPostWriteStatementIsClassifiedForThePublishedLock` in `internal/post/store/published_lock_store_test.go` parses every `-- name:` statement in `internal/post/store/queries/*.sql`. It fails when a `predicateGuarded` body lacks `status <> 'published'`, and when a list names a statement that is gone.
- [ ] `TestEveryGuardedStatementRefusesAPublishedPost` (same file, today at :92) iterates `predicateGuarded` and `transactionGuarded`, so the tables the default-deny test checks are the ones this test runs. A statement added to either table without a case therefore cannot exist.

It fails when a method is in neither of `publishedLockGuarded` / `publishedLockExempt`, or in both. It fails when either list names a method that no longer exists, and when a guarded method has no case.

Every case answers `ErrPostPublished` and leaves `lockedSnapshot` unchanged. The table gains the cases the literal list lacked: `SetObservations`, `SetGeneratedContent` and `SaveDraft` with a 분야.

- [ ] Behavior unchanged: existing tests still pass, including `TestAWriteThatLosesTheRaceToAPublishIsRefusedAsLocked`, `TestIdenticalContentSaveOnAPublishedPostIsStillANoOp`, `TestDeletePostStillRemovesAPublishedPost` and `TestAFieldSaveOnAPublishedPostIsLocked`.
