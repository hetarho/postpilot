## goal
Provide explicit quoted narration generation that preserves compatible speech and owner timing.

## acceptance
- [ ] Speech quotes bind the exact current script/voice/settings and include only missing or stale segments; unchanged segments execute zero new calls and changing voice requires all speech.
- [ ] Successful audio is private, validated and revision-bound; delayed/duplicate/foreign results cannot replace a newer script, selected voice or project.
- [ ] Failure/cancellation/restart preserves compatible speech and the prior result, makes no automatic paid retry and settles reported usage under the ceiling once.
- [ ] Service/job/RPC tests cover partial success, storage/decode failure, null alignment, changing voice during work, removal/profile drift, stop races and too-long replacement audio without silent retiming.

## impl notes
- Add clip_speech.proto with QuoteClipSpeech and StartClipSpeech plus an owned segment-readiness projection. Reuse the initial-generation internal speech runner in T545 through a consumer-owned port, not by issuing nested jobs/admissions.
- Each explicit job freezes ordered stale segment ids, text/settings digest, confirmed voice/profile binding, current entitlement, planned counts and measured-compatible reusable assets. Speech request concurrency starts at one; every call is separately metered under the one frozen approval.
- Use dedicated spoken segment synthesis at natural 1x; no speed fitting, hidden splitting by caption phrases, automatic response correction, context strings or optional extra billable features. If context conditioning is later included, its full input/price must be qualified and frozen first.
- A regenerated segment shorter than its reserved window leaves silence; one longer becomes a durable fit conflict with retained new audio. Do not stretch/cut speech or shift owner cuts/captions. Publication keeps valid asset provenance even while the current plan requires an explicit fit decision.
- Use T533 bounded pure audio inspection for authoritative duration, preserve usable normalized alignment and mark absent/invalid timing explicitly. Stale segment audio may be replayed as identified prior audio but cannot satisfy render readiness.
