# T671 Keep the pick order and reorder photos in ①
> st:todo | ssot:POST-116 | base:POST@39 | dep:T670 | touches:frontend/src/features/upload-photos frontend/src/entities/post frontend/src/pages/editor | lane:-

## goal
Photos picked together confirm in the order they were picked, and ①'s photo strip reorders photos by drag under a pointer and by a one-action move control on a phone.

## acceptance
- [ ] the upload batch confirms its photos in pick order: conversion and PUT stay parallel, but a photo's `ConfirmUpload` waits until every earlier-picked photo of the batch has confirmed or failed for good (skipped, rejected, or a failure the user must retry); a later retry confirms at the end
- [ ] ①'s strip: each confirmed photo tile is draggable onto another photo's place under a fine pointer, and carries 옮기기, which picks it up: every other photo then offers 여기로 (before it), 맨 뒤로 follows the last, and a second press or Escape puts it back; rotate and delete stand aside meanwhile; uploading/failed tiles, videos, a read-only strip and a lone photo offer no move
- [ ] a move patches the cached post's photo order at once and calls `ReorderImages` with the full photo order; a refusal refetches the post and drops the local order
- [ ] read-only while published or while the strip is otherwise read-only (same gate as 회전 and delete)
- [ ] the large view's previous/next, the contact sheet and the storyline space's `빠진 사진` follow the server order with no sort of their own
- [ ] tests: upload-batch test that a photo picked first and converted last still confirms first, and that a permanently failed earlier photo releases the later ones; PhotoStrip tests for the move menu options and the reorder call, and that a video/uploading tile has no move control; Korean and English strings

## impl notes
- the chain is per batch: keep the batch's pick order as the list of item ids and gate each confirm on the predecessors' settled state; videos in a batch confirm without waiting (they are not reordered)
- labels: `{{filename}} 옮기기`, `{{filename}} 옮기기 취소`, 여기로 (`{{filename}} 앞으로 옮기기`), 맨 뒤로 and their English counterparts; no help text (owner rejects help copy on simple controls)
- pick-and-place rather than a position menu: `Menu` and `ActionMenu` draw their panel `absolute`, and the strip is a horizontal scroller that would clip it
- HTML5 drag events do not fire on touch, which is why the move control is the phone's way, not a fallback (POST-96's pattern)
