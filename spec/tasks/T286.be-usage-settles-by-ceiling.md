# T286 the ledger settles by approved ceiling, not by product kind
> st:todo | ssot:ARCH-6 ARCH-7 ARCH-24 | base:ARCH@5 | dep:T272

## goal
Remove every clip name from `internal/usage` by keying the ceiling rules on "this admission has an approved maximum" instead of on a job kind, without changing one line of charge arithmetic.

## acceptance
- [ ] `backend/internal/usage` contains no identifier, comment-level rule or SQL literal naming a product: `chargedClipKind` is gone, `usage/clip_pricing.go` is renamed (e.g. `ceiling.go`) and `ClipCredits ClipReservation PricedCall boundedClipCharge cancelledClipCharge ClipAccounting ClipAccountingForJob` carry generic names
- [ ] the predicate everywhere becomes "the admission carries an approved ceiling": `Hold` sets `approved_max_credits` whenever the start carries one, `Settle` and the accounting projection branch on `ApprovedMaxCredits != nil` / `approved_max_credits IS NOT NULL`, and `ClipAccountingForJob`'s `kind IN ('generate_clip','revise_clip')` filter is replaced by that condition
- [ ] `RecordCall`'s protected 5-second record window and `Record`'s frozen-price branch key on the frozen call price in context (`WithCallPrice`), not on a kind
- [ ] charge arithmetic is frozen: `boundedCharge`, `cancelledCharge`, the quote function, the failed-with-zero-confirmed waiver, the cancellation-fee waiver at policy version 1, the shadow/exempt projection and the overrun `spendUpTo` branch keep their exact expressions and produce identical credits for identical inputs
- [ ] the wire-visible strings are untouched: `CLIP_MODEL_PRICING_UNAVAILABLE`, `CLIP_QUOTE_REQUIRED` and `CLIP_CREDIT_CEILING_EXCEEDED` keep their literal values (the FE and the agent mirror them) even though their Go identifiers change
- [ ] parity is pinned: `usage`'s settlement, cancellation and ceiling tests and `cmd/api`'s settlement/cancellation/metering tests keep their current expected credit numbers with no edits beyond renamed symbols, and one added table test walks (outcome × confirmed cost × ceiling × policy version) asserting the same charges as before the rename
- [ ] behaviour unchanged: ARCH-26 passes and ARCH-28's `pnpm gen:sql` diff is clean

## impl notes
- from review/arch-260919 F15 (part 3 of 3; T285 is subject addressing, T272 the allowance)
- owner decision 260919, option C (de-name, freeze the math): the clip rules in `usage` are already equivalent to "an approved ceiling exists" — `chargedClipKind` gates exactly the paths that read `ApprovedMaxCredits` ([service.go:241](backend/internal/usage/service.go#L241), [service.go:441](backend/internal/usage/service.go#L441)) — so the product name can go without a policy registry on the money path. A per-kind `SettlementPolicy` port was rejected: it adds runtime wiring and a second verification surface to the charge path for a second product that does not exist yet
- do NOT move the quote/charge functions into `clip/app`: `Hold` needs the quote to size the reservation and `Settle` needs the bounded charge at boot from `OpenHolds`, where only the admission row exists. They stay in `usage` as ceiling primitives; only their names change
- cancellation stays keyed on `cancellation_policy_version` (already a generic column) — a version of 1 means "cancellable with fee", which is a ledger fact, not a clip fact
- `ClipAccounting` is the ledger's own projection and keeps its shape; the clip-facing rename happens only at the mapper in `clip/app/credits.go`, which already translates it into `clip.Accounting`
- dep on T272 is a file-collision dep as much as a logical one: both edit `cmd/api/credits.go` and the `usage.Start` reservation field
