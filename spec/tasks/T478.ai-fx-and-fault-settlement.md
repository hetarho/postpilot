## goal
Use one job-frozen KRW-per-USD conversion for quotes, holds and once-only settlement, with confirmed-use-only cancellation and separate service-fault compensation.

## acceptance
- [ ] The service selects the previous business day’s published reference, rounds KRW/USD up to a multiple of ten, and persists source/publication date/reference/applied rate before cost-incurring work. Friday/weekend/holiday, missing publication, fresh cached fallback and stale fallback are tested.
- [ ] The last confirmed publication is usable as a temporary rate only through seven calendar days of age in KST, with an explicit status. Missing/older reference blocks new paid AI before a call, while free-only AI, already-frozen jobs and fixed-KRW checkout remain possible.
- [ ] Quotes/holds and settlement use the same snapshot, integer arithmetic and ceil-once after summing the user-visible job’s billable cost. Zero confirmed cost is zero; base 2 and multiplier 3 disappear from all actual charging consumers. Multi-call/partial-failure jobs do not round each call separately.
- [ ] Provider fault or owner cancellation debits confirmed C only; service or unknown-cause failure debits C and issues ceil(C/2) in one independent seven-day lot. C=7 produces 4 compensation, C=0 produces none; unused holds return to their original expiries and cannot double as compensation.
- [ ] Cancellation/completion races, crash recovery and repeated settlement issue one final debit and at most one compensation lot. Master records shadow usage without spending or minting compensation. Unconfirmed cost is not retroactively billed; clip approved/reserved ceilings remain enforced.
- [ ] Usage/clip result projections expose gross debit, ordinary return, fault cause, compensation/expiry and net effect without rewriting raw provider costs. Fake-provider and real-store tests cover the fault matrix, expired-origin lots, origin-period overrun and frozen-rate replay. Run ARCH-26/28.

## impl notes
- Reuse backend/internal/fxrate’s Korea Eximbank adapter through a consumer-owned rate port; it already returns fixed-point E4 reference values and identifies nonpublication. Read current official documentation before changing adapter configuration under ARCH-33. Do not add a second vendor or public FX knob.
- Persist confirmed publication snapshots/cache records and the admission/approval’s chosen rate. Distinguish publication date from fetch time. A verified nonbusiness day can move lookup backward; a transport/malformed/unverifiable response cannot be interpreted as a holiday. Cache/single-flight daily lookup avoids one external fetch per user.
- Credit math is ceil(sum(cost_microusd) × applied_rate_E4 / 10^10), with checked integer multiplication or exact big-integer intermediates. Estimator milli-credit conversion uses the same denomination but leaves rounding until the represented item total. No binary floating-point prices.
- Freeze rate before issuing a clip approval and reuse that snapshot at admission/settlement while the approval remains valid; changed job inputs invalidate it as before. All-free admissions carry explicit zero-cost mode and require no FX; T480 supplies trusted free-path qualification and prevents a client from claiming it.
- Persist normalized terminal settlement cause; explicit provider errors/user cancellation are classified, service errors are service fault and uncertain errors are unknown. Completion already committed remains successful. A compensation key unique to the admission prevents issuance twice after restart.
- Add compensation to the lot kind constraint and shared read projections on fresh proto tags. Raw supplier usage stays append-only. Keep executor budgets, safe multimodal pricing, provider-unit ceilings and clip correction limits intact; tests must assert no unreserved fallback call.
- T485 renders these settlement fields and cancellation copy. This task completes the server semantics and contract; no cash refund is triggered here.
