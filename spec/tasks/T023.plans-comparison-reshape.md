## impl notes
- THEME-37 is the only reason a border is allowed here, and it allows exactly one marked option per view: the stroke, the shadow and the badge all hang off the single `offer.recommended` flag the server sends, so a second emphasised card is impossible by construction rather than by review
- `md:grid-cols-2` before `lg:grid-cols-4` because four rungs at 768 px leave ~180 px per card and the price, grant and estimate lines wrap into three ragged lines ← the breakpoint is chosen where the content breaks, not where the device is (THEME-14)
- the estimate is display-only and never gates anything: `estimatedPosts` arrives as an integer from `plan.EstimatedPosts` (T020) and the page does no arithmetic on it ← the charge formula is a server-owned product rule the client must not re-implement (QUOTA-8), which is also why the caveat is copy rather than a computed range
- `PlanCard` keeps its `bg-surface-raised rounded-lg p-4` card shape — a rung is THEME-13's sanctioned "item in a grid of peers" — and the recommended stroke is added to that same element, not to a wrapper, so nothing nests a card in a card
- the recommended rung does NOT get a filled CTA: one CTA per view (THEME-18) and every rung's action is disabled until BILLING ships, so emphasis is carried by the badge, the stroke and the shadow alone
- the caveat sits once under the list rather than in every card ← repeating an assumption four times reads as fine print, and the list is one comparison
