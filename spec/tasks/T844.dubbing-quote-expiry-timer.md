# T844 Stop the per-second re-render of the dubbing editor
> st:todo | ssot:ARCH-24 | base:ARCH@25 | dep:T843 | touches:frontend/src/features/regenerate-clip-speech/ui/ClipDubbingEditor.tsx | lane:fe-clip | explore:- | run:claude/sonnet@high

## goal
An open speech quote no longer re-renders the whole dubbing editor every second; the editor re-renders once when the quote expires, and the plan's draft key is computed once per plan.

## acceptance
- [v] The 1 s `setInterval` (ClipDubbingEditor.tsx:66-70) is replaced by one timeout that fires at `quote.quote.expiresAt` (clamped to at least 0 and to the browser's maximum timeout, re-armed when the quote changes and cleared on unmount), so the quote turns invalid at the same moment as before (`Date.parse(expiresAt) <= now`).
- [v] `clipDraftKey(plan)` at line 58 is memoized on `plan`; the derived segment lists may be memoized on `plan` too; the explicit `clipDraftKey(current.current.plan)` calls in the handlers stay.
- [v] A regression test with fake timers proves an open quote causes no re-render or `clipDraftKey` call between opening and expiry (e.g. spy on the key function or count renders) and that the estimate becomes invalid at expiry; it fails on the base code.
- [v] Behavior unchanged: existing regenerate-clip-speech and dubbing tests pass; task check passes (related vitest, ESLint and Prettier on changed files, `tsc -b`, `pnpm lint:fsd`, `pnpm lint:style`).
