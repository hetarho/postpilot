## goal
The browser render waits for its sampling job, draws from assets bound to its render, and delivers the server's scrim.

## acceptance
- [ ] After `StartClipRender`, the render's own progress in ② shows a sampling phase (CLIP-156) and polls the returned job until it ends; a failed job refuses the render with that one reason and leaves server rendering as the choice (CLIP-155); cancelling the render during sampling cancels it.
- [ ] Every `PrepareClipPreview` and `PrepareClipCaptionFrames` call a browser render makes carries its `render_id`; the editing preview never sends one.
- [ ] The sampling phase and its refusal have ko and en copy in the feature's i18n config.
- [ ] Tests: `run-render.test` (the wait, a failed job, cancellation during sampling and the call order), `preview.test` (`render_id` only for renders), `useBrowserRender.test`.
- [ ] Real check with no provider call: render a dev project whose originals are retained in Chrome (Playwright, system channel) and on the server; the outro frames over a bright ground now match within encoding noise (T456 measured a mean absolute difference of about 2–3 of 255 elsewhere and 15 % of pixels off over the outro). The T456 project `37b4a30d091930418f3a52be7307b824` keeps its originals until 260930 00:56 UTC; with none retained, a new generation needs the owner's approval, otherwise the check is recorded as not run.
- [ ] ARCH-31's local gates pass.
