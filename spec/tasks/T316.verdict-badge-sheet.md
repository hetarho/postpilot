# T316 A winner verdict carries badges through one confirmation sheet
> st:todo | ssot:MODEL-32 MODEL-36 MODEL-37 MODEL-42 MODEL-61 MODEL-62 MODEL-63 ARCH-3 ARCH-6 ARCH-7 ARCH-10 ARCH-13 ARCH-20 ARCH-24 THEME-23 THEME-27 | base:MODEL@14 ARCH@7 THEME@17 | dep:T314

## goal
- Every winner verdict opens one badge sheet whose confirm commits the verdict with optional positive, negative and 기타 badges per candidate; badges are stored as immutable verdict metadata and shown on the decided detail.

## acceptance
- [ ] ChooseWinner and DecideWriteExperiment accept per-candidate badge lists (zero or more per candidate, positive and negative mixed) and an optional 기타 note of at most 200 characters, written in the same transaction as the verdict and never changed afterwards; UseSingleCandidate and DismissExperiment accept none.
- [ ] Validation: candidate ids must belong to the experiment; duplicates collapse; `in_voice` / `off_voice` on an observe experiment, a note without `other`, or a note over 200 characters are refused `InvalidArgument` with the new stable reason `EXPERIMENT_BADGES_INVALID`, and the verdict is not recorded.
- [ ] Badges never enter the Elo replay or match counts; the 30-day sweep and the DeletePost purge clear 기타 notes while badge ids stay; account deletion removes them with the experiment.
- [ ] GetExperiment/ListExperiments expose each candidate's badges and note only alongside its revealed identity; nothing about badges crosses the wire before a verdict.
- [ ] Frontend: pressing `이 결과로 선택`, `결과 적용` or `결과 적용하고 활성 모델로 변경` opens a `Sheet` (bottom sheet on a phone, centred on desktop) titled by the action, showing the chosen and the unchosen candidate by their blind A/B labels, each with positive and negative badge toggles and 기타 with a counted note field (200 max); the sheet's confirm performs the one mutation with the badges and shows pending state; cancel changes nothing; zero badges confirm; the voice pair is hidden for observe.
- [ ] Dismissal and the unpaired survivor's action open no sheet; the analyze application confirm dialog is unchanged.
- [ ] The decided detail's reveal band lists each candidate's badges and note beside its model label.
- [ ] Tests: BE service/store (transactional write, every validation case, purge clears notes only, blind mapping), handler (new reason in the allowlist); FE (sheet open/cancel/confirm per action, toggling for both candidates, note limit, observe hides the voice pair, request payload, reveal band, enum mirror pin); ARCH-25/26/28 gates pass.
