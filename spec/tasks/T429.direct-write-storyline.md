## goal
On 바로 글 쓰기 the one write call answers `storyline` first — paragraphs in order, each naming the attachments it uses — and the post along it; the post stores the storyline, GetPost returns it, deleting an attachment takes it out, a revision leaves it and an applied A/B winner keeps its own.

## impl notes
- Rewritten from the flow-first version: GEN-67 now keeps the storyline instead of dropping a `flow` member.
- Wording (keep the meaning):
  - storyline rule `storyline에는 본문을 쓰기 전에 이 글을 어떤 순서로 이야기할지 문단별로 정하세요. 각 문단은 그 부분에서 무엇을 보여주고 말할지 두세 문장의 계획(…를 보여줍니다)으로 쓰고, files에는 그 부분에 놓을 첨부 파일명을 적으세요. 첨부 사진과 영상은 모두 정확히 한 문단에 한 번씩 넣고, 템플릿이 있으면 템플릿의 자리 순서를 따르세요. 본문은 이 storyline을 따라 쓰세요.` / `Before writing, set in storyline how this post will tell things, paragraph by paragraph: each paragraph is a plan of two or three sentences saying what that part shows and says, and files names the attachments that part uses. Put every attached photo and video in exactly one paragraph, following the template's places in order when there is a template. Then write the post along this storyline.`
  - IMAGE line `첨부 사진은 storyline에서 그 사진이 놓인 문단의 자리에 IMAGE 블록으로 정확히 한 번씩 놓으세요. 템플릿의 사진 자리에는 그 자리 주변이 다루는 내용에 맞는 사진을 놓으세요.` / `Place every attached photo exactly once, as an IMAGE block where its storyline paragraph stands; at a template's photo place, put the photos that fit what the section around it is about.`
  - VIDEO line `VIDEO 블록은 첨부 영상 파일명만 쓰고, storyline에서 그 영상이 놓인 문단의 자리에 놓으세요.` / `A VIDEO block may use only an attached video filename. Place it where its storyline paragraph stands.`
- The storyline is written in the frozen target language (GEN-68's form).
- `MadeWith` is the writing stage's attachment list (GEN-12): a photo confirmed after dequeue is neither in the prompt nor in `MadeWith`, so it reads as added.
- Write both columns in one transaction; keep `SetGeneratedContent`'s published guard.
- The notice, the owner's edits and writing from the storyline are later tasks (T434, T436); this task only stores and exposes it.
- Why an allowance: the storyline adds a few hundred output tokens, and a write already risks truncation at the 8192 floor; the allowance keeps the post's own budget and changes the credit hold slightly and nothing else.
- Checking a real model is a paid call: ask the owner first.
