# T215 ② edits captions on their own track
> st:todo | ssot:CLIP-17 CLIP-54 CLIP-55 CLIP-67 CLIP-134 CLIP-138 CDS-60 | base:CLIP@31 CDS@20 ARCH@3 | dep:T210

## goal
Give step ② a caption track independent of cuts: add, remove, retime and rewrite narration captions on absolute output time, keep them in place when cuts change, and show the new notices.

## acceptance
- [ ] `ClipTimeline` renders narration captions in one caption lane spanning the output regardless of cut boundaries — a caption crossing cuts is one bar — and keeps the fixed-region lanes
- [ ] `ClipTextControls` for a narration caption offers text, absolute start and end (`ClipTimeField`), remove, and rapid phrase editing in absolute time; a caption-lane action adds a caption at the playhead with a 2 s default snapped into free room; no position, accent or pace control
- [ ] cut edits keep caption times; `validateClipPlan` flags a caption beyond the output or overlapping another by instance, and 다시 렌더 stays disabled until the owner fixes it (no automatic retime)
- [ ] `edit-plan.ts` and `api/edit-plan.ts` map `narration` and the request-only `creation` marker; `timeline.ts` gains add/edit/remove edits for narration captions with undo/redo
- [ ] `notices.ts` drops the item_* and `copy_not_generated` keys and adds `caption_overlap`, `caption_outside_output`, `caption_floor` with ko/en strings; `ClipAssociationControls` is removed from ②
- [ ] tests: lane rendering across cuts; add, retime and remove with undo; validation flags; notice mapping; verify FE (ARCH-25)

## impl notes
- files (agent map): features/correct-clip/ui/{ClipCorrectionWorkspace,ClipTimeline,ClipTextControls}.tsx · features/correct-clip/model/useClipCorrection.ts · entities/clip-project/model/{edit-plan,timeline,caption-pace,notices}.ts · entities/clip-project/api/edit-plan.ts · resources/{ko,en}/clips.ts
- selection stays `{kind:'text', id: instanceId, phrase?}`; a new caption carries a client-side temporary id until the saved plan returns the server-minted `narration-N`, matched back by start order
- `clipTextTracks` already lays bars in lanes; the change is that a narration bar's interval comes from its own absolute times rather than a cut's offset
- every control is a `shared/ui` primitive; no native select
