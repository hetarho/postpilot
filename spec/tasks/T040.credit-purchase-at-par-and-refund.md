# T040 Buy credits at par at any time, and refund an untouched purchase within seven days
> st:todo | ssot:BILLING-9 BILLING-11 BILLING-12 BILLING-13 BILLING-15 QUOTA-34 QUOTA-12 QUOTA-11 | base:BILLING@2 QUOTA@5 ARCH@2 | dep:T037

## goal
Let any signed-in account with a card buy credits at $1 = 100 credits as one charge that opens a never-expiring `purchased` lot, and refund such a purchase in full while none of it is spent and fewer than seven days have passed, voiding the lot.

## acceptance
- [ ] `billing.proto`: `QuotePurchase{int32 usd_cents}` → `{ int32 credits, int64 krw, int64 krw_per_usd_e4, string rate_date }`, `PurchaseCredits{int32 usd_cents}` → `{ Purchase purchase }`, `RefundPurchase{string purchase_id}` → `{ Purchase purchase }`; `Purchase` carries `id, credits, usd_cents, krw, charged_at, refunded_at, bool refundable`; `pnpm gen:proto` committed
- [ ] `billing.Service.PurchaseCredits(ctx, userID, usdCents)`: refuses `usdCents < 100` (`PURCHASE_TOO_SMALL`, invalid_argument) and a missing payment method (`PAYMENT_METHOD_REQUIRED`); `credits = usdCents` (par, QUOTA-34 — `plan.CreditsPerUSDCent = 1` as a named constant with a test); quotes the rate, charges `KRWFor(usdCents, rate)` with `orderID = "buy:<purchase id>"` outside the tx; on success: `Credits.OpenPurchasedLot(userID, credits)` then in one tx the `credit_purchases` row (with `lot_id`) and a `charge` event; a purchase changes no tier, no anchor, no `auto_renew` (a test asserts the subscription row is byte-identical before and after); a failed charge appends `charge_failed` and opens no lot
- [ ] `billing.Service.RefundPurchase(ctx, userID, purchaseID)`: the purchase must belong to the actor and be unrefunded (`PURCHASE_NOT_FOUND`); `charged_at + 7 × 24 h > now` else `REFUND_WINDOW_CLOSED`; then `Credits.VoidUntouchedLot(lotID)` — the single guarded UPDATE from T036 — and `usage.ErrLotTouched` becomes `PURCHASE_SPENT` (failed_precondition); then `Provider.Refund(paymentKey, "purchase refund")`; if the provider refuses, `Credits.RestoreLot(lotID, credits)` puts the lot back and `REFUND_FAILED` is returned; on success `refunded_at` and a `refund` event with the same USD, rate and KRW as the charge; tests cover the window, the spent lot (spend 1 credit first), the provider failure restoring the lot, and the success path
- [ ] `refundable` in `GetMyBilling` is computed server-side (`refunded_at IS NULL AND charged_at + 7d > now AND remaining == granted`) so the client never decides eligibility; the store exposes `LotUntouched(lotID)` via usage for the projection
- [ ] mails (BILLING-12): `purchase` and `refund` mails to the verified email, naming credits, USD, KRW and rate; templates as pure functions with tests
- [ ] FE `/billing` purchases section: a `Stepper` in whole dollars (min 1, max 100, default 5) showing `credits` and the KRW figure from `QuotePurchase` with the rate sentence, one action `크레딧 구매 / Buy credits` → confirm `Dialog` (amount, KRW, "never expires, spent last") → `PurchaseCredits` → success `Notice`; the list of purchases newest first with `환불 / Refund` on each `refundable` one → confirm `Dialog` stating the seven-day rule → `RefundPurchase`; `PURCHASE_SPENT`, `REFUND_WINDOW_CLOSED`, `REFUND_FAILED`, `CHARGE_FAILED` render localized; a test per state; both actions invalidate `GetMyBilling` and `GetMyPlan`

## impl notes
- par means one US cent buys one credit, no bonus and no minimum beyond a dollar (QUOTA-34); the FE stepper works in whole dollars only for the UI, the proto stays in cents so the rule lives in one place
- the refund voids FIRST, then asks the provider ← the void is the lock that stops a spend racing the refund; SQL's `remaining = granted` predicate is the whole check (QUOTA-11's posture), and a restore is the compensating write when the provider refuses
- the lot is voided, not deleted: history stays readable and the `refund` event references it
- `refundable` on the wire is a projection, never stored; a stale client that shows the button after the window gets `REFUND_WINDOW_CLOSED` and nothing else
- purchases are independent of any subscription (BILLING-9): the service must not read `subscriptions` at all in this path — the byte-identical assertion is the test of that
- receipts are the provider's (BILLING-13): the screen shows the product's own charge history and never generates a receipt or tax document
