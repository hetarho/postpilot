# T247 ②'s dock is the revision composer over 렌더하기 and 확정하기
> st:todo | ssot:CLIP-40 CLIP-131 CLIP-132 CLIP-153 | base:CLIP@36 | dep:T246 T242

## goal
②'s one ActionBar becomes two rows — the revision request's composer at the bottom and 렌더하기 beside 확정하기 above it — with 렌더하기 naming the kind it will use and offering the other.

## acceptance
- [ ] ②'s dock holds exactly two rows: 렌더하기 and 확정하기 above, and the revision composer — target, field and send — below
- [ ] 렌더하기 names the kind it will use and offers the other kind; it reads 다시 렌더 once a render of the current plan exists
- [ ] the composer is reachable without scrolling at every scroll position and at 360 px, and the field grows with its content without pushing the rows out of reach
- [ ] pressing send opens the credit approval over the composer; approving starts the revision and nothing is sent before approval
- [ ] while a revision runs, the composer row is replaced by its progress and cancellation and the page stays where it is
- [ ] no revision panel stands in ②'s page and no download stands in the dock
- [ ] vitest: the dock holds the two rows and nothing else; 렌더하기 names its kind, offers the other and becomes 다시 렌더 once a render exists; sending opens the approval without starting; approving starts once with the typed target
- [ ] verify: `pnpm --filter ./frontend test` · `pnpm lint` · `pnpm lint:fsd` · `pnpm lint:style:probe && pnpm lint:style` · `pnpm build:web`

## impl notes
- `ClipRevisionRequest` moves from the workspace's panel into the dock and loses its `section`/heading shape; its `useClipRevision` wiring, its `flush` prop and its quote/approval path are unchanged
- the composer row is target + field + send on one line from `sm:`, wrapping to field-over-row on a phone; the target stays a `SegmentedControl` of `CLIP_REVISION_TARGETS` and the send is an icon `Button` named by `revision.send`
- the approval is `ClipQuoteApproval` inside a `Popover` anchored to send with `placement="above"`, `align="end"`, `phone="sheet"`, so it opens over the composer rather than growing the dock
- the kind is what T242 put on `StartClipRender`; the offer of the other kind is a secondary control beside 렌더하기, never a native `<select>` (THEME-29) — a `Menu` on the button or a second ghost control, whichever keeps the row at one line from `sm:`
- the browser kind is refused by the service until the browser cluster lands, so this task offers it only where the service would accept it; server is the default the button names
- the character count moves to the field's own message slot; `boundedText` with `CLIP_PROJECT_LIMITS.instruction` is unchanged
- `revision.help` and `revision.title` are cut; `revision.readOnly` stays for the running row; `timeline.render` is re-worded to 렌더하기 with a separate 다시 렌더 key
- THEME-24 already admits a dock of more than one row, so no primitive changes and `ActionBar` keeps `dock="always"`
