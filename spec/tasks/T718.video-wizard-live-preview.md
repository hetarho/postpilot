## goal
The video-template wizard draws the clip its answers make beside the stages, order, headings, per-stage and name steps, placed like the post wizard's preview, instead of only at the design step.

## acceptance
- [ ] From the stages step to 저장 (stages · order · headings · each stage · finish) the wizard shows `ClipWizardPreview`; the target questions show none. At `lg` and wider it stands in a right column, sticky below the app chrome with its own scroll, beside a readable-measure step; below `lg` a floating `미리보기` button above Back/next opens it in a bottom `Sheet` and glows once per change of the built body (not on first paint).
- [ ] `ClipWizardPreview` lists the checked stages in outline order by heading (role name while the heading is blank), marks the stage whose step is on screen with `aria-current="step"` and a ring, and draws `CompositionPreview` over the body the stages make, with the draft's intro/outro presets and allowed caption styles.
- [ ] Before the design step the preview draws the design selection without offering it: no intro/outro selector and no caption-style checkbox. The design step's content is the same `ClipWizardPreview` with presets and caption styles offered and editing the draft; the design step shows no side preview or 미리보기 button.
- [ ] A body that does not parse mid-edit keeps the last parsed preview on show rather than blanking it.
- [ ] The post wizard keeps its behavior with the dock moved to shared UI.
- [ ] Tests: new `features/create-clip-template-wizard/ui/ClipWizardPreview.test.tsx` (stage order and headings, current mark, no design controls without a change handler, design controls with one); `entities/clip-template/ui/CompositionPreview.test.tsx` new case `draws handed-in presets without offering them when no change handler is given`; new `shared/ui/wizard-frame/WizardPreviewDock.test.tsx` (glow only after a change, the button opens the sheet under its label), with the PreviewDock cases moved out of `features/create-template-wizard/ui/WizardPreview.test.tsx`; `pages/video-template/ui/VideoTemplateWizard.test.tsx` new case `shows the live preview from the stages step on, none on the target questions and one on the design step`; existing `TemplateWizard`/`WizardPreview`, `VideoTemplatePage`, `VideoTemplateDesign` and `widgets/creation-setup` tests pass.
- [ ] vitest for the touched slices and their consumers, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm lint:fsd`, `pnpm format:check` (or the repo's prettier check) and the TypeScript build check pass.
