# T236 a selected cut or caption opens its own sheet
> st:todo | ssot:CLIP-53 CLIP-143 CLIP-109 CLIP-17 | base:CLIP@34 | dep:T235

## goal
Selecting a cut or a caption on ②'s timeline opens that item's controls in a bottom sheet carrying the frame they are edited against, and the notice naming that item rides inside it.

## acceptance
- [ ] selecting a cut opens a sheet holding that cut's source range, rate, crop, volume, order and delete controls over its source frame; the page behind keeps the preview and the timeline
- [ ] selecting a caption opens a sheet holding its text, output-time interval, phrase timing, style and the placement stage over the output frame its interval starts in
- [ ] closing a sheet clears the selection, and the timeline's pressed state follows it in both directions
- [ ] a notice naming a cut or a caption appears in that item's sheet and nowhere else in ②
- [ ] the sheet's committing and destructive controls stay reachable at 360 px with the keyboard open, and the sheet's body is the only thing that scrolls inside it
- [ ] vitest: selecting a cut opens its sheet with that cut's controls and its notice; selecting a caption opens the placement stage; closing either clears the selection; no cut or caption control renders in the page while no item is selected
- [ ] verify: `pnpm --filter ./frontend test` · `pnpm lint` · `pnpm lint:fsd` · `pnpm lint:style:probe && pnpm lint:style` · `pnpm build:web`

## impl notes
- use `shared/ui` `Sheet` directly (`open`, `labelledBy`, `header`, `footer`, `onClose`): it is already the phone bottom sheet, portalled, focus-trapped and scroll-locked, and it stacks, so a delete confirmation `Dialog` opened from inside one sits on top
- `open` is derived from `timeline.selection`, never from its own state; `onClose` dispatches `{ type: 'select', selection: undefined }` so one source of truth drives both the sheet and the timeline's `aria-pressed`
- the cut sheet takes the existing `ClipCutAssemblyControls` plus the order/delete row and the `correction.cut`/`timeline.outputRange` heading that `ClipCorrectionWorkspace` renders today; the caption sheet takes `ClipTextControls` and `ClipCaptionStage`, plus the caption's move-up / move-down buttons T235 left in the workspace row
- `ClipCaptionStage` is the frame the caption is placed over and already renders one; the cut sheet's frame is the SOURCE preview at the cut's start (`sourcePreview`), not the composed output, because trimming is source-time work (CLIP-67)
- the workspace's `measureEditingRoom` sizes the page preview against the dock; a sheet is portalled and outside that measurement, so leave the measurement alone and let the sheet own its own height
- notices: `ClipNoticeList` filtered to the open item's `cutId`/`elementId` moves inside the sheet; the workspace keeps only the filter for notices whose target no longer exists, which T237 moves into the info control
- two sheets are never open at once — one selection, one sheet; render one `Sheet` whose content switches on `selection.kind`
