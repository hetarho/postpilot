# T244 ②'s timeline reads at any cut length
> st:done@260918 | ssot:CLIP-54 CLIP-55 CLIP-38 | base:CLIP@37 | dep:-

## goal
Every cut on ②'s timeline shows its own thumbnail from first draw, no track label overlaps its neighbour at any cut length, and undo/redo become icon controls at the timeline's head with the save state left to the one status region.

## acceptance
- [v] every cut draws its thumbnail as soon as the track renders, with no click and no playhead needed; the film glyph remains only where no playable source is available
- [v] the second ruler, the cut labels and the text bars never overlap a neighbour at any cut length; a bar too narrow for its own label carries none
- [v] undo and redo are icon controls at the timeline's head, each named for what it undoes, at the pointer floor THEME-23 sets
- [v] the workspace no longer prints 저장됨 / 저장 중 / 저장할 변경 사항 있음 — that state is the page-top status region's alone
- [v] vitest: a plan of short adjacent cuts renders one label per cut with none overlapping (asserted on the computed geometry, not pixels); every cut has a thumbnail before any selection; undo/redo are reachable by their accessible names
- [v] verify: `pnpm --filter ./frontend test` · `pnpm lint` · `pnpm lint:fsd` · `pnpm lint:style:probe && pnpm lint:style` · `pnpm build:web`

## impl notes
- `ClipTimeline` gates the `<video>` on `active || selected` and falls back to the `Film` glyph; drop the gate and keep the glyph for a cut whose fingerprint has no entry in `localSources` and no resolved playback
- overlap comes from absolutely positioned labels with no width bound: give each ruler tick and each label the width of its own cut, in the same percentage basis the `<li>` already uses, plus `truncate`; drop a ruler tick whose cut is narrower than a new `CLIP_TIMELINE.minLabelPx` in `shared/config` rather than a literal
- the label-fit decision is pure geometry: put it in a helper under `entities/clip-project/model` and test the helper directly, with the component test asserting only the rendered result
- undo/redo icons: `Undo2` / `Redo2` from lucide-react, `Button` `variant="ghost"` `size="icon"`, `aria-label` from `timeline.undo` / `timeline.redo`, moved into the row that heads `ClipTimeline`
- the caption's move-up / move-down buttons stay where they are; T245 takes them into the caption sheet
- delete the `Typography role="status"` carrying `timeline.saving|unsaved|saved` from `ClipCorrectionWorkspace`; `ClipStatusLine` already reports it under CLIP-38, so the keys stay

## result
- `ClipTimeline` draws each cut's `<video>` as soon as the track renders; the `active || selected` gate is gone and the `Film` glyph is kept only for a fingerprint with no entry in `localSources`.
- overlap: every ruler tick now carries the width of its own cut in the same percentage basis the `<li>` uses, plus `truncate`; the cut name, the rate line and each text bar's label are width-bounded the same way. A bar narrower than `CLIP_TIMELINE.minLabelPx` (44, new in `shared/config/clip-composition.ts`) carries no label at all rather than an ellipsis, so two narrow neighbours cannot read as one smear.
- the fit decision is pure geometry in `entities/clip-project/model/timeline.ts` (`timelineBarPx`, `timelineLabelFits`), tested directly in `timeline.test.ts`; the new `ClipTimeline.test.tsx` asserts the rendered result — the laid-out percentages, one label per cut with none reaching its neighbour, the dropped labels at 20 px, a thumbnail per cut with no selection, and the glyph fallback.
- undo/redo are `Button variant="ghost" size="icon"` (`Undo2`/`Redo2`) in a row that heads `ClipTimeline`, named by `timeline.undo`/`timeline.redo` and sized at THEME-23's pointer floor by the primitive. `ClipCorrectionWorkspace` passes them as one `history` prop and no longer prints 저장됨 / 저장 중 / 저장할 변경 사항 있음 — CLIP-38's status region is the only place that state is reported. The three copy keys stay, since `ClipStatusLine` and `useSaveStatus` use them.
- the row that held them now renders only when a caption's cut is selected, instead of staying as an empty flex box.
- dropping a label would have dropped the control's NAME with it, leaving a bar nothing could reach: each cut button now carries `aria-label` from `correction.cut` and each text bar from its own text, so the name is the control's and only the drawing is conditional. `ClipCorrection.test.tsx`'s rapid-phrase case, whose phrase bars are narrower than the floor, is what caught it.
