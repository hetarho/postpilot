# T464 A browser render waits for its sampling job and draws from render-bound assets
> st:done@260929 | ssot:CLIP-192 CLIP-159 CLIP-157 CLIP-156 CLIP-155 | base:CLIP@52 ARCH@14 | dep:T463 | touches:frontend/src/features/render-clip-browser/ frontend/src/entities/clip-preview/

## goal
The browser render waits for its sampling job, draws from assets bound to its render, and delivers the server's scrim.

## acceptance
- [v] After `StartClipRender`, the render's own progress in ② shows a sampling phase (CLIP-156) and polls the returned job until it ends; a failed job refuses the render with that one reason and leaves server rendering as the choice (CLIP-155); cancelling the render during sampling cancels it.
- [v] Every `PrepareClipPreview` and `PrepareClipCaptionFrames` call a browser render makes carries its `render_id`; the editing preview never sends one.
- [v] The sampling phase and its refusal have ko and en copy in the feature's i18n config.
- [v] Tests: `run-render.test` (the wait, a failed job, cancellation during sampling and the call order), `preview.test` (`render_id` only for renders), `useBrowserRender.test`.
- [v] Real check with no provider call: render a dev project whose originals are retained in Chrome (Playwright, system channel) and on the server; the outro frames over a bright ground now match within encoding noise (T456 measured a mean absolute difference of about 2–3 of 255 elsewhere and 15 % of pixels off over the outro). The T456 project `37b4a30d091930418f3a52be7307b824` keeps its originals until 260930 00:56 UTC; with none retained, a new generation needs the owner's approval, otherwise the check is recorded as not run.
- [v] ARCH-31's local gates pass.

## impl notes
- Poll the job the way the server render's progress already does; the sampling phase is the first part of the browser render's own progress, before preparing assets.
- `render_id` reaches `prepare-assets.ts` and the loaders in `entities/clip-preview/api/preview.ts` as an explicit argument; the shared hook keeps working unchanged for the editing preview.

## result

A job that ended otherwise throws `BrowserRenderSamplingError`, withdraws the render and refuses the browser kind with the job's reason and a pointer to the server render; cancelling during sampling cancels the render, which stops the job. The project's `latestJob`, list badge and accounting pass over sampling jobs (`job.Filter.ExceptKinds`, `LatestForProjectExcept`, `clip.ProjectLatestExcept`), so ② never shows the sampling as a project job or switches to the focused job view.
