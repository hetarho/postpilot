## goal
In the video-template block builder each outline entry — the intro, the outro and the badge included — opens its editor inside its own row with its delete there, and a newly added entry opens for typing.

## acceptance
- [v] `entities/clip-template/ui/CompositionBuilder.tsx`: no single `activeEditor` above the outline; each row expands in place with at most one open at a time, its editor and its delete inside the row; removing one line of a multi-line entry stays in the entry's editor and is not the entry's delete
- [v] `add()` opens the new entry in place with focus in its first field
- [v] dragging and the move buttons keep working with a row open (`SortableList`)
- [v] tests: open in place, delete inside the row, add opens and focuses, one open at a time, reorder with a row open
- [v] verify FE: `pnpm --filter ./frontend test` · `pnpm lint` · `pnpm lint:fsd` · `pnpm lint:style:probe && pnpm lint:style` · `pnpm build:web`
