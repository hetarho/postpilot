### the plan-shape change, and why acceptance item 2 was amended
Sequential per-block work ("write a block, convert it, write the next") is not buildable:
after a quote or list conversion both the caret and `body_end` land somewhere that corrupts
the next block. The body is therefore written in two passes — every block as a plain
paragraph in manifest order, then the quote and list conversions — and the conversion pass
runs BACKWARDS, because a quotation adds its citation paragraph and a list adds its remaining
items, each shifting the index of every paragraph after it.

A heading is the exception that stays inline: Naver exports a section title as plain text, so
its conversion is invisible to the projection and a separate conversion step could never move
the snapshot token `Publisher.mutate` requires to move. A quote and a list both change the
block's text, so their conversions are observable on their own.

PUB-21, PUB-36 and PUB-37 pin no per-block adjacency — PUB-37 only requires the whole body
to precede the settings layer — so this is an implementation choice and NOT an SSOT change.
No update-ssot is owed.

### shipped
- `driverPointFn`: `body_end` redefined over the new body-paragraph set, and
  `body_paragraph` added to address one paragraph by index (a negative index counts from the
  end). The set is shared with the new observation function through `bodyParagraphsJS` so the
  two can never drift
- `driverParagraphStateFn`: reads one addressed paragraph's component kind and whether it
  sits in a list item or a citation module. Every conversion asserts it before resolving a
  control and again after activating one, which is the only way a heading conversion can fail
  closed at all
- `CDPPort.Apply`: `heading` (write then convert), `quote` (convert only — it enters no text)
  and `list` (convert, then Enter + insertText per remaining item, with the item count
  asserted against the manifest). `convertParagraph` and `requirePlainParagraph` are shared
- `Prepare`: the two body passes, with `planBlock` replacing `mapBlock` and returning the
  write, the conversion and the block's text on either side of it
- `assertPlanOrder`: three new refusals — a paragraph written after a conversion, conversions
  running forwards, and one paragraph converted twice
