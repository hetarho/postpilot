# T246 the preview carries its scrubber, its info control and its download
> st:done@260918 | ssot:CLIP-53 CLIP-148 CLIP-149 CLIP-109 CLIP-56 CLIP-152 | base:CLIP@37 | dep:T245

## goal
②'s preview keeps one scrubber row stating its own second, one info control holding everything the step says about the whole clip, and one icon download that appears only once a render exists.

## acceptance
- [v] the output-time scrubber is one row with no separate label line and states the second it sits at while it is dragged
- [v] one info control beside the preview opens the preview-to-render difference (timing tolerance, approximate frame, contrast, final loudness) and every notice naming no cut and no caption
- [v] none of that copy stands as permanent text in ②'s flow
- [v] with a render, an icon download sits directly under the preview naming the revision it delivers; with a plan and no render yet, no download stands there and nothing reads as missing
- [v] ③'s docked download is untouched
- [v] vitest: the scrubber exposes its seconds through its accessible value with no separate label element; the info control opens the parity copy and an untargeted notice; the download appears with a render and is absent, without a failure, on a plan carrying none
- [v] verify: `pnpm --filter ./frontend test` · `pnpm lint` · `pnpm lint:fsd` · `pnpm lint:style:probe && pnpm lint:style` · `pnpm build:web`

## impl notes
- `Slider` already carries `valueText`; drop `label={t('preview.outputTime')}` and give it an `aria-label` instead, so the seconds live in `aria-valuetext` and no second row is drawn — in BOTH `ClipCorrectionWorkspace` and `ClipDraftPreview`
- info control: `Popover` with `triggerSize="icon"`, `triggerVariant="secondary"`, `phone="sheet"`, `placement="below"`; `label` is a new `preview.aboutLabel` key in ko/en
- it absorbs the `<details>` carrying `preview.parityLabel` / `preview.parity` / `preview.frameApproximate` in `ClipDraftPreview` and the workspace's remaining `ClipNoticeList` of notices with neither `cutId` nor `elementId`
- download: `ClipDownloadAction` gains an `icon` shape — `buttonStyles({ variant: 'ghost', size: 'icon' })` with lucide `Download` and the existing `finalization.downloadRevision` string as `aria-label`; the `compact` and CTA shapes both stay
- a plan with no render is ②'s FIRST state (CLIP-56), so the absence of a download is silence, not an empty state or a refusal
- the earlier `fix(clip)` commit left `FinalizeClipNotices` and a download in a page section above the dock; this task removes that section — its notices go to the info control, its download under the preview, and T248 takes the finalization copy

Both ②'s output-time scrubber and `ClipDraftPreview`'s own now use it; `PlanEstimator` and `CompositionPreview` keep the labelled two-row shape.
- one row under ②'s preview carries the icon download and the info control: `Popover` with `triggerSize="icon"`, `phone="sheet"`, `placement="below"`, named by the new `preview.aboutLabel`. It holds `preview.parity` (timing tolerance, on-source contrast, final loudness), `preview.frameApproximate` while the displayed frame reports itself imprecise, and the `ClipNoticeList` of notices naming neither a cut nor a caption — which no longer stands in the flow.
- `ClipDraftPreview` lost the `<details>` carrying that copy, and with it the `precise` state and `PreviewVideo`'s `onPrecision` prop: that block was their only reader, and the info control reads the precision each displayed frame already carries. `preview.parityLabel` became `preview.aboutLabel` in ko/en rather than leaving dead copy behind.

Its section survives with the confirmation's own copy (what confirming does, the refusal, the failure alert, the uncertain retry) until T248 takes that into the finalization dialog.
