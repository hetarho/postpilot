## impl notes
- disclosure and CTA are project columns, not answers, because they are choices from a fixed list that the renderer must trust byte-exactly (CDS-31 forbids editing the phrase) and a free-text answer could not carry that guarantee; the preset is a template column because CLIP-4✎ makes it part of the recipe
- facts stay in `clip_project_answers` under reserved Korean labels rather than new columns because the answer form, autosave (T098) and the AI prompt already handle labels, and a reserved label is exactly what the chip shows; the seed makes the labels appear without the owner typing them
- the gate's "two of four" is CDS-1 made checkable at approval time (CLIP-5 collects the template's fields before start); refusing before the quote keeps a failing project from reserving credits (CLIP-19)
- the badge lives from 0 ms rather than the CDS minimum head/tail so V6 is trivially true and the Fair Trade guideline's start-and-end rule is met by construction (CDS-5 default)
- chips render as separate PNG plates on the same overlay chain as copy plates; they do not enter the copy text, so V11 (T105) treats their values as answers, which they are
- `Cut.Chips` is planner output limited to the reserved labels; a manual plan may edit it in T106's correction step under the same ≤ 2 rule

- the render inputs the badge and chips need are read from the PROJECT at render time, not stored with the plan: `EditPlan.Disclosure`, `.Facts` and `.Preset` are filled by `WithFacts` in both render paths. A plan stored before this task therefore still renders, and a re-render always shows the owner's current campaign type. Only `Cut.Chips` is stored with the composition, additively — an older row decodes with no chips.
- the frozen generation payload went to **version 3** and every reader checks one named constant (`generationPayloadVersion`): three readers were checking `2` in three places, and a job approved before the owner could choose a campaign type cannot render a clip that carries one. **A job already queued on the VPS will fail with `CLIP_INVALID_INPUT` and must be re-approved** — including the `20260905-test` job T008 mentions.
- the quote's input digest now covers the disclosure and the CTA: both change the rendered output, so changing one after quoting must invalidate the quote.
- V6 and V10 needed two codes the acceptance did not name: `plan_layout_disclosure` and `plan_layout_kind`, each with its own `CLIP_LAYOUT_*` reason. One badge covering both of CDS-5's windows necessarily spans the whole clip, so that is what V6 checks, and a second badge is refused as a second disclosure.
- a long chip value is CUT with an ellipsis, not squeezed with `lengthAdjust`: the face is fixed (CDS-18) so distorting glyphs is the worse failure. The cut is proportional to the measured width and `textLength` stays as the hard bound; the manifest records the drawn value, never the whole answer.
- a cut shorter than `timing.chip_min_s` (2.0 s, added to the design file) carries no chip rather than flashing one.
- 16:9's two-across chip stack and the per-ratio badge corner come from the design file's own `chip` and `badge` boxes; `placeFurniture` is pure so all three ratios are tested without Docker, and the nonroot smoke renders the badge and two chips through the real resvg and FFmpeg.
- the badge and the chips are their OWN overlay layer, separate from the copy's: the manifest says a badge never moves (CDS-31) and the copy fades and settles (CDS-4), so they cannot share one animated plate. Two looped PNG inputs per cut, the fixed one composited first (CDS-45). The unit test pins both filter chains and the nonroot smoke renders them.
- the reserved labels are not special-cased anywhere in the service: renaming 위치 in a template leaves an ordinary field, and the renderer then finds no fact under it, exactly as the acceptance asks.
- `SeedPresetFields` is authenticated but owner-independent — it answers from the design file alone.
