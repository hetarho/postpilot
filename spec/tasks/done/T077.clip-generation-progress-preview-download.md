## impl notes
- preparation starts before credit admission: poll its durable job immediately, and show an admission refusal from the job failure without assuming that a rejected hold means no job exists
- extend `entities/generation-job` only with the new target/stages; clip-specific start/cache behavior stays in `features/generate-clip`
- keep result URLs out of query persistence and browser storage; a failed media load invalidates the project once to obtain a new signed URL, then shows a normal failure
- the download control is an ordinary authenticated result URL from T076, never a fetched Blob assembled in browser memory
- do not add editing controls here; T079 owns the correction workspace after the first complete result
