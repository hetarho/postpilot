# T331 Quality metrics M1–M4, bands, verdicts and rule texts as pure code

## goal
- Given documents, nouns and a published window, `internal/quality` computes every per-post and account value — absent rather than zero when uncomputable — gives each metric one of four verdicts against the product's own bands and minimums, and renders each over-band metric's one rule text naming the measured string.

## acceptance
- [v] limits.go pins MeasureVersion 1, RunLength 8, TitleWindow 100, PostWindow 20, the QUAL-11 bands (M1 > 0.30, M2 > 0.10, M3 > 0.08 repetition or < 0.50 relevance, M4 ≤ 2 distinct types) and the minimums M1 10, M2 3, M3 1, M4 3.
- [v] M1 over at most the last 100 content titles: value = titles containing the winning noun ÷ titles considered; candidates are the union of those posts' returned nouns; each title is judged under its own post's language; ties go to the longer noun, then lexicographic order; absent when no post in the window has nouns.
- [v] M2 per post is its share against its 20 most recent other published posts; the account value is the median of the last 20 posts' shares, each against its own 20 most recent others; the named run is the one standing in the most of those 20 posts, ties to the longer run, then the earliest.
- [v] M3 per post: repetition share = occurrences of the most contained noun ÷ occurrences of all the post's nouns in the body; title relevance = share of the nouns contained in the title that the body also contains; each half is absent when its denominator is 0, and both are absent with no nouns.
- [v] M4 per post: NFC rune count of TEXT/HEADING/QUOTE/LIST text, IMAGE blocks with a file, distinct block types among the six, and average sentence length over TEXT only (Korean runes, English words), the last absent with no sentence.
- [v] `Median` ignores absent values, averages the two middle values of an even count, and is absent with no value.
- [v] Account verdicts: BELOW_MINIMUM while the published count is under the metric's minimum (no value, nothing named); ABSENT when the minimum is met but no value is computable; otherwise OVER_BAND or WITHIN_BAND as QUAL-11 words each band (exactly 30%, 10%, 8% repetition or 50% relevance is within band; exactly 2 distinct types is over).
- [v] M3's badge is over band when either present half crosses, within band when at least one half is present and none crosses, absent when both are absent; M4's account value needs 3 published posts.
- [v] Per post: M2 is BELOW_MINIMUM, stating minimum 3 and the others count, while fewer than 3 published posts other than this one exist; M3 and M4 are judged against the bands alone; a post with no content has every verdict ABSENT.
- [v] Each metric has exactly the one Korean and one English rule text in impl notes; M1's names its noun and M2's names its run, M3's and M4's name nothing (QUAL-43, QUAL-44), and every text carries its yield to natural writing (QUAL-45); M1 or M2 with nothing measured renders no text, so an account with nothing published names nothing.
- [v] No composite score exists: no type or function combines the four metrics.
- [v] Nothing in `internal/quality/...` depends on `internal/llm`, `internal/provider`, `internal/usage` or `internal/job`.
- [v] Tests (all in `backend/internal/quality`): limits_test.go `TestBandsMinimumsAndWindowsArePinned`; metrics_test.go `TestMetricIdsAreTheCanonicalAsciiIds`, `TestTitleSaturationCountsTitlesContainingTheMostFrequentNoun`, `TestTitleSaturationTiesGoToTheLongerNounThenLexicographic`, `TestTitleSaturationIsAbsentWithoutReturnedNouns`, `TestCrossPostShareIsMeasuredAgainstTheTwentyMostRecentOthers`, `TestTheNamedRunStandsInTheMostWindowPosts`, `TestRepetitionShareAndTitleRelevance`, `TestRepetitionIsAbsentWithoutNouns`, `TestCompositionCountsCharactersPhotosTypesAndSentences`, `TestMedianIgnoresAbsentValues`; verdict_test.go `TestAccountBelowMinimumIsNotAbsent`, `TestBandBoundariesFollowTheirWording`, `TestInPostRepetitionIsOverBandOnEitherHalf`, `TestCompositionNeedsThreePublishedPosts`, `TestPostCrossPostIsBelowMinimumWithFewerThanThreeOthers`, `TestAnAbsentValueNeitherPassesNorWarns`, `TestNothingPublishedNamesNothing`; rules_test.go `TestEveryMetricHasOneKoreanAndOneEnglishRule`, `TestTitleAndCrossPostRulesNameTheMeasuredString`, `TestABlankMeasuredStringRendersNoRule`; boundary_test.go `TestMeasurementCallsNoProviderAndCostsNoCredit`.

## impl notes
- limits.go (R21): `MeasureVersion = 1`, `RunLength = 8`, `TitleWindow = 100`, `PostWindow = 20`, `TitleSaturationBand = 0.30`, `CrossPostPhrasesBand = 0.10`, `RepetitionShareBand = 0.08`, `TitleRelevanceFloor = 0.50`, `DistinctBlockTypesBand = 2`, plus `Minimum(m Metric) int` (one switch case per metric → 10, 3, 1, 3).
- These are code constants with no env override: QUAL's constraints make them product-owned settings, not per-account options, and a changed band rewrites what every badge means (T315's window-constant precedent).
- Bump `MeasureVersion` whenever `MeasureSelf` would give a different answer for the same input; T335 recomputes every stored row whose version differs.
- metrics.go: `type Metric string` with the R3 ids `MetricTitleSaturation "title_saturation"` (M1), `MetricCrossPostPhrases "cross_post_phrases"` (M2), `MetricInPostRepetition "in_post_repetition"` (M3), `MetricComposition "composition"` (M4); `Metrics() []Metric` in that order; `ParseMetric(id string) (Metric, bool)`.
- metrics.go: `Sample{Slug string; Doc Document; Language Language; Nouns []string}`; Language is already resolved through T325's `LanguageOf`; Nouns are the write pass's (GEN-55), nil or empty meaning none.
- Nouns are normalized once per sample: `strings.TrimSpace` then NFC, empties dropped, deduplicated by exact string in first-seen order; containment is always `Contains`/`Occurrences` on `Tokens(noun)` (T325).
- M1 (R17): `MeasureTitleSaturation(window []Sample) TitleSaturation{Value *float64; Noun string}`; window = the first min(len, TitleWindow) samples; candidates = union of the window's normalized nouns.
- M1: per candidate, count the titles t with `Contains(Tokens(t.Doc.Title), Tokens(noun), t.Language)`; winner = highest count, then more runes, then the smaller string (Go `<`); Value = count ÷ len(window); Noun = the winner when its count > 0, else ""; no candidates → Value nil.
- M2: `OthersOf(published []Sample, slug string) []Sample` = the first PostWindow entries of published (newest first) whose Slug differs; R18's "the last 20 excluding the post" is read as its 20 most recent others, so a draft and a published post are both judged against 20 once 20 exist.
- M2: `MeasureCrossPost(post Sample, others []Sample) (Overlap, bool)` = T325's `MatchRuns(post.Doc, docs of others, RunLength)`.
- M2 named run (QUAL-14, R18): window = first min(PostWindow, len) of published; each window post P contributes its runs against `OthersOf(published, P.Slug)`; a run's standing = P plus each `In` entry that is itself a window post; runs merge by token sequence (standings union).
- M2 named run winner: largest standing, then more tokens, then earliest, then the joined text lexicographically; its text is its tokens joined by one space.
- "Earliest" is taken as the run whose oldest standing post was published least recently (largest window index), then the smaller Start in that post; QUAL-14 leaves "earliest" unqualified, and this is the engineering reading.
- M3 (R19): `MeasureRepetition(s Sample) Repetition{Share *float64; TopNoun string; TitleRelevance *float64}`; per noun, body occurrences = Σ over `Units` of `Occurrences(Tokens(unit.Text), Tokens(noun), s.Language)`; total = Σ over nouns.
- M3: total 0 → Share nil; else top = most occurrences, then more runes, then the smaller string; Share = occ(top) ÷ total; TopNoun = top.
- M3: title nouns = nouns contained in `Tokens(s.Doc.Title)`; none → TitleRelevance nil; else TitleRelevance = (title nouns with body occurrences > 0) ÷ (title nouns); no nouns → the zero Repetition, both halves nil (QUAL-9).
- M4 (R20): `MeasureComposition(s Sample) Composition{CharCount, PhotoCount, DistinctBlockTypes int; AvgSentenceLength *float64}`.
- M4 CharCount = Σ over `Units` of `utf8.RuneCountInString(norm.NFC.String(strings.TrimSpace(unit.Text)))`; inner whitespace counts; the title, captions, alt text and slot tokens do not.
- M4 PhotoCount = IMAGE blocks whose `strings.TrimSpace(File)` is non-empty (a VIDEO is not a photo, QUAL-10).
- M4 DistinctBlockTypes = how many of the six types have at least one block carrying something (a non-blank Unit, or a non-blank File for IMAGE/VIDEO); an unfilled template position is not composition.
- M4 AvgSentenceLength = mean over every `Sentences` piece of the TEXT Units only; a Korean sentence's length is the NFC rune count of the trimmed sentence (whitespace included, as CharCount), an English one's is `len(Tokens(sentence))`; no sentence → nil.
- `Self{Repetition Repetition; Composition Composition}` and `MeasureSelf(s Sample) Self` are exactly what T335 stores per revision (R15's columns).
- `Median(values []*float64) *float64`: nil entries ignored; odd count → the middle value; even → mean of the two middle; none → nil.
- verdict.go: `type Verdict string` with `VerdictOverBand "over_band"`, `VerdictWithinBand "within_band"`, `VerdictBelowMinimum "below_minimum"`, `VerdictAbsent "absent"`, and `Verdicts()` in that order (R3's order).
- Bands as QUAL-11 words them: over when M1 > 0.30, M2 > 0.10, M3 repetition > 0.08 or relevance < 0.50, M4 ≤ 2; exactly 0.30, 0.10, 0.08 or 0.50 is within band and exactly 2 types is over.
- Comparisons carry an unexported `bandTolerance = 1e-9` (above: `v > band+tol`; below: `v < floor-tol`; M4: `v <= 2+tol`) because a median averages two floats and (0.2+0.4)/2 computes to 0.30000000000000004; `TestBandBoundariesFollowTheirWording` pins that case.
- M3's badge combines its halves: over band when either present half crosses, within band when at least one half is present and none crosses, absent when both are absent (an absent half neither passes nor warns, QUAL-40).
- Account: `Aggregate(published []Sample, self []Self) Account`; published is newest first (published_at DESC, QUAL-39) and capped at TitleWindow; `self[i]` describes `published[i]` for i < min(PostWindow, len), and a missing entry is measured on the spot with `MeasureSelf`.
- `Account{PublishedCount int; TitleSaturation AccountTitleSaturation{Verdict; Value *float64; Noun string}; CrossPost AccountCrossPost{Verdict; Median *float64; Run string}; Repetition AccountRepetition{Verdict; Share, TitleRelevance *float64}; Composition AccountComposition{Verdict; CharCount, PhotoCount, DistinctBlockTypes, AvgSentenceLength *float64}}`.
- `Account.Verdict(m Metric) Verdict` and `Account.Named(m Metric) string` (M1 → Noun, M2 → Run, "" for M3 and M4) are what T335's RulesFor and handler read.
- Account verdicts per metric: PublishedCount < Minimum(m) → BELOW_MINIMUM with every value nil and nothing named; else every value nil → ABSENT; else the band.
- PublishedCount = len(published) after the cap, which is exact whenever it is below a minimum (every minimum is under both windows).
- Account values: M2 = Median of each window post's share against `OthersOf`; M3 = Median of each half over the window; M4 = Median of each of the four over the window, and the band reads the distinct-types median.
- Per post: `JudgePost(post Sample, self Self, others []Sample) PostMeasurement` and `AbsentPost() PostMeasurement`, the latter being a post with no content: every verdict ABSENT, no value.
- `PostMeasurement{CrossPost PostCrossPost{Verdict; Share *float64; Others int}; Repetition PostRepetition{Verdict; Repetition Repetition}; Composition PostComposition{Verdict; Composition *Composition}}`.
- Per-post M2: Others = len(others) in every state (the count QUAL-36 names beside the minimum); len(others) < Minimum(M2) → BELOW_MINIMUM with Share nil; else `MeasureCrossPost` false → ABSENT; else the band; M3 and M4 per post have no minimum (QUAL-36).
- rules.go: `RuleText(m Metric, lang Language, named string) (string, bool)` returns the Korean text for LanguageKorean and the English one for LanguageEnglish; M1 and M2 insert `named` verbatim; M1 or M2 with a blank `named` → ("", false), so no caller can render a ban with nothing in it (QUAL-15).
- rules.go holds these eight literals verbatim; `%s` is `named`, inserted between 「」 (ko) or double quotes (en), so no particle ever hangs on it. Each substance is decided: M1 a title ban of the named noun (QUAL-14, QUAL-15), M2 no verbatim reuse of the named run (QUAL-14), M3 per QUAL-43, M4 per QUAL-44, and each closes with its yield to natural writing (QUAL-45).
- M1 ko: `제목에 「%s」 단어를 넣지 마세요. 최근 발행한 글 제목에 이미 자주 쓰인 단어입니다. 억지로 비슷한 말로 바꾸지 말고, 제목을 자연스럽게 다시 짜세요.`
- M1 en: `Do not put the word "%s" in the title; it already appears in many of the account's recent published titles. Rather than forcing a near-synonym in its place, rework the title so it reads naturally.`
- M2 ko: `「%s」 문장을 그대로 쓰지 마세요. 최근 발행한 여러 글에 똑같이 들어간 문장입니다. 같은 뜻이 필요하면 이 글의 내용에 맞게 자연스럽게 새로 쓰세요.`
- M2 en: `Do not reuse the passage "%s" word for word; the same passage appears in several recent published posts. If this post needs that point, write it fresh in words that fit this post.`
- M3 ko: `본문에서 한 명사를 계속 되풀이하지 마세요. 반복되는 곳은 읽기에 자연스러운 범위에서 다른 표현으로 바꾸거나 줄이되, 어색한 동의어를 억지로 넣지는 마세요. 제목에 쓴 대상은 본문에서도 다루세요.`
- M3 en: `Do not keep repeating one noun through the body; where it repeats, vary or drop it only as far as it still reads naturally, never forcing an awkward synonym. Cover in the body what the title names.`
- M4 ko: `본문을 문단과 사진만으로 구성하지 말고, 소제목(HEADING)·목록(LIST)·인용(QUOTE) 중 내용에 맞는 것을 섞어 서로 다른 블록 종류를 세 가지 이상 쓰세요. 내용에 맞지 않는 블록을 억지로 넣거나, 블록을 채우려고 자료에 없는 내용을 만들지는 마세요.`
- M4 en: `Do not build the body from paragraphs and photos alone: mix in whichever of HEADING, LIST and QUOTE the material fits, so the post uses at least three distinct block types. Never force a block the content does not fit, and never invent anything the source lacks to fill one.`
- `TestEveryMetricHasOneKoreanAndOneEnglishRule` pins the eight rendered strings byte for byte with a sample `named`, so a wording change is a deliberate diff.
- Constraints any wording meets: POST-81's toggletip quotes it verbatim, so it states or implies no exposure gain (QUAL constraints); it licenses no fact absent from the source (GEN-16).
- Korean wording constraint: a consonant-dependent particle (을/를, 이/가) never hangs on the inserted string; the texts above attach it to 단어 or 문장 instead, and any later wording keeps that.
- boundary_test.go (QUAL-16) follows the `internal/llm/boundary_test.go` recipe: `go list -f '{{.ImportPath}} {{join .Deps " "}}' github.com/postpilot/backend/internal/quality/...`.
