# T184 A plan under the length floor fails as insufficient footage
> st:done@260916 | ssot:CLIP-120 CLIP-7 CLIP-94 | base:CLIP@28 ARCH@2 | dep:-

## goal
Give a readable plan that falls under CLIP-7's 15-second floor its own owner-facing cause instead of reporting it as an unreadable response.

## acceptance
- [v] a readable, schema-valid plan whose assembled output is under the floor fails with an insufficient-selected-footage cause, distinct from CLIP-94's unreadable-response cause
- [v] that failure triggers no correction attempt — the response was read and validated, so resending the prompt cannot help (→CLIP-94)
- [v] the owner-facing sentence points at the footage, and attempt inspection still exposes the completed observations and the validated ranges (→CLIP-85)
- [v] ko and en both render the new cause through the existing failure catalogue
- [v] tests: a plan just under and just over the floor, asserting the cause and that no correction attempt is issued
- [v] verify BE (ARCH-26) and FE (ARCH-25) pass

## impl notes
- the ladder in `ai/plan_ladder.go` is where a check's tier is declared; the floor belongs there as its own `failPlan` check rather than falling through to the decode failures it currently shares a cause with
- the removal path can reach the floor after repair (CLIP-106), so the check runs on the remainder, not on the first response
- keep the existing settlement behaviour: a failed generation settles as it does today, and this task changes the cause only
