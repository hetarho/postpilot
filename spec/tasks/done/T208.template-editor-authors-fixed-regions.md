## goal
Show the shrunken template in the editor: design presets, slot text, the badge, fields, groups and one optional guide, with a legacy template's converted guide shown and saved on the owner's next save.

- [v] a template that opens with `composition_converted=true` shows its converted body and one notice line saying its scenes were moved into the guide; the next save persists it and the notice disappears
- [v] `CompositionTextControls` offers a content text only `basis` `whole|output-start|output-end` and role `badge`; intro/outro slot controls are unchanged
- [v] the 형식 안내 modal and 원문 복사 carry T207's GRAMMAR and example; a refused node renders its new reason string in ko and en
- [v] `CompositionPreview` still judges the design: skeleton, badge and one fixed sample narration sentence at the caption anchor from `composition-sample.ts`
- [v] `validateClipRecipe` (entities/clip-template/model/types.ts) no longer requires `accent` or `captionPace`; `CLIP_ACCENTS` stays exported through `@x` for ① (T209)
- [v] tests: palette contents, conversion notice then save, guide text, preview sample line; verify FE (ARCH-25)
