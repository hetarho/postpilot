## goal
Remove every user-facing automatic publishing path while preserving the existing manual copy/export workflow.

## acceptance
- [v] No tier, including master, sees a Publish navigation entry, final-step publish panel, pairing/configuration/retry/cancel controls or agent-management screen; these feature/entity/widget/page slices and their polling hooks/tests are deleted where no surviving consumer exists.
- [v] A legacy /publishing-agents deep link redirects to /posts without a publishing RPC, setup prompt or replacement feature screen; ordinary signed-out authentication behavior remains consistent with other protected links.
- [v] The finish step still finalizes/learns and exports the same canonical content; Naver title/body/tags, individually ordered photos/captions and the other three formats retain their bytes, feedback, manual fallback, locale/provenance and error behavior. Video export remains usable.
- [v] Korean/English navigation, plans, About and product metadata make no promise of automatic upload, a Mac companion or master publishing privileges; master administration and credit/model access remain unchanged. The publishing i18n namespace and unused registration/type wiring are removed.
- [v] Route/editor tests cover master and non-master, ko/en, old deep links, finalization and manual export, asserting zero publishing RPCs during mount, navigation and copy. Existing relevant export/clipboard/video tests pass.
- [v] ARCH-25 gates pass; preserve concurrent navigation/theme changes rather than replacing route or navigation files wholesale.

## impl notes
- Deploy after the T310 server cutover so cached or older clients cannot continue to publish. Product copy says manual copy/export; avoid a disabled beta tab or a promise that automation will return.
- Remove frontend/src/entities/{publish-job,publishing-agent}, features/{publish-post,pair-publishing-agent,configure-publishing-agent,cancel-publish,retry-publish} and any discovered revoke-publishing slice, widgets/publish-panel, pages/publishing-agents and publishing-specific fixtures after checking actual consumers.
- Keep only a small legacy redirect in the existing route group; remove the feature route/page dependency, not the app-wide NotFound/auth machinery. Remove publish-specific flush wiring while preserving the content queue used by finalization and learning.
- The generated PublishingService/agent definitions and any shared exports required by remaining compile surfaces are removed in T284. Do not hand-edit generated files. Remove UI-only VIDEO_NOT_PUBLISHABLE behavior here, with the shared enum reserved later.
- MKT/THEME header responsiveness and CLIP/CDS Wanted Sans pending changes are outside this task; neither their specifications nor their pending markers are consumed by this retirement work.

## result
- Deleted the publishing page, panel, entity, feature and fixture slices; removed their clients,
  polling, i18n registration and all navigation/editor entry points. The protected legacy URL now
  redirects every signed-in tier to `/posts` without a publishing RPC.
- Kept finalization, voice learning and the four manual export formats intact. Korean and English
  product copy now describes copy/export followed by a manual destination-service publish.
- Added route and editor coverage for both locales and free/master accounts, including legacy auth,
  redirect and zero-publishing-RPC assertions. Full frontend validation passed: 287 test files and
  2,286 tests, lint/format, FSD, style probe/style and production build.
- Generated publishing contracts and reserved shared failure identifiers remain for T284 as planned.
