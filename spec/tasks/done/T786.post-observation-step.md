## goal
`관찰하기` runs observation from ①, ② shows the observations filling in and lets the owner correct them, and re-observation is the owner's explicit choice there.

## acceptance
- [v] ①'s 확인 big action reads `관찰하기` while an attached photo or video has no observation and starts the observation job with an empty selection, carrying the owner to ②; otherwise it reads `다음` and moves without a call; an observe-model refusal stands above the row; the empty-draft reason and 사진 넣기 shortcut stay.
- [v] While an observation, storyline or writing run started from ① or ② is running, a run card with its own bar heads ② (`사진을 살펴보고 있어요` while observing, `글을 쓰고 있어요` otherwise) and the page-top bar fades while it is in view (POST-45); cards not yet observed say so and fill as each batch's snapshot arrives; a finished observation stays on ②, a storyline lands on ③, writing on ④.
- [v] ②'s heading row carries `다시 관찰하기`, opening the re-observation picker and starting the observation job with its selection; ticking an observation edited by hand shows one confirmation that its edits will be lost; GenerationActions and StorylineActions no longer open the picker.
- [v] Each card's scene, mood, visible text, objects (one per line) and, for a video, events (one per line) edit in place and autosave through the draft queue; an edited card shows that it was corrected by the owner; editing is read-only while a job runs, on a published post and while saving is failing.
- [v] With no photo and no video ② says there is nothing to observe and its actions keep POST-108's photo-free confirmation.
- [v] Tests: `features/save-draft/model/draft-queue.test.ts` (observation edits queue, rebase and flush), `widgets/contact-sheet/ui/ContactSheet.test.tsx` (edit, owner mark, read-only states, observing state), `features/generate-post/ui/{GenerationActions,ReobservePicker}.test.tsx` and `model/reobserve.test.ts` (no picker on runs, hand-edit confirmation), `pages/editor/ui/EditorPage.{generate,lifecycle,published}.test.tsx` (관찰하기|다음, run card on ②, landing steps); `pnpm lint`, `pnpm lint:fsd`, `pnpm lint:style` and `pnpm build:web` pass.
