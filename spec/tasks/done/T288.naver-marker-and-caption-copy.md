# T288 the Naver photo marker is a number and the caption copies itself
> st:done@260920 | ssot:EXPORT-5 EXPORT-24 EXPORT-4 EXPORT-9 EXPORT-12 ARCH-24 | base:EXPORT@3 | dep:-

## goal
Replace the Naver photo marker with `사진_<n>_사진` carrying neither filename nor caption, and give each caption its own copy control.

## acceptance
- [v] `toNaver` emits `사진_<n>_사진` for Korean content and `photo_<n>_photo` for English, numbered from 1 in marker order, carrying neither the filename nor the caption
- [v] an `IMAGE` block with an empty `file` still consumes its number, so later photos cannot shift against their markers
- [v] video markers are byte-identical to today, caption included
- [v] an unfilled template slot still renders bracketed and a test asserts it cannot be read as a photo marker now that the photo marker carries no filename
- [v] each caption in the Naver preview is its own copy control under its photo, following the text-copy discipline (1500 ms confirmation, manual fallback, staleness invalidation); a block with no caption renders no control
- [v] the manual fallback for a caption reveals a read-only field holding that caption and selects it, the way the Naver tab reveals its raw text field
- [v] the photo copy control is named by its marker number and its filename
- [v] Tistory, site and Markdown outputs are byte-identical (snapshot tests over the existing fixture)
- [v] vitest covers the numbering, the empty-file hole, a caption copy and a caption fallback
- [v] ARCH-25 passes

## impl notes
- `CopyTarget` gains `caption:${number}`; the number is the marker index, the same identity a photo copy already uses, so one file carrying two markers stays two independent captions
- `naverPhotoOrder` keeps returning one entry per marker including the empty one — the numbering and the strip must agree by position, which is the whole reason that function exists
- the caption leaves the marker, so nothing in the pasted body carries it any more; that is the point of EXPORT-24 and the panel's Naver guidance line has to say so
- i18n strings go into the export slice fragments, not a new namespace

## result
- `features/export-naver/model/convert.ts`: the `IMAGE` marker is `사진_<n>_사진` / `photo_<n>_photo`, numbered from 1 over the one walk `toNaver` already makes, carrying neither filename nor caption. A block with an empty `file` still spends its number, so `naverPhotoOrder` and the markers stay paired by position. Video markers are untouched (their comment now says why they keep filename and caption: a clip has no copy control of its own).
- `shared/lib/blocks/walk.ts`: only the comment — a slot placeholder is now told from a photo marker by its brackets, not by the filename the photo marker used to carry (EXPORT-4).
- `widgets/export-panel`: `CopyTarget` gained `caption:${number}`; `fieldOf` became a switch over a live `captionFields` map so a caption's revealed field is read the same way the three fixed fields are. Each caption in the Naver preview is now its own full-width copy control under its photo (the caption IS the button, as the photo is), with its own status line, its own 1500 ms confirmation, its own manual fallback — a read-only `캡션 텍스트` field revealed and selected on refusal, focus handed back to the caption control when the fallback dissolves — and the same value+content staleness derivations. A block with no caption renders nothing there. The photo control is now named `<n>번 사진 복사 · <file>`.
- i18n (export slice fragment): `captionCopyAria`, `captionCopied`, `captionField` added; `photoCopyAria` carries the number; `guidance.naverPhotos` names `사진_1_사진` and says in both languages that the captions are NOT in the pasted text.
- tests: `convert.test.ts` pins the marker shape, the absence of filename and caption, 1..n numbering against `naverPhotoOrder`, the empty-file hole (marker 1 is the hole, the first real photo is 2) and an unfilled slot that stays bracketed and unreadable as a photo marker; both Naver snapshots regenerated. `ExportPanel.test.tsx` pins the three control names in marker order (the second photo has no caption, so it has no caption control), a caption copy and a caption fallback.
- Tistory, site and Markdown snapshots are untouched — the change is inside the Naver converter only. ARCH-25 green: 2218 vitest tests, `pnpm lint`, `lint:fsd`, `lint:style:probe`+`lint:style`, `build:web`.
- SSOT drift found, NOT fixed here (needs update-ssot): `TMPL-17`, `TMPL-23` and `TMPL-39` still describe the Naver photo marker as `[사진 …]`, and TMPL-23 still says a slot placeholder is told apart from it "by their filename suffix" — EXPORT-4/EXPORT-5 r3 replaced that with the brackets. Root `README.md:14` and `PRD.md:420` carry the old marker too (reference docs; ssot wins).
