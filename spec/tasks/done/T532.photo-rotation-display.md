# T532 show and copy photos turned, and let the owner turn them
> st:done@261004 | ssot:POST-107 EXPORT-15 | base:POST@31 EXPORT@10 | dep:T531 | touches:frontend/src/shared/ui frontend/src/entities/image frontend/src/entities/post frontend/src/features/upload-photos frontend/src/widgets/contact-sheet frontend/src/widgets/export-panel frontend/src/features/edit-storyline frontend/src/widgets/storyline-space frontend/src/shared/lib/clipboard

## goal
Every surface that shows a photo shows it turned by its rotation, the Naver photo copy is turned too, and ①'s photo tile carries a 회전 control.

## acceptance
- [v] a turned photo renders upright in the photo strip, the contact sheet, the storyline space and attachment viewer, the re-observation picker, the reading view (IMAGE and both group layouts) and the Naver export tab, at its turned aspect where the photo keeps its natural shape
- [v] the Naver photo copy encodes the turned pixels (90/180/270)
- [v] ①'s photo tile carries a 회전 control (aria `사진 돌리기 · <파일명>`) that calls RotateImage with (rotation + 90) % 360 and the strip updates from the response; it is disabled while the post is published like the tile's other actions
- [v] vitest: the rotated image primitive's styles for each turn in natural and filled modes, copyImage turning a bitmap, the strip control's request and disabled state, one reading-view case
- [v] ARCH-25 passes

## impl notes
- `entities/image` `PostImage` gains `rotation: number` (mapper from proto `Image.rotation`, default 0).
- one primitive, `shared/ui/rotated-image/RotatedImage.tsx` (ARCH-20), props `{ src, alt, width, height, rotation, fit: 'natural' | 'fill', className, imgRef, ...img attrs }`:
  - `fill` (the img fills a box its parent shapes, as square cells do): the same `<img>` with `style={{ transform: rotate(Ndeg) }}`
  - `natural` with 0/180: a plain img (`rotate(180deg)` for 180) with `width`/`height` reserving the box
  - `natural` with 90/270: a `relative w-full overflow-hidden` wrapper with `aspectRatio: height / width` (the turned shape) and the img `absolute left-1/2 top-1/2` at `width: (width/height)*100%` with `translate(-50%,-50%) rotate(Ndeg)`
  - rotation values other than 0/90/180/270 render as 0
- replace the `<img>`s in `entities/image/ui/Thumbnail.tsx` (fill), `entities/post/ui/BlockList.tsx` IMAGE (natural), `entities/post/ui/PhotoGroup.tsx` cells (fill), `widgets/export-panel` PhotoCopy (natural, or fill in a group cell; keep `crossOrigin`, the ref and the onError), `widgets/contact-sheet`, `features/edit-storyline` viewer/editor, `widgets/storyline-space`, `features/generate-post/ui/ReobservePicker` — wherever an attached photo's `viewUrl` is drawn.
- `shared/lib/clipboard/copyImage(image, rotation = 0)`: draw the bitmap on a canvas sized to the turned dimensions with `translate` + `rotate` before `encodePng`; the promise-first ClipboardItem order stays (EXPORT-14). ExportPanel passes the photo's rotation.
- `entities/post/api` (or `entities/image/api`) `useRotateImage` mutation over `PostService.rotateImage`, updating the post query's image in place from the response; `features/upload-photos` PhotoStrip tile adds the control beside its existing action (lucide `RotateCw`, ghost, 44 px target), names only.
- i18n: `사진 돌리기 · {{file}}` / `Rotate · {{file}}`; the failure reason key from T531.

- at: 1e88a3b9 (working tree; committed as the T532 commit)
- verified: vitest for RotatedImage (each turn, both fits, capped frame), quarter-turn math, encodePng and copyImage turning the canvas, the strip control (request, turned tile, in-flight and read-only), the rotate hook through the fake RotateImage, and turned photos in the reading view; full FE suite 3092/3092; pnpm lint, lint:fsd, lint:style:probe, lint:style and build:web pass
