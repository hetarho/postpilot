# T296 The current destination takes the accent, the second level gets denser, and a list's dock loses its plane
> st:done@260921 | ssot:THEME-18 THEME-24 THEME-26 THEME-38 | base:THEME@14 | dep:-

## goal
Four chrome changes from THEME r14, all in the frontend shell and the two primitives behind it.
- the destination the owner is on is told by the accent at every level and shape (rails, the laptop's primary band, the phone bar, the phone group row), keeping weight and plane so colour never travels alone
- the group rail reads as the SECOND level by density as well as by plane: shorter rows, a smaller glyph, a narrower rail and gutter, with the type role unchanged
- the phone group row is one control: the current destination's glyph, its name and a chevron open the group menu, in place of a name on the left plus a menu button at the far right; the directory under it drops its own top padding below the desk
- `ActionBar dock="list"` carries no plane: the add action floats over the rows at its natural width, right-aligned at every width

## acceptance
- [v] `--link-fg-current` resolves to `--intent-accent`; every plane a current row sits on (rails `surface-base`/`surface-raised`, the phone bar, the page plane) clears AA in both themes
- [v] the group rail's rows are shorter and its glyph smaller than the primary rail's, the rail is narrower, and neither row's type role changed
- [v] the phone group row holds exactly ONE control, named by the current destination, opening the same menu with the current option checked; `navigation.test.tsx` asserts the new shape
- [v] `ActionBar dock="list"` renders no surface, padding or shadow of its own and all seven list pages pass their add action without `w-full`
- [v] `/posts` and `/clips` put their search row directly under the group row on a phone
- [v] verify (ARCH-25): `pnpm --filter ./frontend test` · `pnpm lint` · `pnpm lint:fsd` · `pnpm lint:style:probe && pnpm lint:style` · `pnpm build:web`

## impl notes
- `Menu` gains a labelled trigger (glyph + name + chevron, no plane at rest, panel aligned to its left edge); the icon-only trigger it already has is unchanged
- the seven `dock="list"` call sites: posts, clips, voices, templates, guidelines, memories, video-templates

## result
- `--link-fg-current` now resolves to `--intent-accent`, which marks the current destination in the two rails, the laptop's primary band, the phone bar and the phone group row at once; the four planes a current row can sit on were measured from the OKLCH palette and clear AA (Day 5.30 / 5.54, Night 6.28 / 5.58), and the one pair that would not — accent on `surface-lowest` in Day, 4.03 — cannot occur, since a current row is always one plane up from its rail
- the group rail's rows are `min-h-9 gap-2 px-3` with a `size-4` glyph against the primary rail's `min-h-11 gap-3 px-4` and `size-5`, the rail itself `lg:w-44 lg:px-2 lg:py-3` against `lg:w-52 lg:px-3 lg:py-4`; the type role is unchanged on purpose (→THEME-19's 12px metadata floor), so the level reads as subordinate through the box and the plane, not through smaller letters
- `Menu` gained `triggerLabel`: the trigger then wears glyph + name + chevron on a ghost plane, its visible text is its accessible name and the panel hangs from its left edge. The phone group row is that one control, pulled onto the page gutter with `-ml-4` so the name lines up with the rows under it
- `ActionBar dock="list"` is now `bottom-dock-nav sticky z-20 mt-6 ml-auto w-fit sm:bottom-4 *:shadow-lg` — no surface, corner, padding or shadow of its own, the shadow moved onto the control. All seven list pages dropped `w-full sm:w-auto`, and the two whose gutters live on their blocks (`/posts`, `/clips`) give the dock its right gutter only, since an `mx-*` would replace `ml-auto` in the merge
- `/posts` and `/clips` trade `py-6 sm:py-8` for `pt-4 sm:pt-6 lg:pt-8` and drop the search row's `mt-4` below the desk: 88px between 내 글 and the search field became 16px
- left for later: the other five group directories (말투, 글 템플릿, 지침, 기억, 영상 템플릿) still render their own `display` heading below the desk, so the group row's name and the page's title say the same thing twice there. THEME-38's "a directory's own heading yields to that name below the desk" has been true since r13 and only `/posts` and `/clips` follow it — a pre-existing gap, untouched here because it is not what was asked for
