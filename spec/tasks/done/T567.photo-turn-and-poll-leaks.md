# T567 Keep every photo turn in the cache, and stop per-autosave fingerprint reads and poll listener leaks
> st:done@261004 | ssot:ARCH-24 | base:ARCH@15 | dep:- | touches:frontend/src/features/upload-photos/model/useRotatePhoto.ts frontend/src/widgets/voice-fingerprint/ frontend/src/entities/voice/api/usePostFingerprint.ts frontend/src/features/render-clip-browser/api/run-render.ts frontend/src/entities/clip-preview/api/preview.ts frontend/src/shared/lib/

## goal
Turning two photos quickly keeps both turns on screen, typing in ② no longer triggers a fingerprint read per autosave, and a long browser-render sampling wait no longer piles up abort listeners.

## acceptance
- [v] `useRotatePhoto` updates the cache in the hook-level `onSuccess` (fires for every call), not in the per-call `mutate` options
- [v] the post fingerprint query keys on a content revision that settles: it changes only after the content has been saved and stayed unchanged for the autosave beat (or once the save settles), so a burst of autosaves causes one read
- [v] one shared abortable delay helper (`shared/lib`, e.g. `delay(ms, signal)`) removes its abort listener when the timer fires; `run-render.ts`'s sampling poll and `preview.ts`'s `askAgainWhenBusy` use it
- [v] tests: turning B while A's turn is in flight leaves both turned in the cache; three autosaves within the beat cause one fingerprint fetch; after 10 resolved delays the signal holds no listeners (spy on add/removeEventListener)
- [v] behaviour unchanged otherwise: FE suite, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm lint:fsd`, `pnpm build:web` green
