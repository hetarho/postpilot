## goal
On billing, the header credit control, the account popover and the clip credit surfaces, master sees what a customer sees, with no operator notice or exemption wording, and only the actions the server refuses master are disabled.

## acceptance
- [v] Billing as master: no `operatorCoverage` notice and no `운영자 면제 · 참고 사용량만 기록`. With no subscription it shows the customer's empty copy and /plans link. A held subscription shows its details and its actions as disabled controls. The credit pack section renders with its purchase controls disabled. Payment method registration/removal, history and refund requests behave as for a customer (BILL-20 keeps refunds available).
- [v] Master's balance reads `무제한` / `Unlimited` in the header badge, its accessible label, the account popover meter and billing's benefit summary, with no `운영자` or `exempt` wording; the plan-name badge and the 운영 관리 menu entry stay.
- [v] Clip quote approval shows master the quote exactly as a customer sees it, without the `credits.exempt` line; settlement shows `n 크레딧 사용` (the recorded reference amount) with no exemption clause and no debit/return/compensation lines; the cancel dialog shows the ordinary `cancellation.rule` copy instead of `cancellation.exempt`.
- [v] FE tests cover each surface as master and as a paid non-master (no operator wording, disabled controls present and inert, refund/payment-method controls still enabled for master) in both languages; non-master behaviour is unchanged. Run ARCH-25 verification.

## impl notes
- Billing: replace the `master && operatorCoverage` block and the `!master &&` guards with rendering the customer branch and passing `disabled={master}` into `BillingSubscriptionActions` and `CreditPurchaseSection` (add the prop: every committing control becomes `disabled`, a dialog never opens). Keep `renews={autoRenew && !master}` and BenefitsSummary's `nextPaymentAt` guard, which state facts (a held subscription does not renew, BILL-20). `RegisterPaymentMethodButton`, `RemovePaymentMethodButton` and `RefundRequestSection` are untouched; the server refuses none of them for master.
- Strings: change `balance.unlimited` (ko `무제한`, en `Unlimited`), `badge.labelUnlimited` (ko `플랜 {{tier}}, 크레딧 무제한`, en `Plan {{tier}}, unlimited credits`) and billing's `benefits.exempt` → rename to `benefits.unlimited` with the same `무제한` text. Delete `operatorCoverage` from `entities/plan` i18n once T498 has removed its `/plans` use; if T498 has not landed, delete only the billing use and leave the key for T498.
- Clip: in `ClipCreditSettlement` keep the `exempt` branch but render `credits.used` with the reference amount and drop `credits.usedExempt`; in `ClipQuoteApproval` delete the `balance?.unlimited` exempt line; in `CancelClipAction` drop the `exempt` branch so master falls through to `cancellation.rule`. Delete `credits.exempt`, `credits.usedExempt` and `cancellation.exempt` in both languages. `PostCreditEstimate`'s figure-without-count for an unlimited balance names no operator and stays.
- The checkout page's master guard (`invalid` for master) stays; /plans' disabled buttons mean master never reaches it.
