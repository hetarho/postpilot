# T049 The annual upgrade's span and its unit price
> st:done@260908 | ssot:BILLING-18 BILLING-5 BILLING-4 | base:BILLING@4 ARCH@2 | dep:T047

## goal
Charge an annual upgrade for the months the term still has to run, the running one counted whole, at the difference between a twelfth of each tier's annual price.

## acceptance
- [v] the month already running counts as whole: an upgrade on an annual term's first day is charged for 12 months and one inside the term's last window for 1, so no annual upgrade is ever charged nothing
- [v] the unit is the annual monthly-equivalent, a twelfth of the annual price (BILLING-18), not the monthly list price
- [v] an upgrade on the term's first day therefore costs exactly the difference between the two tiers' annual prices — a test pins that identity, because it is what makes the rule self-consistent
- [v] a monthly-term upgrade is unchanged: the full difference between the two monthly prices, charged once
- [v] `QuoteChange` and `ChangeSubscription` agree on the amount for every case above, so a quote can never differ from what the card is charged
- [v] table-driven tests cover an annual upgrade on the term's first day, mid-term, inside the last window, and one on the anchor boundary itself; plus a monthly-term upgrade
- [v] ARCH-26 passes

## impl notes
- from BILLING@4 r3 (BILLING-5✎ BILLING-18+), raised as a planning gap by review/diff-260908
- `remainingWholeMonths` counts boundaries strictly before `termEnd` starting from the one after `now`, which excludes the running month: measured against a 2026-09-08 annual term it returns 11 on day 0 and 0 on day 360. Counting the running month as whole is the one change that fixes both ends at once — 12 on day 0 matches the twelve windows the term actually grants, and 1 in the last window closes the free-upgrade hole
- the unit changes with it: `plan.MonthlyPriceCents` is the monthly list price, while BILLING-4 prices an annual term at ten months for twelve, so the annual monthly-equivalent is `AnnualPriceCents(monthly) / 12`. Derive it from `AnnualPriceCents` rather than writing 10/12 anywhere, so the discount stays defined in one place
- integer arithmetic: a twelfth of an annual price does not divide evenly, so compute the SPAN's total from the annual difference rather than rounding a per-month figure and multiplying it — `annualDifference * months / 12` keeps the first-day identity exact and never accumulates a rounding drift
- BILLING-17 still holds: this is whole-month arithmetic, not day-level proration
- dep on T047 only to avoid two sessions editing `ChangeSubscription`'s neighbourhood at once; the two changes are independent in substance
