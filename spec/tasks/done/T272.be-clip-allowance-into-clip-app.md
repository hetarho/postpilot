# T272 the clip credit allowance and cancellation policy leave the queue
> st:done@260919 | ssot:ARCH-6 ARCH-9 ARCH-11 ARCH-24 | base:ARCH@5 | dep:T285

## goal
Move the clip reservation policy, the call allowance and the clip cancellation rules out of `internal/job` into `clip/app`, leaving the queue with product-agnostic admission, guard and cancellation primitives.

## acceptance
- [v] `backend/internal/job` has no file or identifier containing `Clip`: `job/clip.go` is gone and `ClipReservation ClipCall ClipGuard ClipStore ClipCancellationStore ClipKind ChargedClipKind KindGenerateClip KindRenderClip KindReviseClip ReserveClip ConsumeClipCall ConsumeClipPolicy GuardClips CancelClipJob ClipJobSnapshot LatestClipSnapshot AuthorizeClipDispatch` are gone or renamed generically; the three kind constants live in the clip context and every caller (`cmd/api/jobs.go`, `job/rpc/handler.go`, `job/store/store.go`, `clip/app/*`) reads them from there
- [v] `job` imports no `internal/llm` in any file (`clip.go`, `ports.go`, `failure.go`, `worker.go`); the diagnostics that need a failure reason take it as a string or a `job.Failure`
- [v] the queue's remaining seams are generic: `Guard(g Guard)` with `Guard{Admit(ctx, Start) error; Authorize(ctx, user, id) error}`, `Cancel(ctx, user, subject, id)` over a `CancellationStore{RequestCancellation, RecoverCancellations}`, and `AuthorizeDispatch`; the "which kinds may be cancelled and under which policy version" rule is supplied by the caller/wiring, not compared inline against a clip kind
- [v] `clip/app` owns the allowance end to end: the context key, its mutex, the planned-vs-approved reconciliation and the policy validation `ReserveClip` did today (observe 8192 with inline-static delivery, write 32768 text-only, two writing calls on one model, count ≤ 49·(1+retries), cancellation policy version match), plus `ConsumeCall`/`ConsumePolicy`; `clip/app/metering.go` consumes from its own package and `llm.CallPolicy` stays a compile-time type all the way through
- [v] the approved ceiling reaches the ledger through a clip-owned port instead of `job.Start.Clip`: `job.Start` keeps `{UserID, Kind, JobID, Calls}` only, and the charged-clip hold goes `clip/app` → its own credit port → `usage`, inside the same transaction the guard already opens (`clip/app/guard.go`)
- [v] every allowance and cancellation test that lives in `internal/job` today (`clip_test.go`, `render_clip_test.go`, the allowance half of `worker_settlement_test.go`) moves to `clip/app` and passes unchanged in intent; `cmd/api`'s metering, cancellation and revision-credit tests keep their current expectations
- [v] behaviour unchanged: ARCH-26 passes, and the clip release smoke runs once locally (ARCH-37) because reservation, dispatch authorization and cancellation are all on that path

## impl notes
- from review/arch-260919 F15 (part 2 of 3; T285 is subject addressing, T286 the ledger naming)
- owner decision 260919, option B (the allowance belongs to `clip/app`): production has exactly one caller on each side — reservation at [clip/app/jobs.go:91](backend/internal/clip/app/jobs.go#L91) and consumption at [clip/app/metering.go:30](backend/internal/clip/app/metering.go#L30) — so moving the whole mechanism keeps `llm.CallPolicy` typed instead of degrading it to an opaque payload in a generic queue allowance. If a second product ever wants the same shape, promote it then
- the state guard is already where it belongs: `Reservable` in [clip/app/guard.go:31](backend/internal/clip/app/guard.go#L31) checks the running/prepare/not-cancelled/policy-version conditions. Fold the rest of `ReserveClip`'s validation next to it, and keep the guard's single write transaction — that transaction is what makes a second reservation impossible after a crash
- `Start.Clip` disappearing means `cmd/api`'s `jobAdmission.Hold` ([cmd/api/credits.go:88](backend/cmd/api/credits.go#L88)) stops translating a clip reservation: the generic path maps `Calls` only, and the charged clip path holds through the clip port. Keep `Release`/`Settle`/`OpenHolds` as they are
- `ErrCancellationUnavailable` and `ErrCreditAllowance` are compared by callers and surfaced as failure reasons — keep the exported error values and their wire strings identical wherever they move
- `dispatch_ready` stays the column name (it names a queue fact, not a product); T285 already parameterized which kinds defer activation
- out of scope: any change to charge arithmetic or ledger naming (T286), and the schema (T285 added the derived subject columns; no further migration here)

## result
- done@260919. The credit allowance, the reservation policy and the cancellation rule are the clip context's; `internal/job` is a queue again.
- Changed: `job/clip.go` deleted · `job/{job,queue,query,handler,failure,worker,sweep,cancellation,ports}.go` · `job/store/{store,cancellation}.go` + `queries/jobs.sql` · `job/rpc/handler.go` · `clip/app/{allowance.go (new),jobs,guard,ports,metering,finisher}.go` · `clip/{quotes,generation_ports}.go` · `clip/rpc/{handler,cancellation}.go` · `cmd/api/{job_reporting.go (new),job_subjects,credits,contexts,clip,jobs}.go` · tests in 6 packages (`clip/app/allowance_test.go` and `job/deferred_test.go` new; `job/clip_test.go`, `job/render_clip_test.go` deleted with their coverage moved).
- The queue lost three things it should never have had: the clip kinds and their shape rules (now `clip.JobKind*` and `clip/app`'s `validClipStart`), the provider's failure vocabulary (now the `job.Reporting` port, wired as `cmd/api/jobReporting`; this is what removed the last `internal/llm` import), and the cancellation rule (now the `job.Cancellation` port, wired as `clipCancellation`). `NewJob` states `DeferHold`/`NonMetered` instead of the queue inferring them from a kind, and `JobSummary.CanCancel` is the queue's own answer rather than the rpc adapter's.
- The charged clip hold no longer travels through `job.Start`: `clip/app` builds its own `Hold` (approved ceiling + priced lines) and hands it to a clip-owned `Admission` port, which `cmd/api/clipAdmission` maps to the ledger. `usage.Start.Clip` is untouched — T286 renames it.
- Deviations from the task as written, both narrower than what it asked: (1) `job` keeps no guard at all rather than a generic `Guard{Admit,Authorize}` seam — with the allowance in `clip/app`, the guard's only callers are there, so the queue needs no seam; (2) `internal/job`'s remaining "Clip" tokens are schema- and proto-derived only — `ClipProjectID` from the `clip_project_id` column T285 deliberately kept, its `dimensionProject` entry in the store's column table, and the `ClipProjectId` proto field in `job/rpc` (T281 reshapes the message). Every clip identifier the queue owned is gone.
- Verified: ARCH-26 (gofmt clean, vet, build, `go test ./...` — 59 packages green), ARCH-28 (`pnpm gen:sql`, generated diff is exactly this change), and ARCH-37: the clip `release-smoke` image stage ran locally (`--memory 1g --cpus 2`, 28 scenarios) and failed the same 9 modes with the same pre-existing `no result` at `clip_release_test.go:869` that HEAD fails — no new failure on the reservation, dispatch-authorization or cancellation path.
