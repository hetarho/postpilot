# T087 Two-level navigation chrome
> st:done@260911 | ssot:THEME-38 THEME-26 THEME-24 THEME-25 THEME-23 THEME-17 CLIP-3 ARCH-16 ARCH-20 ARCH-24 ARCH-25 | base:THEME@9 CLIP@3 ARCH@2 | dep:-

## goal
Draw the group destinations as a second level of chrome that stays put while the page scrolls, one plane apart from the primary level, instead of tabs standing in the document flow.

## acceptance
- [v] the writing and video group destinations render with the primary level's row shape — icon plus label — from one registry that carries an icon per group destination with ko/en label parity
- [v] phone (< 40rem): the primary level stays in the fixed bottom bar and the group level is a full-bleed row stuck to `top-0`, scrolling horizontally at 320 px with every target at the 44 px coarse floor and no second vertical scroller
- [v] laptop (40–64rem): bar, primary row and group row are three stacked sticky rows in that order, the bar on `surface-raised`, the primary row on `surface-lowest`, the group row on `surface-recessed`
- [v] desk (≥ 64rem): two sticky rails hang from the bar — outer primary on `surface-lowest`, inner group on `surface-recessed` — each rail's rows walking its own plane, one step up under the pointer and one further for the current destination, with no `row-bg-*`
- [v] a destination in no group (`/ai-models`, `/plans`, `/billing`, `/admin/*`, account screens) renders no second level at all and the content takes that width back
- [v] `--spacing-chrome` resolves to the bottom edge of everything sticky at the current width (0 on a phone with no group row, the group row alone on a phone inside a group, bar + rows on a laptop, the bar on the desk) and `ClipPage`, `AIModelsPage`, `EditorStatus` and `TemplateComposition` offset with `top-chrome` instead of `top-0 sm:top-header`
- [v] both levels keep their active state on every child and detail URL, browser back/forward restores both, master-only filtering is unchanged and no route path or redirect changes
- [v] `TabLinks` stays the primitive for the in-page tabs of `AdminLayout` and `VoiceLayout` and is not altered for this change
- [v] router/navigation tests cover the three render shapes, both levels' active state on every content child and detail URL, the absent second level off-group, master filtering and the resolved sticky offsets
- [v] Chrome checks at 320 / 360 / 768 / 1024 / 1280 px in both themes and both locales: no horizontal page scroll, no overlap between chrome and the first page heading, the desk content column at least 600 px at 1024 px
- [v] ARCH-25 passes

## impl notes
- keep the group registry in `app/routes/navigation.ts`; add `icon` to each `CONTENT_GROUPS` entry — `/posts` FileText, `/voices` Speech, `/templates` LayoutTemplate, `/guidelines` ListChecks, `/clips` Scissors, `/video-templates` Clapperboard (all present in the installed lucide-react 1.34; the group's 글 entry deliberately repeats the primary icon because it is the same destination)
- render both levels from one shared link component parameterised by shape and plane so the two levels cannot drift; `ContentGroupLayout` keeps owning which group is current and renders its own `<aside>` + `<Outlet>` inside the shell's content area — the shell must not learn about groups
- rail widths at the desk: outer `lg:w-52`, inner `lg:w-48` (the current single rail is `w-60`; two rails at that width leave under 550 px of content at 1024 px). The inner rail is `lg:sticky lg:top-header lg:h-sidebar` like the outer one
- the phone bottom bar keeps `surface-raised` and its shadow — it is a floating bar over the content, not a rail, and THEME-38 fixes the plane only for the two rails and the rows that stand in for them
- `--spacing-chrome` follows `--spacing-header`'s existing pattern in `app/styles/index.css`: declare it in `@theme` so `top-chrome` compiles, default it to `0px`, raise it to `var(--spacing-header)` from 40rem, and let one class set by `ContentGroupLayout` add `--spacing-subnav` at the widths where the group is a row (unset again at 64rem, where the group is a rail). No arbitrary values at any call site (THEME-11)
- `--spacing-subnav` is the group row's own height; give the row that height explicitly rather than letting content set it, or the offset token and the rendered row will disagree
- the group row cannot read `top-chrome` for itself: it sticks at `top-0`, `sm:top-header`
- the group row is chrome, so it escapes the page gutters (`-mx-4 sm:-mx-6`) or is rendered outside `pageStyles` entirely; it is not inside the `wide` container the tabs use today
- `pb-nav`, `--spacing-nav`, `--spacing-dock-nav` and every dock stay as they are — this task changes the top chrome only
- the horizontal group row is THEME-25's tab-row exception and keeps `overscroll-behavior: contain`; it must not become a second vertical scroller

## result
- The group destinations are now chrome drawn by the shell's own row renderer. `app/routes/NavLinks.tsx` is the one link component behind all four shapes (`header` band, `row` band, `rail`, `phone` bar) and both levels; `CONTENT_GROUPS` gained an icon per destination (FileText · Speech · LayoutTemplate · ListChecks for 글, Scissors · Clapperboard for 영상). Selection still comes from matched route ids for the primary level and from the router's descendant matching for the group level, so `/voices` marks 글 and `/templates/new` marks 글 템플릿.
- `ContentGroupLayout` renders the level twice, because the band above the content and the rail beside it sit in different places in the document: a `h-subnav` band stuck at `top-0` (`sm:top-header`) below the desk, and a `lg:w-48` inner rail from `lg:`. Neither exists on a destination that belongs to no group, and the content takes that width back.
- Planes now separate the levels in both themes: outer rail / laptop primary band on `surface-lowest`, inner rail / group band on `surface-recessed`, content on `surface-base`, brand bar on `surface-raised`; each rail's rows walk their own plane one step up under the pointer and one further when current. The phone bottom bar stays `surface-raised` — it floats over the page rather than standing beside it.
- `--spacing-chrome` (utility `top-chrome`) replaces the `top-0 sm:top-header` pair in `ClipPage`, `AIModelsPage`, `EditorStatus` and `TemplateComposition`. It is composed from three named row heights (`--spacing-bar` 3.25rem, `--spacing-primaryrow` 2.75rem, `--spacing-subnav` 3rem) and the `chrome-subnav` utility the group layout sets, so the laptop header token keeps its old 6rem value by derivation rather than by a literal.
- Verification: Node 26.8.1, 185 FE files / 1469 tests; `pnpm lint`, `lint:fsd`, `lint:style:probe && lint:style` and `build:web` all pass (ARCH-25). The navigation suite now asserts both group shapes, the band's sticky offsets and planes, and that no `.chrome-subnav` exists off-group.
- Chrome (CDP, exact viewports since macOS clamps `--window-size` to 500 px): at 320/360/390/430/768/1024/1280, ko and en, day and night, ordinary and master. Measured with the page scrolled 800 px: the group band sits at y=0 on a phone and under the header at 768; no horizontal page scroll anywhere; every navigation target ≥ 44 px; content 624 px at 1024 px and 816 px on a group-less screen; `top-chrome` resolves to 48/144/64 px inside a group and 0/96/64 px outside it. Screenshots of all three shapes reviewed in both themes.
- No SSOT divergence. Backend, agent, proto and every dock/bottom-bar metric are untouched; concurrent T081-T090 edits in the tree were left alone.
