# T015 Video upload in the browser, the attachment strip and the contact sheet
> st:done@260906 | ssot:VIDEO-3 VIDEO-4 VIDEO-5 VIDEO-7 VIDEO-9 VIDEO-18 POST-61 GEN-24 | base:VIDEO@1 POST@2 GEN@2 ARCH@1 | dep:T012

## goal
Let the user pick short videos with the photos, gate them by extension, size, duration and count, upload the file as-is with visible progress, show it in the strip and the contact sheet with its observation, and delete it.

## acceptance
- [v] `shared/config`: `UPLOAD_MAX_VIDEOS_PER_POST` 3 · `UPLOAD_MAX_VIDEO_MB` 200 · `VIDEO_MAX_SECONDS` 60 · `UPLOAD_VIDEO_EXTENSIONS` `['mp4','mov','m4v','webm']`, mirrored from `VITE_*` with those defaults and documented in `.env.example`
- [v] the picker and drop zone accept both kinds (`accept` lists both extension sets; label 사진·영상 추가 / Add photos or videos); the gate sorts a file by extension into photo or video, applies the video ceilings (size, count against confirmed + earlier-in-pick videos) and lists refusals under 건너뜀 with reasons `video-too-large` `video-too-many` `video-too-long` `video-unreadable`; photos keep today's verdicts byte for byte
- [v] a video is never decoded or converted: `shared/lib/video/readMetadata(file)` loads the file into an off-DOM `<video preload="metadata">`, resolves `{durationMs, width, height}` on `loadedmetadata`, rejects as unreadable on `error` or after a bounded wait, and revokes its object URL; a duration over `VIDEO_MAX_SECONDS` is skipped `video-too-long` before CreateUpload
- [v] the handshake sends `kind: VIDEO` on CreateUpload and `durationMs` on ConfirmUpload; the PUT of a video reports progress (0 … 100 %) on its card and the card says 올리는 중 n% ; a confirmed video enters the cached post's `videos` with a local object URL until the next GetPost, like a photo
- [v] `entities/video` (`model/types.ts` `PostVideo`, `api` mappers + `useDeleteVideo`, `@x/post`) mirrors `entities/image`; `entities/post` carries `videos` on the post model and `videoCount`; the editor's ① panel strip shows video tiles after the photo tiles with a duration badge (`m:ss`) and a play glyph, plays inline on tap (`<video controls preload="metadata">`, no autoplay), and offers the same delete control as a photo
- [v] the contact sheet pairs each video with its observation by filename: the card shows the shared fields plus `events` as a short ordered list and `speech` when present, plays the clip inline from its presigned URL, and says 관찰 대기 until an entry exists; `entities/observation` gains `events` and `speech`
- [v] the reobserve picker lists videos after photos with the duration badge; a video with no reusable entry is checked and locked like a photo
- [v] the status line reads 영상 관찰 중 / Observing video while the job's observe stage is past the photo count (the FE knows both counts from the post) and 사진 관찰 중 otherwise
- [v] tests: gate verdicts per ceiling and per kind; `readMetadata` with a mocked `<video>`; handshake sends kind and duration; the strip renders a video tile with badge and delete; contact sheet renders `events`; picker ordering; `pnpm --filter ./frontend test` · `pnpm lint` · `pnpm lint:fsd` · `pnpm lint:style:probe && pnpm lint:style` · `pnpm build:web` pass on Node 24.18.0

## impl notes
- `features/upload-photos/model/filter.ts`: `filterFile(file, held: {photos, videos})` returns `{kind:'accepted', attachment:'photo'|'video'}`; `SkipReason` gains the four video reasons; `partitionFiles` splits into `photos` and `videos`
- `upload-batch.ts`: `UploadItem` gains `attachment` and `progress`; the video branch skips the convert pipeline and calls a new `putWithProgress(url, file, contentType, onProgress)` in `features/upload-photos/api/upload-pipeline.ts` built on `XMLHttpRequest` (`upload.onprogress`) ← `fetch` exposes no upload progress; the photo PUT stays as it is; `UPLOAD_CONVERT_CONCURRENCY` still bounds photo conversion, videos upload one at a time
- `entities/image/api/upload-handshake.ts` grows `createUpload(slug, filename, kind)` and `confirmUpload(uploadId, {width, height, durationMs})` returning `PostImage | PostVideo` discriminated by which response field is set; keep the old call shape for photos
- `entities/post`: `Post.videos: PostVideo[]` via `entities/video/@x/post`; the cache patch on confirm/delete mirrors the image patch (POST-10)
- strip UI: extend `PhotoStrip`/`Thumbnail` with a `VideoTile` in `entities/video/ui` (fixed 128 px square like the photo tile, badge bottom-right, `shared/ui` primitives only); drop-zone copy in the `posts` i18n namespace: `upload.addPhotosOrVideos`, `upload.skip.videoTooLarge` (`{{max}}MB`), `videoTooMany` (`{{max}}개`), `videoTooLong` (`{{max}}초`), `videoUnreadable`, `upload.progress` (`올리는 중 {{percent}}%`)
- contact sheet: the card component takes an attachment union; the video card renders `<video>` where the photo card renders `<img>`; `events` as `<ol>` of at most the first 6 lines with a 더보기 disclosure, `speech` under a `들린 말` label
- status label: `posts.job.observingVideo` `영상 관찰 중` / `Observing video`; the stage progress `done` vs the post's photo count decides which label (pure function in `pages/editor/model`)
- no VIDEO block rendering, export or publishing in this task (T016)

## result
Videos are pickable, uploadable, visible and deletable in the browser: one picker takes both kinds, a clip goes up exactly as it was filmed with its progress on the card, and it appears in the strip, the contact sheet and the re-observation picker beside the photos.

changed
- `shared/config`: `UPLOAD_MAX_VIDEOS_PER_POST` · `UPLOAD_MAX_VIDEO_MB` · `VIDEO_MAX_SECONDS` · `UPLOAD_VIDEO_EXTENSIONS`, mirrored from `VITE_*` and documented in `.env.example`; `positiveIntEnv` became a hoisted function so mirrors can be declared where they read best
- `shared/lib/video`: `readVideoMetadata` (off-DOM `<video preload="metadata">`, bounded wait, object URL revoked on every path, nothing decoded) and `formatDuration`
- `features/upload-photos`: the gate sorts by extension and counts the two ceilings separately (`HeldAttachments`), with four video skip reasons; `putWithProgress` on `XMLHttpRequest` because fetch exposes no upload progress; the batch's video branch reads metadata, applies the duration ceiling BEFORE reserving anything, and PUTs the original file; the picker and drop zone accept both kinds
- `entities/video`: `PostVideo`, mappers, `useDeleteVideo`, `VideoTile` (duration badge, `preload="metadata"`, no autoplay), `@x/post` and `@x/image`
- `entities/image/api/upload-handshake.ts`: `createUpload(slug, filename, kind)` and `confirmUpload(id, {width, height, durationMs})` returning a discriminated `ConfirmedAttachment` — which half comes back is the UPLOAD's kind, never the request's
- `entities/post`: `PostDraft.videos`, mapped in the query and patched in the cache (`appendVideo`/`removeVideo`) for the same reason the photo half is patched
- `PhotoStrip` shows clips after the photos with the same delete and confirm sheet, and reports a percentage while one uploads; `ContactSheet` cards a clip with `events` as an ordered list behind a disclosure and `speech`; `ReobservePicker` lists clips after the photos with a duration badge and the same forced rule, now counting `events`/`speech` as eyesight; the editor status line says 영상 관찰 중 once the observe stage has passed the photo count (`observingVideo` in `pages/editor/model/steps.ts`)
- i18n ko/en: the picker/drop copy, the four skip reasons, `upload.progressPercent`, `observation.events`/`speech`/`eventsMore`/`eventsLess`, `generation.observingVideo`
- the fake transport (`test/posts.ts`) learned kinds and videos, so the handshake and the page tests exercise the real contract

differs from the task, deliberately
- the progress string is `upload.progressPercent`, not `upload.progress`: that key is already an OBJECT (`creatingPost`/`converting`/`uploading`/`completed`) and replacing it would have rewritten the batch-level counter.
- `useDeletePhoto` grew a `deleteVideo` rather than gaining a sibling hook: the strip has ONE delete affordance and one confirm sheet, so which RPC runs is the attachment's business.
- an in-flight clip's tile shows a `0:00` badge until it is confirmed. The duration is known by then, but plumbing it into the batch item only to show it for the seconds before the confirm would add a field two layers deep for a number that is about to arrive anyway.
