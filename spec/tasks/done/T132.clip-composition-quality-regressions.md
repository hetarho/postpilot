## goal
Prove template composition and editing quality together with source retention, cancellation accounting and explicit finalization.

## acceptance
- [v] A pasted several-menu template and an equivalent builder-authored template produce equivalent frozen composition semantics and no compulsory category/price/disclosure fields.
- [v] A corpus with two differently priced dishes, ambiguous lookalikes, absent optional facts, silence, scene changes and manual reordering proves caption-to-item traceability and absence of unsupported claims.
- [v] Frame captures and browser playback compare draft versus final footage, crop, glyph placement and visibility across all ratios, sentence/rapid modes and cross-cut fixed text; parity limitations are stated with evidence.
- [v] Legacy template/project conversion, frozen inputs, deleted reusable templates, revision conflicts and failed rerenders preserve the prior result and owner-authored text.
- [v] Offline end-to-end tests cover source reuse after success/failure/cancel/reload, exact retention expiry, free manual rerender, confirmation save flush, pre-confirmation download and durable post-confirmation original deletion.
- [v] Cancellation at prepare/analyze/plan/render/save boundaries proves the credit formula and one terminal outcome, including race/restart cases; provider fixtures supply confirmed, unavailable and over-ceiling costs without paid calls.
- [v] Full local ARCH gates and existing clip release/media/resource regressions pass with archived commands, artifacts and comparison findings; no product release or Naver publish occurs as part of this task.
- [v] Real-device Naver geometry remains the explicitly tracked CDS-11/T110 gate; it is not claimed to be verified by desktop screenshots or treated as satisfied by this task.
- [v] Run the applicable local ARCH-25 through ARCH-29 checks, deterministic proto/SQL regeneration when changed, and spec lint; use a before/after generation snapshot when unrelated generated changes already exist.
