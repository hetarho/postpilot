## result
- Changed: `internal/clip/app/generation.go` — `Run` (414 lines) became `generationRun`, a per-attempt state struct whose stage methods run in order: `accept` (decode + prove the approved work: batch, quote, recovery, planner/renderer brief checks), `prepare` (proxies, re-qualification on the live document, the one reservation, after which `r.ctx` is the admitted context every later call uses), `analyze` (observe each chunk not held by the recovery, identity/segment checks, merge), `write` (flow or resumed flow, narration, both kept for resume), `layout` (renderer placement), `save` (plan + analysis JSON, legacy portable freeze), `finish` (terminal commit through the finisher, then `cleanup`). `recordFailure` is the old deferred diagnostic fold; the two defers keep their order (wrap with the stage first, record second)
- `attempt.go`, `rerender.go`, `generation.go`: the six bare clocks read the service clock
- Tests: `run_stages_test.go` — `accept` refusals (malformed payload, unsupported version, no approval, another batch, batch not consuming, writer differs from the priced one, quote consumed by another job or priced differently, planner budget drift) and its acceptance; `analyze` refusing an observation about another source before keeping it, and a reused chunk costing no call while a fresh one is kept for recovery; `finish` committing the plan with the job then reporting `cleanup`, and surfacing a refused commit; `recordFailure` keeping the worker's source/chunk position over the parser's indexes
