## impl notes
- geometry source of truth is CDS-89..99; `tmp/region-presets/src/presets.py` is the drawing those lines were written from — copy its drawing shapes (flanked label, dot row, frame, outline numeral, pill, square list, rings + textPath arcs) but take numbers from CDS where they differ (label 34 on the stamp arcs, chip labels floor 34, list body floor 48, bottom edge on the BOTTOM anchor 1380)
- item kinds to add to T401's schema: slot fields `face`/`weight` (nanummyeongjo 800, jua 400), `stroke_width`, `outline` (px, draws fill none + white stroke), `lines: 1`, `width`, `decor` {`flank`:{w,gap,alpha} | `dot`:{r,gap} | `frame`:{pad_v,pad_h,stroke,alpha,min_w} | `plate`:{pad_v,pad_h,radius} | `square`:{size,gap} | `pill`:{pad_v,pad_h,stroke,alpha,fill_alpha}}; group items `{"chips":{slots,gap,row_gap}}`, `{"list":{slots,gap}}`; block fields `rotate`, `side_bar` {w,inset}, `stamp` {r_outer, r_inner, stroke_outer, stroke_inner, fill_alpha, dot_r, arc_len, below_gap}
- stamp layout: centre y 900 on 9:16 moved by CDS-79's fraction; slot 1 on the upper arc (textPath on an arc of radius r_inner+14 drawn left→right), slot 3 on the lower arc (radius r_outer−14, sweep reversed so the text reads upright), slot 2 fitted to 320 px inside the inner ring and centred on the stamp, slot 4 a centred caption 64 px below the outer ring; the whole stamp rotates −8°, the slot-4 caption does not
- `prefixIDs` (caption fragments) already rewrites `href="#…"`; give arc paths ids unique per region instance so two regions on one frame never collide
- scrim: extend `applyDeclaredGround` for hook/ending — sample on the union of the block's copy parts (unchanged), pick radial vs edge from the preset's `scrim` field; the overlay needs a radialGradient ellipse; the scrim part's Region is the ellipse's bounding box clipped to the canvas
- the FE mirror only has to keep compiling against the grown JSON; the browser preview still draws the defaults only
