# T025 The four estimator combos and the unit rates the client multiplies
> st:done@260907 | ssot:QUOTA-39 QUOTA-40 QUOTA-36 QUOTA-26 QUOTA-25 | base:QUOTA@5 ARCH@2 | dep:-

## goal
Give the server four operator-assigned model combos, derive per-photo · per-video · per-1000-character · per-post credit rates from their real prices through the charge formula, publish them in `GetMyPlan`, and delete the worst-case reference post they replace.

## impl notes
- milli-credits, not credits, because the client multiplies: `milli(tokens, ratePerMTok) = tokens × ratePerMTok × ChargeMultiplier / 10_000_000` where `ratePerMTok` is the registry's micro-USD per million tokens (`Charge`'s own `microusdPerCredit` 10 000 and multiplier 3, integer arithmetic throughout, no float)
- the four rates, each already carrying the call overhead it is responsible for:
  - `PerPostBase` = `ChargeBase × 1000` + `milli(writePrompt, writeIn)` — the write call every post makes
  - `PerPhoto` = `milli(320, observeIn)` + `milli(200, observeOut)` + `ChargeBase × 1000 / 4` + `milli(2000/4, observeIn)`
  - `PerVideo` = the same with `15 × 300` tokens instead of 320
  - `Per1000Chars` = `milli(1200, writeOut)`
- the observe call is AMORTIZED over the batch of four rather than counted with a ceiling ← QUOTA-40 publishes rates the client only multiplies, and a partial batch therefore reads up to `ChargeBase × 3/4` (1.5 credits) cheaper than the gate will hold; the figure is labelled an estimate and the refusal stays authoritative (QUOTA-36), so a ~10 % optimism at small photo counts buys a slider that needs no round trip
- the token assumptions are constants and NOT reads of `internal/platform/config` ← `OBSERVE_BATCH_SIZE` and the budgets are per-installation env values, and a comparison figure that moved with an operator's environment would have two deploys quoting different post counts (QUOTA-8); the comment records that they mirror the shipped defaults and must be revisited when those move
- a combo resolves its two `llm.ModelInfo` through the registry the handler already holds; a `Lookup` miss is what "no longer registered" means, and it drops the combo rather than pricing it at zero
- `SetEstimatorCombo` goes on `AdminService` (QUOTA-25's third procedure) while the READ rides `ListCatalog`, which the operator's model tab already calls ← one new privileged procedure instead of two, and the assignment belongs on the screen that curates the models it names
- refs cross the wire as the `ModelRef` message the catalog already uses; the purpose check reuses the registry's registration read rather than a second source of truth (→MODEL-14)
- deleting `estimated_posts` from the wire is safe here: the SPA ships with the server it talks to (ARCH-4), and reserving the number keeps a stale client from reading a future field as the old one

## result
- migration `0028_estimator_combos.sql`: one row per combo, both model ids referencing `catalog_models` with `ON DELETE CASCADE`
- `plan`: the token assumptions, `Pricer`, `Rates` and `EstimatorRates` replace `ReferencePostCredits`/`EstimatedPosts`; `milliCredits` truncates rather than ceiling per unit
- `modelcatalog`: `Combo`/`ComboAssignment`/`ComboRates`, the two store methods, `AssignCombo` (purpose-checked), `Combos` (all four, unassigned included) and `ComboRates` (only what can be quoted, `pricerFor` bridging llm's exact price arithmetic into plan's `Pricer`)
- proto: `AdminService.SetEstimatorCombo`, `EstimatorCombo` on `GetMyPlanResponse`, `EstimatorComboAssignment` on `ListCatalogResponse`, `PlanOffer.estimated_posts` → `reserved 4`
- `auth/rpc`: the new privileged procedure with its own two sentinels, and `masterProcedures` grown to three admin entries; `cmd/api` carries the two adapters that keep auth and plan free of catalog types
- `frontend/src/entities/plan`: `EstimatorCombo`, `postCostMilli`, `postsPerGrant` and the mapper that drops a combo it cannot name; `PlansPage` loses the per-offer estimate line and its three copy keys
- tests: the four rates pinned with their arithmetic in `plan_test.go` and again through a real assignment in `modelcatalog`, the unpriced-model refusal, the purpose refusals, `ComboRates` omitting an unlisted/unpriced/deregistered combo, `GetMyPlan` publishing the rates and surviving an estimator failure, the admin procedure's master-only gate and its two mapped reasons, and the client arithmetic (rounding, negatives, floor, zero)
- three deliberate deviations from the acceptance, each recorded here rather than silently:
  1. the table keys models by `model_id` alone, with no provider columns — `catalog_models` is keyed that way and every ref in this product is `openrouter` (MODEL-18), so provider columns would have been a second source of truth
  2. `EstimatorRates` takes two `plan.Pricer` closures rather than `llm.ModelInfo` — `plan` is deliberately a stdlib-only leaf package (its own doc comment), and importing llm to read two price strings would have broken that; the closures keep the token assumptions in plan and the money arithmetic in llm
  3. the proto field is `per_thousand_chars_milli`, not `per_1000_chars_milli` — the numeric segment generated `Per_1000CharsMilli` in Go
- the estimator's own failure never fails `GetMyPlan`: a comparison with no priced combo shows grants and prices, which is a state the operator can fix
