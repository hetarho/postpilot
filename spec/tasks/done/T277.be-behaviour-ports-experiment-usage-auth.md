# T277 experiment, usage and auth ports are per use-case
> st:done@260920 | ssot:ARCH-6 ARCH-7 ARCH-24 | base:ARCH@5 | dep:T272

## goal
Split the table-shaped `Store` ports of `experiment` (24), `usage` (23) and `auth` (23) into behaviour-shaped interfaces per use-case group.

## acceptance
- [v] no port interface in these three contexts exceeds 10 methods; usage's ledger/lot/anchor behaviours are separate interfaces the T270/T272 tx ports can reuse
- [v] services and fakes consume only the narrow interfaces; behaviour unchanged: ARCH-26 passes

## impl notes
- from review/arch-260919 F17 (part 2)
- depends on T272 so usage's surface is the product-agnostic one before it is split

## result
- experiment: `Store`(24) → `RunLedger`(10) `CandidateLedger`(6) `OutcomeLedger`(6) `RunRetention`(2); the sweeper takes `RunRetention` alone instead of the whole store
- usage: `Store`(23) → `WriteScope`(1) `LotLedger`(7) `PurchasedLotLedger`(4) `SpendLedger`(5) `HoldLedger`(6). The T272/T286 transaction port is `WriteScope`, and `Storage` — the composite — is what a tx-scoped store still hands the callback, so the saga ports are unchanged
- auth: `Store`(23) → `AccountStore`(9) `LoginAttemptStore`(2) `AccountPlanStore`(4) `SessionStore`(5) `LinkStore`(3)
- every behaviour port is ≤10 methods; the three `Storage` composites carry embeds only and are held by the composition root (and, for `WithStore`, by the tx clone)
- two renames the split forced: usage's `SpendLedger` field is `charges`, because `spend` is also a method on the service, and experiment's retention port is `purge`, because `retention` is the window
- verify: ARCH-26 green (gofmt, vet, build, 61 packages)
