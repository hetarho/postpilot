# T168 Replace the information frame with an unplated label/value pair and retune the disclosure pill
> st:done@260915 | ssot:CDS-30 CDS-31 CDS-69 CDS-16 CDS-44 CDS-3 CDS-52 CDS-57 CDS-21 | base:CDS@16 CLIP@23 ARCH@2 | dep:T166 T167

## goal
Information reads as a small dimmed label over a crisp value with no shape behind it, the disclosure keeps the only pill at its new size and padding, and every unplated text is brightness-sampled with a shortfall reported as a notice rather than a refusal.

## acceptance
- [v] `role="info"` renders unplated: `t.label` 36 `text_muted` +0.08em above `t.caption` 44 white, both with `stroke_small` and `shadow.text`, CENTER column
- [v] the CDS-69 drawn frame family added by T163 is removed — the frame assets, the variant selection, `info_frames` in `design.json` (both copies) and the `info.Frame` plumbing in `media/composition_roles.go` and `media/composition_header.go`
- [v] the disclosure badge keeps `radius_chip`, takes `pad_chip` 16 vertical / 28 horizontal and `t.badge` 36/800, white on `badge_ad`, with no motion or accent; `ratios.*.chip` and `spacing.gap_chip` go, and `arrangeDeclaredHeader` aligns the disclosure and header-positioned information on one row with `gap_stack`
- [v] brightness sampling runs for the caption, both region blocks (through T167's `regionBounds`) and the information pair, cropped to the text's bounds; the plated-element exemption is gone
- [v] stroked text measures V3 contrast against `stroke_dark` composited over the sampled mean; a pairing still under 4.5:1 after the scrim records a CLIP-108 notice on that element in the plan the layout returns and renders anyway, and V3 lists that behaviour
- [v] V2 floors read body ≥ 48, caption ≥ 40, label ≥ 34, badge = 36 from `type`, never from literals
- [v] test: a label/value pair over a bright frame gains the scrim and over a dark frame does not; a scrimmed pairing that still falls short yields one notice naming the element and no render failure
- [v] test: the disclosure's measured pill height equals 36 px text plus 16 px padding top and bottom, in all three ratios
- [v] test: no manifest element carries a rounded plate except the disclosure
- [v] `cd backend && test -z "$(gofmt -l .)" && go vet ./... && go build ./... && go test ./...`

## impl notes
- T163 shipped the drawn frame family and r15 retired it before it ever rendered in production; remove rather than deprecate, and delete its SVG assets so nothing unused stays embedded
- the label/value pair is the same typographic idea as the intro block's two slots — small dimmed over crisp — so reuse T167's single-line slot measurement rather than writing it twice
- the disclosure is the single exception to "no pills": CDS-31's reason is that it has to read as an imposed marker, so do not restyle it toward the rest
- the V3 notice rides the notice plumbing T164/T165 built (`plan.Notices`, owner-language text under CLIP-108): `Rendering.Layout` already returns a repaired plan, so append the notice there and keep `Legible` reporting the measurement for diagnostics
- `pad_chip`, `stroke_small` and `badge` sizes are tokens after T166; read them, never a literal (ARCH-21)
