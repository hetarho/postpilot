# T245 a selected cut or caption opens its own sheet
> st:done@260918 | ssot:CLIP-53 CLIP-143 CLIP-109 CLIP-17 | base:CLIP@37 | dep:T244

## goal
Selecting a cut or a caption on ②'s timeline opens that item's controls in a bottom sheet carrying the frame they are edited against, and the notice naming that item rides inside it.

## acceptance
- [v] selecting a cut opens a sheet holding that cut's source range, rate, crop, volume, order and delete controls over its source frame; the page behind keeps the preview and the timeline
- [v] selecting a caption opens a sheet holding its text, output-time interval, phrase timing, style and the placement stage over the output frame its interval starts in
- [v] closing a sheet clears the selection, and the timeline's pressed state follows it in both directions
- [v] a notice naming a cut or a caption appears in that item's sheet and nowhere else in ②
- [v] the sheet's committing and destructive controls stay reachable at 360 px with the keyboard open, and the sheet's body is the only thing that scrolls inside it
- [v] vitest: selecting a cut opens its sheet with that cut's controls and its notice; selecting a caption opens the placement stage; closing either clears the selection; no cut or caption control renders in the page while no item is selected
- [v] verify: `pnpm --filter ./frontend test` · `pnpm lint` · `pnpm lint:fsd` · `pnpm lint:style:probe && pnpm lint:style` · `pnpm build:web`

## impl notes
- use `shared/ui` `Sheet` directly (`open`, `labelledBy`, `header`, `footer`, `onClose`): it is already the phone bottom sheet, portalled, focus-trapped and scroll-locked, and it stacks, so a delete confirmation `Dialog` opened from inside one sits on top
- `open` derives from `timeline.selection`, never from its own state; `onClose` dispatches `{ type: 'select', selection: undefined }` so one source of truth drives both the sheet and the timeline's `aria-pressed`
- the cut sheet takes the existing `ClipCutAssemblyControls` plus the order/delete row and the `correction.cut` / `timeline.outputRange` heading the workspace renders today; the caption sheet takes `ClipTextControls`, `ClipCaptionStage` and the caption's move-up / move-down buttons T244 left behind
- the cut sheet's frame is the SOURCE preview at the cut's start (`sourcePreview`), not the composed output, because trimming is source-time work (CLIP-67); `ClipCaptionStage` already renders the output frame the caption sits on
- `measureEditingRoom` sizes the page preview against the dock; a sheet is portalled and outside that measurement, so leave it alone and let the sheet own its height
- one selection means one sheet: render a single `Sheet` whose content switches on `selection.kind`

## result
- `ClipCorrectionWorkspace` renders ONE `Sheet` whose body switches on `timeline.selection`: `open` is `!!cut || !!text`, its `onClose` and its header's close control both dispatch `{ type: 'select' }` with no selection, so the sheet and the timeline's `aria-pressed` are the same fact in both directions. Everything that was the page's cut block and caption block now sits in that body, and the page keeps the preview, the timeline, the scrubber and the validation list.
- `entities/clip-project/model/timeline.ts`: `createClipTimeline` selects nothing (it selected the first cut, which would have landed ② with a sheet over the very preview CLIP-56 asks the owner to review), `select` takes an absent selection and leaves the playhead where it was, and `survivingSelection` returns undefined for an absent one — without that last change every autosave the server acknowledged would have re-selected a cut and reopened the sheet.
- the cut sheet carries the new `ClipCutSourceFrame` (the owner's own source, seeked to the cut's start, the retained original where the session holds no copy, and nothing at all where neither is playable), then the output range, the order row, `ClipCutAssemblyControls`, the trim `RangeSlider`, the source time fields, the current-frame actions, the transition, the volume, the copies and that cut's `ClipNoticeList`. The caption sheet carries the cut-order row T244 left in the page, `ClipCaptionStage` and `ClipTextControls`.
- both destructive controls are in the sheet's PINNED footer, so they stay reachable while the body scrolls under a phone keyboard: `correction.deleteCut` and `timeline.deleteText`, which `ClipTextControls` no longer renders at the end of its own scroller. Each delete clears the selection, because `survivingSelection` otherwise reopened the sheet on the neighbouring cut it falls back to.
- ② has no crop control and never had one — a cut's focal point comes from its observation (CLIP-127) — so the sheet holds every control the cut actually has.
- `src/test/clips.ts` gained a `getClipCaptionPreview` fake (a box per caption, drawn like the style samples fake) and `notices` on `FakeClipProject`: without the first, ②'s placement stage could not be reached from a page test at all.
- `ClipCorrection.test.tsx` gained the sheet case — nothing selected means no sheet, no item control and no targeted notice; a cut opens its sheet with its own controls, its delete and its notice while the preview and the timeline stay in the page; closing by the close control and by Escape clears the selection; a caption opens the placement stage and its own notice. Its other cases, and two in `ClipLifecycle.test.tsx`, now select a cut before reaching a cut control.
- the untargeted notice is asserted with `getAllByText` rather than `getByText`: `FinalizeClipNotices` still prints the same list in the section above the dock, which is T246's to remove.
