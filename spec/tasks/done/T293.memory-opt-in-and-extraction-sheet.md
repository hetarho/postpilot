## goal
Let a draft opt into its memories in ① and turn a finished post into new ones in ③.

## acceptance
- [v] `기억 사용` is a labelled checkbox at the foot of ①'s panel, under the memo and the template data fields, default off, autosaving through the draft's option-save path
- [v] it appears nowhere in the writing brief, and the brief's other options are unchanged
- [v] `기억으로 저장` sits in ③ beside 말투 학습, enabled whenever canonical content exists, disabled with its reason stated in place otherwise
- [v] pressing it starts the extraction job, polls it, and opens the candidates in the shared `Sheet` — a bottom sheet below `sm:` — as a checkbox list
- [v] 저장 creates only the checked candidates; a per-row refusal (duplicate, cap, bound) stays in the sheet with its catalogue reason while the rest are kept
- [v] a start refusal, a job failure or a closed sheet leaves the post exactly as it was and stores nothing
- [v] vitest covers the checkbox's autosave, every disabled reason, the partial-save path and the discard-on-close
- [v] ARCH-25 passes

## impl notes
- the checkbox is on ①'s panel rather than in the brief because it silently changes what a run may write, which is POST-51's own reason for keeping 말투 and 템플릿 readable without opening anything
- the sheet's save loop is the bulk-approval shape 지침's 전부 수락 already uses: one create per checked row, each refusal left in place with its reason rather than failing the batch
- unchecked candidates are discarded when the sheet closes — do not add a "later" affordance; MEM-15 is explicit that a declined candidate does not queue
- the extraction is repeatable, so the empty-result case says so and offers the button again rather than hiding it

## result
- ①: `features/use-post-memories` renders the labelled `기억 사용` checkbox at the foot of the panel, under the memo and the data fields (`EditorGeneratePanel`), default off, autosaving through `SavePostGenerationOptions` with ONLY the flag — presence is the edit unit there, so it cannot disturb the two numbers the brief owns. It is not in the writing brief, and the brief's own options are untouched. `PostDraft` gained `useMemory` and the entity gained `saveUseMemory`.
- the checkbox answers the press immediately and the POST is the truth: the optimistic value holds until the post catches up (derived, not synced in an effect — `react-hooks/set-state-in-effect` forbids the mirror, and the derivation also stops the box flickering back while the refetch is in flight), and a refused save clears it and says so.
- ③: `features/extract-memories` puts `기억으로 저장` beside 말투 학습 in `EditorFinishPanel`, enabled whenever canonical content exists and stating its reason in place when not. It starts the job, polls it with the shared `useJob`, and opens `MemoryCandidateSheet` — the shared `Sheet`, a bottom sheet below `sm:` — as a checkbox list with every row checked to begin with.
- 저장 creates only the checked rows, one ordinary `CreateMemory` each carrying the post's slug as its source. A per-row refusal keeps its row and its catalogue reason, leaves the sheet open, and unchecks what saved so a retry sends exactly what failed. A start refusal, a failed job or a closed sheet stores nothing.
- unchecked candidates are discarded with the sheet — there is no "later" anywhere, and the empty-result case says so and offers the button again, because the extraction is repeatable on demand (MEM-15).
- entity additions: `useStartMemoryExtraction`, `useMemoryExtraction` (enabled only once the job is done — a running job still carries its input) and `useApproveMemoryCandidates` (the bulk-walk shape 지침's 전부 수락 uses). The candidate list is memoized on the answer, which is what keeps the sheet's checkbox state from re-seeding on every render.
- tests: `UseMemoriesField.test.tsx` (default off, the post's value, the autosave carrying only the flag, turning it off, and the refusal reverting the box) and `ExtractMemoriesButton.test.tsx` (the disabled reason, the credit refusal opening no sheet, the checkbox list saving only what is checked with the source slug, the partial save keeping its reason and re-checking only the failure, the discard on close, the empty result, and a failed job storing nothing). The fakes gained `use_memory` on the post service and the two extraction procedures on the memory service.
