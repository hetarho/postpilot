# T328 shared/ui InlinePopover and Toggletip on one extracted anchored-panel hook
> st:done@260924 | ssot:POST-79 POST-81 THEME-23 THEME-30 THEME-32 THEME-33 ARCH-20 ARCH-24 | base:POST@11 THEME@17 ARCH@9 | dep:-

## goal
- Two new domain-agnostic, press-operable overlay primitives, `InlinePopover` and `Toggletip`, ship in `shared/ui` on the positioning logic Listbox already has, and Listbox's behaviour does not change.

## acceptance
- [v] `shared/ui/anchored-panel/useAnchoredPanel.ts` holds the measurement Listbox's `useLayoutEffect` does today: `fixed` placement from the trigger's rect, a drop below with a flip above when the room below is under the floor and the room above is larger, `maxHeight` capped by the viewport ratio and floored at the minimum, re-measuring on captured `scroll` and on `resize`, and a close once a laid-out trigger (`height > 0`) leaves the viewport. The box is kept by value.
- [v] Listbox calls the hook and its markup, classes, `data-drop`, inline style, focus, keyboard and outside-press behaviour are unchanged. Every existing `listbox/Listbox.test.tsx` case passes unmodified, and so do the Popover listbox cases.
- [v] `InlinePopover` has a native `<button type="button">` trigger that sits inside prose: `inline whitespace-normal text-left break-words`, with `aria-haspopup="dialog"`, `aria-expanded`, and `aria-controls` while open. A click, Enter or Space toggles it under every pointer.
- [v] The InlinePopover panel is portalled to `document.body` on `fixed z-overlay-panel` with `data-anchored-panel`, `role="dialog"`, `aria-label={label}` and `tabIndex={-1}`. A press or key opening moves focus to the panel's first focusable element, or to the panel itself.
- [v] Under `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)` only, a `pointerType === 'mouse'` enter opens the panel without moving focus. Leaving the trigger closes a hover-opened panel after `ANCHORED_PANEL_HOVER_CLOSE_MS` unless the pointer is over the panel. A press on a hover-opened panel pins it open instead of closing it. With the default `setup.ts` stub (no fine hover), hover opens nothing.
- [v] Escape, from inside the panel or on the open trigger, closes only that panel and returns focus to the trigger. A containing `Popover` or `Sheet` stays open, and a second Escape closes the container.
- [v] An outside press closes the panel. Tab from inside the panel moves focus to the trigger synchronously and closes, so the traversal continues in the flow and any containing sheet's trap still owns the target. A scroll that carries the trigger off screen closes the panel, returning focus with `preventScroll` only when focus was inside the panel.
- [v] `Toggletip` is a `Button variant="ghost" size="icon"` (`size-10 pointer-coarse:size-11`) holding a lucide `Info` glyph, with `aria-label={label}` and `aria-expanded`. It carries no `aria-controls`, because its bubble is `aria-hidden`. It has the same press and fine-hover rules, focus stays on the button, and Escape on the button closes only the tip.
- [v] Rendered as a sibling of a checkbox's `<label>`, a Toggletip press opens the tip and leaves the checkbox's `checked` untouched.
- [v] The Toggletip bubble is portalled like the InlinePopover panel (`data-anchored-panel`) and is `aria-hidden`. Its text is mirrored into a `role="status"` `sr-only` span that is always mounted beside the button and filled only while the tip is open, so the text is announced on open.
- [v] `Popover`'s outside-press check treats a press inside any `[data-anchored-panel]` as inside. A test mirroring 'survives a modal opened from inside its own panel' proves that pressing inside an InlinePopover panel, and inside a Toggletip bubble, opened from the Popover's panel keeps the Popover open.
- [v] Classes resolve to existing registered tokens only, and no colour role is added. `pnpm lint:style` passes.
- [v] Tests:
  - new `shared/ui/inline-popover/InlinePopover.test.tsx`: press / Enter / Space open and toggle; focus moves in; hover opens only under a stubbed fine pointer; the hover close delay; a press pins a hover-opened panel; Escape inside an open `Popover` and inside a `Sheet` (container stays, trigger focused); outside press; Tab hand-back; the off-screen close; portalled with `data-anchored-panel`; the prose classes.
  - new `shared/ui/toggletip/Toggletip.test.tsx`: name and `aria-expanded`; the checkbox left unticked; hover only under a fine pointer; Escape inside `Popover` and `Sheet`; outside press; focus stays on the button; the live-region text.
  - `popover/Popover.test.tsx`: new 'survives a press inside an anchored panel opened from its own panel'.
  - `listbox/Listbox.test.tsx` unchanged and green, as the regression.
- [v] Verify: `pnpm --filter ./frontend test` · `pnpm lint` · `pnpm lint:fsd` · `pnpm lint:style:probe && pnpm lint:style` · `pnpm build:web`

## impl notes
- Files (all new unless named):
  - `shared/ui/anchored-panel/useAnchoredPanel.ts` and `anchored-panel/config.ts`;
  - `shared/ui/inline-popover/InlinePopover.tsx`;
  - `shared/ui/toggletip/Toggletip.tsx`;
  - `shared/ui/media-query/useMediaQuery.ts`, moved from `popover/useMediaQuery.ts` as its own doc comment asks once a second primitive needs it. It keeps `SM_MEDIA_QUERY` and gains `FINE_HOVER_MEDIA_QUERY = '(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)'`.
  - Update the imports in `popover/Popover.tsx` and `popover/Popover.test.tsx`.
- `shared/ui/index.ts` exports `InlinePopover` and `Toggletip` only. `useAnchoredPanel` stays internal to `shared/ui`, because slices never hand-roll an overlay (ARCH-20).
- Hook signature: `useAnchoredPanel({ open, triggerRef, panelRef, placement, width, onGone, remeasure }): AnchoredPanelBox | undefined`.
  - `placement` is `{ gapPx, gutterPx, minPx, maxViewportRatio }`.
  - `width` is `'trigger'` (the panel spans the trigger, Listbox) or `'content'` (the panel keeps its CSS width, `left` starts at the trigger's left edge and is clamped into `[gutterPx, innerWidth − gutterPx − panelWidth]`, with `panelWidth` read from `panelRef` once mounted).
  - `onGone(focusWasInside: boolean)` fires when the trigger leaves the viewport. The hook holds `onGone` in a latest-value ref, so callers' inline callbacks never re-run the effect.
  - `remeasure` is an extra dependency. Listbox passes `options`, which is what its effect depends on today.
- `AnchoredPanelBox` is Listbox's `PanelBox` with `width` optional (set only in `'trigger'` mode). `samePanelBox` moves into the hook file. The hook also exports `ANCHORED_PANEL_ATTRIBUTE = 'data-anchored-panel'` for Popover's check.
- Listbox passes its own `LISTBOX_*` constants as `placement` and keeps `listbox/config.ts` unchanged, so every measured number stays identical. The hook adds no attribute to Listbox's panel: Popover already exempts `[role="listbox"]` (THEME-30).
- `anchored-panel/config.ts` (ARCH-21, UI tuning beside the control) holds the new primitives' values: `ANCHORED_PANEL_VIEWPORT_GUTTER_PX = 16`, `ANCHORED_PANEL_TRIGGER_GAP_PX = 4`, `ANCHORED_PANEL_MIN_PX = 120`, `ANCHORED_PANEL_MAX_VIEWPORT_RATIO = 0.5` and `ANCHORED_PANEL_HOVER_CLOSE_MS = 150`.
- Both new primitives call the hook with `width: 'content'` and render their panel only while `open && box` (the Listbox pattern). Each portals with `createPortal(…, document.body)`.
- InlinePopover props: `{ label: string; children: ReactNode /* the inline trigger text */; panel: (close: () => void) => ReactNode; className?: string /* on the trigger */; disabled?: boolean }`. The trigger's accessible name is its own text. `label` names the panel.
- InlinePopover trigger classes: `inline whitespace-normal text-left break-words rounded-sm underline decoration-dotted underline-offset-4 hover:bg-row-bg-hover active:bg-row-bg-active`, with `twMerge` for the caller's `className`.
- InlinePopover panel classes: `bg-surface-overlay z-overlay-panel fixed w-72 max-w-popover overflow-y-auto overscroll-contain rounded-lg p-4 shadow-lg`. `surface-overlay` because the panel can open over a `Sheet` (`surface-highest`), as Listbox's does. `rounded-lg p-4` never shares the trigger's step (THEME-23).
- Toggletip props: `{ label: string; children: ReactNode /* plain text only */; className?: string }`. Its bubble uses the InlinePopover panel classes without the scroller, `text-sm text-content-primary`. Interactive content belongs in InlinePopover, which is why the bubble can be `aria-hidden` behind the mirrored live region. The live region is always mounted because a region inserted with its text already inside announces nothing (the rule `LifecycleSteps` follows).
- Open state is `'' | 'press' | 'hover'`:
  - A press while closed opens as `press` (InlinePopover moves focus in through a microtask, as Listbox and Popover do).
  - A press while `hover` pins the panel as `press`. A press while `press` closes it.
  - Pointer enter opens as `hover` only when `useMediaQuery(FINE_HOVER_MEDIA_QUERY) && event.pointerType === 'mouse'`, so a tap's emulated enter never opens a panel (THEME-32: nothing is reachable only by hovering).
  - Pointer leave from the trigger or the panel starts the close timer for a `hover` panel only, and entering either one cancels it.
- Escape follows THEME-30 and must not be replaced by a capture listener.
  - The panel's React `onKeyDown` and the open trigger's `onKeyDown` each call `preventDefault()` and `stopPropagation()`, then close.
  - Stopping the propagation at the portal or root container is what keeps the event from reaching the document listeners that `Popover` and `Sheet` install (the Listbox comment explains the path).
  - A closed trigger lets Escape propagate, so it still closes the container.
- Outside press is a document `pointerdown` listener while open, ignoring the trigger and the panel. It closes and returns focus as Listbox's `close(true)` does.
- No `touch-manipulation` class: `app/styles/index.css` already sets `touch-action: manipulation` on every `button`.
- Popover change: `onPointerDown` adds `if (target?.closest?.(\`[${ANCHORED_PANEL_ATTRIBUTE}]\`)) return` beside the `[aria-modal="true"]` and `[role="listbox"]` exemptions, with a comment naming the two primitives.
- Doc comment and target size (R37): InlinePopover's comment states that a mark inside a sentence takes WCAG 2.5.8's inline exception, since its size is set by the line-height of the prose around it. It therefore does not grow to THEME-23's 44 px under a coarse pointer, while Toggletip, an icon button, keeps the pointer floor.
- matchMedia in tests: the `setup.ts` stub matches only `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, so fine hover is off by default. The hover cases override `window.matchMedia` to match `FINE_HOVER_MEDIA_QUERY` inside `try/finally`, the way Popover's 'stays a popover from sm: up' test does. Use `user.hover` / `user.unhover` and `waitFor` for the delay.
- Out of scope: any domain copy or consumer. T349 mounts Toggletip beside the quality-rule checkboxes, and T351 builds `ReplacementMark` over InlinePopover and grows the tag chips' hit area.

Changed: `listbox/Listbox.tsx` (calls the hook with its own `LISTBOX_*` numbers; markup untouched), `popover/Popover.tsx` (the `[data-anchored-panel]` exemption, the new import path), `popover/Popover.test.tsx` (the new case, the import path), `index.ts` (exports `InlinePopover` and `Toggletip` only).
- One addition beyond the named files: `anchored-panel/useAnchoredDisclosure.ts` holds the `'' | 'press' | 'hover'` state, the hover close timer, the outside press and the Escape stop that both primitives share, so the two cannot drift apart. It stays internal to `shared/ui`, like the hook.
- The hook measures once more after the panel first mounts (a `mounted` dependency), which is what lets a `content` panel read its own width and clamp its left edge. For Listbox that one extra measurement yields the same box, which `samePanelBox` keeps.
- A hover-opened panel closed by an outside press does not pull focus back to its trigger; only a press-opened one does, as Listbox's `close(true)` does.
- The Popover exemption test was mutation-checked: without the `[data-anchored-panel]` line it fails, with it it passes.
- Verify: FE 296 files / 2376 tests (`listbox/Listbox.test.tsx` unchanged and green); `pnpm lint`, `lint:fsd`, `lint:style:probe && lint:style`, `build:web` pass. No backend change.
