# T317 Leaderboard rows tally their badges
> st:done@260922 | ssot:MODEL-38 MODEL-41 MODEL-63 ARCH-3 ARCH-6 ARCH-24 | base:MODEL@14 ARCH@7 | dep:T315 T316

## goal
- Each leaderboard row shows the badges its model collected inside the same scope, stage and window, positive and negative counted separately and 기타 counted without its notes.

## acceptance
- [v] GetLeaderboard entries carry `badge_tallies` (badge, count) computed from the winner verdicts of the same window and scope; `other` is a count only; tallies change no rating, match count or order.
- [v] `all` scope tallies expose per-model counts only — no notes, accounts or experiments.
- [v] A row shows up to three positive and three negative badges with counts, ordered by count then catalog order, and nothing when it has none; phones keep the three-column row with the chips wrapping under the label; ko/en.
- [v] Tests: BE store (tally query per scope/window, `other` counted, notes absent), domain (tallies attach to the right entry and never touch ranking), handler mapping; FE (tally rendering, empty, ordering); ARCH-25/26/28 gates pass.

## impl notes
- Proto: `message BadgeTally { VerdictBadge badge = 1; int32 count = 2; }`; `LeaderboardEntry.badge_tallies = 19` (`repeated BadgeTally`). `pnpm gen:proto`.
- Store: `ListBadgeTalliesForLeaderboard` (+ `All` variant without `user_id`): join `model_experiment_badges` to `model_experiment_candidates` on `(experiment_id, id)` and to `model_experiments`, `WHERE e.stage = ? AND e.outcome = 'winner' AND e.decided_at >= ?`, grouped by the candidate's model ref columns and badge; returned by `OutcomeLedger.LeaderboardData` as a third slice (`[]BadgeTally{Model, Badge, Count}`). ASCII-only query file; `pnpm gen:sql`.
- Domain: `LeaderboardEntry.BadgeTallies []BadgeTally` attached in `BuildLeaderboard` after ranking, sorted by count desc then catalog order, only for models already on the board.
- FE: `LeaderboardEntry.badgeTallies` in the entity type and mapper; `widgets/model-leaderboard` renders `Badge` chips (`tone` by polarity) reading `{{label}} {{count}}`, with labels through the entity's badge helper from T316.

## result
- Proto: `BadgeTally` and `LeaderboardEntry.badge_tallies`. A tally carries a badge and a count and nothing else, pinned by a test that asserts the message has exactly two fields.
- Store: `ListBadgeTalliesForLeaderboard` and its all-account twin join the badge rows through the candidates to their model, grouped by model ref and badge, bounded by the same window the ratings use. The note column is never selected, so no private text can reach a board.
- Domain: `BuildLeaderboard` takes the tallies and attaches them after ranking, most often first and then in catalog order, and only to a model the board already holds — a badge earned by a model whose verdicts all aged out has nothing to explain here. A service test asserts the same verdicts rank identically with and without badges.
- Frontend: `badgeTallies` on the entry and its mapper, which drops a badge this build does not know rather than rendering a blank chip. The leaderboard row shows at most three positive and three negative chips under the label, toned by polarity, reading label and count.
- Committed together with T315 and T316: all three were implemented in the same session and share every backend file they touch.
