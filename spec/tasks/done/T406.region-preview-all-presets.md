# T406 The composition preview draws every intro and outro preset as the renderer lays it out

## goal
The browser's composition preview draws all eight intro and seven outro presets — decoration, chips, list, stamp arcs and rotation included — at the renderer's positions, and the template editor's preview lets the author look at the outline in any of them.

## acceptance
- [v] the TS port (`entities/clip-design/model/region-layout.ts`) mirrors Go `LayoutRegion` for every preset: edge and left anchors with the left measure cap, slot measures and decoration widths, frame/plate padding in the stack, flank/dot/frame/plate/pill/square/side bar/rings/ring dots as shapes, chip rows, the list's shared size, stamp arcs and the below slot, rotation and the block's scrim kind; the renderer-only gate is gone
- [v] the Go fixture `testdata/region-layouts.json` (+ FE mirror) covers all 15 presets on every ratio with short, shrinking or wrapping, and partly empty rows, and records each line's x, alignment, arc and turn plus every shape and the rotation; the port reproduces it
- [v] `CompositionDesignFrame` draws a region's lines at their own x and alignment, arcs as `<textPath>` on per-frame path ids, outline text as a white stroke with no fill, a slot's own stroke width, every shape (rect with radius, circle, fill and/or stroke, shadow) and the turned parts inside one rotation group; a slot's decoration is drawn by the entry that draws the slot, the block's rules and decoration by the entry that paints them; the caption keeps out of every shape as well as slots and rules
- [v] `CompositionPreview` offers the intro and the outro preset beside the ratio (preview only, starting at the new-project defaults, nothing saved), and the frame draws the chosen ones
- [v] tests: port vs fixture for all presets; frame tests draw decoration, arcs and the rotation group for representative presets; the preview's preset choice changes the drawn preset; FE gate (ARCH-25) and BE gate (ARCH-26) green

## impl notes
- CLIP-214 leaves preview implementation to tasks; the port stays the preview's layout (T401) because the frame redraws on every keystroke of the template editor, which a server round trip per edit cannot follow
- the drawing follows region-v2's markup (`overlay/presets/region/overlay.svg`): same paint rules, shapes before lines, arcs after lines, turned parts in one group; no scrim, since the preview has no footage ground (CDS-44)
- preset names reuse `composition.design.intro_<id>` / `outro_<id>`; the selects are the existing `CompositionSelect`, no help copy

## result
The browser's composition preview now draws all eight intro and seven outro
presets as the renderer lays them out: decoration, chips, the list, stamp
arcs and rotation included. The template editor's preview lets the author look
at the outline in any of them.

TS port (entities/clip-design/model/region-layout.ts)
- Mirrors Go LayoutRegion line for line:
  - edge and left anchors with the left measure cap
  - per-slot measures and decoration widths, frame/plate padding in the stack
  - flank/dot/frame/plate/pill/square/side bar/rings/ring dots as shapes
  - chip rows that wrap, the list at its smallest fitted size
  - stamp arcs and the below slot, block rotation, the block's scrim kind
- The renderer-only gate is gone. Lines carry x, alignment, arc and turn.
- The pill/ring fill mirrored from the Go layout is one named constant under a
  style-escape pragma, since it is not a UI colour.

Fixture
- testdata/region-layouts.json (+ FE mirror) now covers all 15 presets × 3
  ratios × short, shrinking/wrapping and partly empty rows (135 cases). It
  records each line's x, alignment, arc and turn, every shape and the
  rotation.
- The port reproduces every case within 0.01, shapes and turns included. It
  sits on top of fcb6e477's one-step tolerance for arm64/amd64 rounding.

Frame (CompositionDesignFrame)
- Draws each region line at its own x and alignment, arcs as `<textPath>` on
  per-frame path ids, outline text as a white stroke with no fill, a slot's
  own stroke width, and every shape (rect with radius, circle, fill and/or
  stroke, shadow).
- Turned parts go in one rotation group, in region-v2's order: rules,
  decoration, lines, arcs.
- A slot's decoration is drawn by the entry that draws the slot. The block's
  rules and decoration are drawn by the entry that paints them.
- The caption keeps out of every shape as well as slots and rules. No scrim:
  the preview has no footage ground.

Template preview (CompositionPreview)
- Offers the intro and the outro preset beside the ratio, starting at the
  new-project defaults. Preview only, nothing saved, no help copy (CLIP-14).

Checked in a real browser
- Chrome through the dev server on the template editor: all 15 presets draw
  as the renderer's own samples do.

Tests
- Port vs the 135-case fixture, plus every design.json preset laying out.
- Frame vs fixture (baselines, textPath count, shape count, rotation
  centre), plus outline paint, frame, Jua stroke and plate inside the turn,
  pills, list squares, stamp rings/arcs and the upright caption below the
  stamp.
- CompositionPreview: choosing presets redraws the frame in them.

Gates: FE (ARCH-25), BE (ARCH-26), pnpm test:dev.
