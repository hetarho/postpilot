## goal
Apply one trusted entitlement decision to catalog projections, saved selections and all AI starts, including the completed voice-check/comparison paths.

## acceptance
- [v] Per-purpose classification accepts separate free and the four paid grades; unclassified registrations are confined to operator curation. Admin single/bulk operations validate free zero-cost eligibility and bulk preview/export/apply round-trip free without partial writes.
- [v] Free accounts can run compatible curated free models at zero balance and cannot run any paid grade even with retained credit. Light/Basic/Pro/Max have cumulative value/balanced/premium/top rights; master bypasses product grade/balance gates but not supplier, capability or price-safety constraints.
- [v] Free qualification checks the applicable path, not only the id suffix or prompt price: unknown/positive applicable input/output/media/conditional pricing refuses free execution without silently charging or switching models. No compatible free path and provider-limit refusal have stable distinct reasons.
- [v] SaveSelection/pair/preset application, generation/revision/storyline, voice analysis/check, post and voice comparisons, clip quote/start and winner adoption invoke the shared gate. Seven-slot presets fail atomically with all offending refs grouped by cause; each experiment remains one admission.
- [v] Downgraded or unaffordable saved refs/history remain stored and visible with plan/affordability reasons; real deregistration still uses conditional missing-row removal. New starts require current eligibility; admitted work keeps its frozen entitlement but rechecks live capability/pricing.
- [v] BE tests exercise a tier×grade matrix, trusted all-free zero-FX admission, forged paid refs at each start seam, mixed free/paid jobs, atomic preset refusal, downgrade mid-job and pricing drift. Run ARCH-26/28.

## impl notes
- Consume the voice endpoints and reflection experiment produced by T473/T474; this is why T474 is a real dependency. Audit existing enqueue and registry boundaries instead of adding a parallel per-feature wallet or duplicated plan catalogs.
- Extend the purpose-registration level constraint with free; do not infer a grade from cost. Keep MODEL-51’s models-v1 format and use the explicitly allowed free token. Operator curation may retain unset classifications without exposing them as ordinary selectable refs.
- The consumer-owned entitlement projection carries entitled, required plan, classification and unavailable reason independently from required credits. A locked ref is not missing. Admission records the entitled refs/tier and passes trusted context to execution; clients cannot assert an operator or free exemption.
- Use existing catalog/endpoint pricing and capability qualification; zero prompt/completion metadata alone is insufficient when that request path has another applicable charge. Refuse unsafe free-path drift before calls, keep raw provider ledger costs if supplier-reported usage unexpectedly appears, and never convert a zero-cost job into a hidden paid debit.
- Expose stable typed reasons/parameters in proto/domain mappers, including required tier, free-path unavailable and supplier temporary limitation; T481 owns ko/en rendering. Do not encode a product daily free-job counter, hardcoded supplier limit or automatic paid retry.
- Preserve shipped recommendation ids, their seed-existence test, old experiment snapshots and ordinary deregistration behavior. Current operator curation stays authoritative; do not pick or register live model names as part of this task.
