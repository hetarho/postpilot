## goal
The template editor asks for the intro preset, the caption treatment and the outro preset before showing the body, and the body's intro and outro are fixed forms whose only editable parts are each slot's text and the block's interval.

## impl notes
- the selection is three root attributes on the body, not recipe fields: `ClipRecipe` gains nothing, the pickers read and patch `<clip>` through `patchCompositionSource`, and the preselected defaults for a legacy body come from the same b/bold/e the backend's `ReadStored` applies
- design before body is a UI order, not a lock: a saved template may reopen straight into the body with the design step collapsed above it, and the pickers stay reachable there
- the memory rule "no native selects" applies to every picker here; the thumbnails reuse `CompositionPreview`'s drawing so the design step and the representative preview cannot disagree
- the skeleton forms are the builder's `CompositionTextControls` specialised by role — do not fork the component; branch on `role` inside it and keep the content-region path unchanged
- the category `preset` field (restaurant · cafe · …) is untouched and unrelated; name everything here `intro`, `outro`, `caption` to keep the two apart
