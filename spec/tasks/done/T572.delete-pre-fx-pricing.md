# T572 Delete the pre-FX pricing regime from the ledger
> st:done@261005 | ssot:QUOTA-14 QUOTA-59 ARCH-24 | base:QUOTA@34 ARCH@15 | dep:T569 | touches:backend/internal/usage/ backend/internal/clip/app/ backend/internal/plan/ backend/cmd/api/credits.go backend/internal/platform/db/migrations/

## goal
Every hold, settlement, estimate and quote prices through the FX path alone; the legacy branches, their duplicate reservation loops and the per-renew legacy-lot expiry are gone, so a pricing change has one regime to reason about.

## acceptance
- [v] `usage.Service` requires a rate selector (constructor argument or a required wiring checked at build, ARCH-40); every `s.rates == nil` branch and every `legacy` branch in Hold, matchExistingHold, Settle/SettleCause, CreditsFor and the clip quote is removed, keeping the FX behaviour exactly
- [v] `ReservationCredits` (the non-FX copy of `ReservationCost`) and `plan.Charge`/`boundedCharge`/`cancelledCharge` go when nothing but the removed branches used them; one reservation-cost loop remains
- [v] `ExpireLegacyMonthlyLots` no longer runs on every renew: a forward migration expires any still-open legacy monthly lots once, and the query, its port method and T557's `LegacyMonthlyLotOpen` probe are deleted
- [v] open admissions written by the pre-FX regime (no frozen rate) are handled by the same migration: settled at their held credits as consumed-nothing refunds or deleted with their hold-lot rows — pick the one that leaves every balance equal to "nothing was charged for unfinished pre-FX work", and test it on a seeded DB
- [v] tests construct the ledger with a fixed-rate selector helper instead of the legacy path; FX behaviour tests unchanged; the seeded-migration test above passes
- [v] behaviour unchanged otherwise: ARCH-26 green; `pnpm gen:sql` clean (ARCH-28)
