## goal
A reader compares monthly versus annual plan prices with one control above the cards and starts a new subscription with that tier and term already selected in checkout.

## acceptance
- [v] A keyboard-accessible monthly/annual control immediately above the five cards starts at monthly and switches all paid prices together; free remains free, annual shows the full upfront KRW amount, an approximate monthly equivalent and the true ten-for-twelve saving, and no 20% claim appears.
- [v] Each eligible paid card has one clearly labelled Subscribe action; selecting annual on a first purchase opens checkout with both tier and annual term, and checkout quotes the selected term before the owner commits payment. A monthly selection starts monthly.
- [v] A valid term survives hosted card registration's return to checkout. An absent or invalid term defaults to monthly; an unknown tier remains an invalid checkout selection.
- [v] For an active subscriber, a higher-tier action retains the current term and shows its actual prorated quote in checkout; the period control is visibly a price comparison and directs term changes to Billing. Current-tier, lower-tier, free and master actions keep BILL's existing rules.
- [v] FE tests cover both periods and annual math on cards, tier/term handoff and return, invalid search values, active upgrade term, and the operator with no checkout action; `/about` remains public and has no checkout controls. Run ARCH-25 verification.

## impl notes
- Use `PlanOffer.monthlyKrw` and `annualKrw` from GetMyPlan for authenticated prices; the selected term is local `/plans` presentation state, with no write or quote request on a toggle. `/about` keeps its code-owned public mirror and no selector.
- Extend checkout search with optional `term: 'monthly' | 'annual'`; initialize the checkout control from that validated value, and keep it in the payment-method `returnTo` URL. Quote and final payment still use BILL's existing server-confirmed path.
- Active upgrades pass/use the subscription's existing term regardless of price-preview selection; keep term switching on Billing's scheduled-change path. Do not introduce a combined immediate upgrade plus term switch.
- Give PlanLadder a selected-term input for authenticated price presentation without changing the public `/about` card contract; no frontend price constant or discount percentage is authoritative.
