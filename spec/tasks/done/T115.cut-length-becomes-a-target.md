# T115 Cut length becomes a target, not a refusal
> st:done@260912 | ssot:CDS-37 CDS-50 CLIP-19 ARCH-24 | base:CDS@3 CLIP@6 ARCH@2 | dep:-

## goal
Hold every cut inside its target length where the approved timeline allows, and never fail a generation for missing one.

## acceptance
- [v] `design.CutBounds` takes the template preset as well as the scene and answers with the TARGET range: the preset's own `cut_min_s`/`cut_max_s` where the template names one, the shared 1.2–6.0 s where it does not, and a `food` scene capped at `cut_max_food_s` either way
- [v] `holdCutLengths` returns no error: a cut over its target is trimmed to it, a cut under it grows into its own segment as today, and one that still cannot reach the target is LEFT AS IT IS — `plan_cut_length` is deleted from the ai package and from the reason list in `internal/job/worker.go`
- [v] the target reconciliation keeps the target as the shrink floor only while some cut still has room; when no cut does, the floor drops to `2×FadeMS+1` (the existing `plan_cut_fade` bound) so a reachable timeline is never refused
- [v] `composeTimeline` passes `in.Template.Preset` through; nothing new is plumbed — `PlanningInput.Template` already carries it
- [v] `ai/service.go`'s exposure-extension ceiling reads the same `CutBounds`
- [v] tests: a preset narrows the target and a template with none takes 1.2–6.0 s · a food scene is capped at 4.0 s under every preset · a plan whose cuts cannot reach the target still compiles and still meets the approved duration · the 카페 case — a 15 s target with four cuts of 4 s, which cannot fit and must compile short rather than fail · the recorded live single-take response, which now compiles instead of being refused
- [v] the `release-smoke` multi-source fixture is restored to its recorded lengths (the 1000 ms cut T109 raised to 1200 ms) and still passes, because no length now refuses a plan
- [v] ARCH-26, ARCH-28 and ARCH-25 pass

## impl notes
- CDS-37 r3 is the whole change: the numbers stay, their force does not. The only HARD length rule left is `ValidateEditPlan`'s existing `plan_cut_fade` (a cut must outlast the transitions that eat into it), which is a renderability bound and not a rhythm one — do not add a new one
- the preset is a target and not a gate, so a 카페 template (4–6 s) with a 15 s goal does not have to land exactly three cuts; it lands what the model gave and holds what it can
- T108's `TestACutThatCannotReachTheFloorIsRefused` inverts: it becomes the proof that such a plan COMPILES. `TestRecordedLiveClipResponses` loses its refusal branch for the same reason — the recorded single take is executable again and the test says so
- `design.CutBounds` is the one place the preset range is read; `design.json`'s preset entries already carry `cut_min_s`, `cut_max_s` and `rhythm`, and `rhythm` stays unread (CDS-36 r3 is one flat 40 %)
