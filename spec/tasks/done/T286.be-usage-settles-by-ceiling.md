# T286 the ledger settles by approved ceiling, not by product kind
> st:done@260920 | ssot:ARCH-6 ARCH-7 ARCH-24 | base:ARCH@5 | dep:T272

## goal
Remove every clip name from `internal/usage` by keying the ceiling rules on "this admission has an approved maximum" instead of on a job kind, without changing one line of charge arithmetic.

## acceptance
- [v] `backend/internal/usage` contains no identifier, comment-level rule or SQL literal naming a product: `chargedClipKind` is gone, `usage/clip_pricing.go` is renamed (e.g. `ceiling.go`) and `ClipCredits ClipReservation PricedCall boundedClipCharge cancelledClipCharge ClipAccounting ClipAccountingForJob` carry generic names
- [v] the predicate everywhere becomes "the admission carries an approved ceiling": `Hold` sets `approved_max_credits` whenever the start carries one, `Settle` and the accounting projection branch on `ApprovedMaxCredits != nil` / `approved_max_credits IS NOT NULL`, and `ClipAccountingForJob`'s `kind IN ('generate_clip','revise_clip')` filter is replaced by that condition
- [v] `RecordCall`'s protected 5-second record window and `Record`'s frozen-price branch key on the frozen call price in context (`WithCallPrice`), not on a kind
- [v] charge arithmetic is frozen: `boundedCharge`, `cancelledCharge`, the quote function, the failed-with-zero-confirmed waiver, the cancellation-fee waiver at policy version 1, the shadow/exempt projection and the overrun `spendUpTo` branch keep their exact expressions and produce identical credits for identical inputs
- [v] the wire-visible strings are untouched: `CLIP_MODEL_PRICING_UNAVAILABLE`, `CLIP_QUOTE_REQUIRED` and `CLIP_CREDIT_CEILING_EXCEEDED` keep their literal values (the FE and the agent mirror them) even though their Go identifiers change
- [v] parity is pinned: `usage`'s settlement, cancellation and ceiling tests and `cmd/api`'s settlement/cancellation/metering tests keep their current expected credit numbers with no edits beyond renamed symbols, and one added table test walks (outcome × confirmed cost × ceiling × policy version) asserting the same charges as before the rename
- [v] behaviour unchanged: ARCH-26 passes and ARCH-28's `pnpm gen:sql` diff is clean

## impl notes
- from review/arch-260919 F15 (part 3 of 3; T285 is subject addressing, T272 the allowance)
- owner decision 260919, option C (de-name, freeze the math): the clip rules in `usage` are already equivalent to "an approved ceiling exists" — `chargedClipKind` gates exactly the paths that read `ApprovedMaxCredits` ([service.go:241](backend/internal/usage/service.go#L241), [service.go:441](backend/internal/usage/service.go#L441)) — so the product name can go without a policy registry on the money path. A per-kind `SettlementPolicy` port was rejected: it adds runtime wiring and a second verification surface to the charge path for a second product that does not exist yet
- do NOT move the quote/charge functions into `clip/app`: `Hold` needs the quote to size the reservation and `Settle` needs the bounded charge at boot from `OpenHolds`, where only the admission row exists. They stay in `usage` as ceiling primitives; only their names change
- cancellation stays keyed on `cancellation_policy_version` (already a generic column) — a version of 1 means "cancellable with fee", which is a ledger fact, not a clip fact
- `ClipAccounting` is the ledger's own projection and keeps its shape; the clip-facing rename happens only at the mapper in `clip/app/credits.go`, which already translates it into `clip.Accounting`
- dep on T272 is a file-collision dep as much as a logical one: both edit `cmd/api/credits.go` and the `usage.Start` reservation field

## result
- done@260920. `internal/usage` names no product: `clip_pricing.go` is `ceiling.go`, and `ClipCredits ClipReservation ClipAccounting ClipAccountingForJob boundedClipCharge cancelledClipCharge ErrClipPricing ErrClipApproval FailureReasonClipCreditCeiling Start.Clip` are `ReservationCredits Reservation ReservationAccounting AccountingForJob boundedCharge cancelledCharge ErrPricingUnavailable ErrApprovalRequired FailureReasonCreditCeiling Start.Approval`. A grep for clip/Clip in the context (outside the wire constants) returns nothing.
- Changed: `usage/{ceiling.go (was clip_pricing.go),accounting,service,usage}.go` · `usage/store/{accounting.go,queries/usage.sql,sqlc/*}` · `clip/app/{ports,credits}.go` · `cmd/api/{credits,contexts,job_subjects}.go` · usage tests renamed (`reservation_test.go`, `ceiling_test.go`) plus `usage/ceiling_parity_test.go` (new).
- The predicate is the root's list, not a kind literal: `usage.NewService(..., approvedKinds...)` takes the kinds that may not start without an approved ceiling (`cmd/api/approvedCeilingKinds`), `Settle` and the accounting projection read `approved_max_credits` off the admission row, and `Record` prices from the frozen call policy in context.
- Differs from the task as written, in the direction of keeping the charge identical: the accounting query's `kind IN ('generate_clip','revise_clip')` did NOT become `approved_max_credits IS NOT NULL`. An admission written before the ceiling column existed has a NULL ceiling, and that swap made those legacy rows invisible to the owner's accounting view (`TestClipAccountingDistinguishesPendingZeroLegacyAndMaster` caught it). The filter now takes the kind list as a JSON parameter the ledger passes down, so the row set is exactly today's and the product name still leaves the context. For the same reason `Hold` keeps a kind-driven "this work must be approved" check rather than inferring it from the presence of a reservation — a charged start that lost its approval must still be refused, not silently priced from its planned calls.
- Charge arithmetic is untouched: `boundedCharge`, `cancelledCharge`, `ReservationCredits`, the failed-with-zero waiver, the cancellation fee, the shadow/exempt projection and the overrun branch keep their expressions. Parity is pinned by every pre-existing settlement, cancellation and metering test passing unedited beyond renamed symbols, plus `TestApprovedSettlementChargesAreUnchanged`, a table over outcome × confirmed cost × ceiling × policy version asserting the exact credits and fees.
- Wire strings unchanged: `CLIP_MODEL_PRICING_UNAVAILABLE`, `CLIP_QUOTE_REQUIRED`, `CLIP_CREDIT_CEILING_EXCEEDED` keep their literal values behind the renamed identifiers.
- Verified: ARCH-26 (gofmt clean, vet, build, `go test ./...` — 59 packages green) and ARCH-28 (`pnpm gen:sql`, generated diff is exactly this change).
