## goal
Write, revise and test prompts ask for at least `tag_count` tags with broad labels filling the tail, the writer infers flavor/texture/aroma from inputs as experience, and every changed rule is edited in place.

## acceptance
- [v] The write prompt's stage line reads `title, 한 줄 summary, N개 이상의 tags, blocks를 반환하세요.` / `Return title, a one-line summary, at least N tags, and blocks.`; no prompt text in `internal/generation` or `internal/guideline` still says 최대 N개, 0개여도, 채우려고 덧붙이지, at most N tags, upper bound or never pad for tags.
- [v] The parser keeps every returned tag in model order up to `post.TagOutputMax` (30), whatever `tag_count` is; a shorter or empty list is accepted; a revision returning the current tags byte-for-byte keeps them, a changed list is cut only at 30.
- [v] The `tags` 기본 지침 leads with names, then area/entity combinations, fills the rest of the count with broad category/topic labels, allows purchase/sponsor labels only when the material says so, and no longer carries its revision sentence.
- [v] The `impressions` 기본 지침 is renamed 내 감상은 지키고 맛은 재료로 살리기 / Keep my impressions, draw taste from the material and lets the writer infer flavor, texture and aroma from inputs as experience; the source-honesty contracts classify such inference as AI-added instead of forbidding it; `memory_impressions` no longer names the old title.
- [v] Neither no-voice golden's system part (`write_prompt_no_voice.golden`, `revise_prompt_no_voice.golden`) grows in rune count against its task-start version (GEN-85).
- [v] Tests: `internal/generation/tag_count_test.go` (minimum sentence ko/en, keeps more than the count, cuts at 30, shorter/empty accepted, revision unchanged-equality and 30 cut); `internal/guideline/defaults_test.go` `TestPostRecommendationsKeepPlanClaimsSeparateAndAttributeTitleAdvice` (new tag and impression needles, old ones absent) and the `memory_impressions` assertion; every prompt golden that changes; `cmd/api/t674_tag_minimum_integration_test.go` replacing `t634_tag_cap_integration_test.go`; prompt-evaluation checks/fixtures renamed to the minimum.
