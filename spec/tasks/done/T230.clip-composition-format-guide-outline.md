# T230 형식 안내 and 원문 teach the outline grammar
> st:done@260918 | ssot:CLIP-58 CLIP-71 CLIP-113 | base:CLIP@33 ARCH@3 | dep:T227

## goal
Rewrite the copyable format guide and the 원문 refusal copy so an outside AI is told the outline grammar alone and a paste is refused only for what the grammar cannot read.

## acceptance
- [v] `GRAMMAR` and `CLIP_COMPOSITION_EXAMPLE` teach the outline: entry kinds, their order, bindings and the size ceilings — and no design attribute, no `basis`/`start`/`end`, no slot count, no per-preset character table, no `invalid_skeleton`
- [v] the example declares a caption entry and more than one intro line, and parses under `ParseTemplate` in the existing guide test
- [v] the guide states that where an entry is drawn follows the clip's own design, chosen in ①
- [v] refusal copy (`builder.reasons`) covers the reasons that survive and drops the retired ones, in ko and en
- [v] a pasted body is refused only with an entry id and a line from the parser — no surface refuses a paste for a preset, a slot count or an absent region
- [v] tests: the guide's example parses, every reason key the parser can emit has copy, and a paste with an unknown tag shows its line
- [v] ARCH-25 verify clean

## impl notes
- one file owns the grammar text (`entities/clip-template/model/composition-guide.ts`); the ko/en `composition.guide` strings interpolate it, so the grammar is written once.
- CLIP-113 binds the guide and the grammar to change together: if T227 changed a construct, this task is where the guide catches up — list the constructs against `composition/README.md` rather than against memory.
- keep `원문 복사` as it is: handing the saved body back to the AI is half the round trip the owner asked for.
- parallel sessions: `resources/{ko,en}/clips.ts` collides with T229 and T232.

## result
- `composition-guide.ts`: `GRAMMAR` teaches the outline — the entry kinds, that their order is the only position they declare, that a text carries no basis/start/end, that how many intro and outro lines are drawn is the clip's own design and a surplus one is noticed rather than refused — and teaches no preset attribute, no interval, no per-preset character table and no `invalid_skeleton`. `CLIP_COMPOSITION_EXAMPLE` now shows two stages between the entries and a caption entry, so the example is itself an outline.
- the surrounding prose in ko and en says the same in one paragraph, and the corpus case that pins the example follows it byte for byte (renamed to "the copied guide example").
- refusal copy: `invalid_design` is no longer a parse reason and left the TS `CompositionReason` vocabulary — the key stays only for the RENDER's own refusal in the attempt inspection, reworded for that; `invalid_skeleton` now says what the outline still refuses (region text outside a row, a region declaring position/alignment/style).
- `composition-sample.ts`: a template that declares its own caption entries is previewed with those instead of the sample line.
- BUG found and fixed while testing the paste path: a body with more region lines than the preset holds crashed the whole editor — `CompositionDesignFrame` read `slots[i]` past its end and the error boundary replaced the page. The preview now leaves a surplus line out exactly as the render does (CLIP-147), with its own test.
- tests: the guide's grammar and its retired vocabulary in both languages, the example parsing under `ParseTemplate` with its outline order and stages, a paste refused only for what the grammar cannot read (naming the entry and its 1-based line) while three intro lines, a caption and a stage are accepted, and the preview drawing only the lines the preset holds.
- ARCH-25 green (2069 tests) and ARCH-26 still green.
