# T580 Lapse a refused renewal even when the subscription changed after its order
> st:done@261005 | ssot:BILL-8 | base:BILL@9 | dep:- | touches:backend/internal/billing/fixed_settlement.go backend/internal/billing/store/

## goal
A renewal Toss refuses outright ends paid coverage (BILL-8) whenever the subscription is still on the term that renewal was for, instead of only when the row is byte-for-byte the one the order was created from — so a scheduled change or a cancel made while the charge was pending no longer leaves an unpaid account active and every later pass colliding with the same renewal order id.

## acceptance
- [v] `failFixedIntent` lapses a `renew` order's subscription when it is active and its `TermEnd` equals the order's `EffectiveAt` (the term the renewal extends), the condition T570 uses for a refunded renewal; a subscription already moved to another term only records `charge_failed`
- [v] real-store test: a renewal whose first charge answer is transient stays pending; the owner schedules a tier change; the next pass's charge is refused (`REJECT_CARD_PAYMENT`) — the order fails, the account lapses to free with one `renewal_failed` event and the renewal-failed mail, and a further pass returns no error and charges nothing
- [v] behaviour unchanged otherwise: ARCH-26 green

## impl notes
- found while implementing T570 (review perf-cost-261004 follow-ups); decided under the owner's run-to-push instruction of 261005
- comparing `UpdatedAt` was meant to detect a stale order, but any owner action bumps it; the term is what the renewal pays for, so the term is what to compare
