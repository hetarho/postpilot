# T490 A master account keeps master and is never charged
> st:done@260930 | ssot:QUOTA-63 BILL-1 BILL-20 | base:QUOTA@26 BILL@6 | dep:- | touches:proto/postpilot/v1/error.proto backend/internal/auth backend/internal/billing frontend/src/features/manage-users frontend/src/pages/admin frontend/src/pages/billing frontend/src/pages/plans

## goal
A master account leaves master only by another master's SetUserPlan or `api setplan`: self-assignment is refused, billing never starts a payment for it and no billing write moves its tier.

## acceptance
- [v] `AdminService.SetUserPlan` whose `user_id` is the calling account answers `failed_precondition` with reason `MASTER_SELF_PLAN` for every target tier (master included) and writes nothing; another master changing that account still works and still meets the last-master guard (QUOTA-4)
- [v] `Subscribe`, `QuoteChange`, `ChangeSubscription` (plain and quoted) and `PurchasePack` for a master account answer `failed_precondition` with reason `BILLING_MASTER_ACCOUNT`, insert no intent and call no payment provider
- [v] every billing tier write leaves a master account on master: subscription settlement, renewal, upgrade, scheduled change, lapse (cancelled or failed) and refund reversal; the support path (`AssignSupportTier`) can still move a master to another tier
- [v] a due subscription held by a master account at term end lapses without a charge whatever its auto-renew flag, the tier stays master and the owner gets the operator-ended mail, not the "changed to free" one
- [v] 계정 관리 renders the caller's own row as the fixed tier label with the note `본인 플랜은 바꿀 수 없어요` and no SegmentedControl; every other row keeps the control
- [v] the billing screen for a master account shows the operator-coverage note in the subscription section, no `/plans` link, no subscription actions on a held subscription (its end date reads as coverage end) and no credit-pack section; refund requests and payment method stay
- [v] `/plans` for a master account shows the operator-coverage note and still no per-offer action
- [v] ko/en copy for both reasons in `errors.ts` and the `AppFailureReason` mirror; the enum-walking mirror test passes (ARCH-3)
- [v] tests: `backend/internal/auth/rpc` (self refusal, other-master success), `backend/internal/auth` (AssignTier keeps master, ReassignTier moves it), `backend/internal/billing` (four refusals, master renewal lapse without charge and its mail, refund reversal keeps master), FE `AdminPage` / `BillingPage` / `PlansPage` master cases
- [v] ARCH-25, ARCH-26 and ARCH-28 pass
