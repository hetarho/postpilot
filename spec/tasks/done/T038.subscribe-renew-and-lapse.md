# T038 Subscribe from a rung, renew on the anchor day, and drop to free the moment a renewal fails
> st:done@260908 | ssot:BILL-1 BILL-2 BILL-3 BILL-4 BILL-8 BILL-12 BILL-15 BILL-16 QUOTA-28 QUOTA-35 QUOTA-37 QUOTA-38 MKT-15 ARCH-10 ARCH-11 | base:BILL@2 QUOTA@5 MKT@3 AUTH@2 ARCH@2 | dep:T037

## goal
Make a rung on `/plans` buyable: the first KRW charge at the previous business day's rate sets the tier and the anchor and opens the monthly lot; a worker charges and grants on every anchor day, releases an annual term's credits month by month, and drops a failed renewal to `free` at once with a mail.

## impl notes
- the charge happens BEFORE the write transaction and never inside it (ARCH-10); the unique `order_id` per term start is what makes a crash between the two repairable — the next attempt asks the provider whether that order is already `DONE` instead of charging again
- the first subscription from `free`: the tier moves through `AssignTier` (no credit top-up — QUOTA-35's top-up is for an upgrade within a cycle, T039) and the new tier's lot opens for the new anchor window; the account's old free lot keeps its calendar or creation-day expiry and burns first by consumption order, so nothing is taken away
- the renewal worker is a ticker inside the API process rather than a job row (ARCH-11): it is scheduled work with no client polling it, and each subscription row IS the durable record — `next_grant_at` advancing inside the same transaction as the event is the idempotency; `RunDue` at boot covers a process that was down across an anchor day
- `RunDue` processes rows one at a time with the provider call between two short transactions: read the due row, charge, then write; two API processes never run (one container per stack), so no lease is needed
- "dropped to free at once" (BILL-8) is `AssignTier(free)` in the same transaction as the `lapsed` status; the account keeps using whatever credits it has
- mails name amounts as machine values formatted server-side in plain digits (`$5.00 · 6,962원 · 1,392.50원/$`) — there is no locale to consult (T029's language note)
- the checkout is BILL's screen even though the START is on `/plans` (QUOTA-28): `/plans` chooses the rung and hands it over by search param; nothing on `/plans` charges
- `MKT-15`: the assignment sentence must change in THIS task, the change that makes checkout real; leave nothing saying the operator assigns plans on `/about`, while `api setplan` stays for support (BILL-1)

## result
- Added the authenticated `Subscribe` RPC and a transaction-composed billing service that records the charge, subscription, tier transition, and monthly credit lot atomically. Provider order lookup makes response-loss retries idempotent.
- Added anchored annual grants, renewal, immediate lapse on failed renewal, cancellation lapse, verified-address bilingual mail, and a boot-plus-10-minute renewal worker. Active subscriptions now own the usage anchor and lapsed subscriptions fall back to account creation.
- Connected paid plan rungs to a localized monthly/annual checkout, including daily KRW quotes, card registration return, refusal messages, and subscription success handoff. Billing now shows subscription timing and newest-first money history; public and account copy no longer says operators assign subscriber plans.
