# T669 Flip a measured panel only when its content does not fit below
> st:done@261011 | ssot:THEME-29 THEME-30 | base:THEME@33 | dep:- | touches:frontend/src/shared/ui/anchored-panel/useAnchoredPanel.ts frontend/src/shared/ui/listbox/Listbox.test.tsx | lane:-

## goal
A measured anchored panel (`Listbox`, `Toggletip`) opens below its trigger and flips above only when the room below cannot hold its content and the room above is larger.

## acceptance
- [v] `useAnchoredPanel` compares the room below with the panel's unbounded height — its `scrollHeight`, capped at the viewport ratio and never under the floor — measured once the panel mounts and before it paints; before mount, and with no layout, the floor alone decides
- [v] a list that fits below still drops down; the ratio cap and the floor on `maxHeight` are unchanged
- [v] `Listbox.test.tsx` › `flips above a trigger whose room below cannot hold the list` and `keeps dropping down when the list fits the room below`; the existing placement cases (`bounds the open panel to the room below the trigger`, `stops at half the viewport…`, `flips above a trigger with no room under it…`) keep passing
- [v] in Chrome, `/posts/new` at 1280x900: the dock's template field (133px below it) opens a list taller than that upward, and a two-row list still downward

## impl notes
- the re-measure on mount already existed for a `content` panel's width (`mounted` dependency); the height reuses it, so the panel's first painted frame is already placed
- `scrollHeight` is independent of the panel's own `max-height` and position, so the flip cannot oscillate between measurements
- jsdom reports `scrollHeight` 0, which falls back to the floor — every existing placement test keeps its meaning; the new tests state the height with a `scrollHeight` getter spy
- `Popover` measures with its own code and is out of scope

## result
- outcome: The writing dock's template and voice lists, and any Listbox or Toggletip with less room below than its content, now open upward when the room above is larger; lists that fit below still drop down.
