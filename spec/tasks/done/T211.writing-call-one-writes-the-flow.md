# T211 Writing call 1 writes the footage flow
> st:done@260916 | ssot:CLIP-135 CLIP-136 CLIP-121 CLIP-127 CLIP-128 CLIP-129 CLIP-99 CLIP-7 CLIP-123 | base:CLIP@31 CDS@20 ARCH@3 | dep:T210

## goal
Replace the single composition writer with a flow call that orders and cuts footage from the instruction, the template guide, the facts, the item hints and the observations, and returns cuts alone.

## acceptance
- [v] `ai.Service.Flow(ctx, model, clip.PlanningInput) (clip.EditPlan, llm.Usage, error)` builds `flowPrompt` and `schemas/flow.schema.json` — `cuts[]{id, source_id, start_ms, end_ms, rate_permille, focal, volume, observation_refs}` and nothing about sections, groups or items — parses through the existing `composeTimeline` and CLIP-7 reconciliation, and returns a plan holding cuts and the resolved fixed regions (intro/outro/badge) and no narration
- [v] the request carries `project_instruction`, `template_guide` (root guide prose), `global_values`, `item_groups`, `source_order` (source ids in batch order), `item_hints` (owner associations stated as facts about footage), `analyses` with observation ids and per-source `retains_original_audio` beside `has_audio`, `ratio`, `target_duration_ms`, `fade_ms`
- [v] the contract states: order, rhythm and count follow the instruction; without one, `source_order` then source time with CDS-37's targets; the cut budget follows the target duration and the observed footage, with no admitted sections; every cut lies inside one observed segment and no two cuts of a source share a millisecond
- [v] the contract names the observed facts a rate is read from (recorded activity, camera motion, scene type, speech), states 1x as what an unsupported span stays at, bounds the transformed share at 40 % with no minimum, keeps a speech span at 1x while its source retains sound, and never offers a rate as a way to reach the target
- [v] an over-share or speech-rate-violating response is planned and rendered as written; `composition_section_order` and `composition_item_order` are never recorded; source, range, coverage, contained-scene, usability, duplicate-id and overlap checks apply unchanged
- [v] `Plan` stays only for the legacy non-native path; a native composition input routes to `Flow` (and `Narrate`, T212)
- [v] tests (pattern: ai/composition_service_test.go opens the built payload): every request field; the rate, share and speech sentences; an over-share response survives planning; a section-free plan resolves its fixed regions; verify BE (ARCH-26)

## impl notes
- reuse `completeValidated` (CLIP-94 loop, budget policy, usage) and `executionPolicy` with a new `FlowCompletionTokens`; `Budgets` becomes `{Observe, Flow, Narration}` and both writing budgets are 32768 for now — the owner chose generous ceilings first, to be lowered with measured usage
- prompt: keep the cut, rate and duration paragraphs of `compositionPlanPrompt` verbatim minus every sentence about sections, items and copy; drop the `compositionAuthority` swap — the instruction is always the content authority and the template guide follows it (CLIP-121)
- this task carries what T197 and T198 asked for (their acceptance is folded above); the legacy `planPrompt` is compatibility-only for queued payloads without a composition snapshot and is left alone
- `source_order` is whatever order `SourceBatch.Sources` lists; T216 makes that order owner-arranged, and until then it is confirmation order
- `item_hints`: each `SourceAssociation` becomes `{source_id, group_id, item_id, start_ms, end_ms}` under a sentence saying it is a fact about that footage the writer may state, and nothing depends on it (CLIP-123)
- the response carries no text, so `attachCompositionCopy` is not called here; fixed regions come from `ResolveSelectedComposition` on the section-free document (T210)
