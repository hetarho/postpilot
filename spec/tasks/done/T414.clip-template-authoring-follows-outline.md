## goal
A caption declares no position or align and the badge no align anywhere, the invisible `guide` entry leaves the outline grammar on both sides (stored bodies and snapshots included), a stage's line says what the stage is about, the copied guide teaches only the outline grammar, and a stored root accent or pace value never refuses a paste.
