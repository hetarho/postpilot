# T081 Clip failure settlement by usage evidence
> st:done@260910 | ssot:QUOTA-10 QUOTA-11 QUOTA-15 QUOTA-16 QUOTA-17 QUOTA-21 QUOTA-44 QUOTA-46 CLIP-26 ARCH-6 ARCH-7 ARCH-11 ARCH-24 ARCH-31 | base:QUOTA@8 CLIP@3 ARCH@2 | dep:T076

## goal
Settle failed clip AI attempts from confirmed billable usage, refunding the entire hold including the base charge when none is confirmed.

## acceptance
- [v] a terminal failed `generate_clip` job with no usage, only unavailable cost, or authoritative zero-cost usage settles at zero, refunds every held credit to its original lots once and does not rewrite the provider ledger
- [v] a failed clip with positive reported cost or a valid positive estimate from reported usage retains that evidence and charges the existing formula, capped by its reservation; an unknown remainder is not charged later
- [v] successful clips retain the normal formula, including its base; non-clip settlement, master exemption, purchased lots, subscriptions and previously settled jobs remain unchanged
- [v] worker completion and boot recovery provide the same persisted terminal outcome to settlement; a failed job-status write cannot cause a successful job to receive a failure waiver or a running job to settle
- [v] repeated settlement, concurrent recovery, cancellation and a failed refund transaction cannot duplicate refunds, lose an open hold or make a lot negative; a refund does not extend a lot's expiry
- [v] usage/service and real SQLite tests cover absent/unknown/reported-zero/estimated-positive/reported-positive/mixed evidence, a failed call carrying usage, partial failure, successful zero-cost work, master and historical idempotence
- [v] job worker/sweep tests cover a failed `Finish`, interrupted running work, terminal recovery and duplicate settlement without replaying any provider call
- [v] ARCH-25 through ARCH-28 and ARCH-31 local gates pass; no paid provider call or historical balance adjustment is part of verification

## impl notes
- extend the consumer-owned `job.Admitter` settlement contract with a terminal outcome and map it at `cmd/api` into a usage-owned settlement input; usage must not import job internals or read `generation_jobs`
- `job/worker.go` currently settles even after `store.Finish` fails: settle only after a successful terminal write or an owner-store reread confirming the terminal outcome; otherwise leave the hold open for recovery, using the existing detached bounded finish context
- `job/sweep.go` already reads the authoritative job: pass its actual terminal outcome through the same seam; missing-job holds still release, queued/running holds remain open and `render_clip` stays outside AI admission
- extend the usage-owned cost projection to distinguish positive priced evidence from unavailable cost, preserving `reported` precedence over token estimates; provider-reported zero wins over a positive token-based estimate for that event
- the waiver is `kind == generate_clip && outcome == failed && no confirmed positive billable cost`; missing cost is unknown, not evidence that the supplier charged zero, and neither a hold nor an attempted call is billable evidence
- perform evidence aggregation, charge selection, same-lot refunds and the existing `settled_at IS NULL` transition in one write transaction; no migration or retroactive repricing is needed for this task
- retain append-only metering and usage recorded after a failed provider response; late evidence may remain diagnostic but must never reopen a settled clip debit
- this task fixes settlement only; the approved maximum and its second settlement cap arrive in T082, before any new inline execution path is enabled
