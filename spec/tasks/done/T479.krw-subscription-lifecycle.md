## goal
Make subscriptions, renewals, scheduled changes and fixed credit packs follow the new KRW offer and exact paid-coverage lifecycle.

## acceptance
- [v] Quotes and charges use QUOTA-7’s VAT-inclusive KRW prices and twelve-month/ten-payment annual offer; checkout needs no FX. Packs accept only the three fixed server-owned ids/amounts in QUOTA-34 and only during active paid coverage.
- [v] Monthly and annual upgrades charge their respective remaining-period fraction rounded up, from the current tier; successful confirmation applies T477’s current benefit-month deltas once. Failed/unresolved payment changes no tier or grants; quote invalidation handles concurrent tier/coverage changes.
- [v] Monthly→annual is scheduled at the next monthly renewal; annual→monthly and annual downgrade wait for paid annual expiry. Pending changes replace/cancel cleanly, an immediate tier upgrade clears a scheduled downgrade, and repeated changes do not stack grants.
- [v] Cancellation/resume preserves paid coverage and anchors until expiry. Annual subscribers continue daily/monthly benefits between payments. Final renewal failure returns to free with no retry/grace; unknown outcomes remain unresolved, card replacement grants nothing and a paid rejoin starts a new anchor.
- [v] Registering any payment method issues zero bonus. A pack payment creates exactly one purchased lot with no expiry; purchases do not move anchors or buy model rights/export counts. Charge/grant/history/email values are whole KRW and are not decorated with a fictitious checkout FX rate.
- [v] Real-store and provider-adapter tests cover repeated/reordered notifications, charge succeeded then local commit failed, simultaneous upgrades, boundary renewal/downgrade, stale quotes, cancellation/resume and free top-up refusal. Run ARCH-26/28 and entity mapper tests.

## impl notes
- Extend existing billing Subscription/Change/Purchase services and the installed Toss adapter, rather than adding another checkout system. Reuse provider order ids, notification records and reconciliation: no grant follows a browser success redirect alone. Verify current official provider request/notification semantics before adapter changes.
- Quotes persist or bind the current tier/term, coverage/benefit boundaries, exact quoted-at instant and integer KRW amount. Revalidate against that state at payment creation; an accepted in-flight charge settles from its frozen quote, never a later recalculation. Only finally failed provider outcomes lapse coverage.
- Use T476’s period math and T477’s transaction-scoped benefit operations. Separate monthly benefit progression from annual payment progression, and preserve the original anchor rather than adopting each clamped renewal’s date.
- Purchase ids identify 3000→1000, 9000→3000 and 30000→10000 packs; reject client-supplied arbitrary amount/credit combinations. Correlate each lot and upgrade delta to its funding payment for T484’s refund review.
- Extend billing proto on fresh fields for fixed KRW and exact dates. Update FE subscription entity mappings enough to keep consumers compiling; full checkout/account presentation is T485. Deprecated USD fields are retired at T487 after every consumer moves.
- Payment I/O happens outside SQLite transactions. Persist intent, reconcile authoritative server-side outcome, then atomically apply coverage/entitlements once. Provider failure must never strand a captured payment as an untracked error. Existing required billing emails remain localized and reachable.

## result
- outcome: Fixed-KRW checkout now persists quoted upgrades and payment intents, reconciles authoritative Toss orders before one atomic grant, advances anchored monthly/annual coverage, and sells three paid-only credit packs.
