# T257 The approval surface quotes the longest render its styles can produce
> st:done@260918 | ssot:CLIP-145 CDS-81 CLIP-19 | base:CLIP@38 CDS@23 | dep:-

## goal
State before generation how much longer the selected styles can make the render, since the narration names each caption's style only after the owner has approved.

## acceptance
- [v] with no plan yet, the quote states the longest render the selection admits — the whole target duration drawn a frame at a time — rather than how many sequence styles were selected
- [v] a selection holding no sequence-rendered style quotes no added time at all
- [v] with a plan, the quote keeps counting that plan's own captions, as it does now
- [v] the approval panel states seconds in both cases, and the string that only counted styles is gone
- [v] nothing refuses over the number: no ceiling is checked at selection, at approval or at render start
- [v] `SequenceCaptionCost`'s comment no longer calls CLIP-145 open
- [v] a test covers the no-plan worst case, the all-static selection and the unchanged with-plan count
- [v] ARCH-26 passes

## impl notes
- the worst case is the whole output, not a caption count: captions never overlap (CLIP-66), so the most frames any narration can draw a frame at a time is the target duration's own frames — `TargetDurationMS * FPS / 1000 * SequenceFrameCostMS`
- `FromPlan` stays the flag the surface reads; it now means "these are the plan's captions" rather than "seconds are known", because seconds are known either way
- `SelectedStyles` stays on the proto and in the response ← it is what makes the pre-generation number explicable, and removing a field costs a client migration for nothing
- the ko/en `credits.sequenceStyles` strings are replaced rather than extended: the pre-generation line now says the same kind of thing the post-plan line does, in seconds
- no ceiling anywhere is deliberate (CLIP-145): the surface informs and the owner accepts, so this task adds no refusal path and no config bound
