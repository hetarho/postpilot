# T339 발행됨 is the fourth status everywhere, and a published post reads as locked
> st:done@260924 | ssot:POST-13 POST-21 POST-44 POST-66 POST-74 POST-76 POST-86 ARCH-13 ARCH-16 ARCH-20 ARCH-24 THEME-29 | base:POST@11 ARCH@9 THEME@17 | dep:T334

## goal
- The FE knows `published` wherever the status union is exhaustive, and a published post reads as locked. ② is read-only, names the way out, and its dock holds only 글 완성으로 가기. ①'s inputs are read-only, with 생성 and A/B 비교 disabled and the reason said once. ③'s 말투 학습 and export stay available.

## acceptance
- [v] `PostStatus` is `'draft' | 'review' | 'finalized' | 'published'`, and `postStatusLabel('published')` is 발행됨 (en Published). Every exhaustive list or `Record` over the union covers it: steps, badge tone, filter values, URL status and the label guard.
- [v] `PostDraft` carries `publishedUrl` and `publishedAt` (both `''` when unset), mapped in `toPostDraft` from the Post message's new fields (T322).
- [v] The editor opens a published post on ③ (`stepForStatus('published') === 'finish'`), and a `finalized → published` status change keeps the reader on ③.
- [v] `/posts`:
  - the status filter lists 전체 · 초안 · 검토 · 확정 · 발행됨, and 발행됨 narrows to published rows;
  - `?status=published` is honoured on load and survives a reload;
  - a published row wears a 발행됨 badge.
- [v] ② on a published post shows one `Notice` line saying a published post cannot be changed and that clearing its URL on ③ reopens it, followed by the prose as `BlockList`. There is no `BlockEditor`: no block edit or move controls, no 제목과 요약, 태그 수정 and no 문단 추가, and no content save can start.
- [v] ②'s dock on a published post holds exactly one control, 글 완성으로 가기, which moves to ③. The revision field is not rendered.
- [v] ① on a published post:
  - 가제 and memo are `readOnly` and show the server's `post.title` / `post.memo`;
  - the template data fields, 기억 사용, the 말투 and 템플릿 listboxes, 글 언어, 목표 글자 수 / 태그 개수, photo add (picker and drop zone) and photo/video delete are disabled or absent;
  - 생성 and A/B 비교 are disabled;
  - the same lock sentence renders exactly once on the screen, and no other reason renders beside a disabled ① control;
  - nothing sends SavePostDraft, SavePostGenerationOptions, CreateUpload, DeleteImage, DeleteVideo, StartGeneration or StartWriteExperiment.
- [v] The brief's model selects (관찰/작성 모델, 작성 A/B 후보) stay usable on a published post: they are account settings, not post writes.
- [v] ③ on a published post keeps 말투 학습 enabled under the same gates as a finalized one (`revisionFinalized` true), keeps 문장 의견 after a completed learning run, and leaves the export panel unchanged.
- [v] A draft save refused with `POST_PUBLISHED_LOCKED` (the post was published in another tab) is an answer.
  - The queued text and every pending assignment are taken back and no retry is scheduled.
  - The status line never reads 저장하지 못했어요 · 다시 시도 중.
  - The GetPost entry is invalidated so ① re-renders locked.
  - A content save refused the same way stops instead of retrying.
- [v] A reopened post shows the server's 가제 and memo, not text the lock refused. Reopening means the URL was cleared: `published → finalized`.
- [v] Tests:
  - `entities/post/model/types.test.ts`: the published label.
  - `pages/editor/model/steps.test.ts` and `useDraftSteps.test.ts`: published → ③, and `finalized → published` stays on ③.
  - `features/generate-post/model/preconditions.test.ts`: `published` wins over a deleted voice and an active job, for both functions.
  - `features/save-draft/model/draft-queue.test.ts`: a locked refusal drops the text and the assignments, rejects flush waiters and schedules no retry.
  - `features/edit-post-content/model/content-queue.test.ts`: a locked refusal is not retried.
  - `features/filter-posts/model/narrow.test.ts`: the published filter.
  - `pages/posts/ui/PostsPage.test.tsx`: badge, filter option, `?status=published`.
  - `widgets/refine-dock/ui/RefineDock.test.tsx`: a published post gets only the road onward.
  - `pages/editor/ui/EditorPage.test.tsx`, new `describe('a published post')`: the ③ landing; ② read-only with its one-control dock; ① read-only with one reason and no write calls; the brief's model select still enabled; 말투 학습 and export available; the status line reads 발행됨; the draft save locked mid-edit, sent once and never retried, with ① locked after the refetch.
- [v] Verify: `pnpm --filter ./frontend test` · `pnpm lint` · `pnpm lint:fsd` · `pnpm lint:style:probe && pnpm lint:style` · `pnpm build:web`

## impl notes
- `entities/post/model/types.ts`:
  - `PostStatus` gains `'published'`.
  - Add `POST_STATUSES: readonly PostStatus[] = ['draft', 'review', 'finalized', 'published']`, in lifecycle order, which is also the filter order.
  - Add `isPostStatus(value: unknown): value is PostStatus` and `isPublished(post: { status: string }): boolean`. The latter is the one predicate every lock below uses (R4).
  - `postStatusLabel` switches its hand-written `||` chain to `isPostStatus`.
  - Export all four from `entities/post/index.ts`.
- `PostDraft` gains `publishedUrl: string` and `publishedAt: string`, with `''` meaning none, mirroring T322's `string published_url` / `string published_at` fields. `toPostDraft` copies `post.publishedUrl` and `post.publishedAt`. `PostListItem` does not change.
- `entities/post/config/i18n.ts`:
  - `status.published`: 발행됨 / Published.
  - New top-level `published.locked`: '발행된 글은 바꿀 수 없어요. 글 완성에서 발행 URL을 지우면 다시 고칠 수 있어요.' / 'A published post cannot be changed. Clear its URL in Finish to reopen it.' This is the one sentence ① and ② both say (POST-86).
  - ko and en keep identical keys (R38).
- `pages/editor/model/steps.ts`: `BY_STATUS` gains `published: 'finish'` (POST-44). The unknown-status fallback stays.
- `/posts`:
  - `pages/posts/model/search.ts` types `status?: PostStatus` and accepts it with `isPostStatus`.
  - `features/filter-posts/ui/PostListControls.tsx` builds `STATUSES = ['all', ...POST_STATUSES]`.
  - `pages/posts/config/i18n.ts` adds `list.filter.published`: 발행됨 / Published. `list.filter` lives there today and `PostsPage` also reads it for the no-match copy.
  - `PostsPage`'s `postStatusTone` becomes `const STATUS_TONE: Record<PostStatus, BadgeTone> = { draft: 'neutral', review: 'accent', finalized: 'success', published: 'success' }`, read through `isPostStatus`, with `neutral` for anything else. Published is a done state, and the label is what tells it from 확정 (THEME-29: colour never travels alone).
- ② (`pages/editor/ui/EditorRefinePanel.tsx`): when `isPublished(post)` and `result` exists, render the language-mismatch notice as today, then `<Notice tone="info" role="status" className="mb-4">` with `published.locked`, then `<BlockList content={result} images={post.images} videos={post.videos} />`. `BlockEditor` is not mounted, so no content queue attaches (POST-86).
- ②'s dock: `widgets/refine-dock/ui/RefineDock.tsx` returns only `<FinalizeActions …/>` when `isPublished(post)`, with no `ReviseForm`. `features/finalize-post/ui/FinalizeActions.tsx` treats `post.status === 'finalized' || isPublished(post)` as the road-onward case, rendering 글 완성으로 가기 (`finalize.goFinish`). `EditorStepDock` is unchanged.
- ③: in `features/finalize-post/model/useVoiceLearning.ts`, `finalizedNow` becomes `(post.status === 'finalized' || isPublished(post)) && post.finalizedRevision === post.contentRevision` (R11, POST-21). `EditorFinishPanel`, `VoiceLearningPanel`, `SentenceFeedback`, `ExtractMemoriesButton` and `ExportPanel` are untouched.
- ① blocker (`features/generate-post/model/preconditions.ts`):
  - `GenerationBlocker` gains `'published'`.
  - `ordinaryGenerationPreconditions` and `comparisonGenerationPreconditions` take a trailing `published = false`, forwarded to `sharedPreconditions`. It checks it FIRST: `{ ok: false, reason: i18next.t('published.locked', { ns: 'posts' }), blocker: 'published' }`.
  - `published` is not in `SETUP_BLOCKERS`, so `needsBrief` stays false.
- `GenerationActions`:
  - its `post` Pick gains `'status'`, and it passes `isPublished(post)` to both functions;
  - when `ordinary.blocker === 'published'`, it renders one `Typography variant="label" as="p" role="status"` with `ordinary.reason` above the (disabled) pair, in place of `reasons()`.
  - That is the single place ① says it (POST-86).
- ① controls take a lock flag, and none of them renders a reason of its own:
  - `DraftEditor` computes `const locked = post ? isPublished(post) : false` and threads it to `LifecycleSteps` → `EditorGeneratePanel`, to `EditorDockHeader`, and to the answer fields it builds.
  - `TitleField` and `MemoField` gain `readOnly?: boolean`, passed to the `Textarea`. While locked, `DraftEditor` passes `post.title` / `post.memo` as their `value`.
  - `TemplateAnswerFields` uses its existing `disabled`. While locked, `DraftEditor` renders fields built from `answerFields(selectedTemplate, post.templateAnswers)` alone, without the local edits. The `answers`, `title` and `memo` handed to `useAutosave` stay the local ones, so locking queues nothing.
  - `UseMemoriesField` uses its existing `disabled`.
  - `EditorPhotos` gains `locked?: boolean`, which disables `PhotoDropZone` and `PhotoPicker` and passes `readOnly` to `PhotoStrip`. `PhotoStrip` gains `readOnly?: boolean`, which omits its delete controls.
  - `PostVoiceSelect` and `PostTemplateSelect` gain `disabled?: boolean`. `EditorDockHeader` passes `blocked={locked ? '' : reassignmentBlocker(post)}` and `jobRunning={!locked && …}`, so no second reason appears.
  - `GenerationBrief` gains `locked?: boolean`, which disables `PostLanguageSelect` (new `disabled?: boolean`) and ORs into `GenerationOptions`' `disabled`. `StageModelSelect` and `CandidatePairSelect` stay enabled.
- Reopen reseed:
  - `DraftEditor` keeps `const [wasLocked, setWasLocked] = useState(locked)` and adjusts during render (the `useSaveStatus` pattern).
  - When `wasLocked && !locked`, it calls `setTitle(post.title)`, `setMemo(post.memo)` and `setAnswerEdits([])` and updates `wasLocked`. When `!wasLocked && locked`, it only updates `wasLocked`.
  - While locked, the local state is not rendered, so a refused keystroke never resurfaces.
- Save-draft backstop (`features/save-draft/model/draft-queue.ts`):
  - `attachDraftQueue` options gain `retry?: (cause: unknown) => boolean`, default `() => true`, named like `shared/lib/autosave`'s `retry` option. It is stored on the `Queue` and re-taken on every attach, as `send` is.
  - In `run`'s catch, after the existing voice / template / target-language take-backs: when `queue.slug && !(queue.retry?.(cause) ?? true)`, set `queue.pending = undefined`, `queue.failed = false`, `queue.attempts = 0`, `clearTimers(queue)`, `publish(queue)`, `rejectFlushes(queue, cause)`, `collect(queue)` and return. No retry timer is set, which is the reassignment path's answer rule applied to the text.
  - `useAutosave` passes `retry: (cause) => appFailureFromConnect(cause).reason !== 'POST_PUBLISHED_LOCKED'`.
- Refetch on a lock refusal happens inside the entity, because nothing outside `entities/post` may name a post key (`test/arch-post-cache-keys.test.ts`). `useSavePostDraft` and `useSavePostContent` each gain an `onError` that, when `appFailureFromConnect(error).reason === 'POST_PUBLISHED_LOCKED'` and the request had a slug, invalidates `getPostQueryKey(transport, slug)` and `listPostsQueryKey(transport)`.
- Content-queue backstop: `features/edit-post-content/model/content-queue.ts` passes `retry: (error) => appFailureFromConnect(error).reason !== 'POST_PUBLISHED_LOCKED'` to `createAutosaveQueue`. This ends ②'s background retry for a post published in another tab, and the refetch then unmounts `BlockEditor`.
- `features/review-model-experiment/ui/ExperimentActions.tsx`'s `postWritable` (`draft || review`) already excludes published and does not change.
- `frontend/src/test/posts.ts`:
  - `FakePostRow` gains `publishedUrl?: string` and `publishedAt?: string`. `Row` gains `publishedUrl: string` and `publishedAt: string`, which `toRow` defaults to `''`.
  - A `refuseIfPublished(row)` helper throws `connectAppError('POST_PUBLISHED_LOCKED', Code.FailedPrecondition)`. It is applied in `savePostDraft` (existing slug, after the forbidden check and before any validation or write), `savePostContent` (only when the content differs, since an identical save stays a no-op, R7), `savePostGenerationOptions`, `finalizePost`, `createUpload`, `deleteImage` and `deleteVideo`.
  - New option `publishOnDraftSave?: string`: the next SavePostDraft on that slug first turns the row published (`publishedUrl: 'https://blog.naver.com/alice/1'`, `publishedAt` set) and is then refused as locked, modelling a publish from another tab.
- Generation fakes (`test/jobs.ts`, `test/experiments.ts`) are unchanged. ① never sends StartGeneration or StartWriteExperiment on a published post, which the tests assert through `calls`. The server refusal is T334's and is pinned there.
- Out of scope:
  - the ③ URL field (T343);
  - hiding measurements (T346) and replacement marks (T351) on a published post;
  - the 분야 picker's lock (T345), which reuses this task's `isPublished` and single reason.

The full FE suite (297 files, 2415 tests) and every FE gate pass, as does the retired-terms scan.
- Deviation from the impl notes: while locked, `DraftEditor` hands `useAutosave` the server's title, memo and answers, not the local ones. Measured: after a locked refusal the refetch maps a fresh `PostDraft`, whose `templateAnswers` array gives `answers` a new identity, and the still-local refused title was queued and sent a second time. The server's text is what the queue already holds, so the lock queues nothing; the local state is still what the reopen reseed replaces. No SSOT change.
- A locked refusal also takes back an assignment chosen while the refused request was out (not only the ones it carried), so no waiter hangs on a queue that will not retry.
- Mutation check: 32 mutations over the entity, the steps, the list, ①, ②, ③, both queues, the autosave, both hooks and the fake were all caught. One first survived: dropping the autosave's retry rule, because the lock hand-over cancels the pending retry once the refetch lands. The rule is the backstop for the gap before that, so it is now pinned in `useAutosave.test.tsx`, whose harness now hands one stable answers array as `DraftEditor`'s memo does (a fresh `[]` per render re-queued the refused text on the queue's own state change).
- No SSOT deviation.
