## acceptance
- [v] Preset amounts come from the code-owned paid monthly bonuses and default to thirty days after explicit redemption; custom credits/validity and sold/given bookkeeping remain available.

## impl notes
- Reuse voucher and usage transaction ports; resolve authoritative paid coverage at redemption rather than trusting the session’s cached label. Do not redeem automatically after payment or from a page load.
- Preserve the bearer token’s secrecy, one-use unique constraint and bank-transfer refund handling outside the product. Pack purchase and voucher redemption share paid eligibility but remain distinct kinds and histories.
- Use provider-independent coverage behavior from T479 and published plan offers for preset labels; no repeated numeric constant table in the admin UI. No public voucher checkout, bulk issuance or partial redemption is added.
