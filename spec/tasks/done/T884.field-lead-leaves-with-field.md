# T884 Drop a skipped field's lead lines with the field

## goal
When an optional field is off or blank, the literal lines written directly above it (its lead — a wizard section's heading and fixed text) leave the frozen body with it, so a skipped section no longer leaves an empty heading.

## acceptance
- [v] a body `제목\n<ask label="a">주제</ask>\n<slot kind="photo"/>` with `a` blank renders exactly as `<slot kind="photo"/>` preceded by what stood before the lead; a lead of several lines (heading + fixed text) leaves whole (deterministic: `ask_resolution_test.go`, task stage)
- [v] a blank line between a line and the field keeps the line; text before the field on its own line stays; a line that holds a tag is never lead; the area's first line can be lead (deterministic: `ask_resolution_test.go`, task stage)
- [v] fields stacked directly one under another share the lead above the first: it stays while any of them has a value and leaves when all are removed; a required field never removes its lead (deterministic: `ask_resolution_test.go`, task stage)
- [v] `TestAnUnusableFieldRendersAsIfItWereNotInTheBody` compares against the body with the field and its lead deleted (deterministic, task stage)
- [v] the 형식 안내 in both languages says lines glued directly above an optional ask leave with it and a blank line keeps them; golden files updated (deterministic: `guide_test.go`, task stage)

## impl notes
- all in `resolveAsks` (`expand.go`), which already runs for the body and the title area alike; keep the single-pass render
- mark each `NodeAsk` that is unusable (existing `usable()` test); then find runs: an ask, and each following ask separated from the previous by nothing or by a `NodeLiteral` whose `Text` is only whitespace holding at most one `\n`
- the run's lead lives in the `NodeLiteral` directly before the run's first ask, only when that literal's `Text` ends with `\n` (the field starts its own line): split the text before that final `\n` on `\n`; walk the segments from the last backwards taking non-blank segments (after `strings.TrimSpace`) until a blank segment; the FIRST segment of the literal counts only when the literal is the area's first node (otherwise it is the tail of a tag's line) — stop there otherwise
- when every ask of the run is removed and the lead is nonempty, cut the lead segments and their newlines from that literal's `Text` and `Source` (raw slices; entities never hold `\n`); a literal left empty is dropped
- update `TestAnUnusableFieldRendersAsIfItWereNotInTheBody`: `askBody` starts with `방문 기록\n<ask label="방문일"/>`, so `withoutBoth` becomes `"\n<write>인트로</write>\n\n<slot kind=\"photo\"/>"`; add table tests for the cases in acceptance, including a wizard-shaped body (heading + 고정 문구 lines + ask + photo slot, then the next heading)
- guide (`guide.go`, both `guideKorean` and the English text): one rule line in [지켜야 할 규칙] — ko: `선택 입력 ask 바로 위에 빈 줄 없이 붙여 쓴 줄은 그 입력란이 비면 함께 빠집니다. 늘 남길 줄은 빈 줄로 띄우세요.` and the matching English sentence; regenerate `testdata/guide/ko.txt` and `en.txt` from the real output
- the frozen-copy and literal rules are unchanged: a kept literal still renders verbatim; no TS change (the client never resolves answers)
