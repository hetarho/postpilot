# T189 The owner binds a source to an item before generating
> st:done@260916 | ssot:CLIP-123 CLIP-62 | base:CLIP@28 ARCH@2 | dep:-

## goal
Let the owner bind one whole selected source to one item of a declared group where the sources are chosen, so every cut from that source inherits the item.

## acceptance
- [v] each selected source offers the items of the project's declared groups, and choosing one records an association covering that source's whole duration
- [v] a source is bound to at most one item, the choice is clearable, and a source with no declared group offers nothing
- [v] every cut taken from a bound source resolves to that item whether observation names no subject or several, and automatic association applies only to unbound sources (→CLIP-62)
- [v] the binding survives as a project input: it is saved with the project, frozen at generation and visible in the correction workspace beside the preview (→CLIP-69)
- [v] replacing or removing a source removes its binding; a binding whose fingerprint no longer matches any selected source is ignored rather than refused
- [v] tests: a bound source whose observation names two candidate items still resolves to the bound one, an unbound source keeps today's automatic behaviour, and the binding round-trips through save and generation
- [v] verify BE (ARCH-26) and FE (ARCH-25) pass

## impl notes
- the wire and the store already carry this: `ClipSourceAssociation` and `CompositionInputs.Associations` are saved with the project, so no migration and no proto change are needed — what is missing is the pre-generation surface and the whole-source range
- record the range as `[0, duration_ms)` from the source's own metadata with its fingerprint; `BindCutItem` (`evidence.go:166`) already prefers an owner range that covers the cut completely and treats a partial one as a conflict, so a whole-source range needs no new branch
- the control belongs with source selection (`features/upload-clip-sources`), reading the declared groups through the project's composition document; the correction workspace keeps its existing per-range controls
- this is the fix for the failure that motivated the change: footage of one cut of meat carries nothing that distinguishes it from another, so subject matching cannot bind it and the owner is the only authority

## result
- surface: `ClipSourceStrip` gained an app-drawn `Listbox` per source tile offering the items of the project's declared groups, labelled by group and the item's own facts; `ClipSourcePicker` passes it through as a `binding` prop and never offers it on a read-only surface
- binding: the new `features/bind-clip-source-item` hook records `[0, durationMs)` from the source's own metadata with its fingerprint, so `BindCutItem`'s existing complete-owner-range rule covers every cut from that source with no new branch; choosing again replaces rather than accumulates, and the empty option clears it
- validation: `ValidateSourceAssociations` now refuses only a range the project's own observations contradict. A binding matching no retained observation is ignored rather than refused (CLIP-123) — before generation there are none, and a replaced source simply stops matching. A whole-source range spans every segment by design and is checked against the source's duration
- staleness: a whole-source binding whose source is no longer selected is dropped on the next write, and the server ignores one that survives; ranges bound beside the preview keep their own extent and are never pruned by this control
- tests: `TestWholeSourceBindingReachesEveryCutAndLeavesOthersAutomatic` pins the inheritance and that an unbound source keeps today's automatic behaviour; `TestWholeSourceBindingSurvivesSaveAndGeneration` saves a binding with no observations present and reads it back out of the frozen attempt payload; `TestCompositionAssociationHoldsToObservationsAndBindsTheQuote` now asserts the ignored-not-refused rule; `ClipSourceBinding.test.tsx` covers the per-source control, at-most-one-item with clearing, and a template declaring no group offering nothing
- verify: BE (ARCH-26) green, FE (ARCH-25) green — test 2016, lint (1 pre-existing DraftEditor warning), lint:fsd, lint:style, build:web. ARCH-28 not run: no proto or SQL source changed
- SSOT: the old refusal of a binding with no matching observation is now an ignore, which CLIP-123 asks for; nothing else diverged
