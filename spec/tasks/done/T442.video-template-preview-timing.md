# T442 The video-template preview plays an illustrative timed clip
> st:done@260928 | ssot:CLIP-169 CLIP-170 CLIP-171 | base:CLIP@49 | dep:T441

## goal
The template preview's sample duration runs 15–90 s, the intro and the outro own their spans with no other text beside them, captions of 3–4 s fill the span between back to back in the template's allowed styles in turn, and the scrubber's end shows the last frame.

## acceptance
- [v] the sample-duration slider runs 15…90 s (`entities/clip-design/config/clip-composition.ts`, minimum 15 s)
- [v] timing (`entities/clip-template/lib/composition-sample.ts` and the preview's interval model): the intro takes 0–2.5 s and the outro the last 3 s with only the badge beside them; between them captions of 3–4 s back to back with no gap — the outline's own caption entries first in outline order, then numbered sample sentences — each in the next allowed style in turn; a region with no entry leaves its span to the captions; a caption entry the span cannot hold is left out
- [v] the scrubber's end shows the outro and the badge as they stand at the final instant, never an empty frame
- [v] tests: sample timeline units (spans, back to back, style rotation, overflow left out, no intro, no outro), a preview test for the end frame
- [v] verify FE: `pnpm --filter ./frontend test` · `pnpm lint` · `pnpm lint:fsd` · `pnpm lint:style:probe && pnpm lint:style` · `pnpm build:web`

## impl notes
- Sample sentence copy `샘플 자막 {{n}}` / `Sample caption {{n}}`.
- The preview's timing is illustration only and reaches no project (CLIP-169).
- End frame: evaluate the last instant as inside the last interval (e.g. clamp the time to `duration - 1 ms` when drawing), rather than changing interval semantics elsewhere.
