## goal
The 글 (`/posts`) and 영상 (`/clips`) lists stop being tall cards with two or three links to the same item. Each item is one shared work row: the whole row opens the item, the title on one truncated line, one meta line (state badge · 말투 or 템플릿 · time; a running job's progress or a failure in its place), and at most one trailing action that opens somewhere else (a post's 이전 AI 결과 while a comparison waits, else 발행한 글 ↗; a clip's 영상 다운로드). The newest unfinished item stays accent-tinted inside 작성 중 with `이어서` before its chevron. About twice as many items fit on a phone screen.
