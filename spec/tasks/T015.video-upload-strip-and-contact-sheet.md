# T015 Video upload in the browser, the attachment strip and the contact sheet
> st:todo | ssot:VIDEO-3 VIDEO-4 VIDEO-5 VIDEO-7 VIDEO-9 VIDEO-18 POST-61 GEN-24 | base:VIDEO@1 POST@2 GEN@2 ARCH@1 | dep:T012

## goal
Let the user pick short videos with the photos, gate them by extension, size, duration and count, upload the file as-is with visible progress, show it in the strip and the contact sheet with its observation, and delete it.

## acceptance
- [ ] `shared/config`: `UPLOAD_MAX_VIDEOS_PER_POST` 3 · `UPLOAD_MAX_VIDEO_MB` 200 · `VIDEO_MAX_SECONDS` 60 · `UPLOAD_VIDEO_EXTENSIONS` `['mp4','mov','m4v','webm']`, mirrored from `VITE_*` with those defaults and documented in `.env.example`
- [ ] the picker and drop zone accept both kinds (`accept` lists both extension sets; label 사진·영상 추가 / Add photos or videos); the gate sorts a file by extension into photo or video, applies the video ceilings (size, count against confirmed + earlier-in-pick videos) and lists refusals under 건너뜀 with reasons `video-too-large` `video-too-many` `video-too-long` `video-unreadable`; photos keep today's verdicts byte for byte
- [ ] a video is never decoded or converted: `shared/lib/video/readMetadata(file)` loads the file into an off-DOM `<video preload="metadata">`, resolves `{durationMs, width, height}` on `loadedmetadata`, rejects as unreadable on `error` or after a bounded wait, and revokes its object URL; a duration over `VIDEO_MAX_SECONDS` is skipped `video-too-long` before CreateUpload
- [ ] the handshake sends `kind: VIDEO` on CreateUpload and `durationMs` on ConfirmUpload; the PUT of a video reports progress (0 … 100 %) on its card and the card says 올리는 중 n% ; a confirmed video enters the cached post's `videos` with a local object URL until the next GetPost, like a photo
- [ ] `entities/video` (`model/types.ts` `PostVideo`, `api` mappers + `useDeleteVideo`, `@x/post`) mirrors `entities/image`; `entities/post` carries `videos` on the post model and `videoCount`; the editor's ① panel strip shows video tiles after the photo tiles with a duration badge (`m:ss`) and a play glyph, plays inline on tap (`<video controls preload="metadata">`, no autoplay), and offers the same delete control as a photo
- [ ] the contact sheet pairs each video with its observation by filename: the card shows the shared fields plus `events` as a short ordered list and `speech` when present, plays the clip inline from its presigned URL, and says 관찰 대기 until an entry exists; `entities/observation` gains `events` and `speech`
- [ ] the reobserve picker lists videos after photos with the duration badge; a video with no reusable entry is checked and locked like a photo
- [ ] the status line reads 영상 관찰 중 / Observing video while the job's observe stage is past the photo count (the FE knows both counts from the post) and 사진 관찰 중 otherwise
- [ ] tests: gate verdicts per ceiling and per kind; `readMetadata` with a mocked `<video>`; handshake sends kind and duration; the strip renders a video tile with badge and delete; contact sheet renders `events`; picker ordering; `pnpm --filter ./frontend test` · `pnpm lint` · `pnpm lint:fsd` · `pnpm lint:style:probe && pnpm lint:style` · `pnpm build:web` pass on Node 24.18.0

## impl notes
- `features/upload-photos/model/filter.ts`: `filterFile(file, held: {photos, videos})` returns `{kind:'accepted', attachment:'photo'|'video'}`; `SkipReason` gains the four video reasons; `partitionFiles` splits into `photos` and `videos`
- `upload-batch.ts`: `UploadItem` gains `attachment` and `progress`; the video branch skips the convert pipeline and calls a new `putWithProgress(url, file, contentType, onProgress)` in `features/upload-photos/api/upload-pipeline.ts` built on `XMLHttpRequest` (`upload.onprogress`) ← `fetch` exposes no upload progress; the photo PUT stays as it is; `UPLOAD_CONVERT_CONCURRENCY` still bounds photo conversion, videos upload one at a time
- `entities/image/api/upload-handshake.ts` grows `createUpload(slug, filename, kind)` and `confirmUpload(uploadId, {width, height, durationMs})` returning `PostImage | PostVideo` discriminated by which response field is set; keep the old call shape for photos
- `entities/post`: `Post.videos: PostVideo[]` via `entities/video/@x/post`; the cache patch on confirm/delete mirrors the image patch (POST-10)
- strip UI: extend `PhotoStrip`/`Thumbnail` with a `VideoTile` in `entities/video/ui` (fixed 128 px square like the photo tile, badge bottom-right, `shared/ui` primitives only); drop-zone copy in the `posts` i18n namespace: `upload.addPhotosOrVideos`, `upload.skip.videoTooLarge` (`{{max}}MB`), `videoTooMany` (`{{max}}개`), `videoTooLong` (`{{max}}초`), `videoUnreadable`, `upload.progress` (`올리는 중 {{percent}}%`)
- contact sheet: the card component takes an attachment union; the video card renders `<video>` where the photo card renders `<img>`; `events` as `<ol>` of at most the first 6 lines with a 더보기 disclosure, `speech` under a `들린 말` label
- status label: `posts.job.observingVideo` `영상 관찰 중` / `Observing video`; the stage progress `done` vs the post's photo count decides which label (pure function in `pages/editor/model`)
- no VIDEO block rendering, export or publishing in this task (T016)
