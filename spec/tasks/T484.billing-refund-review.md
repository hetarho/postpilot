# T484 Request and review payment refunds with confirmed entitlement reversal

## goal
Let owners submit refund requests and operators resolve them against payment-funded benefit use and authoritative provider outcomes.

## acceptance
- [ ] The owner can request a subscription or top-up refund from billing; requests within seven days with none of that payment’s paid benefits used qualify for full refund. Used/later requests remain submit-able for operator review, and normal cancellation is a separate action.
- [ ] The operator can inspect funding payment, charged/refunded KRW, request/reason, benefit-use evidence and proposed disposition, then record approval/rejection and the reviewed amount. Review routes/RPCs are master-only and another owner cannot read or mutate a request.
- [ ] Benefit-use evidence includes paid model work, daily/monthly credit consumption and successful server exports funded by the payment, including an upgrade; purchasing unrelated packs and using free models do not falsely consume subscription benefits. Active reservations are accounted for before deciding unused eligibility.
- [ ] Provider confirmation appends refund history once and voids only the refunded payment’s affected remaining entitlements; unrelated credits, other payment-funded rights, posts, videos and audit history survive. Unknown provider outcomes remain processing and reconcile rather than refund twice.
- [ ] No automatic time-prorated cash formula or blanket seven-day legal cutoff is presented. Credit fault compensation remains a separate ledger operation. Refund notices show the actual confirmed amount and decision/status.
- [ ] Real-store and adapter tests cover exactly/after seven days, used/unused subscription and pack funding, upgrade-funded benefits, active jobs, concurrent consumption/refund, unauthorized access, provider timeout/replay and unrelated-lot preservation. FE tests cover request and operator review states; run ARCH-25/26/28.

## impl notes
- Add billing-owned refund requests/decisions/provider-outcome records correlated to payment ids, not only subscription ids. Keep review/history append-only and prevent duplicate active refund intent for the same refundable payment amount.
- Get use evidence through usage/model admission and clip ports using T477/T479 funding identities. Track actual use, not a balance comparison: grants can expire unused, and the ability to select a higher grade alone is not a consumed benefit. Live holds cannot be declared untouched until settled/released; keep the request pending instead of cancelling a running job.
- Within-seven-day unused cases have a full-amount decision; other cases are explicitly operator reviewed. Persist the reviewed amount and the payment-funded entitlement disposition shown to the operator; no automatic daily cash ratio or reversal of unrelated funding is inferred from it. Revalidate refundable remaining amount and concurrent usage before dispatch.
- Extend the existing provider refund port to carry the reviewed amount/idempotency identity if needed; verify official Toss refund/partial-refund/reconciliation semantics under ARCH-33. Preserve original provider payment identity and confirm its authoritative cumulative refund amount before local completion.
- Use a durable intent and guard affected spendable funding during refund processing; provider I/O stays outside DB transactions. Confirmed failure releases that guard, unknown outcome keeps it pending, and confirmed success applies one scoped reversal. Preserve already-admitted jobs’ original reservations and prevent new consumption of funds being returned.
- Compose an operator refund-review section under the existing admin account-management tab and an owner request/status feature on billing; T485 supplies the complete billing layout. UI never presents an operator judgment as an automatic statutory entitlement determination.
