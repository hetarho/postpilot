# T348 The dev seed shows published posts, nouns, candidates, a phrase list and a title-area template without Naver keys

## goal
- `pnpm dev --seed` makes every surface of this delta visible locally with no Naver credentials (R42): published posts that meet every QUAL minimum, stored nouns, replacement candidates on review posts, one 분야 phrase list, and a template with a title area assigned to a draft.

## acceptance
- [ ] The fixture spread becomes draft / review / finalized / published: `free` 0/0/0/0, `base` 2/1/0/0, `pro` 3/2/1/2, `max` 4/3/7/0, `master` 5/4/3/11 — every account's total is unchanged (0, 3, 8, 14, 23).
- [ ] Every seeded published post has status `published`, a `https://blog.naver.com/...` URL accepted by the post context's own URL rule, and a `published_at` after its creation, before now and distinct within its account; `master`'s 11 meet M1's minimum of 10 and every smaller one.
- [ ] Every non-draft post stores its topic's nouns (`content_nouns` non-NULL, each noun contained in that post's text by QUAL-7's rule); drafts store none.
- [ ] Every post of `pro` and `master` carries the 분야 `daily_life` (일상·생각); every other post has none.
- [ ] Every review post of `pro` and `master` carries three replacement candidates — title, tag 0 and body block 1 — whose source stands at that place in the stored content and whose phrases come from the seeded list (GEN-53), so ② renders all three marks.
- [ ] `master` holds one template `하루 기록` with a non-empty title area that the template context accepted, and `master`'s first draft is assigned to it (TMPL-50).
- [ ] `field_phrase_lists` holds a `daily_life` row with the fixture's phrases, written only when that field has no row; a row already present — from an earlier seed or a real batch — is left byte-identical, and the wipe never deletes it (ARCH-44).
- [ ] No guideline or guideline preset row is seeded (GUIDE-34), and the seed makes no network call.
- [ ] Running the seed twice yields the same rows; the command's report adds a published column and says whether the phrase list was written or left standing; README's seed notes match.
- [ ] Tests: `backend/internal/devseed/devseed_test.go` (new: `TestPublishedArticlesCarryAValidURLAndASpreadPublishedAt`, `TestGeneratedArticlesCarryNounsTheirTextContains`, `TestReviewCandidatesPointAtTextThatIsThere`, `TestThePhraseListFixtureIsAValidList`, `TestOneAccountMeetsEveryPublishedMinimum`, `TestRunEnsuresThePhraseListAfterTheWipe`, `TestRunAssignsTheTitleAreaTemplateToAFirstDraft`; `TestRunRefusesAMissingCollaborator` extended to the two new ports; existing spread tests follow the new counts); `backend/cmd/seed/main_test.go` (new: `TestSeedWritesThePublishedQualityFixtureAndIsIdempotent`, `TestSeedLeavesAnExistingPhraseListStanding`; `TestSeedCreatesShortEmailFreeAccountsThatCanLogIn` still passes unchanged).

## impl notes
- ARCH-44 holds as at HEAD: `internal/devseed` declares consumer-owned ports and describes the installation in its own spellings; `cmd/seed/main.go` adapts each from the owning context's store or service; no raw SQL outside tests; devseed never imports post, template or quality outside `_test.go`.
- `fixtures.go`: `StatusPublished = "published"`; `Account` gains `Published int`, `Field string` (devseed's `FieldDailyLife = "daily_life"`, set on `pro` and `master`) and `Template bool` (`master` only); `Account.Posts()` and `AccountReport.Posts()` add `Published`. `Articles` appends the published group after finalized, so published posts are the oldest, and takes the account's template id: `Articles(voiceID, templateID string, now time.Time)`.
- `Article` gains `Field`, `TemplateID` (set on the account's first draft only), `PublishedURL string` and `PublishedAt time.Time` (zero unless published). `PublishedURL` = `fmt.Sprintf("https://blog.naver.com/seed-%s/%d", loginID, 224000000000+index)`; `PublishedAt` = `CreatedAt.Add(3 * time.Hour)` (still in the past: the newest published post is `(Drafts+Reviews+Finalized+1)*19h` old).
- `Content` gains `Nouns []string` and `Replacements []Replacement`; `type Replacement struct { Surface string; Index int; Source string; Phrases []string }` with devseed's `SurfaceTitle = "title"`, `SurfaceTag = "tag"`, `SurfaceBody = "body"`. Nouns and replacements are what a write run returns beside the content, not part of it, so the adapter stores them apart from `PostContent` (R16, R29).
- `topic` gains `nouns []string`: 4–6 per topic, each contained in that topic's title, heading or body by QUAL-7's rule (a Korean 어절 starts with it — e.g. `보말칼국수`, not `칼국수`, for `보말칼국수를`), at least one contained in the title. Every review, finalized and published article carries its topic's nouns.
- Candidates: review articles of an account with a `Field` get three, derived so they stay valid whatever topic lands there — title (index 0, source = the content title's first 어절), tag (index 0, source = `tags[0]`), body (index 1 = the first TEXT block, source = its first two 어절) — each offering the first two `PhraseList.Phrases` that differ from its source.
- `var PhraseList = PhraseListFixture{Field: FieldDailyLife, CorpusSize: 300, Phrases: []string{"소소한 일상", "일상 기록", "오늘 하루", "주말 나들이", "동네 산책", "집밥 메뉴", "혼자 보내는 주말", "소소한 행복", "하루 루틴", "새로운 취미 생활", "간단한 저녁 메뉴", "기록하는 습관"}}` — 2–5 tokens each, observational only (QUAL-21), fixture data that T338's pass would replace.
- `var TitleAreaTemplate = Template{Name: "하루 기록", Description: "제목에 장소를 받고 본문은 사진 순서대로 쓰는 기록용 템플릿", TitleArea: "<ask label=\"장소\"/>에서 보낸 <write>그날을 한마디로</write>", Body: "<write>그날 무엇을 했는지 한두 문장으로 쓰세요</write>\n<repeat each=\"photo\">\n<slot kind=\"photo\"/>\n<write>이 사진에 대한 설명</write>\n</repeat>\n<ask label=\"한 줄 소감\">마지막 한 줄 소감을 쓰세요</ask>"}` — literal, `<write>` and `<ask>` only in the title area (TMPL-50), labels unique across both areas.
- `devseed.go` new ports, both required in `Deps` and in `valid()` (ARCH-40): `Templates interface { Create(ctx context.Context, t Template) (id string, err error) }` and `PhraseLists interface { EnsureList(ctx context.Context, list PhraseListFixture, at time.Time) (written bool, err error) }`. `Template` carries `UserID` set by `seedAccount`.
- `Run` order: DeleteAll → DeleteAllSourceBatches → per account (Create → OpenMonthlyLot → EnsureDefault → the template when `Template` → posts) → `PhraseLists.EnsureList(PhraseList, now)` once. `Report` gains `PhraseListWritten bool`; `AccountReport` gains `Published int`.
- The package doc's "left alone" paragraph adds phrase lists: `field_phrase_lists` carries no `user_id`, the `users` cascade never reaches it, and the seed only inserts a missing row — a real list collected on a keyed box is installation-wide data like the curated models.
- `cmd/seed` `templates` adapter: `template.NewService(templatestore.New(handle.Writer, handle.Reader), template.Limits{...})` with the same cfg fields `buildContexts` passes (including T333's title-area limit), then T333's `Create` with the title area and `template.Numbers{}`; the service's own parse is the fixture's guard, and the created id is returned.
- `cmd/seed` `phraseLists` adapter over `qualitystore.New(handle.Writer, handle.Reader)`: T335's phrase-list get; a found row returns `false` untouched; otherwise T335's replace-upsert with the fixture's phrases and corpus size, `refreshed_at = at` and `next_refresh_at = at` — due at once, so a box with Naver keys replaces the fixture on its next pass while a box without keys keeps it (QUAL-42).
- `cmd/seed` `posts.Write`, in this order because T334 refuses every write on a published post: `CreatePost` → when `Field` is set, a `quality.Known` guard then T337's `AssignField` store method (query `AssignPostField`) → when `TemplateID` is set, `store.AssignTemplate(ctx, slug, userID, &id, post.TemplateNumbers{}, createdAt)` (require `true`) → drafts stop here → `ValidateContent` → T341's store `UpdateGeneratedContent` with `post.WriteAnnotations{Nouns, Candidates}` (empty candidates on non-review posts, which the store writes as NULL) → `Finalize` for finalized and published → for published, T329's `ParseNaverBlogURL` guard then T329's `PublishPost` store write with the normalized URL and `PublishedAt` (require one row).
- Candidates map through a closed switch from devseed's surface spelling to T341's `post.ReplacementCandidate` surface; an unknown spelling fails the seed, as an unknown status would.
- `printReport`: add a `PUBL` column after `FINAL`, and after the table `  phrase list daily_life: written` or `  phrase list daily_life: left standing`.
- `TestOneAccountMeetsEveryPublishedMinimum` compares `Published` against the largest minimum T331 put in `internal/quality/limits.go` (M1's 10) rather than a literal, so a raised minimum fails the fixture instead of silently hiding a row state.
- `TestGeneratedArticlesCarryNounsTheirTextContains` checks containment with T325's containment function; `TestReviewCandidatesPointAtTextThatIsThere` checks title containment, `tags[index]` containment, `blocks[index]` is a TEXT block containing the source, phrases ⊂ `PhraseList.Phrases`, at most 3, none equal to the source; `TestThePhraseListFixtureIsAValidList` checks 2–5 `strings.Fields` tokens, at most 50, no duplicates, and `quality.Known(PhraseList.Field)`.
- `TestSeedWritesThePublishedQualityFixtureAndIsIdempotent` runs `run(ctx)` twice on one temp DB and after each asserts: per-account status counts; `master` 11 and `pro` 2 published rows, all with a `https://blog.naver.com/` URL and distinct non-NULL `published_at`; `content_nouns` non-NULL exactly on non-drafts; `field = 'daily_life'` exactly on `pro`'s and `master`'s posts; `replacement_candidates` a 3-element JSON array exactly on their review posts; one `master` template with `title_area <> ''` and `master`'s first draft's `template_id` equal to it; exactly one `field_phrase_lists` row, `daily_life`, with the fixture phrases; identical results from the second run.
- `TestSeedLeavesAnExistingPhraseListStanding`: after one run, `UPDATE field_phrase_lists SET phrases = '["다른 문구"]'` in the test, run again, and assert every column of the row is unchanged. `TestRunEnsuresThePhraseListAfterTheWipe` covers the report: `PhraseListWritten` mirrors what `EnsureList` answered, and `EnsureList` is recorded after both deletes and after the last account.
- README "테스트 데이터 시드": the table gains a 발행 column (`초안 / 검토 / 확정 / 발행`) with the counts above; a paragraph says `pro` and `master` posts carry 분야 일상·생각, `master`'s 11 published posts meet every quality minimum while `pro`'s 2 show the below-minimum rows, review posts show replacement marks, `master` has the 하루 기록 title-area template on its first draft, the 일상·생각 phrase list is written only when absent and survives every seed, and none of it needs Naver keys.
- Out of scope: running the Naver batch (T338); seeding a guideline preset or any guideline (GUIDE-34); quality ticks; measurement rows (computed on first read, T335); any product behaviour change.
