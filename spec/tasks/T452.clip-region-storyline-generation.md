## goal
Generate enabled slot text with the existing approved storyline/flow calls and preserve reviewed or owner-fixed words through building and retries.

## acceptance
- [ ] Storyline-first writes body paragraphs and generated intro/outro slot text in its one approved writing call. Direct generation writes slots with the storyline in its existing flow call and then narrates; neither path adds per-slot calls or an extra credit approval.
- [ ] Prompts include enabled slot instructions, exact owner/bound text and current preset bounds, including without a template. Blank instructions request context-appropriate content and never inject a product-specific business label. Disabled and owner-fixed slots are not regenerated; an explicit owner-fixed blank stays blank.
- [ ] Building from a reviewed storyline copies its current final region text unchanged through flow and narration. Explicit storyline regeneration/rewrite may update generated slots and preserves owner-fixed text. Plan revision requests preserve storyline content and its canonical region words.
- [ ] A successful storyline rewrite synchronizes changed generated region text with an existing plan under T451 without rewriting its cuts/captions. Body-only changes await an explicit build; failure/cancellation preserves prior canonical body, slots, plan and result.
- [ ] Quotes, frozen job payloads, input budgets and recovery compatibility include region enablement, preset, instructions and text/ownership. Editing these values invalidates an affected quote/candidate while retaining compatible observations; a retry cannot restore stale slot content.
- [ ] Generated text that cannot fit follows the existing generated-text repair/notice rules and leaves the slot editable; all-empty enabled regions remain identifiable as unresolved. No preset selection, manual save, preview or render makes a provider call to fill them.
- [ ] Extend Go prompt/schema/parser, application job and store tests for both creation paths, reviewed-story builds, mixed generated/fixed/blank slots, no-template inputs, full-input admission, stale quotes, failure/cancellation and recovery. Assert provider call counts and unchanged credit reservation/settlement semantics, then pass ARCH-31's local gates.
