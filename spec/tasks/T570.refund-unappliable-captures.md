# T570 Refund a captured payment the product cannot apply, automatically
> st:todo | ssot:BILL-22 BILL-12 BILL-15 ARCH-10 ARCH-24 | base:BILL@9 ARCH@15 | dep:- | touches:backend/internal/billing/ backend/internal/tosspay/client.go

## goal
An order whose payment Toss captured but the product cannot apply no longer sits in `review` locking the account: the next billing pass refunds it in full, fails the order, records the refund once and mails the customer (BILL-22).

## acceptance
- [ ] each billing pass finds orders in `review` and, for each, cancels the full captured amount through the provider with a stable idempotency key (`unapplied:<orderID>`), outside any write transaction (ARCH-10)
- [ ] after the provider answers, the payment is read back; a cancel of the full amount confirmed → in one transaction the order becomes `failed`, a `refund` history event of that amount is appended (BILL-15) and the account's pending-order lock is gone; then a refund mail is sent (BILL-12)
- [ ] an unresolved answer (timeout, 5xx, 429) leaves the order in `review` for the next pass; a definitive refusal whose read-back shows the money already returned completes as above; any other refusal stays and is logged with user and order
- [ ] T562's routing of unapplicable captures into `review` stays; the renewal of an account whose order is being refunded is skipped and logged as T563 does until the order leaves `review`
- [ ] tests (billing/store, real SQLite, fake provider): a stale pack order in review is refunded in full, failed, recorded and mailed, and the account can buy again on the same pass's next call; a 500 on cancel leaves it in review; a replayed pass after success does nothing (idempotent); the provider cancel carries the idempotency key
- [ ] behaviour unchanged otherwise: ARCH-26 green

## impl notes
- from review/perf-cost-261004 F12, decided as BILL-22+ r9
- reuse T563's definitive-cancel refusal set and read-back helper rather than a second copy
- the refund event and mail wording follow the existing refund event/mail (a new subject line naming that the payment could not be applied is fine); no operator review row is created — BILL-22 says without operator review
- only orders already in `review` are refunded; an order still `pending` keeps its normal settlement
