## goal
Create an owner-scoped spoken-voice library separate from writing-style voices.

## acceptance
- [ ] Owned drafts, candidate sets, confirmed voices and audition assets survive reload; every foreign-id read/write/playback returns NotFound and unauthenticated RPCs are refused.
- [ ] Confirmed bindings and audio references are immutable; rename is metadata-only, sound revision creates a separate voice, and removal blocks new selection without invalidating stored audio.
- [ ] New fields/RPCs do not rename or mutate existing VOICE records, training, analysis, projections or post selections; migration and mapper tests pin compatibility.
- [ ] Real-SQLite, RPC authorization and private-storage tests cover stale revisions, duplicate requests, removal/reference races and revoked short-lived media access.

## impl notes
- Place the independent spoken aggregate under existing backend/internal/voice/spoken with its own store and RPC edges, using separate spoken_voice_drafts, spoken_voice_candidates, spoken_voices and spoken_audio_assets tables. ARCH-5 keeps voice as the root context; do not reuse writing-profile ids/tables or expand the unrelated ARCH pending scope.
- Add spoken_voice.proto and SpokenVoiceService for owned draft CRUD, list/detail, metadata rename, removal, audition acknowledgement and authorized sample access; T537 adds its job actions. Creation state derives from persisted draft/candidates/confirmation, not a frontend-only step. Server-generated ids, expected_revision guards and idempotency keys are required.
- Store private object references, content hashes, measured duration/format and immutable model/profile/voice provenance; supplier handles stay internal. Expose short-lived authorized playback URLs at read time only. Names are trimmed 1..60 code points; descriptions and sample limits come from the chosen profile.
- An audition acknowledgement references the exact successfully played candidate; confirmation requires that candidate selected and acknowledged. A browser event proves playback initiation, not subjective listening quality; the owner remains the confirmation authority.
- Removal is a selection tombstone; confirmed speech remains independently playable. Candidate/media upload failure cannot establish a confirmed voice. Record recoverable orphan cleanup intents and publish removal/account-cleanup behavior through consumer-owned ports without touching another context table.
