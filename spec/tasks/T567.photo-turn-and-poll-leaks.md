## goal
Turning two photos quickly keeps both turns on screen, typing in ② no longer triggers a fingerprint read per autosave, and a long browser-render sampling wait no longer piles up abort listeners.

## acceptance
- [ ] `useRotatePhoto` updates the cache in the hook-level `onSuccess` (fires for every call), not in the per-call `mutate` options
- [ ] the post fingerprint query keys on a content revision that settles: it changes only after the content has been saved and stayed unchanged for the autosave beat (or once the save settles), so a burst of autosaves causes one read
- [ ] one shared abortable delay helper (`shared/lib`, e.g. `delay(ms, signal)`) removes its abort listener when the timer fires; `run-render.ts`'s sampling poll and `preview.ts`'s `askAgainWhenBusy` use it
- [ ] tests: turning B while A's turn is in flight leaves both turned in the cache; three autosaves within the beat cause one fingerprint fetch; after 10 resolved delays the signal holds no listeners (spy on add/removeEventListener)
