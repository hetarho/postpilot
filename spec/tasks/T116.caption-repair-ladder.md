# T116 The caption repair ladder
> st:todo | ssot:CDS-55 CDS-52 CLIP-17 ARCH-24 | base:CDS@3 CLIP@6 ARCH@2 | dep:-

## goal
Repair a compiled clip whose manifest fails a check by walking style, then anchor, then dropping the copy, so a design-system violation costs a caption rather than the clip.

## acceptance
- [ ] `design.Verify` reports WHICH caption failed: it returns a typed error carrying the violation and the slot (cut index, copy index), and the furniture slot `-1` for a badge, card or chip; `Legible` keeps its boolean shape
- [ ] a repair pass runs on a COMPILED plan (`EditPlan.Compiled()`) when `VerifyLayout` fails before any download: rung 1 the failing caption's style → 깔끔하게, rung 2 its anchor → that style's own default, rung 3 the copy is dropped; after each rung the plan is laid out and verified again, and the pass gives up only when the ladder is exhausted for every failing caption
- [ ] a violation whose slot is furniture fails the render at once with the check named — it is a renderer defect, not a composition the ladder can repair
- [ ] a plan a PERSON corrected is still refused with the failing check named and never silently moved (the CDS-52 constraint, today's `Compiled()` branch); only the compiler's own plans walk the ladder
- [ ] the existing contrast fallback becomes rung 1 of this one ladder rather than a second mechanism beside it, and the post-sample verify keeps using it
- [ ] every rung taken is recorded on that cut's `Composition.Fallback` (`contrast` stays, `style`, `anchor` and `dropped` join it) so step ② can say what happened
- [ ] the verify AFTER the cuts are rendered stays a hard failure: the footage is already encoded there, and a repair would mean re-rendering — document that in `RENDER.md` beside the two verify points
- [ ] tests: each rung repairs and the next one takes over when it does not · a furniture violation fails at once · a person's plan is refused rather than repaired · a clip whose every caption is dropped still renders its footage · the ladder is recorded per cut · `RENDER.md` documents the ladder
- [ ] ARCH-26, ARCH-28 and ARCH-25 pass

## impl notes
- CDS-52 has always said "reapplies the fallback rules"; the renderer implemented that for V3 alone and failed on the other eight. CDS-55 r3 names the ladder so the rest is implementable — this task is the code catching up to the SSOT, not a new policy
- 깔끔하게 is rung 1 because its plate answers V2, V3 and V5 by itself (CDS-16's α ≥ 0.72 ink under white text clears the contrast floor over any frame), so the rung that fixes the most checks comes first
- V13 and V14 are sequence rules with no single failing element: report the LATER of the pair as the slot, which is the one whose style or anchor the ladder can change without invalidating what came before
- bound the pass: at most one full ladder per caption and one re-layout per rung, so a plan of N captions costs at most 3N layout passes and no downloads — the repair runs before any source byte is fetched, which is the whole reason the pre-download verify exists
- do not let the ladder touch the hook or ending card, the badge or a chip: they are the design system's own and CDS-45 fixes their placement
