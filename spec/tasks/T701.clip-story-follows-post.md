# T701 Build the storyline in the linked post's order and place videos by capture order
> st:doing@261016.q8s4 | ssot:CLIP-217 CLIP-218 CLIP-141 CLIP-178 CLIP-181 CLIP-135 GUIDE-51 | base:CLIP@63 GUIDE@23 | dep:T699 T700 | touches:backend/internal/clip backend/cmd/api frontend/src/widgets/clip-workspace | lane:-

## goal
With a linked post, the storyline (스토리라인 먼저 and 바로 만들기) follows the post's sections. Each video is placed by capture order and confirmed by its scenes. Every generation also leaves out a template stage that nothing supports.

## acceptance
- [ ] A pure function, `clip.CaptureNeighbors(sections, sources)`, gives each source video its candidate sections:
  - It orders the post's photos together with the video by capture time when the video and the photos both have one.
  - Otherwise it uses the camera file sequence when the photos and videos share one, meaning the same file-stem prefix and digit count, compared numerically.
  - The candidates are the sections of the nearest photo before and the nearest photo after the video, so there are one or two.
  - A video with no usable order gets no candidates.
- [ ] The storyline and 바로 만들기 flow prompts receive `linked_post.sections[i]` with its photos, and `capture_neighbors` per source.
- [ ] The prompt rule says six things:
  - Paragraphs follow the sections' order.
  - Each section that has footage becomes its paragraph.
  - A section without footage is left out.
  - A video's scenes go to whichever of its candidate sections they fit; if they fit none, they go to the section whose text they match.
  - A video that matches no section stays unused.
  - Capture order is never written as time passing.
- [ ] The storyline and flow prompts tell the writer to leave out a template stage that neither the footage nor the owner's material gives anything to, whether or not a post is linked (CLIP-141).
- [ ] ②'s storyline space lists, by file name, the sources that no paragraph uses. This is computed from the storyline and the sources, with no new field. With nothing unused it shows nothing.
- [ ] Tests:
  - `backend/internal/clip`: `TestCaptureNeighborsByTime`, `TestCaptureNeighborsByFileSequence`, `TestCaptureNeighborsNoSharedSequence`, `TestCaptureNeighborsBothSides`.
  - `backend/internal/clip/ai`: `TestStorylinePromptCarriesSectionsAndNeighbors`, `TestEmptyStageRuleInStorylineAndFlowPrompts`.
  - `widgets/clip-workspace`: `lists unused sources by file name`.
  - A fixture from the 261015 참선주면옥 case: photos 31134–31163.jpg in seven sections and videos 31136–31165.mp4. It gives 31136 the sections {외관, 주문}, 31153 the sections {냉면, 미나리국밥}, and 31164 and 31165 the last section only.
- [ ] Relevant go test (`-count=1`), vitest, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm lint:fsd` and the ARCH-24 checks pass. No paid model call is made; the real-model check stays the owner's call, as in feedback-no-paid-api-calls.

## impl notes
- CLIP r62–r63 source-colour decisions do not change section placement; preserve qualified SDR/HLG decoding and original identity.
- Extend T699's `LinkedPostSection` with `Photos []LinkedPhoto { File string; CapturedAt *time.Time }`, in the post's block order. IMAGE and GALLERY blocks add their files to their section; VIDEO blocks add nothing. The adapter reads `images.captured_at` from T700.
- Source side: use the lease's `filename` and `captured_at` from T700.
- File-sequence parse: take the stem without its extension and match `^(.*?)(\d+)$`. Photos and videos share a sequence when the prefix and the digit count are equal. Mixed prefixes, or no digits, mean there is no shared sequence.
- When capture time and file sequence are both available, capture time wins. Only one is used per video, never a mix of the two.
- Why the nearest neighbors on both sides: post sections reorder photos (참선주면옥 put 31151 after 31147), so neither the predecessor nor the successor is right alone. The scenes decide between the two candidates, which is the "observed scenes confirm the fit" check.
- `capture_neighbors` is computed in `clip/app` when the payload is frozen, and stored in the frozen payload as hints with the linked post. The prompt builders only render it.
- The order rule outranks GUIDE-42's 일어난 순서대로 while a post is linked (CLIP-217 over the 기본 지침). Say so in the prompt rule rather than editing the guideline text.
