## goal
Prove the whole publishing path against MinIO and a fake Naver, then perform one explicitly authorized live smoke publish to the owner's designated Naver test blog, and reflect the verified reality into PUBLISH (legacy job 25 T021–T023; legacy plan 12 acceptance A1 A2 A6 A8 A9 A10 A14).

## acceptance
- [ ] against MinIO and a fake Naver: durable queueing while the Mac is off and later claiming without another phone action, immutable R2 copies untouched by a later post edit or delete, reconnect and phone reload resuming status, revocation immediately failing the revoked token while the other connection keeps working, terminal cleanup and the orphan sweep never deleting a live asset, and every commit-fault invariant (a fault after the fence yields `outcome_unknown` and never a second post)
- [ ] a user can pair two accounts on one Mac with distinct Keychain tokens, browser profiles and job directories, each seeing only its account's jobs and publishing through its own verified Naver identity
- [ ] one explicitly authorized live smoke publish to the designated test blog, from a finalized fixture containing every block type and eight photos, produces one Naver post whose title, plain-text block order, eight JPEG positions, captions, tags, category and visibility match the frozen manifest, and the verified URL is stored and rendered; title, order, captions, tags, category, visibility and URL are recorded — no credentials or cookies
- [ ] expired login, CAPTCHA, 2FA or account mismatch becomes `needs_attention` with nothing sent to the VPS, and an explicit safe retry before commit finishes the same job after local repair
- [ ] if a real Naver test account is unavailable, the task is marked `blocked` with that reason and none of the live items above is claimed
- [ ] PUBLISH is updated through update-ssot to state only verified reality (the "not yet built" constraint removed or narrowed), and the legacy claim in MARKETING-4 is revisited in the same pass
- [ ] every gate in ARCH-25 ARCH-26 ARCH-27 ARCH-28 passes

## impl notes
- the owner must designate the test blog and authorize the live run explicitly before it happens; record the authorization in `## result`
- the smoke is one publication; do not loop or retry it automatically — a second post through the system would itself be a failed acceptance
- reflection: PUBLISH's `not yet built` constraint line and MARKETING-4's "live verification still in progress" wording are the two SSOT edits that follow a successful smoke
