# T327 The template grammar parses a title area in lockstep in Go and TypeScript
> st:todo | ssot:TMPL-20 TMPL-43 TMPL-50 TMPL-55 ARCH-24 | base:TMPL@10 ARCH@9 | dep:-

## goal
- Both parsers read a template's title area (literal text, `<write>`, `<ask>`), refuse a slot, repeat or note there as `not_in_title`, name the area of every error, and share one label namespace and one ask ceiling with the body, title first, pinned by the shared fixture and corpus.

## acceptance
- [ ] `backend/internal/template/testdata/grammar/cases.json` carries the twelve title-area cases listed in impl notes, plus three optional case keys documented in `_comment`: `titleArea` (default `""`), `titleNodes`, and `error.area` (`title_area` or `body`, default `body`).
- [ ] Go `ParseTemplate(titleArea, body, opts)` and TS `parseTemplate(titleArea, body, options)` pass every fixture case. An accepted case yields its `nodes` and `titleNodes`, and both areas serialize back byte-exactly. A refused case yields its line, reason and area.
- [ ] A `slot`, `repeat` or `note` in the title area is `not_in_title` at its opening tag's line. The check runs once the tag head reads and before any rule of that kind: `<slot kind="video"></slot>` in a title is `not_in_title`, while `<slot kind=photo/>` there stays `malformed_tag`.
- [ ] One namespace, title first:
  - a duplicate inside the title is `duplicate_ask_label` in `title_area`;
  - a body ask reusing a title label is `duplicate_ask_label` in `body`, at the body line;
  - `too_many_asks` counts the title's asks first and then the body's.
- [ ] Order of refusal: title structure, then body structure, then the shared namespace. A body `unknown_tag` outranks a cross-area duplicate. Line numbers are 1-based within each area.
- [ ] With `titleArea == ""`, `ParseTemplate` and `Parse(body)` give the same verdict on every fixture case. Every error from `Parse` of a body carries `Area == "body"`.
- [ ] `corpus.json` gains a `titleCases` key: 200 generated (title, body) pairs with the Go verdict, from their own seed. The 600 `cases` entries are unchanged, and the diff adds only the new key. The TS suite matches every title case on verdict, line, reason and area.
- [ ] TS `templateAskFields(titleArea, body, options)` returns the title's fields, then the body's. It returns `[]` when either area fails. `parseTemplate`, `templateAskFields` and `type TemplateArea` are exported from `entities/template/index.ts`.
- [ ] `builder.reasons.not_in_title` exists in ko and en. `guide.test.ts`'s every-reason check passes.
- [ ] No caller changes behaviour. `Parse`, `parse`, `askFields` and `TEMPLATE_PARSE_OPTIONS` keep their verdicts, because nothing passes a title area until T333 and T336.
- [ ] Tests:
  - `backend/internal/template/grammar_test.go`: `TestParseAgainstSharedFixtures`, which reads the new keys and checks the empty-title equivalence;
  - `backend/internal/template/corpus_test.go`: `TestCorpusMatchesTheCommittedFile`, now with title cases;
  - `frontend/src/entities/template/lib/grammar.test.ts`: fixture, corpus and title-case suites, plus a `templateAskFields` suite covering title first, `[]` on a title failure, `[]` on a body failure, `[]` on a cross-area duplicate, and an empty title equal to `askFields(body)`;
  - `frontend/src/entities/template/model/guide.test.ts`: the parse-reason copy check.
- [ ] Verify: `cd backend && test -z "$(gofmt -l .)" && go vet ./... && go build ./... && go test -timeout 30m ./...` · `pnpm --filter ./frontend test` · `pnpm lint` · `pnpm lint:fsd` · `pnpm lint:style:probe && pnpm lint:style` · `pnpm build:web`

## impl notes
- Land the fixture first (TMPL-20). Add the cases.json entries, watch both harnesses fail, then change the parsers.
- The twelve cases, as name: titleArea | body → expectation:
  - `a title area of literal text, a write and a data field`: `맛집 | <ask label="가게 이름"/> <write>메뉴를 한 줄로</write>` | `<write>인트로</write>` → titleNodes `literal{raw:"맛집 | "}`, `ask{label:"가게 이름",text:""}`, `literal{raw:" "}`, `write{text:"메뉴를 한 줄로"}`; nodes `write{text:"인트로"}`
  - `a photo position in the title area`: `맛집 후기\n<slot kind="photo"/>` | `<write>인트로</write>` → line 2, `not_in_title`, `title_area`
  - `a repeat in the title area`: `<repeat each="photo"><write>a</write></repeat>` | `""` → line 1, `not_in_title`, `title_area`
  - `a note in the title area`: `<note>짧게</note>` | `""` → line 1, `not_in_title`, `title_area`
  - `the place outranks the attributes in the title area`: `<slot kind="video"></slot>` | `""` → line 1, `not_in_title`, `title_area`
  - `a structural error in the title area names the title area`: `<write>메뉴` | `""` → line 1, `unclosed_tag`, `title_area`
  - `two data fields with one title in the title area`: `<ask label="가게"/> <ask label="가게"/>` | `""` → line 1, `duplicate_ask_label`, `title_area`
  - `a body data field reusing a title area label`: `<ask label="가게"/>` | `<write>인트로</write>\n<ask label="가게"/>` → line 2, `duplicate_ask_label`, `body`
  - `the data-field ceiling counts both areas, title first`: `<ask label="a"/> <ask label="b"/>` | `<ask label="c"/>\n<ask label="d"/>` → line 2, `too_many_asks`, `body` (the fixture's `askMaxPerBody` is 3)
  - `the title area is read before the body`: `<note>톤</note>` | `<writer>` → line 1, `not_in_title`, `title_area`
  - `a body structural error outranks a cross-area duplicate`: `<ask label="가게"/>` | `<ask label="가게"/>\n<writer>` → line 2, `unknown_tag`, `body`
  - `an empty title area is the body alone`: `""` | `<ask label="방문일"/>` → titleNodes `[]`, nodes `ask{label:"방문일",text:""}`
- Go `grammar.go` gains `ReasonNotInTitle = "not_in_title"` and the area constants `AreaTitle = "title_area"` and `AreaBody = "body"`. They are untyped strings because the same value is the fixture's `error.area` and the wire param T333 emits.
- `ParseError` gains `Area string`, and `Error()` becomes `template <area> line <n>: <reason>`.
  - Inner functions keep building `&ParseError{Line, Reason}`.
  - The public entry points stamp the area on the way out through one helper, `inArea(err error, area string) error`, which uses `errors.As`. So no inner call site changes.
- `ParseOptions.TitleArea bool` means "parse this text as a title area". `ParseTemplate` sets it per area. A caller parsing one area on its own sets it itself; T340's title builder does.
- The `not_in_title` check sits in `parseTag`, right after `parseTagHead` succeeds and before the `switch`:
  - `if opts.TitleArea` and `tagNames[name]` is `NodeSlot`, `NodeRepeat` or `NodeNote`, return `&ParseError{Line: line, Reason: ReasonNotInTitle}`;
  - this mirrors `ask_in_repeat`'s placement: the head must read first (so an unreadable head stays `malformed_tag` or `unclosed_tag`), and the place outranks every attribute rule;
  - `write` and `ask` parse as today, and a stray `</note>` stays `unexpected_close`.
- Split `Parse` into two passes, which are then combined:
  - `parseArea(text string, opts ParseOptions) ([]Node, error)` is today's structure pass: `parseNodes` plus the top-level `unexpected_close` check, with no ask rules.
  - `checkAsks(title, body []Node, maxPerBody int) error` walks the title nodes, then the body nodes, with one `seen` map and one count. It stamps `AreaTitle` or `AreaBody` from the list the node came from. Duplicate before count on the same node stays.
- `Parse(text, opts)` runs `parseArea`, then `checkAsks` with the nodes in the slot `opts.TitleArea` names. Every error carries that area. A body parses exactly as before, so the 600 corpus verdicts do not move.
- `ParseTemplate(titleArea, body string, opts ParseOptions) (title, nodes []Node, err error)` ignores `opts.TitleArea` and runs three steps: `parseArea(titleArea, TitleArea=true)`, then `parseArea(body, TitleArea=false)`, then `checkAsks(title, nodes, opts.AskMaxPerBody)`. Structure is checked before asks, as `Parse` already does.
- `Asks(nodes)` and `Serialize` are unchanged. T333 calls `Asks` once per area.
- TS `lib/grammar.ts` mirrors Go one for one:
  - `export type TemplateArea = 'title_area' | 'body'`;
  - `'not_in_title'` is appended to `ParseReason` and `PARSE_REASONS`;
  - `ParseOptions.titleArea?: boolean`, where absent means body, so `TEMPLATE_PARSE_OPTIONS` and every caller stay unchanged;
  - `ParseFailure` becomes `{ line; reason; area: TemplateArea }` with `area` required. The inner scanners return an unexported `{ line, reason }` type, and `parse` and `parseTemplate` add the area;
  - `parseTemplate` returns `{ ok: true; titleNodes: TemplateNode[]; nodes: TemplateNode[] } | { ok: false; failure: ParseFailure }`.
- `templateAskFields(titleArea, body, options): AskField[]` runs `parseTemplate`, then maps the title's ask nodes followed by the body's, the same way `askFields` maps them (decoded label, flavor by text). It returns `[]` on any failure, because ① is not where a broken template is fixed. `askFields(body, options)` stays until T336 moves ① over.
- The required `area` breaks two test literals; add `area: 'body'` to both:
  - `ui/TemplateSource.test.tsx`: `failure={{ line: 1, reason: 'unclosed_tag' }}`;
  - `model/blocks.test.ts`: `toEqual({ line: 1, reason: 'unknown_tag' })`.
- The fixture harnesses change the same way on both sides.
  - Go: `fixtureCase` gains `TitleArea` (`json:"titleArea"`), `TitleNodes` (`json:"titleNodes"`) and `Error.Area`.
  - Every case runs `ParseTemplate(tc.TitleArea, tc.Body, fixtureParseOptions)` and asserts either nodes, titleNodes and both serializations, or reason, line and area (absent means `body`). When `tc.TitleArea == ""`, the case also asserts that `Parse(tc.Body)` returns the identical verdict.
  - TS `grammar.test.ts` does the same with `toEqual({ area: 'body', ...testCase.error })`.
- The corpus keeps the 600-body loop exactly (same pieces, seed `20260904`) and adds a second loop with `rand.New(rand.NewSource(20260923))`, which builds 200 unique (titleArea, body) pairs:
  - titleArea is 1–6 pieces drawn from `"<write>메뉴</write>"`, `"<write>"`, `"</write>"`, `"<ask label=\"가게\"/>"`, `"<ask label=\"총평\">별점</ask>"`, `"<ask label=\"총평\"/>"`, `"<ask/>"`, `"</ask>"`, `"<note>톤</note>"`, `"<slot kind=\"photo\"/>"`, `"<slot kind=\"video\"></slot>"`, `"<repeat each=\"photo\">"`, `"</repeat>"`, `"<writer>"`, `" | "`, `" "`, `"\n"`, `"가"`, `"3 < 5"`, `"&lt;"`;
  - body is one of `""`, `"<write>본문</write>"`, `"<ask label=\"총평\"/>"`, `"<ask label=\"a\"/>\n<ask label=\"b\"/>"`, so a cross-area duplicate and a cross-area `too_many_asks` are both reachable;
  - the verdict comes from `ParseTemplate`. An entry is `{titleArea, body, ok, line?, reason?, area?}`, with omitempty on the last three;
  - an accepted pair must serialize both areas back, or the test calls `t.Fatalf`;
  - it is stored as the `titleCases` key of the same map. `json.MarshalIndent` sorts the keys, so `_comment` and `cases` keep their bytes. Leave `_comment` as it is;
  - regenerate with `TEMPLATE_CORPUS_REGEN=1 go test ./internal/template/ -run TestCorpus`, and read the diff.
- TS reads `titleCases` beside `cases`. For each title case it runs `parseTemplate` and collects disagreements on ok, line, reason, area and round trip, the same way the corpus suite does.
