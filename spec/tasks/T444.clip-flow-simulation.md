## goal
② can show the current draft as a flow simulation — each cut one still frame from its start, the intro, the captions, the outro and the badge appearing and disappearing by their intervals as the scrubber moves — opened from a control on the preview's frame and sharing its scrubber.

## acceptance
- [ ] a control on the preview's frame beside the info control (`entities/clip-preview/ui/ClipDraftPreview.tsx` 474-492) switches between the video preview and the simulation, keeping the output second across switches
- [ ] per cut a still frame at its source start — from matching local media or an authorized retained original when one is available, otherwise the cut's number on a neutral ground; the server-drawn overlay assets (region blocks, each caption in its own style, the badge) shown by their intervals and held still (a sequence style shows one frame, CDS-85); no playback, motion or sound
- [ ] it follows the draft as it autosaves and needs no original; the scrubber's end shows the last frame, not an empty one
- [ ] the info control's copy says the simulation is still frames and not the delivered render (CLIP-176)
- [ ] item sheets (CLIP-53) keep opening over whichever of the two is showing
- [ ] tests: frame choice by time, the missing-frame fallback, overlay intervals, the switch keeping the second, the end frame
- [ ] verify FE: `pnpm --filter ./frontend test` · `pnpm lint` · `pnpm lint:fsd` · `pnpm lint:style:probe && pnpm lint:style` · `pnpm build:web`

- Copy: control `흐름 보기` / `Flow view` and back `영상 보기` / `Video view`; info line `멈춘 장면으로 흐름만 보여줘요. 실제 렌더와 다를 수 있어요.` / `Still frames show the flow only; the render may differ.`
