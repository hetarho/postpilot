## goal
Complete a new comparison with one atomic ranking of all successful candidates, or an explicit skip, and keep candidate-specific result actions separate.

## acceptance
- [ ] A new comparison in review/partial/failed can complete once with a dense ranking starting at 1 that contains every successful candidate exactly once when at least two succeeded, or with an explicit skip and no ranks. Equal ranks are valid. Missing, failed, unknown or duplicate candidate ids, rank gaps/invalid ranks, badges without a ranked candidate and notes over 200 characters are rejected atomically. Repeating the same completion is idempotent; a conflicting resubmission cannot edit ranks.
- [ ] A single successful candidate can only skip; after ranking or skip, owner RPCs reveal model identity, latency/token accounting and stored ranks/badges, while supplier cost remains master-only. Before completion they expose only opaque candidate ids, stable display positions and outputs. The completion and reveal survive reload, restart and content purge.
- [ ] Evaluation/skip never changes a post or active model. After completion an editor may apply one successful write result, optionally adopting that model; a lab owner may apply one successful post result while its post is draft/review and may independently adopt one successful model. A voice comparison offers adoption only. Applied/adopted candidate ids and timestamps make retries idempotent and reject a second different choice; publication, changed voice, unavailable model and failed application keep the existing guards and visible retry behavior.
- [ ] A completed but unapplied editor comparison remains the post's unresolved work; lab completion does not block ordinary generation. Ranking and result actions create no new provider call or credit debit.
- [ ] Historical two-candidate decided/dismissed/unpaired records, their badges and pending apply/adopt actions remain readable and actionable through their legacy RPCs; none is converted into a candidate ranking. A new record never writes a pairwise winner/loss outcome.
- [ ] Sweep/post purge delete private snapshots, outputs and free-text notes after the existing 30-day clock but retain ranks, badge ids, model/accounting snapshots and result-action metadata. Go domain, store and RPC tests cover every boundary above, legacy compatibility and account isolation; regenerate proto/sqlc and pass ARCH-26/28 checks.

## impl notes
- Add a forward migration after T500: extend the experiment status CHECK with `completed`, add a nullable positive `rank` on candidate rows and `completed_at`, `applied_candidate_id`, `adopted_candidate_id` on experiment rows. Use T500's persisted `review_mode` to route new and legacy RPCs. Preserve existing outcome/winner/decided fields for pairwise rows and all current indexes/foreign keys during SQLite table rebuilds. One transaction writes all submitted ranks/badges and the terminal completion timestamp.
- New `CompleteExperimentReview` takes `{experiment_id, repeated CandidateRank(candidate_id, rank, badges, other_note), skip}`. Exactly one of a complete ranking or `skip=true` with an empty list is required. Validate dense ranks and all-success coverage server-side. The response is the updated experiment.
- Add `ApplyCandidateOutput(experiment_id, candidate_id, adopt_model)` and `AdoptCandidateModel(experiment_id, candidate_id)` for new completed records. Keep the existing winner RPCs for legacy records only. Mark apply/adoption intent before the external post/provider selection call, then record success/failure separately so retries cannot repeat a write. The editor's apply-plus-adopt must not adopt until apply succeeds.
- Reuse the existing candidate badge catalog, normalized failure mapper, post write behavior and ownership ports. Store badges only for ranked candidates; no rank is inferred from a badge, a legacy winner or an applied result.
- Use `completed_at` for the new retention clock; keep legacy `decided_at` handling. `GetExperiment` must preserve the server-side blindness boundary for both record formats.
