# T108 Conditional transitions and loudness normalisation
> st:todo | ssot:CDS-35 CDS-36 CDS-37 CDS-52 CLIP-16 CLIP-18 CLIP-17 ARCH-21 ARCH-24 ARCH-32 | base:CDS@1 CLIP@5 ARCH@2 | dep:T105 T106

## goal
Join cuts with a hard cut unless the scene type changes, and deliver every clip at −16 LUFS with 60 ms audio cross-fades, without breaking the integer timeline the owner corrects.

## acceptance
- [ ] `clip.Cut` gains `TransitionMS` (the transition INTO this cut: 0 = hard cut, 200 = fade; 300 = fade-through-black accepted but never chosen by the compiler); the plan duration is `Σ cut lengths − Σ TransitionMS`, `ValidateEditPlan` requires cut 0 at 0 and every value in {0, 200, 300}, and `MatchRenderBatch`/`composeTimeline` use the per-cut value instead of `FadeMS × (n − 1)`
- [ ] the compiler sets 200 where the `scene` of consecutive cuts differs and 0 otherwise, then clears fades beyond 40 % of the boundaries keeping the earliest ones (CDS-36); cut lengths are held to 1.2–6.0 s and `food` ≤ 4.0 s (CDS-37) with the exposure extension of T105 allowed to exceed the maximum only by the copy's minimum
- [ ] the composition graph emits `xfade` and `acrossfade` only for boundaries with a transition and a `concat` for hard cuts, keeping cumulative frame rounding exact; the one-frame duration tolerance still holds on the probe
- [ ] audio: two-pass `loudnorm=I=-16:TP=-1.5:LRA=11` over the assembled track (first pass measures with `print_format=json`, second pass applies the measured values), a 60 ms `acrossfade` at every boundary including hard cuts, and T107's hook dip applied before normalisation; a fully silent plan still has no audio track
- [ ] V12: the output probe checks 30 fps, H.264 High, AAC 48 kHz and integrated loudness −16 LUFS ±1 (measured with a third `loudnorm` or `ebur128` pass on the result); a miss fails the render as a codec/format mismatch does today
- [ ] proto `ClipEditCut.transition_ms` (9); `ClipEditingState.fade_ms` is retired from the FE arithmetic: `editClipPlan` and `validateClipPlan` compute the duration from each cut's `transitionMs`, and step ② shows a per-cut 전환 `Listbox` (컷 · 페이드 200 ms) whose first cut is fixed to 컷; ko/en copy
- [ ] `RENDER.md` documents the transition rule, the two-pass loudness and the V12 additions
- [ ] tests: duration arithmetic with mixed transitions · the 40 % cap and scene-change rule · the length bounds · filter-graph goldens for concat-only, fade-only and mixed plans · the loudness measurement parser on a fixture JSON · the Docker smoke asserting −16 ±1 LUFS on a two-source plan with differing scenes · FE arithmetic and the transition control
- [ ] ARCH-26, ARCH-28 and ARCH-25 pass

## impl notes
- the transition belongs to the cut it leads into so a reorder in step ② keeps each cut's own entry and the first cut is always a hard cut; the FE reducer already recomputes the duration on every edit and only the formula changes
- two-pass `loudnorm` is the FFmpeg-documented way to hit an integrated target on a short file; single-pass drifts with dynamic mode. The measurement pass runs on the assembled intermediate before the final encode so the final render stays one encode
- `fade_ms` stays on `ClipEditingState` for one release as a compatibility field (200) and is removed by a follow-up when no client reads it; the FE stops reading it in this task
- CDS-36's fade-through-black is accepted by validation so a manual plan may choose it later, but no control offers it now (off by default)
