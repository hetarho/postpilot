## impl notes
- CDS-37 r3 is the whole change: the numbers stay, their force does not. The only HARD length rule left is `ValidateEditPlan`'s existing `plan_cut_fade` (a cut must outlast the transitions that eat into it), which is a renderability bound and not a rhythm one — do not add a new one
- the preset is a target and not a gate, so a 카페 template (4–6 s) with a 15 s goal does not have to land exactly three cuts; it lands what the model gave and holds what it can
- T108's `TestACutThatCannotReachTheFloorIsRefused` inverts: it becomes the proof that such a plan COMPILES. `TestRecordedLiveClipResponses` loses its refusal branch for the same reason — the recorded single take is executable again and the test says so
- `design.CutBounds` is the one place the preset range is read; `design.json`'s preset entries already carry `cut_min_s`, `cut_max_s` and `rhythm`, and `rhythm` stays unread (CDS-36 r3 is one flat 40 %)
