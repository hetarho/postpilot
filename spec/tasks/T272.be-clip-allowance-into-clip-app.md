# T272 the clip credit allowance and cancellation policy leave the queue
> st:todo | ssot:ARCH-6 ARCH-9 ARCH-11 ARCH-24 | base:ARCH@5 | dep:T285

## goal
Move the clip reservation policy, the call allowance and the clip cancellation rules out of `internal/job` into `clip/app`, leaving the queue with product-agnostic admission, guard and cancellation primitives.

## acceptance
- [ ] `backend/internal/job` has no file or identifier containing `Clip`: `job/clip.go` is gone and `ClipReservation ClipCall ClipGuard ClipStore ClipCancellationStore ClipKind ChargedClipKind KindGenerateClip KindRenderClip KindReviseClip ReserveClip ConsumeClipCall ConsumeClipPolicy GuardClips CancelClipJob ClipJobSnapshot LatestClipSnapshot AuthorizeClipDispatch` are gone or renamed generically; the three kind constants live in the clip context and every caller (`cmd/api/jobs.go`, `job/rpc/handler.go`, `job/store/store.go`, `clip/app/*`) reads them from there
- [ ] `job` imports no `internal/llm` in any file (`clip.go`, `ports.go`, `failure.go`, `worker.go`); the diagnostics that need a failure reason take it as a string or a `job.Failure`
- [ ] the queue's remaining seams are generic: `Guard(g Guard)` with `Guard{Admit(ctx, Start) error; Authorize(ctx, user, id) error}`, `Cancel(ctx, user, subject, id)` over a `CancellationStore{RequestCancellation, RecoverCancellations}`, and `AuthorizeDispatch`; the "which kinds may be cancelled and under which policy version" rule is supplied by the caller/wiring, not compared inline against a clip kind
- [ ] `clip/app` owns the allowance end to end: the context key, its mutex, the planned-vs-approved reconciliation and the policy validation `ReserveClip` did today (observe 8192 with inline-static delivery, write 32768 text-only, two writing calls on one model, count ≤ 49·(1+retries), cancellation policy version match), plus `ConsumeCall`/`ConsumePolicy`; `clip/app/metering.go` consumes from its own package and `llm.CallPolicy` stays a compile-time type all the way through
- [ ] the approved ceiling reaches the ledger through a clip-owned port instead of `job.Start.Clip`: `job.Start` keeps `{UserID, Kind, JobID, Calls}` only, and the charged-clip hold goes `clip/app` → its own credit port → `usage`, inside the same transaction the guard already opens (`clip/app/guard.go`)
- [ ] every allowance and cancellation test that lives in `internal/job` today (`clip_test.go`, `render_clip_test.go`, the allowance half of `worker_settlement_test.go`) moves to `clip/app` and passes unchanged in intent; `cmd/api`'s metering, cancellation and revision-credit tests keep their current expectations
- [ ] behaviour unchanged: ARCH-26 passes, and the clip release smoke runs once locally (ARCH-37) because reservation, dispatch authorization and cancellation are all on that path

## impl notes
- from review/arch-260919 F15 (part 2 of 3; T285 is subject addressing, T286 the ledger naming)
- owner decision 260919, option B (the allowance belongs to `clip/app`): production has exactly one caller on each side — reservation at [clip/app/jobs.go:91](backend/internal/clip/app/jobs.go#L91) and consumption at [clip/app/metering.go:30](backend/internal/clip/app/metering.go#L30) — so moving the whole mechanism keeps `llm.CallPolicy` typed instead of degrading it to an opaque payload in a generic queue allowance. If a second product ever wants the same shape, promote it then
- the state guard is already where it belongs: `Reservable` in [clip/app/guard.go:31](backend/internal/clip/app/guard.go#L31) checks the running/prepare/not-cancelled/policy-version conditions. Fold the rest of `ReserveClip`'s validation next to it, and keep the guard's single write transaction — that transaction is what makes a second reservation impossible after a crash
- `Start.Clip` disappearing means `cmd/api`'s `jobAdmission.Hold` ([cmd/api/credits.go:88](backend/cmd/api/credits.go#L88)) stops translating a clip reservation: the generic path maps `Calls` only, and the charged clip path holds through the clip port. Keep `Release`/`Settle`/`OpenHolds` as they are
- `ErrCancellationUnavailable` and `ErrCreditAllowance` are compared by callers and surfaced as failure reasons — keep the exported error values and their wire strings identical wherever they move
- `dispatch_ready` stays the column name (it names a queue fact, not a product); T285 already parameterized which kinds defer activation
- out of scope: any change to charge arithmetic or ledger naming (T286), and the schema (T285 added the derived subject columns; no further migration here)
