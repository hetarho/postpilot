# T185 The writer's cut budget follows the sections the answers admit
> st:todo | ssot:CLIP-103 | base:CLIP@27 ARCH@2 | dep:T183

## goal
Derive the assembly writer's cut budget from the sections a project's own answers actually admit, not from the template's authored prose.

## acceptance
- [ ] the budget counts a section only when the project's answers admit it: a repeated group contributes its admitted item count, and an optional section whose binding is blank contributes nothing (→CLIP-60)
- [ ] a template whose prose describes a section the answers leave empty produces a budget that the selected footage can fill
- [ ] the budget stays a bound on the plan, never an instruction to reach it — a shorter plan remains valid (→CLIP-99)
- [ ] tests: the same template with and without the optional answers, asserting the different budgets, and a repeated group at its minimum and above it
- [ ] verify BE (ARCH-26) passes

## impl notes
- the admitted-section count is the same derivation T183 puts beside `Document.Maxima`; read it rather than re-walking the document
- the budget feeds the writer's prompt bounds, so changing it changes the stated contract, not a post-hoc check
