# T272 job and usage expose primitives; clip composes them
> st:todo | ssot:ARCH-6 ARCH-11 ARCH-7 ARCH-24 | base:ARCH@5 | dep:T270

## goal
Remove every clip-specific method and type from `internal/job` and `internal/usage`, replace them with product-agnostic primitives, and move the clip compositions into `clip/app`.

## acceptance
- [ ] `internal/job` has no file or identifier containing `Clip`: `job/clip.go` and `ClipReservation ClipCall ClipGuard ClipStore ClipCancellationStore` are gone; `job.Queue` offers `Reserve(kind, subject, payload)`, `Activate(id)`, `SweepUnactivated(kind, ttl)`, `Guard(id, fn)`, `Snapshot(id)`, `AuthorizeDispatch(id)` keyed by job kind + opaque payload
- [ ] `job.Store.ActiveForPost/ActiveForVoice/ActiveModelExperiment` collapse into `ActiveFor(subjectKind, subjectID)`; `job` imports no `internal/llm`
- [ ] `usage/clip_pricing.go` is gone; `usage` exposes lot/ledger primitives and `clip/app` holds `ClipCredits`, `ClipAccounting`, `ClipReservation` as compositions
- [ ] the clip activations/reservations/sweeps/guards live in `clip/app` over the new primitives, with the moved tests passing
- [ ] behaviour unchanged: ARCH-26 passes and the clip release smoke runs once locally (ARCH-37) because reservation and cancellation are on the path
