# T189 The owner binds a source to an item before generating
> st:todo | ssot:CLIP-123 CLIP-62 | base:CLIP@27 ARCH@2 | dep:-

## goal
Let the owner bind one whole selected source to one item of a declared group where the sources are chosen, so every cut from that source inherits the item.

## acceptance
- [ ] each selected source offers the items of the project's declared groups, and choosing one records an association covering that source's whole duration
- [ ] a source is bound to at most one item, the choice is clearable, and a source with no declared group offers nothing
- [ ] every cut taken from a bound source resolves to that item whether observation names no subject or several, and automatic association applies only to unbound sources (→CLIP-62)
- [ ] the binding survives as a project input: it is saved with the project, frozen at generation and visible in the correction workspace beside the preview (→CLIP-69)
- [ ] replacing or removing a source removes its binding; a binding whose fingerprint no longer matches any selected source is ignored rather than refused
- [ ] tests: a bound source whose observation names two candidate items still resolves to the bound one, an unbound source keeps today's automatic behaviour, and the binding round-trips through save and generation
- [ ] verify BE (ARCH-26) and FE (ARCH-25) pass

## impl notes
- the wire and the store already carry this: `ClipSourceAssociation` and `CompositionInputs.Associations` are saved with the project, so no migration and no proto change are needed — what is missing is the pre-generation surface and the whole-source range
- record the range as `[0, duration_ms)` from the source's own metadata with its fingerprint; `BindCutItem` (`evidence.go:166`) already prefers an owner range that covers the cut completely and treats a partial one as a conflict, so a whole-source range needs no new branch
- the control belongs with source selection (`features/upload-clip-sources`), reading the declared groups through the project's composition document; the correction workspace keeps its existing per-range controls
- this is the fix for the failure that motivated the change: footage of one cut of meat carries nothing that distinguishes it from another, so subject matching cannot bind it and the owner is the only authority
