## goal

## acceptance
- [v] Source sound decodes selected ranges plus bounded decoder/WSOLA guard samples instead of allocating the whole long original AudioBuffer.
- [v] Every supported fixed rate preserves pitch and output sample boundaries; disabled sources contribute nothing and existing fades/hook dip affect source audio only.
- [v] Exact approved narration hashes, natural speed, placement and independent volume are preserved; required missing/stale/unsupported speech refuses before silent output can be produced.
- [v] AAC priming/tail handling, stereo rematrix, loudness/true-peak and video-track duration checks retain the current output contract.
- [v] PCM, intermediate arrays, encoded packet queues and speech reuse have named finite budgets; failure/cancellation releases buffers and siblings.
- [v] Tests compare selected-range audio to native/current reference cases for every rate, source-only/narration-only/mixed output, long originals, seams and priming; real-browser checks and required local CI pass.
