## goal
Process only selected original-audio ranges while preserving pitch, exact narration and final output loudness.

## acceptance
- [ ] Source sound decodes selected ranges plus bounded decoder/WSOLA guard samples instead of allocating the whole long original AudioBuffer.
- [ ] Every supported fixed rate preserves pitch and output sample boundaries; disabled sources contribute nothing and existing fades/hook dip affect source audio only.
- [ ] Exact approved narration hashes, natural speed, placement and independent volume are preserved; required missing/stale/unsupported speech refuses before silent output can be produced.
- [ ] AAC priming/tail handling, stereo rematrix, loudness/true-peak and video-track duration checks retain the current output contract.
- [ ] PCM, intermediate arrays, encoded packet queues and speech reuse have named finite budgets; failure/cancellation releases buffers and siblings.
- [ ] Tests compare selected-range audio to native/current reference cases for every rate, source-only/narration-only/mixed output, long originals, seams and priming; real-browser checks and required local CI pass.

## impl notes
- Use Mediabunny AudioSampleSink/AudioBufferSink for range access and existing @soundtouchjs/core 2.1.1 plus local loudness/encoder-delay code; no background-music feature or new voice supplier is introduced.
- Account for the complete selected PCM footprint before allocation, including stretch/mix/normalization work buffers. Sharing one mutable array must not corrupt concurrently encoded output.
- Use exact source sample offsets and codec warmup/tail handling; narration is never subjected to footage-rate stretching.
- Preserve canonicalSpeechBuffer and immutable speech fingerprints. T539/T550's real voice/listening qualification remains an independent unmet gate.
- Realtime preview may consume the same schedule and bounded cached ranges; final AAC/loudness proof comes from encoded-output verification.
- Do not change T586/T587 registration/pricing contracts; consume their resulting public speech surface after normal freshness checks.
