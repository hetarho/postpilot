## goal
설정 and 결제 stop drawing two copies of the credits card, and a paid account's 충전 stops sending the owner to another screen and scrolling to packs near its bottom. One shared credits card (balance figure, tier, the credits illustration, 충전) serves both. On a paid tier 충전 opens the existing pack purchase (pack choice → quote → confirm dialog → purchase) in a sheet over the screen it was pressed on; `/billing#billing-charge` opens that sheet on arrival; the billing screen loses its packs section and opens on a large title like 설정. A free account's 충전 still goes to `/plans`; master sees the packs' own disabled state.
