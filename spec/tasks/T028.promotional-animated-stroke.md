# T028 The plan ladder earns its animated stroke
> st:todo | ssot:THEME-37 THEME-27 THEME-12 THEME-28 QUOTA-28 | base:THEME@4 QUOTA@5 ARCH@2 | dep:T027

## goal
Give every rung and the estimator a rotating gradient stroke, with the recommended rung carried further, inside the design language's own tokens and motion rules.

## acceptance
- [ ] a `shared/ui/promo-frame` primitive wraps arbitrary content in the effect: a rounded container whose stroke is a rotating conic-gradient layer on a pseudo-element, masked so only the stroke shows, with the content on `surface-raised` above it
- [ ] the animation moves `transform` only — the gradient layer rotates — and never a background position or a layout property (THEME-28); one shared `@keyframes` and `duration`/`ease` tokens, no per-call-site timing literal
- [ ] the gradient stops resolve to registered roles: three new functional roles on the accent foundation (`--stroke-promo-from` `--stroke-promo-via` `--stroke-promo-to`) defined once and exposed through `@theme inline`, mapped in both semantic maps and never a palette literal (THEME-11, THEME-15); `pnpm lint:style` and `lint:style:probe` stay green
- [ ] `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` leaves the stroke visible and still rather than removing it, and a test asserts the animation class is absent under that preference while the stroke element remains
- [ ] the recommended rung is carried further than its peers — a brighter stroke and `shadow-md` — and its meaning is still carried by the `Badge` text, never by the glow alone (THEME-7's "no state by colour alone")
- [ ] the four rungs and the estimator strip use it; nothing else in the app does, and a test asserts no other route renders the primitive
- [ ] content contrast is unchanged: the stroke sits outside the padding box, so every figure inside keeps the `surface-raised` pair it was audited against (THEME-7)
- [ ] `PromoFrame.test.tsx` covers the still fallback, the recommended variant, and that the effect is a stroke rather than a fill (the content element keeps its own surface class)
- [ ] `pnpm --filter ./frontend test` · `pnpm lint` · `pnpm lint:fsd` · `pnpm lint:style:probe && pnpm lint:style` · `pnpm build:web` pass

## impl notes
- the mechanism: a relative container, `::before` absolutely inset with `background: conic-gradient(from 0turn, <from>, <via>, <to>, <from>)`, `animation: promo-spin var(--duration-promo) linear infinite`, and a `padding`-sized inner element on `surface-raised` covering all but the stroke ← masking with `mask-composite` is the tidier trick but its Safari support still needs the `-webkit-` pair, and the inner-element version needs no prefix and no `border-image`
- animate a full turn of `rotate` on the gradient layer, not `background-position` ← THEME-28 allows `transform` and `opacity` only, and a rotation is a compositor-only change so four of these cost no layout work
- reduced motion is honoured by NOT applying the animation class, leaving the same gradient painted at rest ← removing the stroke entirely would make the recommended rung indistinguishable for a reader who asked only for less motion
- `duration-promo` (a slow turn, several seconds) joins the duration scale rather than living at the call site; the existing three steps are for hover, reveal and page transitions and none of them describes an ambient loop (THEME-28)
- the estimator strip and the cards share one primitive so their radii and stroke widths cannot drift; `rounded-lg` for the cards keeps THEME-27's radius scale and the frame adds no second radius of its own
- this is the one place THEME-27's "no gradients on surfaces" is relaxed, and THEME-37 scopes it to promotional surfaces; the primitive lives in `shared/ui` but its NAME says what it is for, so a reviewer sees the exception being reused rather than a generic decoration spreading
