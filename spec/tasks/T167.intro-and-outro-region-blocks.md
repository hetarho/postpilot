# T167 Render the intro and outro blocks instead of the hook and ending cards
> st:todo | ssot:CDS-70 CDS-71 CDS-72 CDS-73 CDS-74 CDS-28 CDS-29 | base:CDS@15 ARCH@2 | dep:T166

## goal
A clip opens and closes with the fixed, card-free region blocks the owner chose, filled only with template text.

## acceptance
- [ ] `role="hook"` renders the intro block: hairline at y 846, slot 1 `t.title` 72 Paperlogy white with `shadow.text` centred on y 960, hairline at y 1010, slot 2 `t.label` 36 `text.muted` +0.02em on y 1090; a hairline is 520 × 2 px `text.white` α0.55
- [ ] `role="ending"` renders the outro block: slot 1 `t.label` 36 `text.muted` +0.08em on y 860, slot 2 `t.hook` 132 Paperlogy white on y 980, accent rule 160 × 6 px on y 1030, slot 3 `t.caption` 44 white α0.8 on y 1110; every slot carries `stroke.dark` and `shadow.text`
- [ ] slots come from the element's `<row>` list in declared order — at most 2 for intro, at most 3 for outro — and a further row is an authoring error
- [ ] a slot whose text resolves empty is omitted and the remaining slots keep their fixed y; nothing reflows (CDS-73)
- [ ] the `ink.900s` plate, `radius.card`, `shadow.card` and the card geometry (`hook_card`, `end_card`) are gone from the renderer and from `design.json`
- [ ] automatic caption placement uses the CENTER column only; LEFT and RIGHT remain reachable only from an explicitly authored element (CDS-74)
- [ ] the hairline and accent rule are `rect` manifest elements within the existing `ElementKinds`, so V10 still passes
- [ ] test: both blocks in all three ratios stay inside the safe area and meet the V2 size and V3 contrast floors
- [ ] test: an empty middle slot leaves the others at their declared y
- [ ] test: a third intro row or a fourth outro row is refused before rendering
- [ ] `cd backend && test -z "$(gofmt -l .)" && go vet ./... && go build ./... && go test ./...`

## impl notes
- the chosen geometry is intro variant B and outro variant E from `clip_sample_tmp/explore/`; `gen.py` there holds the exact y values and is the reference the numbers were read from
- the grammar keeps the role names `hook` and `ending` — only what they draw changes — so no stored template needs a new role
- `layoutDeclaredCard` in `media/composition_roles.go` is the function that becomes the two block layouts; card measurement in a scratch workspace can go with it
- the rules are new manifest parts but need no new kind: draw them as `plate` rects, which V10 already admits
