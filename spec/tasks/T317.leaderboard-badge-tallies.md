# T317 Leaderboard rows tally their badges
> st:todo | ssot:MODEL-38 MODEL-41 MODEL-63 ARCH-3 ARCH-6 ARCH-24 | base:MODEL@14 ARCH@7 | dep:T315 T316

## goal
- Each leaderboard row shows the badges its model collected inside the same scope, stage and window, positive and negative counted separately and 기타 counted without its notes.

## acceptance
- [ ] GetLeaderboard entries carry `badge_tallies` (badge, count) computed from the winner verdicts of the same window and scope; `other` is a count only; tallies change no rating, match count or order.
- [ ] `all` scope tallies expose per-model counts only — no notes, accounts or experiments.
- [ ] A row shows up to three positive and three negative badges with counts, ordered by count then catalog order, and nothing when it has none; phones keep the three-column row with the chips wrapping under the label; ko/en.
- [ ] Tests: BE store (tally query per scope/window, `other` counted, notes absent), domain (tallies attach to the right entry and never touch ranking), handler mapping; FE (tally rendering, empty, ordering); ARCH-25/26/28 gates pass.

## impl notes
- Proto: `message BadgeTally { VerdictBadge badge = 1; int32 count = 2; }`; `LeaderboardEntry.badge_tallies = 19` (`repeated BadgeTally`). `pnpm gen:proto`.
- Store: `ListBadgeTalliesForLeaderboard` (+ `All` variant without `user_id`): join `model_experiment_badges` to `model_experiment_candidates` on `(experiment_id, id)` and to `model_experiments`, `WHERE e.stage = ? AND e.outcome = 'winner' AND e.decided_at >= ?`, grouped by the candidate's model ref columns and badge; returned by `OutcomeLedger.LeaderboardData` as a third slice (`[]BadgeTally{Model, Badge, Count}`). ASCII-only query file; `pnpm gen:sql`.
- Domain: `LeaderboardEntry.BadgeTallies []BadgeTally` attached in `BuildLeaderboard` after ranking, sorted by count desc then catalog order, only for models already on the board.
- FE: `LeaderboardEntry.badgeTallies` in the entity type and mapper; `widgets/model-leaderboard` renders `Badge` chips (`tone` by polarity) reading `{{label}} {{count}}`, with labels through the entity's badge helper from T316.
