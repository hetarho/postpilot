## goal
Replace monthly lump-sum renewal with lazy, idempotent daily/monthly benefits and reservations that keep their original period.

## acceptance
- [ ] Initial confirmed paid coverage creates the full first daily grant, monthly bonus and monthly export window once; repeated reads/admissions/payment confirmation cannot replenish spent amounts. Free accounts receive neither daily grants nor a fictitious reset time.
- [ ] A read/admission after inactivity creates only the current eligible daily and monthly windows; missed days/months do not accumulate. Annual coverage renews monthly benefits without another payment; paid expiry stops grants even before a delayed renewal worker catches up.
- [ ] An upgrade atomically changes current rights, adds the ceil-prorated monthly bonus and floor-prorated export allowance, preserves anchors and leaves today’s daily entitlement unchanged even if its lot had not yet been materialized. The next daily window uses the new tier.
- [ ] Lots spend by expiry then creation, with nonexpiring promotional lots ahead of purchased lots. A reservation remembers original lot/window ownership across reset and expiry; unused expired funds do not become spendable and a non-clip overrun cannot draw a new window’s grants.
- [ ] GetMyPlan can project owned lots, daily/bonus grant and next reset, paid coverage and benefit-window identity; export usage counts are completed by T482. Support assignment uses the same atomic entitlement transitions without charging a card or duplicating grants.
- [ ] Real-SQLite tests race reads/admissions/renewal/upgrade, cross a reset with an active hold, retain purchased/voucher lots, exercise coincident boundaries and verify rollback of failed composite mutations. Run ARCH-26/28.

## impl notes
- Extend the existing credit_lots/credit_hold_lots/usage_admissions rather than create a second wallet. Add daily lot identity, originating coverage/window identifiers, issuance cause and payment/upgrade correlation. Retain monthly as the monthly-bonus kind and bonus for independent operator grants; compensation is added by T478.
- Use a unique grant key based on account + coverage identity + benefit kind + exact window start, not a KST date alone. Preserve granted and remaining history; grant creation is INSERT-if-absent, never an upsert that refills spent funds. Index current-window and expiry-order reads.
- Consumer-declared paid-coverage/tier-effective-at ports must give the daily grant tier at its window start, so upgrading before the first read cannot grant the higher daily allowance early. Persist the tier transition/coverage identity instead of deriving every historical window from users.plan.
- Billing owns payment coverage; usage owns generic lots/admissions; clip owns monthly export-window limits and later reservations. Compose their transaction-scoped ports in the owning context’s app package under ARCH-6 and reuse the existing one-writer transaction pattern. Do not query another context’s tables or perform provider I/O in the transaction.
- Prepare clip’s monthly benefit-window open/raise behavior here so billing can update credits and export allowance atomically; T482 attaches render reservations/completion. Repeated upgrade/payment ids must not reapply either delta.
- Keep explicit operator-assigned access distinguishable from card-funded coverage, preserving the existing support assignment path and last-master guard; assignment must not fabricate provider charges, auto-renewal or cash-refund eligibility. Preserve independent credit lots on plan changes.
- Ordinary admission snapshots the eligible funding window/lot set, not a user-wide permission to spend any later-created grant. Clip admissions still use their approved/reserved ceiling. An internal retry of the same admitted work retains its snapshot; an explicit new job must re-admit.
