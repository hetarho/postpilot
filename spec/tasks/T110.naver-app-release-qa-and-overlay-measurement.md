# T110 Release QA on the Naver app and the overlay measurement
> st:todo | ssot:CDS-10 CDS-11 CDS-53 CLIP-27 ARCH-24 | base:CDS@1 CLIP@5 ARCH@2 | dep:T107 T108

## goal
Play a rendered clip on the real Naver app, measure where its overlays actually fall, and turn the estimated safe area into a measured one or confirm it.

## acceptance
- [ ] the owner uploads a rendered 9:16 clip privately through the Naver Clip picker and captures screenshots of the clip tab with the right action column, the bottom information block, the information-tag chips and the top tabs visible; the agent never presses a publish or submit control (the T074 rule)
- [ ] the screenshots are normalised to 1080×1920 and the four overlay bounds are measured and written to `docs/design/naver-clip-overlay-measurement-YYMMDD.md` with the device, app version and method
- [ ] if any measured bound lies outside the SA-N estimate by more than 20 px, the `safe` and SA-N constants in `design.json` are changed in a follow-up commit and the T102 pin test updated; if not, the estimate is confirmed in the same document
- [ ] the CDS-53 checklist is run on three rendered clips (one per preset used so far) and recorded: muted recall of two facts, subject known within 1.5 s, copy reads as editor-placed, tone constant, disclosure legible
- [ ] `update-ssot CDS` closes CDS-11 with the measured or confirmed values (owner's call on 16:9 and 1:1 display, which the same session records)
- [ ] no automated gate beyond `ARCH-26` on the constants change; the measurement is manual by nature and its evidence is the document

## impl notes
- this task needs the owner present with the Naver app; it does not block T102–T109 and can run as soon as T107 produces a card-bearing render
- only `design.json`'s `safe` values may change here (CDS-10: constants, not rules); a rule change goes back through update-ssot
