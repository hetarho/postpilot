# T110 Release QA on the Naver app and the overlay measurement
> st:blocked@260912 | ssot:CDS-10 CDS-11 CDS-53 CLIP-27 ARCH-24 | base:CDS@1 CLIP@6 ARCH@2 | dep:T107 T108

## goal
Play a rendered clip on the real Naver app, measure where its overlays actually fall, and turn the estimated safe area into a measured one or confirm it.

## acceptance
- [ ] the owner uploads a rendered 9:16 clip privately through the Naver Clip picker and captures screenshots of the clip tab with the right action column, the bottom information block, the information-tag chips and the top tabs visible; the agent never presses a publish or submit control (the T074 rule)
- [ ] the screenshots are normalised to 1080×1920 and the four overlay bounds are measured and written to `docs/design/naver-clip-overlay-measurement-YYMMDD.md` with the device, app version and method
- [ ] if any measured bound lies outside the SA-N estimate by more than 20 px, the `safe` and SA-N constants in `design.json` are changed in a follow-up commit and the T102 pin test updated; if not, the estimate is confirmed in the same document
- [ ] the CDS-53 checklist is run on three rendered clips (one per preset used so far) and recorded: muted recall of two facts, subject known within 1.5 s, copy reads as editor-placed, tone constant, disclosure legible
- [ ] `update-ssot CDS` closes CDS-11 with the measured or confirmed values (owner's call on 16:9 and 1:1 display, which the same session records)
- [ ] no automated gate beyond `ARCH-26` on the constants change; the measurement is manual by nature and its evidence is the document

## impl notes
- this task needs the owner present with the Naver app; it does not block T102–T109 and can run as soon as T107 produces a card-bearing render
- only `design.json`'s `safe` values may change here (CDS-10: constants, not rules); a rule change goes back through update-ssot

## result
blocked: every acceptance item needs the owner in front of a real phone — the Naver app, its Clip picker, its own screenshots. Nothing here can be measured from this machine, and guessing the four overlay bounds would put a wrong number into `design.json`, which is the one thing CDS-10 forbids.

Ready for the owner, in order:
1. **Render a 9:16 clip to upload.** Any current clip project through step ③, or `docker build --target media-smoke -f backend/Dockerfile .` with `CLIP_SMOKE_EXPORT=<dir>` set to keep the smoke's own vertical render. It must carry a hook card, an ending card, the disclosure badge and at least one chip — otherwise the bottom information block cannot be measured against anything.
2. **Upload it privately through the Naver Clip picker and stop.** No publish, no submit: T074's rule. Screenshot the clip tab with the right action column, the bottom information block, the information-tag chips and the top tabs all visible.
3. **Send the screenshots back.** Normalising them to 1080×1920, measuring the four bounds and writing `docs/design/naver-clip-overlay-measurement-YYMMDD.md` (device, app version, method) is mechanical and this session can do it from the images.
4. **Then the decision:** any bound more than 20 px outside SA-N changes `safe`/SA-N in `design.json` (and the T102 pin test) in a follow-up commit; otherwise the estimate is confirmed in the same document. Either way CDS-11 closes with `update-ssot CDS`, and the owner calls the 16:9 and 1:1 display question in that session.
5. **CDS-53 on three rendered clips**, one per preset used so far: muted recall of two facts, subject known within 1.5 s, copy reads as editor-placed, tone constant, disclosure legible. That is the owner's own viewing, recorded in the same document.

Verified here, so it is not what the owner has to re-check: the Docker `media-smoke` stage passes on the real binaries as nonroot — `TestMediaSmoke`, `TestMediaProfileSmoke` and `TestRenderSmoke` — over the three ratios, the mixed transition timeline and both cards. What is missing is only what the phone can show.
