## goal
Let a template author say "ask the post's author for this" on an AI가 쓰는 글 or 고정 문구 row, and teach the copyable 형식 안내 the construct that says it.

## acceptance
- [ ] `shared/ui/switch`: a `Switch` primitive — `role="switch"` with `aria-checked`, its own 44 px hit area over a drawn track the way `Checkbox` does it, tokens only, disabled state at 50%, labelled by the caller
- [ ] the expanded AI가 쓰는 글 and 고정 문구 rows carry a `데이터 받기` switch; the 사진, 사진마다 반복 and AI에게만 하는 말 rows do not
- [ ] turning it on reveals a `제목` field seeded from the row's own text with a live counter against `TEMPLATE_ASK_LABEL_MAX_CHARS`; turning it off restores the row's authored text and drops the title
- [ ] a 고정 문구 row with the switch on shows the 제목 field in place of its text field (the title IS that row's authored content); an AI가 쓰는 글 row keeps its instruction field beside the title
- [ ] on a row nested in 사진마다 반복 the switch renders disabled with its reason stated in place, never hidden
- [ ] the collapsed row keeps its kind badge and adds the 데이터 받기 mark plus the title in its one-line summary
- [ ] flipping the switch makes the draft dirty by itself, and 저장 refuses a duplicate title with the same in-place message the parser's `duplicate_ask_label` produces
- [ ] `entities/template/model/guide.ts` teaches `<ask>` — both flavors, `label` required and unique, not inside a repeat, the ceiling, and that a field the post's author leaves off disappears from the post — with no retired construct and the tags left as they are
- [ ] tests: the switch appears on exactly two kinds · on → 제목 → body carries `<ask label="…">`, off → back to `<write>`/literal · the nested row's switch is disabled and says why · a duplicate title is refused · the collapsed summary · the guide text names `ask` and the guide snapshot test still passes

## impl notes
- the switch is a NEW `shared/ui` primitive, not a reused `Checkbox` (owner's call): a checkbox says "this row is selected" and this control says "this position's data comes from outside", and ARCH-20 forbids a slice hand-rolling either. THEME-29's roster owes a `Switch` line — see STATE next; nothing else in the design language changes
- the composition editor stays in `entities/template/ui` (→TEMPLATE-33): this is a controlled input over the entity's own `body` field and performs no mutation
- the row is the same block edited in place (→TEMPLATE-44), so the switch writes through the existing complete-block contribution path in `blocks.ts` (T055 already round-trips `ask`) — do not introduce a second representation of a row's state
- seeding the title from the row's text is what makes the flip cheap for the body the owner already wrote (a 고정 문구 line reading `총평 별점` becomes the question); the seed happens once, on the flip, and the two fields are independent afterwards
- duplicate titles are caught in the editor rather than only at save because the parser refuses the body outright — showing it on the offending row is the only way the author can tell WHICH two collided
- the guide and the grammar rule ship together (→TEMPLATE-41): a construct added to TEMPLATE-18 lands in the guide in the same change, so the guide edit is part of this task and not a follow-up
