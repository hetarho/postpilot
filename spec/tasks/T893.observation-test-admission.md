## goal
The experiment side accepts, admits, stores and projects a writing test with output `observation`: its shape is validated, its photos are attached at admission, its candidate outputs hold observations, and reads return photos and blind observations.

## acceptance
- [ ] Deterministic, unit (`experiment`): `ValidateTestShape` accepts output `observation` only with factor `model`, stage `observe`, model entrants and 1..`UPLOAD_MAX_PHOTOS_PER_POST` unique photo ids, and with no source post, material text, attachments, writer/observer models, voice, template, guideline slot, template answers or quality rules; output `post` refuses photo ids; an unspecified wire output reads as `post`; the start fingerprint covers output and photo ids.
- [ ] Deterministic, unit (store): `AdmitTest` writes `output` and attaches the photos in the admission transaction; photos not ready, foreign or attached to another test refuse with `ErrTestMaterialInvalid` and nothing is written; the row's output maps back onto `WritingTest.Output`.
- [ ] Deterministic, unit (output + rpc): an observation output round-trips through `EncodeTestOutput`/`DecodeTestOutput` under its own version while version-1 post outputs decode unchanged; `writingTestToProto` sets `output`, lists the frozen photos with a signed `view_url` while the payload exists (none after purge) and each succeeded candidate's `observations` with `model` empty until reveal; `ApplyOutput` refuses an observation test; adopting an observe-stage champion goes through the existing model adoption.
- [ ] Deterministic, unit: affected `cd backend && go vet ./internal/experiment/... && go test ./internal/experiment/...` plus the ARCH-24 task lint pass. Hand off `go test ./cmd/api/...` and the full BE suite to the batch gate.
