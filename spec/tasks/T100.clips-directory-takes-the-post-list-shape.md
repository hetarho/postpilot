# T100 The clip directory takes the post list's shape
> st:todo | ssot:CLIP-41 POST-43 POST-65 POST-66 POST-67 POST-68 POST-69 | base:CLIP@5 POST@5 THEME@10 ARCH@2 | dep:T097 T099

## goal
`/clips` presents one row per project the way `/posts` does — edge to edge, one status badge, a relative time, and a search and status filter carried in the URL — instead of five stacked `meta` lines with no state and an absolute date.

## acceptance
- [ ] `shared/ui/list-controls`: one domain-agnostic `ListControls` composed from `FieldLabel` + `TextField` + `SegmentedControl`, taking the search label, placeholder, value and change handler plus a `filter` (`value`, `options`, `onChange`, `ariaLabel`); no product noun in its name or props, no debounce (the scan is over one loaded answer), and it renders at every item count
- [ ] `features/filter-posts`' `PostListControls` becomes a thin typed wrapper over it and its existing tests pass with no expectation changed
- [ ] `features/filter-clips`: `ClipNarrowing = { q?: string; status?: ClipState }`, a pure `narrowClips(projects, narrowing)` matching the title case-insensitively on the trimmed query and the status on `clipState`, and a `ClipListControls` wrapper over `ListControls`
- [ ] the `/clips` route gains `validateSearch` for `q` and `status` ∈ `draft | refining | finished`, dropping anything it cannot honour so a hand-edited URL shows the list rather than an error, exactly as `/posts` does
- [ ] narrowing is written with `replace` and an emptied field drops the param rather than carrying `?q=`
- [ ] the page: `pageStyles({ width: 'wide', gutters: false })`, the heading and the controls inside the page's own gutters, and `divide-divider divide-y` rows that run edge to edge
- [ ] a row is one whole-row `Link` at `min-h-11` carrying the title truncating, then one `Badge`, the video template's name, the ratio and a `<time>` of `formatRelativeTime(updatedAt)`; stacked on a phone and one line from `lg:` up, the title taking the free space and the metadata settling right

- [ ] a load failure is a `Notice tone="danger"` with a `pending={isFetching}` retry; one live region carries both the pending and the empty state; a narrowing that matches nothing names what is narrowing and offers 초기화

- [ ] ko/en `clips` gains the search, filter, no-match and reset copy plus the dock's aria label; the locale parity test passes

## impl notes
- `ListControls` goes to `shared/ui` rather than being copied into `features/filter-clips`: `PostListControls` is 52 lines whose only domain-specific parts are its namespace, its statuses and its type, and two slices reaching for one shape is a missing primitive (THEME-10, ARCH-20). `filter-clips` cannot import `filter-posts` — same-layer cross-import is forbidden (ARCH-13) — so the only alternatives were the shared control or a second copy
- the badge reads `clipState` from T097, so the directory and the working screen share one vocabulary; a page-local status would let a row say 초안 while the step bar says 다듬기
- `latestJob` on a list row arrives with T099. Without it every row badges from the revisions alone and a running generation is invisible, which is the dep
- the ratio and the template are metadata AFTER the badge, not columns of their own: the five stacked `meta` lines are what this replaces, and at 360px a fourth object on the row is what pushed the post title down to ten Hangul
- `formatRelativeTime` and `shared/lib/datetime.ts` are reused as they are — no new formatter, and the absolute `toLocaleDateString` goes
