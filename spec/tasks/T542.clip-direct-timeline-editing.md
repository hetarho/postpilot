## goal
Make the timeline behave predictably for common video edits on pointer, touch and keyboard.

## acceptance
- [ ] Background seek and playhead drag share one output clock; cut-edge drag trims valid source ranges, cut drag reorders and splitting at the playhead creates valid source-bound cuts.
- [ ] Every drag has a reachable touch/keyboard or precise numeric alternative; pointer cancel/Escape discard a drag, undo restores it once, and focus/scroll/selection remain stable.
- [ ] Cut/rate/transition edits recompute output intervals while owner caption/speech placements stay unchanged; invalid bounds appear on their items instead of silently retiming/deleting them.
- [ ] Pure-operation and Testing Library tests cover transition overlap, non-1x source/output conversion, boundaries, minimal widths, same-source splits, cancellation, keyboard and one undo record per completed drag.

## impl notes
- Keep timeline geometry and operation reducers in features/correct-clip/model using clip-plan pure timeline helpers. Use Pointer Events and pointer capture with explicit drag intent; block drag commits on pointerdown and separate item selection from reorder activation.
- Use existing SortableList/dnd helpers where their touch/keyboard semantics fit; otherwise add the smallest domain-neutral handle primitive in shared/ui. Do not add a large third-party timeline editor or new visual language.
- Convert edge delta on output time to source-time delta by each fixed rate and clamp to its observed source interval/minimum valid span. Preserve transitions through existing validation; split rejects positions in unsupported overlap instead of inventing a source frame.
- Keep ruler labels outside narrow bars, visible source/output unit labels in properties, precise entry and move-up/down alternatives. Autosave only validated final operations; intermediate pointer movement previews a transient draft with no RPC per pixel.
