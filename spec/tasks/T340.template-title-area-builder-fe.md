# T340 The template screen authors a title area in the builder and in 원문
> st:todo | ssot:TMPL-2 TMPL-20 TMPL-26 TMPL-30 TMPL-43 TMPL-50 ARCH-20 ARCH-24 THEME-29 | base:TMPL@10 ARCH@9 THEME@17 | dep:T336

## goal
- The template screen gains an optional 제목 형식 section above 템플릿 구성, driven by the same 블록/원문 switch.
- The builder offers only AI가 쓰는 글 and 고정 문구 there, each with 데이터 받기.
- 원문 shows the title as its own textarea with its own error and counter.
- A parse failure is shown in, and fixed from, the area that failed.

## acceptance
- [ ] One `SegmentedControl` (블록 · 원문) sits above both sections and switches both. The 제목 형식 (선택) section comes first, then 템플릿 구성.
- [ ] The title builder behaves as follows:
  - its toolbar (`제목에 추가`) offers exactly AI가 쓰는 글 and 고정 문구, never 사진, 사진마다 반복 or AI에게만 하는 말;
  - both rows carry the 데이터 받기 switch;
  - every field is single-line;
  - a counter runs against `TEMPLATE_TITLE_AREA_MAX_CHARS`;
  - rows are joined by one space;
  - builder → title area → builder is byte-identical for anything the builder produced (TMPL-29).
- [ ] A body row asking under a label the title area already uses:
  - shows `builder.reasons.duplicate_ask_label` on the body row (TMPL-43);
  - is left out of the body, and 저장 stays disabled;
  - returns to the body once the title stops using that label.
- [ ] In 원문, `제목 원문` is its own textarea above `원문`.
  - Each textarea shows only the failure whose area it names, at that area's line, and has its own counter.
  - 원문 복사, 형식 안내 복사 and 형식 안내 보기 belong to the body alone (TMPL-26, TMPL-42).
- [ ] 저장 is enabled only when all of these hold:
  - the draft is dirty, with `titleArea` compared untrimmed;
  - `canSaveTemplate` passes;
  - both numbers are valid;
  - `parseTemplate(titleArea, body)` succeeds;
  - neither area reports an ask conflict;
  - no save is pending.
- [ ] A title area that does not parse on its own shows the title section as unreadable with the two actions of TMPL-30. The body section is untouched.
  - `원문에서 고치기` opens 원문 with the caret in `제목 원문` and the error shown at its line.
  - `제목 비우고 다시 만들기` empties only the title area in the draft and writes nothing.
- [ ] A body that does not parse behaves exactly as today.
- [ ] In 블록 mode, a failure that exists only across both areas (e.g. `too_many_asks` counting title and body) renders its reason under the area it names.
- [ ] The FE spec for `TEMPLATE_PARSE_FAILED` accepts an optional `area`, so T333's refusal no longer degrades to `UNKNOWN_FAILURE`.
- [ ] Grammar stays in 원문: with a title area and every title row open, the list and the builder render no grammar.
- [ ] Tests:
  - `entities/template/model/blocks.test.ts`: the title-area round trip, space join and one-space strip; `canInsert` refusing photo, repeat and note in a title; `duplicateAskTitles` and `toValidBody` with `taken`;
  - `entities/template/ui/TemplateComposition.test.tsx`: the title variant's palette (exactly two kinds), single-line 고정 문구, counter and space-joined emit; `takenAskTitles` raising the row message and leaving the row out, then re-emitting when the label is released;
  - `entities/template/ui/TemplateSource.test.tsx`: the title variant's label, counter and failure, with no copy or guide controls;
  - `pages/template/ui/TemplatePage.test.tsx`: a title edit alone makes the draft dirty and saves `titleArea`; the builder → 원문 → builder title round trip; the cross-area label message and refused 저장; title unreadable → 원문에서 고치기 focuses `제목 원문` with its error; 제목 비우고 다시 만들기 clears only the title; `too_many_asks` across both areas shown under the body;
  - `pages/template/ui/GrammarStaysInternal.test.tsx`: the fixture gains a title area, the builder test opens every row in both areas, the 원문 test reads `제목 원문`, and an unreadable title shows no grammar.
- [ ] Verify: `pnpm --filter ./frontend test` · `pnpm lint` · `pnpm lint:fsd` · `pnpm lint:style:probe && pnpm lint:style` · `pnpm build:web`

## impl notes
- `entities/template/model/blocks.ts` gains an area variant (R36), with `TemplateArea` imported from `lib/grammar` (T327):
  - `export const TITLE_AREA_PALETTE: readonly PaletteKind[] = ['write', 'text']`;
  - `canInsert(kind, position, area: TemplateArea = 'body')` also refuses `photo`, `repeat` and `note` when `area` is `title_area`, and `insertAt(blocks, position, kind, area = 'body')` passes the area on. The palette hides the buttons; the model enforces the rule;
  - `toBody(blocks, area = 'body')` joins with `'\n'` for the body and `' '` for the title ← a title is one line and its parts are words, so newline joins would put line breaks into the post's title;
  - `fromBody(body, decodeText, options, legacyNames, area = 'body')` parses with `{ ...options, titleArea: area === 'title_area' }`;
  - in the title it strips exactly one `' '` from each literal edge (`stripOneSpace`, the twin of `stripOneNewline`, which also drops a whitespace-only literal as a separator), so `toBody` and `fromBody` stay inverses;
  - `duplicateAskTitles(blocks, taken: ReadonlySet<string> = new Set())` starts with `seen` seeded from `taken`;
  - `toValidBody(blocks, area = 'body', taken = new Set())` leaves out every colliding row, including one that collides with `taken`;
  - with the defaults, every existing call behaves byte for byte as today.
- `entities/template/ui/TemplateComposition.tsx` gains two props, `area?: TemplateArea` (default `'body'`) and `takenAskTitles?: ReadonlySet<string>`. In the title variant:
  - `readBody` and `push` pass the area on;
  - `AddToolbar` shows `TITLE_AREA_PALETTE` under the label `composition.titleArea.add`. The group needs its own name because page tests find the body toolbar by `블록 추가`;
  - that toolbar is not sticky ← two sticky bars would stack under the header while the body scrolls;
  - the empty state is `composition.titleArea.empty`;
  - the 고정 문구 `Field` is single-line (`TextField`); AI가 쓰는 글 and 입력란 제목 are single-line already;
  - `BodyCount` counts against `TEMPLATE_LIMITS.titleArea` (T336);
  - `Unreadable` uses `composition.titleArea.unreadable` and `composition.titleArea.clearAndRestart`.
- Only the body composition receives `takenAskTitles`. Title labels come first in the namespace (TMPL-55, T327's order), so the body row carries the message and the title composition is never blamed.
- The body re-emits when `takenAskTitles` changes. A `useEffect` keyed on the sorted, `\u0000`-joined labels skips its first run. On each later change it emits `toValidBody(blocks, area, taken)` whenever that differs from `emitted.current`.
  - Without this, a body row that stops colliding would stay out of the saved body: the silent drop TMPL-44 forbids.
  - Skipping the first run stops a hand-written stored body from being rewritten, and made dirty, when the screen opens.
- `TemplateComposition` also gains `failure?: ParseFailure | null`: a failure the screen found across both areas. It renders `builder.reasons.<reason>` as a `FieldMessage role="alert"` under the list, with the same `{ max, askMax }` interpolation `TemplateSource` passes.
  - It renders nothing while the composition shows `Unreadable`.
  - It renders nothing for `duplicate_ask_label`, which the offending row already states.
- `entities/template/ui/TemplateSource.tsx` gains `area?: TemplateArea`, and the body variant is unchanged. The title variant uses:
  - the label `source.titleAreaLabel` (제목 원문);
  - textarea id `template-title-source` and error id `template-title-source-error`;
  - `rows={2}`, and a counter against `TEMPLATE_LIMITS.titleArea`;
  - no copy buttons, no status line and no guide `<details>`.
- A hand-written newline inside a title literal is lost once that row is edited in the builder, because the field is single-line. 원문 keeps it. This is the same class as the body builder's reflow of hand-written spacing (TMPL-29 covers builder-produced text only).
- `pages/template/ui/TemplatePage.tsx`:
  - dirty adds `trimmed.titleArea !== baseline.titleArea`;
  - `field()` accepts `'titleArea'`;
  - `const parsed = parseTemplate(trimmed.titleArea, trimmed.body, TEMPLATE_PARSE_OPTIONS)` replaces `parse(trimmed.body, …)`, and `blocked` uses it;
  - `askConflict` splits into two `useState` booleans, `titleAskConflict` and `bodyAskConflict`, and `blocked` holds while either is true. Each composition's `onAskConflict` receives its setter directly: a stable reference, as `setAskConflict` is today. A fresh arrow would re-run the composition's report effect on every render;
  - `titleAskLabels = useMemo(() => new Set(askFields(draft.titleArea, { ...TEMPLATE_PARSE_OPTIONS, titleArea: true }).map((f) => f.label)), [draft.titleArea])` feeds the body's `takenAskTitles`;
  - `focusSource` becomes `TemplateArea | null`. The body's `onFixInSource` sets `'body'` and the title's sets `'title_area'`. Each `TemplateSource` gets `autoFocus={focusSource === area}`, and picking 블록 clears it;
  - in 원문, each `TemplateSource` gets `failure={!parsed.ok && parsed.failure.area === area ? parsed.failure : null}`;
  - in 블록, each `TemplateComposition` gets the same filtered failure through its new `failure` prop;
  - the title's `onClear` empties only `titleArea`. Nothing is written until 저장 (TMPL-30).
- Layout (THEME-29 primitives only, tokens only):
  - the `SegmentedControl` moves from inside the composition section to directly after the number fields (`mt-8`), keeping `ariaLabel` `screen.mode.aria` and `controls={COMPOSITION_PANEL_ID}`;
  - one `role="tabpanel"` with id `COMPOSITION_PANEL_ID` wraps both sections;
  - the title section is `<section aria-labelledby="template-title-area-heading">`: a `Typography variant="title"` heading `screen.titleArea.heading` plus `form.optional` (common), the help line `screen.titleArea.help`, then the editor for the current mode;
  - the composition section keeps its heading 템플릿 구성, its mode help and the body editor.
- `frontend/src/shared/api/app-failure.ts`: `TEMPLATE_PARSE_FAILED: { required: ['line', 'reason'], optional: ['area'] }`. The `errors.ts` copy is unchanged.
