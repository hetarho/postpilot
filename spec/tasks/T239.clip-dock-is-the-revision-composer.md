# T239 ②'s dock is the revision composer over 다시 렌더 and 확정하기
> st:todo | ssot:CLIP-40 CLIP-131 CLIP-132 | base:CLIP@34 | dep:T238

## goal
②'s one ActionBar becomes two rows — the revision request's composer at the bottom and 다시 렌더 beside 확정하기 above it — with the credit approval opening over the composer and nothing else in the dock.

## acceptance
- [ ] ②'s dock holds exactly two rows: 다시 렌더 and 확정하기 above, and the revision composer — target, field and send — below
- [ ] the composer is reachable without scrolling at every scroll position and at 360 px, and the field grows with its content without pushing the rows out of reach
- [ ] pressing send opens the credit approval over the composer; approving starts the revision and nothing is sent before approval
- [ ] while a revision runs, the composer row is replaced by its progress and cancellation and the page stays where it is
- [ ] no revision panel stands in ②'s page and no download stands in the dock
- [ ] vitest: the dock holds the two rows and nothing else; typing and sending opens the approval without starting; approving starts once with the typed target; a running revision shows progress and cancellation in the dock
- [ ] verify: `pnpm --filter ./frontend test` · `pnpm lint` · `pnpm lint:fsd` · `pnpm lint:style:probe && pnpm lint:style` · `pnpm build:web`

## impl notes
- `ClipRevisionRequest` moves from the workspace's panel into the dock and loses its `section`/heading shape; its `useClipRevision` wiring, its `flush` prop and its quote/approval path are unchanged
- the composer row is target + field + send on one line from `sm:`, wrapping to field-over-row on a phone; the target stays a `SegmentedControl` of the three `CLIP_REVISION_TARGETS` and the send is an icon `Button` carrying `revision.send` as its name
- the approval is `ClipQuoteApproval` inside a `Popover` anchored to the send control with `placement="above"`, `align="end"` and `phone="sheet"` — the dock default — so it opens over the composer rather than growing the dock
- the character count moves to the field's own message slot; `boundedText` with `CLIP_PROJECT_LIMITS.instruction` is unchanged
- `revision.help`, `revision.title` and the panel's read-only line are cut; keep `revision.readOnly` for the running row
- THEME-24 already admits a dock of more than one row, so no primitive changes; `ActionBar` keeps `dock="always"`
- the workspace's `revision` prop and its panel slot are removed; `ClipPage` passes the composer to the dock instead
