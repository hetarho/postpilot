# T702 Choose the HDR-to-SDR conversion by measuring it on real phone footage

## goal
Pick the one versioned conversion CLIP-219 needs: how a BT.2020 HLG or PQ source becomes SDR BT.709. The pick must look right and must be the same in the browser and in native.

## acceptance
- [ ] Two candidates are built and measured.
  - A, signal pass-through: HLG is converted with the BT.2020 matrix only and shown as BT.709. In the browser, the pinned decoder colour space becomes `{matrix: 'bt2020-ncl', primaries: 'bt709', transfer: 'bt709'}`. In native, `scale=in_color_matrix=bt2020`. A does not cover PQ.
  - B, a fixed 33³ 3D LUT applied after the matrix conversion. It follows ITU-R BT.2408 for HLG and the BT.2390 EETF for PQ, both to BT.709 at 100 nits. In native it runs through ffmpeg `lut3d`; in the browser, through one WebGL2 3D-texture pass in the frame pipeline.
- [ ] Each candidate is measured four ways:
  1. A frame grid of the same moments, compared with AVFoundation's own tone-mapped export (`avconvert --preset Preset1920x1080`) as the display-intent reference.
  2. Browser cost per frame at 1440×1920 on the identified Mac (ARCH-67), cold and warm.
  3. Browser-against-native parity: per-channel mean absolute difference on sampled frames.
  4. Added asset and bundle size.
- [ ] exit: when 3 h have passed, result records the chosen candidate with the grids' location, timings and parity numbers. If A wins, CLIP-219's PQ half has no conversion: record that update-ssot must narrow CLIP-219 to HLG and refuse PQ by name. If neither looks acceptable, record why and propose update-ssot instead of shipping one.

## impl notes
- Real footage:
  - Use any HLG phone clip; the 참선주면옥 originals are HEVC Main 10, BT.2020, `arib-std-b67`, from 1080×1920 to 1440×1920. The owner's footage is only measured locally and is never committed.
  - Make a PQ sample locally with `libx265` (`-x265-params colorprim=bt2020:transfer=smpte2084:colormatrix=bt2020nc`) from an HLG clip or `testsrc2`, labelled as synthetic.
- The production ffmpeg is `--disable-everything` with an allowlist (`backend/build/media-tools.sh`). B needs `lut3d` added there, and the LUT ships as a versioned asset beside the fonts. Measure B natively inside the media image, not with Homebrew ffmpeg (memory: the ffmpeg filter allowlist).
- `frontend/src/shared/lib/media/video/source-color.ts` holds today's SDR-only rule and `NATIVE_SOURCE_COLOR_VERSION`. Every consumer that draws a source reads it through `range-video.ts` and `analysis-copy.ts`.
- This is measurement only. No production code changes and no paid model call. T703 implements the pick.
