# T235 ②'s timeline reads at any cut length
> st:todo | ssot:CLIP-54 CLIP-55 CLIP-38 | base:CLIP@34 | dep:T234

## goal
Every cut on ②'s timeline shows its own thumbnail from first draw, no track label overlaps its neighbour at any cut length, and undo/redo become icon controls at the timeline's head with the save state left to the one status region.

## acceptance
- [ ] every cut draws its thumbnail as soon as the track renders, with no click or playhead needed; the film glyph remains only where no playable source is available
- [ ] the second ruler, the cut labels and the text bars never overlap a neighbour at any cut length; a bar too narrow for its own label carries none
- [ ] undo and redo are icon controls at the timeline's head, each named for what it undoes, at the pointer floor THEME-23 sets
- [ ] the workspace no longer prints 저장됨 / 저장 중 / 저장할 변경 사항 있음 — that state is the page-top status region's alone
- [ ] vitest: a plan of short adjacent cuts renders one label per cut with none overlapping (asserted on the computed geometry, not pixels); every cut has a thumbnail before any selection; undo/redo are reachable by their accessible names
- [ ] verify: `pnpm --filter ./frontend test` · `pnpm lint` · `pnpm lint:fsd` · `pnpm lint:style:probe && pnpm lint:style` · `pnpm build:web`

## impl notes
- `ClipTimeline` currently gates the `<video>` on `active || selected` and falls back to the `Film` glyph; drop the gate and keep the glyph for a cut whose fingerprint has no entry in `localSources` and no resolved playback
- overlap comes from absolutely positioned labels with no width bound: give each ruler tick and each label the width of its own cut (`left`/`width` in the same percentage basis the `<li>` already uses) and `truncate`; drop a ruler tick whose cut is narrower than `CLIP_TIMELINE.minLabelPx` — add that token to `shared/config` rather than a literal
- the label-fit decision is pure geometry, so compute it in a helper under `entities/clip-project/model` and test the helper directly; the component test only asserts the rendered result
- undo/redo icons: `Undo2` / `Redo2` from lucide-react, `Button` `variant="ghost"` `size="icon"`, `aria-label` from `timeline.undo` / `timeline.redo`; they move into the row that heads `ClipTimeline` rather than the workspace's own control row
- the caption's move-up / move-down buttons stay where they are in this task — T236 takes them into the caption sheet
- delete the `Typography role="status"` carrying `timeline.saving|unsaved|saved` from `ClipCorrectionWorkspace`; `ClipStatusLine` already reports it under CLIP-38, so the keys stay in the resource files
