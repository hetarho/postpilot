## goal
Keep clip editing previewable and downloadable until explicit confirmation, with focused progress and clearly priced cancellation while a job runs.

## acceptance
- [ ] A successful first render and every rerender stay in ②; current draft preview and identified latest successful MP4 download remain available before confirmation, including after a failed/cancelled attempt or source expiry.
- [ ] One ② ActionBar carries primary 확정하기 plus 다시 렌더/download; confirmation flushes pending settings, association and plan saves and refuses stale/missing renders before opening ③ on successful finalization.
- [ ] Finalized projects reopen in ③ with only result preview/download plus existing list/delete navigation; editing steps and mutations cannot be reopened, and download alone never changes project lifecycle or retention.
- [ ] Queued/running/cancelling work shows one central real-stage/progress region and cancellation/list navigation, hides settings/steps/inspection/correction controls and duplicate progress, and preserves mounted queues, uploads and polling across navigation within the page.
- [ ] Cancellation pricing and original-retention/finalization effects are visible before their actions; settled use, cancellation addition, total debit and refunded credits are distinct, with cancelling/free/exempt/unknown states correctly translated.
- [ ] Returning to a running job restores focused progress; cancelled work has its own list/status label and explicit retry with a new AI quote, preserved previous result and reusable originals, without automatic restart.
- [ ] UI/browser tests cover double clicks, pending/failed saves, plan-render conflicts, ambiguous finalize/cancel responses, completion races, refresh, source expiry, previous-result download, navigation during work, both locales/themes, keyboard focus and 320/360/390/430 px layouts.
- [ ] Run applicable ARCH-25 through ARCH-29 local gates, deterministic proto generation when changed and spec lint; preserve unrelated generated changes using a before/after snapshot.

## impl notes
- Update pages/clip/model/steps.ts and clip list projections: finalized marker alone yields completed/③; otherwise editing state yields ② regardless of matching MP4. Keep ①/② selectable while idle and unfinalized; selecting unreached ③ only explains confirmation and links to ②. A finalized record exposes no route back into editable panels.
- Compose cancel-clip and finalize-clip action features through entities/clip-project APIs from the page. Reuse shared/ui ActionBar, Button and progress/status primitives; introduce no feature-to-feature imports, generic editor framework or second vertical scroller. The focused job block belongs to the clip page unless another actual consumer needs a widget.
- Adapt the existing post finalization interaction and save-flush contract, not its learning popup: one explicit 확정하기 button, no extra confirmation modal. Show beside it that confirmation deletes originals and ends editing; disable it with a concrete reason if no matching export exists. Keep secondary 다시 렌더 and the latest-render download in that same ActionBar.
- Drain all pending save queues in order, then use the returned saved revision and matching result identity for FinalizeClipProject. Keep local drafts until confirmation succeeds; conflict/failure stays in ② with correction intact. For an ambiguous RPC result, refetch the owned project before permitting a retry or further mutation; idempotent finalized responses open ③.
- Render the busy presentation after an accepted start and whenever polling reports queued/running, including cancel_requested_at. Keep upload/save/query hooks mounted outside conditionally visible panels and pause unused video playback without destroying retained media identity. One live region announces stage changes; use actual progress units/indeterminate state, never a synthetic overall percentage or duplicate top bar.
- Place the cancellation rule at credit approval and beside 취소, using T134's published rate, rounding and job-specific reservation. Before reservation say no charge yet; after reservation show the formula, not an exact refund unless the server has enough confirmed evidence. After click show 취소 중 and disable duplicate submission until authoritative state; returning 목록 never sends cancellation.
- Enable the versioned cancellation contract only when the approval surface displays it and the start request echoes that version. Older in-flight jobs retain their legacy policy and display cancellation unavailable for that attempt. Legacy accounting without a breakdown stays absent rather than invented; master shows zero debit with optional shadow breakdown distinctly labelled.
- Download uses the existing result URL and identifies its rendered revision when the draft differs; it stays credit-free and does not flush/overwrite the draft or imply finalization. After finalization revoke local source URLs and clear source access caches; keep only confirmed-result viewing/downloading and read-only retained project metadata required by the result screen.
- Preserve T124 observations in non-running editable steps, including previous-generation labelling after failure/cancel; hide them during focused work and after finalization. The source-retention notice has its own policy meaning and must not duplicate the live job/save status line.
