## goal
③'s 이 체험으로 클립 만들기 creates a clip in the chosen clip channel from the post's title, memo, 체험단 글, guide and videos, then opens it.

## acceptance
- [ ] Offered whenever canonical content exists (published posts too); a dialog picks 네이버 클립 or 인스타 클립 and confirms; cancel creates nothing.
- [ ] The clip is created vertical in that channel with the post's title, its memo as the instruction (when the memo exceeds the clip instruction bound of 1000 characters, the first 1000 are carried and the dialog says the rest stays in the post), its campaign switch, and the guide's text, keywords and hashtags.
- [ ] Each attached post video is read through its view URL in the browser and added as a clip source through the existing source upload pipeline within CLIP-6's limits; videos beyond the limits are left out and named; photos are not carried.
- [ ] No AI starts, the post is unchanged, and the browser lands on the new clip in its channel context; a failed video transfer leaves the clip with the videos that arrived and says which did not.
- [ ] Tests: `pages/editor/ui/EditorPage.clip-from-post.test.tsx` `creates a clip in the chosen channel from the post`, `cuts a long memo at the instruction bound and says so`, `carries no photos`; the source pipeline test for a fetched blob.
- [ ] vitest for touched slices, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm lint:fsd` pass.
